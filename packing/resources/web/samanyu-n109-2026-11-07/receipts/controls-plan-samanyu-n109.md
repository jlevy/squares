# Maintained Replay Controls for Samanyu’s 109-Square Witness

The pinned source is `best_fit_101_500.csv` at Samanyu-dev/squares_in_squares
`53551d77513a9f5c180a731f291baf7eda7c3009`. The raw CSV has SHA-256
`377b9177febf1b89f08d655531880fb683666e494a3b303f10645e7440996c04`.
The input to the maintained campaign is the canonical fact emitted by the CSV
adapter with interval-derived rational half-angle tangents.

The small 21 November preflight verified the positive input through both maintained
routes and checked that a tiny nonzero tilt survives the conversion. This plan governs
the official packet replay and its controls.

Both maintained routes must accept the unmodified witness. Both must refuse the
duplicate-square overlap and the square translated beyond the container. Each route
must decide all 5,886 pairs for each of the three jobs.

The sharp controls take an exact wall or pair margin to contact and then cross
it by (10^{-30}). Each route must accept unit geometry at contact and refuse the
crossing while reporting the expected witness. The enlarged-square controls must
also detect non-unit shape.
