## 2. Diagnosis of the exactsolve failure: one near-rattler in the corner, not the packing
* Witness: not a local minimum only at the 1e-11 level.  `exact/descend.py` (MILP direction kick + slp2) reaches
  17.5972493911567 in one round (dS* then −5.5e-8 at tol 1e-6, below the stop threshold); same value as the batch polish.
* Polish: contacts and equilibrium are fine (tol 1e-9, jam-LP residual 5.6e-9), but the Gauss–Newton projection stalls
  (1.2e-13 → 6.6e-24 in 21 steps, linear rate ~0.3): the "independent" contact subset (rank tol 1e-9) has one singular value
  1.9e-7 (cond 2.9e7).  Its singular vector is the rotation of **square 0** (top-left corner, θ = 89.99997°, i.e. tilted
  4.8e-7 rad in the polish, 2.0e-5 rad in the witness) and the 4-contact loop {0 corner on left wall, 15's side ↔ 0's
  corner, 15 ↔ 14, 14 on left wall}.  Square 0 sits in the corner with 0.0275 of slack to its right neighbour (square 1);
  its bottom-right corner rests on square 15's top-left corner.  A tilted corner gives it a spurious load path; the exact
  configuration has it axis-aligned and force-free (a corner–corner touch with 15).  Rotating a corner whose lever arm is
  ~sin θ makes the Jacobian nearly singular, so f64 Newton directions are wrong in that direction and projection stalls.
* **Fix (input only, no code change):** set square 0 to (0.5, S − 0.5, 0°) in the polish (`runs/s292/fix0a.txt`; variant
  `fix0b` with y lowered 0.02 gives the identical S).  exactsolve then converges in 5 projection steps (→ 4.7e-69) and 7
  Newton evaluations (cond 5.2e3):

  **S = 17.597249391156465040471442005510389607032374738062745629985644408** (exact KKT point, 80 digits);
  certificate S' = **17.597249391156465040647414499422**, VALID under `verify_cert.py` and `verify_cert2.py`
  (`runs/s292/ex_fix0a/`, gitignored; tracked copies `candidates/s292_fix0a.{input.txt,cert,exact.txt,json,log}`).  Δ vs register **−4.31e-11**: the analytic optimum of Couzo's
  packing (f64 slack removed), like the 50 other batch entries below the register; *not a new structure*.
  Strict local minimum modulo 119 exact flat motions (PD on the rest of null(J_A)); min λ = 2.15e-4 over 1398 load-bearing
  contacts; jammed in every corner–corner branch (234 touches); 15 free squares.
* Possible tool fix (not done): in `KKT.polish`/`setup`, drop rows whose R-diagonal is < ~1e-6·max (here 1.9e-7 vs the next
  9.2e-3), or snap near-axis squares (|θ| < 1e-4) whose only load path is a corner to θ = 0 before solving.
