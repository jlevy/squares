## Exact Mathematical Argument

For each candidate, the exact auditor expands each listed rectangle into its eight $D_4$
images, assigns each image one eighth of its listed mass, and sums rational densities
where images coincide.
All masses are nonnegative and every positive-mass rectangle lies in $[0,L]^2$. The
expanded density integrates exactly to the listed total mass, which is strictly less
than the requested number of squares.
The source and expanded density admission caps also hold.

| Premise | n84 | n85 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Container side $L$ | $3769/400$ | $379/40$ |
| Total mass $M$ | $8399999/100000$ | $8499999/100000$ |
| Mass gap $n-M$ | $1/100000$ | $1/100000$ |
| Source rectangles | 815 | 838 |
| Distinct expanded rectangles | 6,464 | 6,672 |
| Core side $B$ | $9977/10000$ | $9977/10000$ |
| Half-angle tangent step $D$ | $83/40000$ | $83/40000$ |
| Net indices | $0,\ldots,200$ | $0,\ldots,200$ |

Both independently recomputed nets have last tangent $t_{200}=83/200$ and
$t_{200}^2+2t_{200}-1=89/40000>0$, placing their last angle beyond $\pi/4$. The last-bin
lower endpoint satisfies $(t_{200}-D/2)^2+2(t_{200}-D/2)-1=-4544311/6400000000<0$. The
exact shrink product is $B(1+D)=399908091/400000000<1$, with margin $91909/400000000$.
The stronger admitted tangent expression is also below one:
$B(1+D/(1-D^2/4))=63985225828447/63999931110000<1$.

Fold each individual unit square’s orientation into $[0,\pi/4]$ using a $D_4$ symmetry
of the container and measure.
For its half-angle tangent $t$, some declared node has $|t-rD|\le D/2$. Writing $\delta$
for the angular difference, $z=\tan(\delta/2)=|t-rD|/(1+trD)\le D/2$. The concentric
core’s extent in either unit-square axis is $B(1+2z-z^2)/(1+z^2)\le B(1+D)<1$. Pulling
this core back by the same symmetry gives a core strictly inside the original unit
square with the same capture.
This argument applies separately to each square; it does not require one reflection to
fold all packing orientations simultaneously.

At node $r$, put $a_r=\max(0,rD-D/2)$ and $\rho(a)=(1+2a-a^2)/(2(1+a^2))$. Because the
unit-square half-width increases on the folded angular range, its centre is in
$[\rho(a_r),L-\rho(a_r)]^2$. Quarter turns preserve both the density and the square at a
fixed net orientation, reducing that domain to $[L/2,L-\rho(a_r)]^2$. This is exactly
the candidate’s `per-bin` domain.
The fold is at $\pi/4$, not at the last net angle beyond it.
Every recomputed control pose, positive point and near witness satisfies this exact
quadrant-domain test.

The retained native verification supplies capture at least one throughout each such
domain. If $n$ unit squares packed in the container, their strictly interior cores would
be pairwise disjoint, giving $n\le\sum_i\mu(Q_i)\le M=n-1/100000$, a contradiction.
This establishes each claimed lower bound under the native verification assumptions.
The corresponding source arguments are N0–N4, C1 and D in
[`SOUNDNESS.md`](../../../.worktrees/review-668-math-20261018/packing/sqverify_fast/SOUNDNESS.md).

## Audit Method, Reproduction and Limits

The [local driver](audit_checkpoint.py) reuses the inspected, unchanged
[`exact_geometry.py`](../n68-certificate-math/exact_geometry.py) and small validation
helpers from the [n68 auditor](../n68-certificate-math/audit_checkpoint.py).
It does not invoke the n68 case driver.
The case-specific changes require the observed successful outer exits, corrupt a refused
index for the all-refused control partition, and enforce a hard wall ceiling with
`SIGALRM` plus internal deadlines and bounded Git reads.
The native binary is never invoked.

The geometry uses only the standard library.
It constructs the rational rotated square, clips it against each intersecting
axis-aligned rectangle, computes area by the shoelace formula, and sums rational density
times area. This clipping direction is the reverse of the maintained auditor’s
rectangle-to-square clipping.
Floating-point saved coordinates are converted with `Fraction(float)`, retaining the
exact binary64 points used by the native run; axis rational strings remain exact.

The reused [analytical check](../n68-certificate-math/check_geometry.py) passed all 12
full, half, quarter and zero-area fixtures at three rational rotations in 0.024863
seconds under its own 30-second alarm.
Its [result](geometry-sanity.json) is retained.
Each case audit rejected three in-memory corruptions: a duplicate capture index, a
duplicated and missing control index, and an altered exact refusal fraction.
The genuine checkpoints were read only.
