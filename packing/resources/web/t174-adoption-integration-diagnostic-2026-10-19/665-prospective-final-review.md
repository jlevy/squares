For a rational centre and half-angle parameter $t$, the vectors
$u=((1-t^2)/(1+t^2),2t/(1+t^2))$ and $v=(-2t/(1+t^2),(1-t^2)/(1+t^2))$ are perpendicular
unit vectors. The four corners $c\pm u/2\pm v/2$ therefore describe a unit square.
Exact containment of those corners in $[0,L]^2$ and non-overlap of the square interiors
give a feasible packing and hence $s(n)\le L$. For convex squares, separation on an
edge-normal axis of either square is sufficient and necessary; equality permits touching
boundaries. The alternative exact intersection-area route tests zero overlap area.
These conclusions require the certificate’s declared count, side, scalar representation
and source geometry to agree with what each route read.

`packing/frontier/evidence.yaml:35311` records complete checks at all 64 rational

The maintained routes share parsing, half-angle conversion, Python `Fraction` arithmetic
and the separating-axis method.
The third route shares rational arithmetic and format meaning but uses half-extent
containment and intersection area.
None of those statements establishes optimality, a local minimum, rigidity, new
construction authorship or human oversight.
The evidence explicitly retains those limits.
