# Base cases k = 7, 8, 9

The family covers k ≥ 10. The three smaller cases are separate certificates by three groups.

| k | n | result | author | record | level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 44 | s(44) = 7 | Khalili, Gharabaghi | [T-252](https://squaremath.org/results.html#t-252), jlevy/squares#842, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23769766 | V3/C3, confirmed |
| 8 | 59 | s(59) = 8 | H. Hosono | [T-066](https://squaremath.org/result/t-066.html), jlevy/squares#280; Lean proof T-235 | V3/C3, confirmed |
| 9 | 76 | s(76) = 9 | B. Greff | [T-184](https://squaremath.org/result/t-184.html), jlevy/squares#594 | V3/C3, confirmed (raised 2 Nov 2026) |

k = 10 (s(95) = 10 and s(96) = 10, Greff, T-191, V3/C3 since 2 Nov 2026) is also covered by the family; it is not needed as a base case.

k = 5, 6 (n = 20, 31) are open. Covers of this kind very likely cannot prove them (float LPs put the optimal cover
above n), so the claim starts at k = 7.

All base cases are at V3/C3 (checked 3 Nov 2026).
