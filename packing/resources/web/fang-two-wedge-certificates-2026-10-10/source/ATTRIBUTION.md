## Builds on

- M. Z. Arslanov, S. A. Mustafin and Z. K. Shangitbayev, "Improved packings of n(n − 1) unit squares in a square", Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 28(4) (2021) P4.22, doi 10.37236/8586: the k² − k + 1 two-wedge family.
- David Ellsworth and Tej Stead: the n = 241, 273 and 307 packings on the Kingbird table (https://kingbird.myphotos.cc/packing/squares_in_squares.html), used as seeds.
- Evan Daniel (https://github.com/evand/square-packing): the certificate text format and the strict verifier convention.
- SQUISH (Nate Chaoweeraprasit): the json certificate layout.
