checked in both dimensions by `rectcheck.py` and by the research repository's `squarepack.verify --strict`
(below). Daniel's checker reads only `n W` from a rectangle header, so on the rectangles it checks the
W × W square, i.e. the width. The 70 square certificates (11 direct, 19 Daniel's, 2 inputs, 38
composites) pass all three checkers. CPU time: verify.py 53 min, Daniel's 53 min, rectcheck.py 53 min (on a loaded machine).
