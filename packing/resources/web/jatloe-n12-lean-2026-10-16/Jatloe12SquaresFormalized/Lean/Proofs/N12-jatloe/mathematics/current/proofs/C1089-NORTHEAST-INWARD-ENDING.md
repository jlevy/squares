# C1089: the northeast-inward sector closes by two strict-point alternatives

All decimals denote exact rationals. The actual owners and finite local ownership entrance are retained exactly from C1089-CONTINUOUS-REDUCTION.md, Section 1. Their common side satisfies \(1<a\le1.001\), their open interiors are disjoint, and their closures lie in the original container. Excluded points may be tangent.

**Theorem.** Every C1089 configuration in the complete closed sector
\[
 Y_8\le2,\qquad Y_{10}\ge2,\qquad X_{11}\ge2                 \tag{1}
\]
is impossible.

## 1. Two compulsory central pairs from the actual wall signs

Write \(A=Q4,S=Q5,E=Q6,N=Q7\) for the southwest, southeast, northeast and northwest original central owners. By Proposition 2 of C1089-CONTINUOUS-REDUCTION.md,
\[
 F4,G3\in A.                                             \tag{2}
\]
The actual right and top wall signs in (1) give
\[
 \{3\}\times[2.22,2.39]\subset Q10,\qquad
 [2.22,2.39]\times\{3\}\subset Q11                         \tag{3}
\]
strictly, by the wall-sign chord theorem. In particular F9 belongs to Q10 and G7 belongs to Q11.

The continuous northeast proof in C1092-MMMM-CONTINUOUS-REDUCTION.md, Section 2, uses only these two wall chords, the NE original marker, the NE original covering rows, and the actual paired-corner point (3,3). All these are present in C1089: that corner is Q2. It therefore applies unchanged to E. Explicitly, the F/G alternatives reduce to F5 or F7 and G6 or G9. Simultaneous F7,G9 ownership contradicts OUTWARD-CORNER-TWO-GAPS.md with \(r=t=.39\), since its two gap points are the ends (2.39,3),(3,2.39) of the two actual chords and its corner is in Q2. If F5 were absent, E would own F7,G6 and omit G9; the quarter-turned mixed-span theorem with excluded F5,G7,F9,G9 gives a contradiction. If G6 were absent, E would own F5,G9 and omit F7; the reflected mixed-span theorem with excluded G6,F9,G7,F7 gives the same contradiction. Consequently
\[
 F5,G6\in E.                                             \tag{4}
\]

The actual Q9 owns F6,G5 in every original C1089 alternative. Equations (2),(4) and the southeast covering rows force
\[
 F8,G8\in S,\qquad G6\notin S.                            \tag{5}
\]
Indeed F4,F6 are foreign to S, so its F row forces F8; G5,G6 are foreign, so its G row forces G8. No exact southeast label or optional auxiliary is selected.

## 3. An exhaustive F7 ownership split gives a duplicate strict point

If E excludes F7=(2.4,3), the reflected inward theorem, Theorem 1 of C1084-DUPLICATE-POINT.md, applies to E's marker,F5,G6. Its three exclusions are the SE marker in S, NW marker in N, and F7 by this branch. It gives
\[
 (2.46,2.01)\in E.
\]
Theorem 2 of that same chapter, reflected through y=2, applies to S using the complete table in Section 2. It gives the identical strict point in S, contrary to disjoint interiors.

If instead E strictly owns F7, then Theorem 2 of C974-C976-DUPLICATE-POINT.md applies. Its strict M,A,D,T are E's marker,F5,G6,F7; its excluded F is G7, strict in actual Q11 by (3), and its excluded L,B are the original NW and SE markers in the distinct N,S. It gives
\[
 (2.50,2.04)\in E.
\]
Theorem 1 of that same chapter, reflected through y=2, applies to S with the Section 2 table and gives this identical strict point in S. This is again impossible.

Every point is either strictly owned by E or excluded from its open interior, so the split includes a boundary contact of F7 in its first branch. The shared local theorems treat every angle and exterior tangency; strictness of the duplicate points follows from their \(a>1\) conclusions. This proves the full closed sector (1). ∎

## 4. Scope

Together with C1089-CONTINUOUS-REDUCTION.md and C1089-REVERSE-CYCLIC-ENDING.md, this leaves only the two C1089 sectors
\[
 (Y_8\ge2,Y_{10}\ge2,X_{11}\le2),\qquad
 (Y_8\ge2,Y_{10}\le2,X_{11}\ge2).
\]
They are the MMMP configuration and its simultaneous diagonal transpose. In both, the missing original corner remains the southwest corner. Their complete actual-owner ending remains a separate obligation; no assertion that the whole C1089 family is finished is made here.
