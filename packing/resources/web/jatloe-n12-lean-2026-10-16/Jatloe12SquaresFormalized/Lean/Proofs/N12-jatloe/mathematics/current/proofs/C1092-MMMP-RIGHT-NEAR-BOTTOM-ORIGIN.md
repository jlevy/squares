## 1. A short bar excludes the negative inward sign

**Lemma 1.** Let C,W be disjoint open squares of common side a>1. Give C the negative canonical frame

    u=(c,-s), v=(s,c), c>=s>=0, c^2+s^2=1.

Suppose C strictly owns

    M=(.35,.35), P=(0,.26), Q=(.26,0),

and excludes O=(0,0), R=(1,.4), A=(.26,1), and every point of

    J={ (x,1):0<=x<=.26 }.

Suppose the actual W strictly owns S=(1.086,0), T=(1,.26), has closure in x<=2, and has center ordinate at least zero. Then this configuration is impossible.

**Proof.** Let U,V be C's lower faces. Both u bands of O follow from P,Q, and its upper v band follows from M. Its exclusion and strict Q therefore give

    0<=V<.26s.                                      (2)

In particular s>0. At R its lower u and v bands follow from Q; the first projection increment is .74c-.4s>=.34c>0. Its upper v band follows from

    v(R)=s+.4c<=.7(c+s)<=.7sqrt(2)<1<a+V.

Thus R can leave only through the upper u face:

    U+a<=c-.4s.                                    (3)

At A its upper u band follows from M, with projection gap .09c+.65s>0. Its lower v band follows from Q, with gap c>0. If it left through the lower u face, (3) would give

    a<=.74c+.6s<=sqrt(.9076)<1.

Its only remaining exit is consequently upper v:

    V+a<=c+.26s.                                   (4)

Put z=s/(1+c). Equations (2),(4) and a>1 give c+.26s>1. The exact identity

    c+.26s-1=2z(.26-z)/(1+z^2)

therefore implies

    0<z<.26.                                      (5)

Theorem1 of `C1092-MMMP-FULL-INWARD-CENTER.md` now applies to these same actual C,W. Its complete inputs are C's strict P,Q and V>=0, W's strict S,T, its genuine x<=2 wall and its nonnegative center ordinate. It puts (z,1) strictly inside C. By (5) this point belongs to the excluded whole segment J, which is a contradiction.

The argument retained both negative canonical endpoints: s=0 was contradicted by (2), while the diagonal endpoint is excluded by (5). No claim of ownership of R beyond its exclusion from C was used. ∎
