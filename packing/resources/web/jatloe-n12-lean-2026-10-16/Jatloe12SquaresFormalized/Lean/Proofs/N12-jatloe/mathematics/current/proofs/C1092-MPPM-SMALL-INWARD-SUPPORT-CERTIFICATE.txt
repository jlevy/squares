All numbers are exact rationals. No sampled angle or floating bound is used.

2. Exact Bernstein identities on full rectangles

On each rectangle below put x=(53/500)xi and y=l+(r-l)eta,
where 0<=xi,eta<=1 and l,r are the displayed rational endpoints.
For the stated degrees n,m define B_i^n(v)=binom(n,i)v^i(1-v)^(n-i).
The table of coefficients beta_(i,j) is the exact identity
P((53/500)xi,l+(r-l)eta)=sum_(i=0..n)sum_(j=0..m)
  beta_(i,j) B_i^n(xi) B_j^m(eta).

Every coefficient can be verified directly by expansion. More explicitly,
if the transformed power polynomial is sum a_(h,k)xi^h eta^k, then
beta_(i,j)=sum_(h<=i,k<=j) a_(h,k) binom(i,h)/binom(n,h)
                                      * binom(j,k)/binom(m,k).
Each row below fixes i, with columns j=0,...,m.

3. Positivity on every point of the domains

The Bernstein weights are nonnegative and sum to1 on each whole rectangle.
All coefficients are strictly positive except beta_(0,0)=0 in the first
P_plus rectangle. In that rectangle every row i>=1 is strictly positive,
and x>0 gives positive total weight to those rows, including x=53/500.
Thus P_plus>0 whenever 0<x<=53/500 and 0<=y<=5/12.
Both P_C and P_E are strictly positive on their whole closed rectangle.
Their displayed denominators are positive, so the corresponding support
functions are strictly positive over the complete stated angle domains.
This proves the continuous signs, including all shared rectangle boundaries.
