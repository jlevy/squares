# C1092 MMMP: the southwest outward alternative is impossible

All terminating decimals are exact rational numbers. The squares below are the actual original open owners, with common side 1<a<=1001/1000, disjoint interiors, and their genuine container walls. An excluded point may lie on a face. A separating closure-support inequality may be equality.

**Theorem.** In the whole original MMMP family,

    F0=(1,1.74) strict in Q8
        implies F2=(1.6,1) and G2=(1.74,1) strict in Q4.

The F2 implication is the preceding theorem `C1092-MMMP-LEFT-LOWER-INCIDENCE.md`. This chapter proves the new G2 implication. The proof uses the same actual northwest Q7 in its separation from actual Q6 and from actual Q4. Its two face coordinates cancel. No independently chosen replacement for any of these squares is introduced.

## 3. Central faces and the complete separating-axis reduction

For any square Q let L_Q(n),R_Q(n) denote the minimum and maximum closure projections in direction n. If n has components A_0,B_0 in its frame and the lower face coordinates are z_1,z_2, then

    L_Q(n)=A_0 z_1+B_0 z_2
             +a min(A_0,0)+a min(B_0,0),
    R_Q(n)=L_Q(n)+a(|A_0|+|B_0|).                     (13)

Two disjoint open squares have a weak projection separation on one of their four face-normal axes. Thus both orders on the two C axes and two B axes are an exhaustive list.

    u_C=dx-ry, v_C=rx+dy,

## 8. Cancellation and the exact contradiction

Because a>1, (30) implies the strict inequalities with right sides

    D_1=1+k_1-H_1=1-b_1+b_1r-t_1(1-d),
    D_2=1+k_2-H_2=1-b_2-b_2r-t_2(1-d).

Multiply the first by alpha_2>0, the second by k_1>0, and add. The u coordinate cancels:

    (k_1k_2-alpha_1alpha_2)v
        >alpha_2D_1+k_1D_2.                          (31)

The exact identity

    k_1k_2-alpha_1alpha_2=-(b_1b_2-t_1t_2)<=0

and v>0 make the left side nonpositive. On the other hand (29) gives

    alpha_2>693/1000,
    D_1>13/140,
    D_2>-29/875,
    0<k_1<=1.

The last upper bound is Cauchy--Schwarz for the two unit vectors (b_1,t_1) and (r,d). Whether D_2 is negative or nonnegative, k_1D_2>-29/875. Therefore

    alpha_2D_1+k_1D_2
       >(693/1000)(13/140)-29/875
       =4369/140000>0.                                (32)

This contradicts (31). The contrary outward Q4 alternative cannot exist.

Consequently, under actual Q8 ownership of F0, G2 cannot be excluded from Q4. It is strictly owned by Q4. Together with the earlier strict F2 implication, this proves the theorem.

## 9. Scope and retained alternatives

The theorem does not exclude Q8 ownership of F0 itself. It proves that such ownership requires Q4's ordinary bottom pair F2,G2, though Q4 may still have additional original auxiliaries. All its other original labels must be filtered only after accounting for which auxiliaries are actually foreign in the remaining branch.

Every support calculation above concerns the full actual squares of the common side a. Both canonical signs of Q4 and both signs of Q7 are covered continuously; the axial and diagonal endpoints are either included or excluded by the displayed strict width inequalities. All weak face exits and support tangencies are retained. The new conclusion uses the already established MMMP top origin only through the exact actual Q6 face table (5).
