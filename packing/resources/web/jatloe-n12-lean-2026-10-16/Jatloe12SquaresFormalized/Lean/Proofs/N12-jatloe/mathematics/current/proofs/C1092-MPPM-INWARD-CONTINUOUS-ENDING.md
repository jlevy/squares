# C1092 MPPM: the complete nonordinary central ending

Retain the complete original no-omission MPPM entrance, its twelve actual
open squares, common side 1<a<=1.001, genuine container and original
closed center signs. All strict ownership statements refer to the
original owners. A point on a face is treated as omitted. No auxiliary
is assigned an owner merely because it is omitted elsewhere.

## 1. Complete exclusion of every nonordinary inward configuration

**Theorem 1.** In the original no-omission MPPM family, every central
owner owns its ordinary pair:

    Q4: F0,G0; Q5: F6,G5; Q6: F9,G9; Q7: F3,G4.    (1)

**Proof.** The complete no-omission reduction gives the ordinary,
inward and residual-elbow cover. The all-angle theorem
C1092-MPPM-ELBOW-EXCLUSION.md excludes every residual elbow. Count an
owner with both ordinary and inward pairs as ordinary. Any remaining
owner missing its ordinary pair is therefore genuinely inward.

Theorem 1 of C1092-MPPM-INWARD-OPPOSITE-REDUCTION.md proves that every
such inward owner has an inward opposite, with two ordinary neighbors.
It already excludes the whole OOOI pattern, for every actual center
and outer-point alternative. Thus a configuration with a genuinely
inward owner must have the OIOI pattern.

Normalize the two inward owners to Q4,Q6. At most one can strictly
contain O=(2,2), because their open interiors are disjoint. There are
exactly two remaining center possibilities:

1. One of Q4,Q6 strictly contains O.
2. Both Q4,Q6 omit O.

The first possibility, in either simultaneous orientation, is excluded
by C1092-MPPM-CENTERED-INWARD-OIOI-ENDING.md. Its complete actual
two-neighbor argument sharpens the centered owner's angle and upper
face. The opposite inward owner's genuinely owned outer G point
supplies a right-wall advance; both central support comparisons first
force the outer halfpoint and then the previously unavailable wall
origin. The final two-source comparison gives the contradiction for
both outer-point alternatives of the centered owner.

The second possibility is excluded by
C1092-MPPM-OPPOSED-MISSING-INWARD-ENDING.md. Each inward owner's
missing-center classification forces its outer G point and omits its
outer F point. The proof retains both actual ordinary neighbors, the
whole actual lower bar and the genuine opposite-G wall advance. Its
continuous predecessor bound and successor comparison contradict
every possible actual square-normal source.

These alternatives include all boundary positions of O. Neither
ending requires an absent auxiliary to acquire an owner, and both
cover the full permitted angle intervals and tangent supports.
Therefore OIOI is impossible as well. No genuinely nonordinary
central owner remains, proving (1).

## 2. The exact residual all-ordinary scope

Theorem 1 supplies the genuine premises of
C1092-MPPM-ORDINARY-CENTRAL-REDUCTION.md. Its original component
filter then gives the four exact actual wall labels

    Q8=2050, Q9=4100, Q10=262400, Q11=131200,

The all-four-forward-owned alternative is precisely the historical
last assignment, already excluded by
C1092-DIRECT-LAST-ASSIGNMENT.md for every angle sign. The
all-four-forward-omitted alternative is excluded by the complete
missing-forward wall-center cycle. Its two-sign local inputs are
C1092-MPPM-MISSING-NEGATIVE-BOUNDARIES.md and
C1092-MPPM-MISSING-POSITIVE-BOUNDARIES.md.

Thus the residual all-ordinary case has a mixture of owned and omitted
forward points. Up to a simultaneous rotation, its four membership
patterns can be described by the set of owners retaining their own
forward point:

    {Q4}, {Q4,Q5}, {Q4,Q6}, or {Q4,Q5,Q6}.           (2)

Every other indicated forward point in each row is omitted from its
indicated owner. This is a classification of those four memberships,
not a new complete auxiliary assignment. Other actual auxiliary
memberships and all continuous orientations remain subject to the
original entrance. In particular no omitted point is assigned to a
different square in (2).

Every missing-forward sector has its strict local wall-center order
for either sign, without a preceding-origin premise, by

    C1092-MPPM-MISSING-NEGATIVE-WALL-ORDER.md,
    C1092-MPPM-MISSING-POSITIVE-WALL-ORDER.md.

The new all-ordinary halfpoint theorem

    C1092-MPPM-ORDINARY-NEGATIVE-HALFPOINT.md

also gives every nonpositive central its own strict outer halfpoint
and its next actual wall origin, regardless of predecessor sign or
forward membership. Its complete proof retains the actual predecessor

The continuous ending of the mixed-forward alternatives in (2)
remains a separate obligation. Theorem 1 completes every genuinely
