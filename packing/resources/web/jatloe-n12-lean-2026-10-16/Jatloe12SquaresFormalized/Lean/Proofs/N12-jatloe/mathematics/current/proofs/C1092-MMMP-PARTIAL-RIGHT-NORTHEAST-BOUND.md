# C1092 MMMP: the partial-right branch also forces the northeast half-angle below one third

All terminating decimals are exact rationals. Every square is the actual original open square of common side a>1. All owned points are strict. Exterior points, actual wall conditions and separating supports may be equality. The argument covers complete continuous angle intervals.

**Theorem.** In the original MMMP family, suppose actual Q10 owns F8 and actual Q5 owns G8. Then Q7 has strictly positive canonical angle, and the positive northeast angle theta6 satisfies

    tan(theta6/2)<1/3.

The proof uses the newly derived southeast floor for this ownership branch. It does not retain the sine bound from the contrary G8-exclusion branch.

## 2. The northwest square cannot have a nonpositive angle

    u_W(B)=-d+.4r<0<A,
