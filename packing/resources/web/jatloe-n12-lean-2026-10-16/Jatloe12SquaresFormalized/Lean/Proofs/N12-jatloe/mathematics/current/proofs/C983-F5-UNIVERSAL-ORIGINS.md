## 4. The top origin is also universal

## 5. Scope and immediate actual segments

The southeast crosspoint (2), right origin (7) and top origin (8) are unconditional throughout merged label8224. Their proofs use neither a merged vertical crosspoint nor a merged center. In particular the ordinary inward crosspoint used in Section 1 belongs to actual Q5, whose two required foreign central markers are indeed excluded from that square.

The right origin can now be joined to any other point owned by the same actual Q10. For example, a Q10 that owns G9 has the strict segment {3}×[2,2.4]; a Q10 that owns G8 has {3}×[1.6,2]. The top origin similarly joins to its actual F3 or F7 according to its own component. These are consequences inside one actual wall owner; no segment joining points in different owners is asserted.

The full F5-only configuration still needs a coupled-square ending. This chapter supplies actual points for that ending and does not claim that all of label8224 has been excluded.
