# C1092 MPPM: every positive forward-owned ordinary square has a central upper cap

All terminating decimals are exact rationals. Retain the original no-omission OOOO entrance, with the actual twelve owners and common side

    1<a<=1.001.

Owned points are strict. Exclusion includes equality at a face. No omitted auxiliary is assigned to another square.

**Theorem 1.** Normalize a positive ordinary square that owns its forward point to C=Q4. Translate by (-2,-2), use the positive canonical frame

    u=(c,s), v=(-s,c), c>=s>0, c^2+s^2=1,

and write U,V for the lower faces and A=U+a,H=V+a for the upper faces. Then

    U>=-c-s, V>=.4s-c, H<=s,
    A<=0.                                         (1)

If the backward inner point I=(0,-.26) is excluded from C, the sharper cap

    A<=-.26s                                      (2)

holds. If I is instead strict in C, only the weaker cap in (1) is asserted. Both alternatives imply that the actual C excludes the central point O=(0,0).

**Proof.** The actual ordinary points, marker and forward point are

    P=(-1,-.26), G=(-1,-.4), M=(-.35,-.35),
    T=(-.26,0).

Theorem1 and Corollary2 of C1092-MPPM-FORWARD-OWNED-POSITIVE-D.md give the genuine preceding-origin exclusion D=(-1,0) and the first three faces in (1), without assuming any other forward membership. That same corollary gives (2) in the alternative that I is excluded. This already gives A<0 in that alternative.

Suppose instead that I is strict in C. The actual C now owns both its ordinary pair P,G and its inward pair I,T. Theorem1 of C1092-MPPM-CENTERED-POSITIVE-OVERLAP.md applies literally to this overlap and proves that O is not strict in C. Its ordinary neighbors are Q5,Q7 from the OOOO entrance, its wall and corner inputs are original actual owners, and its stated side upper bound is exactly the one retained here.

The strict T,I give

    U<u(T)=-.26c<0,
    V<v(I)=-.26c<0,
    H>v(T)=.26s>0.

Thus O has its lower u-band and both v-bands strict in C. Its exclusion can only be through the upper u-face, proving A<=0. No face equality has been discarded.

The I-owned and I-excluded alternatives are exhaustive, so (1) holds in every positive forward-owned ordinary square. Simultaneous quarter-turns of the actual configuration give all four sectors. ∎

A backward point is the next actual central sector's forward point. Its omission by that next owner is not used to infer its omission by the present owner. Instead both actual membership alternatives are checked above.

This theorem supplies a simultaneous face cap for the remaining mixed-forward cycle. It does not claim a positive wall-center order for an individual forward-owned sector; such an order requires additional actual-neighbor coupling.
