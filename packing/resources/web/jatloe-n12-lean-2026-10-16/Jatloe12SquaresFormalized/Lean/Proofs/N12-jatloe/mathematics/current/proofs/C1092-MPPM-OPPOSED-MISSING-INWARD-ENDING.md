# C1092 MPPM: two opposite inward owners cannot both miss the center

Every terminating decimal is an exact rational. All squares below are the
actual open squares of the common side a>1. Owned points are strict;
excluded faces and separating supports may be equalities. Whole foreign
segments belong to one named actual square. No sampled angle statement
is used.

Retain the original no-omission MPPM entrance, the completed elimination
of elbows, and the proved inward sign and missing-center reductions.
Thus, if the two opposite genuinely inward owners Q4 and Q6 both omit
O=(2,2), they are negative, Q4 owns G0 and omits F0, and Q6 owns G9
and omits F9. Their actual ordinary neighbors are Q5 and Q7. The theorem
of this chapter excludes this entire remaining alternative.

The proof has three continuous steps. The whole bottom bar bounds the
lowest vertex of Q4. Its actual ordinary predecessor then forces its
half-angle below 1/10. The genuinely owned outer G9 in Q6 advances the
actual right square a little beyond its prescribed anchor; that advance
makes the two possible Q4-to-Q5 supports impossible.
