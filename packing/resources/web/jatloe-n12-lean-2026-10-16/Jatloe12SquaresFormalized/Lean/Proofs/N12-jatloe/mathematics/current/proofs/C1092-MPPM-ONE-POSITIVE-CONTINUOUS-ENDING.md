# C1092 MPPM: the complete ordinary ending with exactly one positive central angle

and real-wall contacts may be weak. Retain the original no-omission OOOO

## 4. Exhausting the one-positive cycle

Rotate so that the sole positive central is P=Q4. In cyclic order
the other three are Q5,Q6,Q7, all nonpositive. As noted in Section 1,
P is missing and the last negative Q7 is retaining. Each retaining
negative is strictly negative by the excluded-origin face in (2), and
each missing negative is strictly negative by its missing profile.

If all three Q5,Q6,Q7 retain their forward points, Sections 2--3 of
the reviewed all-negative ending exclude those three consecutive
negative retaining owners. Their actual ordinary data, markers,
corners, origins and bars are (1), and the next forward point is used
only in those three stipulated retaining owners. That local proof
has no sign premise on the fourth central P.

If Q5,Q6 are both missing, Theorems 2 and 3 of
C1092-MPPM-MISSING-NEGATIVE-BOUNDARIES.md contradict each other
on the same actual common wall center. The true origins and bars (1)
supply their full inputs. Thus two consecutive negative missing owners
are impossible here, independently of P's sign.

There are exactly two membership patterns left:

| Q4 | Q5 | Q6 | Q7 |
|---|---|---|---|
| positive missing | negative retaining | negative missing | negative retaining |
| positive missing | negative missing | negative retaining | negative retaining |

In the first row, apply Lemma 1 to W=Q6,C=Q7, then Lemma 2 to
that same C=Q7 and its actual positive missing successor Q4.
Its strict X is supplied by the universal positive crosspoint theorem.
The actual bottom bar in the rotated chart is Q8's full bar in (1).
This is a contradiction.

In the second row, apply Lemma 3 to W=Q5,C=Q6,E=Q7. The next
central after E is the actual positive Q4, whose crosspoint is strict.
The bar used for E's final native clearance is again the actual Q8
bar; its origin follows from Q7's nonpositive halfpoint, and its far
advance follows from Q4's ordinary F0. Thus every source in Lemma 3
is present in the same actual owners. This row also contradicts the
lemma.

These are exhaustive strict forward-membership alternatives. The sole
positive angle case is impossible, proving Theorem 1. ∎

The proof never treats a forward omission as a reassignment. The only
new point used from the positive square is its independently proved
strict crosspoint. Every clearance retains a whole segment in one
actual wall square, and all scalar angle intervals were handled by
the displayed continuous monotonicity or concavity arguments.
