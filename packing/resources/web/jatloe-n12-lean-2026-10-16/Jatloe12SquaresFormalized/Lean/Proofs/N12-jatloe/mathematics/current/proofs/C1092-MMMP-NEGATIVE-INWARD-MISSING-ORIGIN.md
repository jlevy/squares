# C1092 MMMP: the negative inward southeast square has a strict right tip and a short missing-origin angle interval

All terminating decimals are exact rationals. All squares are actual original open squares of common side a>1 with disjoint interiors. Owned points are strict; excluded points and separating supports may be equality. Every scalar argument covers its complete stated angle interval.

**Theorem 1.** Suppose the actual southeast Q5 owns F4,G6 and has a nonpositive original canonical angle. Translate by (-2,-2), write E=Q5, and use the frame

    u=(c,-s), v=(s,c), c>=s>=0, c^2+s^2=1,
    U,V for the lower faces, A=U+a,H=V+a.

Then s>0. Put z=s/(1+c). The same actual E satisfies

    0<=U<.26s, A>1,
    P=(1,-z) strictly in E, H>z,
    H<=min(s,.47s+.12c),
    V>=.4s-c.                                      (1)

**Theorem 2.** If in addition actual Q9 excludes its bottom origin (2,1), then

    V>=.7s-c,
    a<=1.12c-.23s,
    0<z<77/500=.154.                               (2)

These statements assume no ownership or exclusion of F8 or G8 in E. In particular the theorem does not assign an owner to an omitted auxiliary. The profile (2) is a conditional reduction, not a completed contradiction of its branch.

## 1. The original bottom wall supplies the actual half-angle tip

The actual E owns its southeast marker M=(.35,-.35) and its stipulated inward pair

    I=(0,-.26), D=(.26,0).

The actual northwest Q7 strictly owns O=(0,0), so O is excluded from E. Its two v bands follow strictly from I,D, and its upper u band follows from M. Thus O is lower u and

    0<=U<.26s.                                    (3)

The strict upper inequality is from I. It excludes s=0 and gives A=U+a>1.

Apply the simultaneous quarter-turn

    (X,Y)=(-y,x)

to these translated original squares. The actual E still has negative canonical frame (c,-s),(s,c). Its new lower u face is -H and its new lower v face is U. Its strict I,D become (.26,0),(0,.26). The actual bottom W=Q9 strictly owns

    (1.086,0), (1,.26)

in this chart, from its original marker (2,.914) and universal G5=(2.26,1). Its genuine bottom wall y>=-2 becomes X<=2. Its original MMMP center condition X9>=2 becomes center_Y(W)>=0.

Every hypothesis of Theorem1 of C1092-MMMP-FULL-INWARD-CENTER.md therefore holds for these same actual E,W, with its lower transverse face U>=0 supplied by (3). That complete continuous wall theorem gives

    -H<-z,
    (z,1) strictly in the quarter-turned E.

Returning to the original translated chart proves

    H>z, P=(1,-z) strictly in E.                  (4)

The theorem applies over the entire negative canonical range, so initially 0<z<=sqrt(2)-1. No numerical angle enclosure or missing-bottom-origin hypothesis was needed for (4).

## 2. Three actual foreign points give the other faces

The universal right origin R0=(1,0) is strict in actual Q10 and is excluded from E. Its lower u and lower v bands follow from D. Its upper u band follows from A>1 and u(R0)=c<=1. Hence R0 has only an upper-v exit:

    H<=s.                                        (5)

The universal northeast point K=(.47,.12) is strict in actual Q6, by Theorem1 of C1092-MMMP-NORTHEAST-LOWER-TIP.md. Its lower u band in E follows from I:

    u(K)-u(I)=.47c-.38s>=.09c>0.

Its upper u band follows from u(K)<=.47<1<A. Its lower v band follows from D, with increment .21s+.12c>0. Therefore K is upper v and

    H<=.47s+.12c.                                (6)

Finally F6=(.4,-1) belongs strictly to the actual Q9 universally. Its lower u band follows from I, with increment .4c+.74s>0. Its upper u band follows from

    u(F6)=.4c+s<=1.4/sqrt(2)<1<A.

Here .4cos(q)+sin(q) increases throughout 0<=q<=pi/4, where q is the negative angle magnitude. The upper v band follows from D, with gap c-.14s>0. Thus F6 is lower v:

    V>=.4s-c.                                   (7)

Equations (3)--(7) prove Theorem1. All three exclusions have explicit different actual owners.

## 3. A missing bottom origin gives a whole actual bar

Suppose now that actual Q9 excludes its origin (2,1). Apply Theorem11 of C981-WALL-CYCLES.md to this same Q9 under the original-coordinate map

    (X,Y)=(x-2,1-y),
    e=.086, r=.4, z_wall=.8.

Its strict marker and F6 become (0,.086),(.4,0); its actual bottom wall becomes Y<=1; its excluded origin is (0,0). The required range .8<2sqrt(2)-2 follows from 1.4<sqrt(2). That theorem puts (2.8,1) strictly in Q9. Together with its universal G5, this gives the strict whole segment

    [.26,.8] x {-1} in the actual Q9             (8)

At T the lower u and upper v bands follow from strict M, with positive increments .35c+.65s and .65c-.35s respectively. Hence its only possible exits are upper u or lower v. We exclude the first using the whole bar (8).

## 4. The proposed upper face at T forces an impossible bar crossing

Suppose

    A<=.7c+s.                                   (9)

Since A>1, one has .7c+s>1. The exact identity

    (1+z^2)(.7c+s-1)=(1-z)(17z-3)/10

and 0<z<1 imply

    z>3/17.                                     (10)

At z0=3/17 the cosine and sine are exactly

    c0=140/149, s0=51/149.

The function 1.12c-.23s decreases strictly with the entire angle magnitude q, since its derivative is -1.12s-.23c<0. At q0=2 arctan(3/17), its value is

    1.12c0-.23s0=14507/14900<1.

Consequently (10) gives

    1.12c-.23s<1.                               (11)

Consider the lowest closed vertex of E,

    Z=(cA+sV,-sA+cV).

By A>=a, (7), and a>1,

    Z_x>=ac+.4s^2-cs>c+.4s^2-cs>=.4.            (12)

For the last bound, cs<=1/2 and c>=1/sqrt(2)>2/3 give

    1-c=s^2/(1+c)<.6s^2,
    c+.4s^2-cs>=.5-.2s^2>=.4.

On the other hand, (6),(9) imply

    Z_x<=c(.7c+s)+s(.47s+.12c-a)
        =.7+s(1.12c-.23s-a)<.7,                 (13)

where (11) and a>1 make the final inequality strict.

The same actual face (6), together with A>=a, also gives

    Z_y<=-a(c+s)+.47cs+.12c^2.                   (14)

Define J(q)=c+s-1-.47cs-.12c^2. Its exact derivative is

    J'=(c-s)[1-.47(c+s)]+.24cs>0

throughout q0<=q<=pi/4: the first term is nonnegative because .47sqrt(2)<1, and the second is positive. At q0,

    J(q0)=2751/111005>0.

Thus (10),(14) imply Z_y<-1.

Equations (12),(13) put Z_x strictly between .4 and .7, while the strict point M has M_x=.35 and M_y=-.35. The segment from M to Z therefore crosses y=-1 before reaching Z, at an abscissa strictly between .26 and .7. Every point before the closure vertex is interior to E, since all four linear bands interpolate a strict point and a closure point. The crossing also lies in the strict actual Q9 bar (8), contradicting disjoint interiors.

The upper-u exit (9) is impossible. The only remaining exit of T is lower v, so

    V>=v(T)=.7s-c.                              (15)

The strict contradiction covered all weak face contacts in (9); no tangent endpoint was discarded.

## 5. The exact final angle cap

Combining (6),(15) gives

    a=H-V<=1.12c-.23s.                           (16)

The right side decreases strictly with q over the entire canonical interval. At z=77/500 its exact value is

    1.12(244071/255929)-.23(77000/255929)
       =6391238/6398225<1.

Its deficit from1 is6987/6398225>0. Since a>1, (16) therefore forces z<77/500, proving Theorem2.

This proof keeps the same actual E, bottom Q9, right Q10, northeast Q6 and northwest Q7 throughout. The strict half-angle point in (4) is supplied by the actual bottom wall with its original center sign. The stronger face and angle bounds use the entire missing-origin bar of that same actual Q9. No conclusion that either conditional profile is already impossible is asserted here.
