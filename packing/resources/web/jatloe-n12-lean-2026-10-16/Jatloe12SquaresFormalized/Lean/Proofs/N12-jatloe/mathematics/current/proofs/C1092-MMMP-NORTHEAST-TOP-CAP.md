# C1092 MMMP: the top origin sharpens the actual northeast angle

Every terminating decimal is an exact rational. The squares are the actual original owners, with common side 1<a≤1001/1000, disjoint interiors and their genuine container walls. Exclusions permit tangency.

**Theorem.** In the whole original MMMP family the northeast owner is exactly

    Q6=131200={F7,G7}.

In the quarter-turned chart (X,Y)=(2−y,x−2), choose its strictly positive canonical frame

    u=(b,t), v=(−t,b), b≥t>0, b²+t²=1,

and let p,q be its lower face coordinates. Then

    p≥−b,       p+a≤t−.4b,
    q≥.4t,      q+a≤b+t,                         (1)

and consequently

    a≤.6b+t,
    t/(1+b)>1/4,   b<15/17,   t>8/17.           (2)

## 1. The literal remaining component

The full-core reduction and the independent unit-span exclusion leave precisely the northeast alternatives131200={F7,G7} and655488={F7,G7,G9}. Corollary1 of `C1092-MMMP-RIGHT-FAR-AUXILIARY.md` already proves G9 strictly in actual Q10 throughout the whole MMMP family, including the branch where Q10 owns G8. Disjointness therefore removes655488 from actual Q6. The exact remaining label is131200.

The angle sign and all four earlier face inequalities are the conclusions of `C1092-MMMP-NORTHEAST-ANGLE.md` and Corollary2 of `C1092-MMMP-RIGHT-FAR-AUXILIARY.md`. In particular they give

    p≥−1.086b, p+a≤t−.4b,
    q≥.4t, q+a≤b+t.                            (3)

No owner is assigned to an omitted auxiliary in this step.

## 2. The newly proved top origin has a unique exit

The theorem `C1092-MMMP-TOP-ORIGIN.md` puts (2,3) strictly in actual Q11. In the present chart it is

    O_T=(−1,0),

and is therefore excluded from Q6. The strict Q6 point H=(−1,.26), namely G7=(2.26,3), puts O_T strictly below both upper faces: its two projection increments are .26t>0 and .26b>0. Thus O_T can leave Q6 only through a lower face.

Its lower-v alternative would give q≥v(O_T)=t. Together with the upper-v bound in (3), this would imply

    a≤b≤1,

contrary to a>1. Hence it is a lower-u exit and p≥u(O_T)=−b. This proves (1). A weak exterior equality causes no exception to the argument.

## 3. The exact full-interval angle bound

The two u faces in (1) give a≤.6b+t. Set h=t/(1+b). Since 0<t≤b and b²+t²=1, we have 0<h≤sqrt(2)−1<1. The exact identity

    (1+h²)(.6b+t−1)=2(1−h)(4h−1)/5

and a>1 therefore imply h>1/4. On 0<h<1 the half-angle formulas

    b=(1−h²)/(1+h²),   t=2h/(1+h²)

are respectively strictly decreasing and strictly increasing. Their values at h=1/4 are15/17 and8/17, proving (2).

This is a stronger unconditional entrance for the remaining whole MMMP contradiction. It does not assert that the northeast square alone, or an isolated relaxation of its neighboring owners, is impossible.
