# C1092 MPPM: a negative retaining predecessor gives a narrow positive angle

Every terminating decimal is an exact rational. All strict points and
faces refer to the same named actual open squares. Tangencies are
permitted in every exclusion and separation. The local proof needs
only the common side a>1; its original application retains the full
no-omission all-ordinary MPPM entrance.

## 1. The local statement with the actual leftmost-vertex clearance

Use coordinates centered at (2,2). Let C have the positive frame

    u=(c,s), v=(-s,c), c>=s>0, c^2+s^2=1,
    U<u dot Z<A=U+a, V<v dot Z<H=V+a.

Suppose C strictly owns

    M=(-.35,-.35), P=(-1,-.26), T=(-.26,0),

and its actual lower face satisfies

    V>=.4s-c.                                      (1)

Let the predecessor W have the negative frame and faces

    w=(b,-r), z=(r,b), b>=r>0, b^2+r^2=1,
    L<w dot Z<J=L+a, K<z dot Z<D=K+a.

Suppose W strictly owns M_W=(-.35,.35), and retains the simultaneous
bounds

    b>.87, D<=b, J<=.35(b-r), bL+rK>-1.            (2)

The last inequality in (2) is the leftmost-vertex clearance of the
actual W from a genuine actual wall-square segment. It is not a
container-wall hypothesis or an independently selected center bound.

**Theorem 1.** If C and W have disjoint open interiors, then

    s/(1+c)<11/100.                                (3)

In particular the positive angle is less than 2 arctan(11/100).

## 2. The actual separating normals and the two changes of frame

## 3. C's northwest normal fails for every positive angle

The exact lower W support on v is

    lower_W(v)=-ell J+kK.

Writing the actual clearance in (2) in upper-face coordinates gives

    bJ+rD>a(b+r)-1.

Therefore, using D<=b and the exact identity k+ell r/b=c/b,

    lower_W(v)
      <ell/b-a[ell(b+r)/b+k]+(ell r/b+k)D
      <=c+ell/b-a(ell+c/b)
      <(1-b)(ell-c)/b.                            (7)

The last coefficient of a is positive. Also

    ell-c=c(r-1)+sb<sb.

Consequently (7) is strictly less than (1-b)s<.13s. This is below
H>.26s from the same actual C. The source v is impossible, without
any lower bound on C's positive angle.

## 4. C's northeast normal also fails for every positive angle

The exact lower W support on u, bounded by the two upper faces in
(2), is

    lower_W(u)=kL+ell K
      <=k[.35(b-r)-a]+ell(b-a).

Its comparison with A>-.26c gives a strict support gap greater than

    a(k+ell)-.26c-.35k(b-r)-ell b.

The coefficient k+ell is positive. On replacing a by 1, this becomes

    F_u=cP0+sQ0,
    P0=b+r-.26-.35b^2-.65br,
    Q0=b-r-b^2+.35r(b-r).                          (8)

To see Q0<0, use b(1-b)=br^2/(1+b)<=r^2/2 and b-r<=1:

    Q0=b(1-b)-r[1-.35(b-r)]
       <=r(.5r-.65)<0.

Since s<=c, (8) gives

    F_u>=c(P0+Q0),
    P0+Q0=2b-.61-b^2-.3br
           >1-.13^2-.61-.15=.2231>.22.            (9)

Here 2b-b^2=1-(1-b)^2 and br<=1/2 were used. Thus F_u>.22c>0.
This excludes u on the entire positive canonical interval.

## 5. The remaining W-normal gives the angle bound

The exact upper C support in direction z, using (6), satisfies

    upper_C(z)=ell A+kH
      >-.26ell c+k(a+.4s-c).

Subtract lower_W(z)=K<=b-a. Its strict gap is greater than

    a(1+k)-b-.26ell c+k(.4s-c).

The coefficient 1+k is positive. After lowering a to 1, the exact
result is

    F_z=1-P1 b-Q1 r,
    P1=1-c+c^2-.14cs,
    Q1=s-cs+.26+.14s^2.                            (10)

Suppose x=s/(1+c)>=11/100. Throughout the canonical interval,
x<=sqrt(2)-1<5/12. Direct substitution gives

    1-P1^2-Q1^2=R(x)/[2500(1+x^2)^2],
    R(x)=-20169x^4-9400x^3+8738x^2+1400x-169.
                                                     (11)

This polynomial is positive on the whole interval under consideration
by one uniform coefficient bound:

    8738-9400x-20169x^2
      >=8738-9400(5/12)-20169(5/12)^2
      =63349/48>1250.

It follows that

    R(x)>1250x^2+1400x-169
         >=1250(11/100)^2+1400(11/100)-169
         =1/8>0.                                 (12)

The quadratic is strictly increasing for x>=0. Thus P1^2+Q1^2<1,
and Cauchy--Schwarz gives P1b+Q1r<1. The strict support gap in
(10) is positive, so W's last possible actual normal also fails.

All sources in (4) are impossible in the hypothetical range x>=11/100.
This proves Theorem 1. Every equality of physical support lines and
each canonical endpoint was retained; the strictness comes from a>1,
strict owned anchors, and the actual clearance in (2).

**Corollary 2.** A positive forward-owned ordinary central immediately
after a nonpositive forward-owned ordinary central has half-angle

remaining forward membership, either remaining central sign, and
