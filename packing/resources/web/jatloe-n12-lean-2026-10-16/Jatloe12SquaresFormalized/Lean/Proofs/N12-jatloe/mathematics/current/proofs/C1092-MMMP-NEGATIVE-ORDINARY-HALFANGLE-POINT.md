# C1092 MMMP: a negative ordinary square supplies an actual half-angle point

All terminating decimals are exact rational numbers. The squares are the original actual open squares of common side a>1. Owned points are strict. Exterior points, physical walls and separating supports may be equality. Every canonical orientation, including axial and diagonal representatives, is retained.

**Theorem.** In the original MMMP family, suppose actual Q5 owns F8=(3,1.74) and G8=(3,1.6), and actual Q9 excludes (2,1). Then, with z from the proved negative profile below, Q5 strictly owns (3,2-z), and actual Q10 strictly owns the entire segment {3}x[2,2.56].

The proof retains the same actual southeast Q5, right boundary Q10 and northeast Q6. It uses the actual paired southeast corner and the whole actual bottom bar through the preceding missing-origin reduction.

## 1. The missing-origin profile supplies a strict half-angle point

Suppose the bottom origin were absent from Q9. The complete theorem
`C1092-MMMP-ORDINARY-SOUTHEAST-MISSING-ORIGIN.md` applies. Translate by (-2,-2) and write E=Q5. It gives E the strictly negative frame

    u_E=(c,-s), v_E=(s,c), c>=s>0,
    c^2+s^2=1, z=s/(1+c), 0<z<.13.

With lower faces U,V and upper faces A=U+a,H=V+a, its actual profile is

    U>=0, V>=.8s-c, H<=.47s+.12c,                (1)

and D=(.26,0), the original G6, is strict in this actual E. In particular

    U<.26c<1, A=U+a>=a>1.                       (2)

Define

    P=(1,-z).

The half-angle identities give

    u_E(P)=c+sz=1,
    v_E(P)=s-cz=z.                              (3)

The u bands are strict by (2). For the lower v band, (1) and a>1 give

    V=H-a<sqrt(.47^2+.12^2)-1<0<z.

For the upper v band, the lower face in (1) gives

    H=V+a>1+.8s-c>z.                            (4)

Indeed 1-c>=0 and

    .8s-z=s[.8-1/(1+c)]>0,

because c>=1/sqrt(2)>1/2 and hence 1/(1+c)<2/3<.8. Thus P is strict in E. In original coordinates it is the concrete point (3,2-z).

## 2. The genuine right wall reaches above its origin

Let R=Q10 in the same translated chart. Since F8 is strict in the different actual E, it is excluded from R. R strictly owns its original marker (3.086,2), its universal G9=(3,2.4), and its universal origin (3,2).

Reflect the actual right wall across x=2 and apply Theorem1 of
`C974-C976-CONTINUOUS-REDUCTIONS.md`. Its strict marker and outer anchor are the reflected right marker and G9; its excluded near point is the actual E-owned F8; its excluded central marker is the original northeast marker in Q6. The genuine closure condition x<=4 becomes the theorem's left wall. Thus

    (3,2.56) is strict in the same actual Q10.

Together with its actual origin this supplies the strict whole segment

    {1}x[0,.56] in R.                            (5)

The original right marker is S=(1.086,0) in the present chart. Its owner is this same R. No right-center sign or artificial point owner is introduced in this advance.

## 3. The exact remaining right-face alternatives

The point P=(1,-z) is excluded from R because it is strict in the different actual E. The following are the legitimate alternatives from the named strict right points.

If R has nonnegative frame (d,r),(-r,d), the strict origin (1,0) supplies both upper bands of P. Writing L,K for the lower faces, P must therefore leave through at least one of its lower faces:

    L>=d-zr, or K>=-r-zd.                        (5)

If R has negative frame (d,-r),(r,d), the strict T=(1,.56) supplies P's lower-u band and the strict origin supplies its upper-v band. Consequently P must leave through at least one of

    L+a<=d+zr, or K>=r-zd.                       (6)

The strict top endpoint does not, by itself, supply P's lower-v band. In particular, the bound on the projection gap from T to P is not an upper bound on R's upper v face. Thus this theorem asserts (5),(6), without removing the lower-v exits.

In the upper-u alternative of (6), the actual strict marker gives

    1.086d<L+a<=d+zr,
    r/d>.086/z>43/65>1/2.

It follows that w_R=r/(1+d)>1/5>z, and hence

    L<d+zr-1=r(z-w_R)<0.                        (7)

This particular alternative can already be excluded using the actual northeast square, as recorded next.

## 4. The negative-right upper-u alternative is impossible

Let N=Q6. Its positive frame is u_N=(b,t),v_N=(-t,b), with b>=t>0 and t/b>8/15. Its actual top-cap table gives H_N=V_N+a<=b. It strictly owns

    M=(.35,.35), F=(.4,1), Y=(.5,1).

The point Y is the original (2.5,3), proved in `C1092-MMMP-RIGHT-ORIGIN.md`; the angle and face table are from `C1092-MMMP-NORTHEAST-TOP-CAP.md`.

Suppose the upper-u alternative of (6) held. The strict pair M in N and (1,.35) in the actual R bar forces every N-to-R separating normal to have positive horizontal component. Its complete actual sources are

    u_N, -v_N, w=(d,-r), w_perp=(r,d).

The source u_N is excluded by Y and the strict R origin, whose displacement has projection .5b-t<0. The source w_perp is excluded by F and that origin, since .6r-d<0.

For w, the upper N support is greater than or equal to the nonnegative marker projection .35(d-r), whereas the lower R support is L<0 by (7). Thus w fails.

For -v_N=(t,-b), put h=td+br>0 and k=bd-tr>=0. The upper v face of R is positive because its strict origin has v projection r>0. Thus

    lower_R(-v_N)=hL-k(K+a)<0,
    upper_N(-v_N)=-V_N>=a-b>0.

This eliminates the last source, including k=0. The negative-right upper-u alternative is therefore impossible.

Consequently the precise surviving right-face alternatives are the two lower faces in (5), or the lower-v face in (6). These are conditional bounds on the same actual R. Excluding them requires further actual geometry; this chapter does not yet conclude bottom-origin ownership.
