# C1092 MPPM: a negative missing-forward central cannot precede a positive missing-forward central

All points described as owned belong strictly to their named actual open squares. Retain the original no-omission OOOO MPPM entrance and its range 1<a<=1.001. This proof compares the two bounds on the center of the same actual intervening wall square; it uses no independently chosen wall poses.

## 1. The actual signed edge

**Theorem 1.** A nonpositive ordinary central omitting its forward point cannot be immediately followed by a positive ordinary central omitting its forward point.

**Proof.** Simultaneously rotate the actual configuration so that the alleged edge is W=Q4 followed by C=Q5. Thus Q4 omits G3=(1.74,2) and Q5 omits F4=(2,1.74). The missing-negative profile excludes the zero-angle representative of W.

Use coordinates (x,y)=(X-2,Y-2) and let (x_B,y_B) be the center of the actual common boundary B=Q9. Theorem 1 of C1092-MPPM-UNIVERSAL-CENTRAL-ORIGIN-EXCLUSIONS.md supplies the genuine exclusion of W's preceding D=(1,2). Lemma 1 and Theorem 2 of C1092-MPPM-MISSING-NEGATIVE-BOUNDARIES.md now apply to W and B and give

    x_B>-1/5.                                    (1)

Their actual sources are the original Q4 marker and ordinary F0/G0, its missing-forward halfpoint (1,1.5), the stated G3 omission, the paired Q0 inner point (1,1), the genuine D exclusion, and the one actual Q9 bar from (1.44,1) to (1.74,1). The bar's far endpoint is the unconditional advance from actual Q5's ordinary G5. Q9 also owns its original marker, F2 and the origin (2,1), the last supplied by Q4's missing-forward halfpoint and actual paired-corner transfer. Its whole closure obeys the real bottom wall. Thus every input to (1) is available at the same actual W and B, regardless of the Q5 sign.

Now use C=Q5's own southwest chart. The change from the current centered coordinates is

    (xi,eta)=(y,-x).

It is the inverse actual quarter turn and preserves all square sides, orientations and ownership relations. The preceding wall of this positive missing-forward C is still the same actual Q9, and its center ordinate in that chart is

    eta_L=-x_B.

Theorem 1 of C1092-MPPM-POSITIVE-MISSING-LEFT-CENTER.md gives

    -x_B>1/5,                                    (2)

for every positive canonical C angle, without a boundary-origin premise. Its literal source ledger is the simultaneous quarter turn of the one in that chapter. Equations (1),(2) contradict each other. This proves Theorem 1. ∎

## 2. The surviving type of negative-to-positive edge

Together with Corollary 2 of C1092-MPPM-NEGATIVE-MISSING-POSITIVE-ENDING.md, Theorem 1 excludes a negative missing-forward predecessor before every positive recipient, for both recipient memberships. The axial missing-forward predecessor is already impossible.

Also, C1092-MPPM-NEGATIVE-OWNED-POSITIVE-OWNED-EXCLUSION.md excludes a nonpositive retaining predecessor before a positive retaining recipient. Consequently every nonpositive-to-positive edge in any remaining OOOO configuration must have precisely the following actual memberships:

    nonpositive retaining -> positive missing-forward.

This is a restriction on that signed edge. It does not claim that the displayed remaining edge is impossible in isolation, and no omitted forward auxiliary has been assigned to another square.
