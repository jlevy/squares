# Reviews and tests (all by AI systems; no human referee yet)

The results of this paper were developed with AI assistance (see "Use of AI" in the paper). Each piece of the proof was first written as a separate proof draft. Each draft then received one independent review and one independent numerical test, and the assembled argument received a review and an end-to-end numerical test (all on 6 October 2026). Independent means here: a separate AI session that worked from the written definitions, statements and proof drafts, did not reuse the author's code, and wrote its own code for every computation (one reviewer also re-ran the author's constants script on a copy, after its own computation); where a report records a comparison with the author's output, it was made after the session's own analysis. The manuscript was then written from the drafts by several AI writers and integrated by AI editors (7 October 2026). The reviews and tests in all rows but the last therefore concern the drafts; the last row is a blank-slate review of the present text as a whole (7 October 2026). Verdict words of the reports are quoted. Minor and wording items (a wrong or missing one-line justification, a numerical slip that did not change a conclusion, notation) were addressed in the manuscript, except those that concerned only internal notes and have no counterpart in it. A numerical test that finds no counterexample is a search, not a proof. The programs and the recorded results of the numerical tests are in `code/numerical_tests/`, one folder per row of tests below, each with a README and quick checks.
