## 1 -- setting, the two classes, the per-wall ROLES, exclusive extents (F1 closed forms)

## 1.1 Setting [T]
Window W = [3.82, s-hat], s-hat = 3.877083590022814.  On W every corner box of a non-image packing is doubly met
(K0-FREE'), at most one toucher per wall per box (SAME-WALL), a both-wall toucher is alone (WEDGED-SEAL).  The brief's
two classes classify the PAIR corner (touchers A on the floor, B on the wall; THIRD = PAIR + floaters, same touchers):
  class F : one meeter FLAT (sense 0), the other tilted   -- both flat is ONE-TILTED [T]; the flat is [d, 1+d] x [0,1]
            (or its wall mirror) with 0 < d < 1 (d = 0 is the slot square = simple corner); the tilted partner is a
            POKER (A248: cap below height 1 in (0, d], sin th <= d, TILT-MIN d_B <= (d - sin th) tan th, TOP-2 y_T >= 2).
  class T : both meeters tilted (A249: T-CELLS, T-TIP, T-SLOTS).
JAM / FLOAT2 corners have a floater in place of a toucher; the wall-role bookkeeping below applies to their touchers and
their floaters contribute no wall role (they are counted as pocket squares by CAP-2, A277).  Framing: every line is
ROOM [T] (any packing at side s) unless tagged [T-cond] (optimal packing; conditional on the k >= 1 window rows).

## 1.2 Wall ROLES [T]
Each corner puts one meeter on each of its two walls; per wall a corner contributes a role
  Phi (a flat), Pi (the poker of an F corner: tilted, beside a flat on the OTHER wall), tau (a tilted meeter of a T corner).
So a wall is one of six PAIR TYPES {PhiPhi, PhiPi, PiPi, Phi-tau, Pi-tau, tau-tau}; an F corner is (Phi on one wall,
Pi on the other), a T corner is (tau, tau).  Counting roles: 2#PhiPhi + #PhiPi + #Phi-tau = f = 2#PiPi + #PhiPi + #Pi-tau.
Coordinates: wall x = 0, its START corner c at the origin (floor y = 0), END corner c' at (0, s).  A wall toucher Q of
frame tilt th in (0, 90 deg) has wall vertex L = (0, y_L), and (going round) V = L + (sin th, -cos th), R = V +
(cos th, sin th), T = L + (cos th, sin th); w(th) = cos th + sin th; upper boundary y_up(x) = y_L + x tan th on
[0, cos th], = y_T - (x - cos th) cot th on [cos th, w]; lower boundary y_lo(x) = y_L - x cot th on [0, sin th],
= y_V + (x - sin th) tan th on [sin th, w].

## 1.3 Exclusive extent along a wall [T]
Def.  The exclusive extent E(Q) of a wall toucher Q (from the START corner) is the least y such that every OTHER
wall toucher of the same wall with wall vertex above Q's has all its points at height >= y.
Lemma EXT.  (a) flat Q = [0,1] x [d, 1+d]: E = 1 + d.  (b) tilted Q with wall vertex y_L: E = min(y_L, y_R) =
y_L + min(0, sin th - cos th), i.e. E = y_L when th <= 45 deg and E = y_R = y_L - (cos th - sin th) otherwise.
Proof.  Another toucher Q' above Q has its lowest vertex at abscissa sin th' in [0, 1) <= ... and must lie outside Q;
Q's upper boundary is concave on [0, w] with minimum min(y_L, y_R) at an endpoint, so Q' lies above min(y_L, y_R);
the flat case is y_up = 1 + d.  Sharp: Q' nesting on the low end vertex attains it.  QED
Corollary (poker): for the poker B of an F corner (flat A = [d, 1+d] x [0,1] on the floor), y_L = 1 - d_B + cos th
>= 1 + psi(th), psi(t) := (1 - sin t)/cos t, so E(B) >= 1 + psi(th) for th <= 45 deg, and for th > 45 deg
E(B) = y_V + sin th >= 2 - cos th (TOP-2's inequality (1 - sin th) tan th <= w(th) - 1); in every case E(B) >= 1 +
min(psi(th), 1 - cos th) > 1.  The full REACH of B along its wall is y_T >= 1 + psi(th) + sin th >= 2 (TOP-2), but only E(B) is
exclusive: a tilted neighbour may nest under B's high vertex (STACK / the mutual pair), a flat neighbour may not
(FLAT-ABOVE: its bottom >= y_T).
Lemma EXT-tau.  A T-meeter B of frame tilt th and offset d_B (its low vertex at distance d_B from the far wall,
A249's frame) has E = d_B + min(cos th, sin th); with d_B -> 0 this tends to min(cos th, sin th) < 1: a T corner's
wall meeter has NO along-wall exclusive cost bounded below by 1 -- the reason no analogue of TWO-POKERS exists (A249).

## 2 -- F1 SIDE: the six wall pair types, closed forms, which are dead on W

Wall x = 0, START corner at 0 with flat / poker / T-meeter as in part 1, END corner at (0, s) with the mirror frame
(END's frame offset d' and tilt th' are A249's: END's wall meeter B' has its low vertex at height s - d' - w(th') and
abscissa cos th', its wall vertex at height s - d' - cos th').  Interior-disjointness of the two wall touchers is the
chord condition  y_lo(B') (x) >= y_up(START's toucher)(x) on the common abscissae (TWO-POKERS' argument, A248 s.3.2).

**PiPi [T, dead on W].**  TWO-POKERS: s >= 2 + psi(th) + psi(th_f) + sin th_f + max(cos th_f tan th, sin th -
(cos th - cos th_f) cot th_f) >= s* = 3.9342370 > s-hat.  (A248; Lean `two_pokers_window`.)

**PhiPi [T].**  Flat A = [0,1] x [d, 1+d] at START, poker B' at END (beside END's flat on the ceiling).  B''s low vertex
(cos th_f, y_T') has y_T' <= s - 1 - psi(th_f) - sin th_f (A248 mirrored) and, lying at abscissa < 1, must be >= 1 + d:
    SIDE(PhiPi) :  s >= 2 + d + psi(th_f) + sin th_f = 3 + d + (1 - sin th_f)(sec th_f - 1) >= 3 + d.
So a PhiPi wall exists on W iff the flat sits within s - 3 <= 0.877 of its corner (d < s - 3), and FLAT-ABOVE is the
same inequality read from the poker's side (the flat's bottom is >= its top vertex).  The flat NEVER meets the poker's
slot [0,1] x [s-2, s-1] (1 + d <= s - 2); the poker ALWAYS meets the flat's slot [0,1] x [1,2] (y_T' <= s - 2 < 2).

**PhiPhi [T].**  s >= 2 + d + d'; both flats meet each other's slot iff d, d' > s - 3, compatible with d + d' <= s - 2
iff s < 4.  A common-tilt ROW iff d + d' = s - 2 (the flat row [0,1] x [d, d+2], A258 s.3.4).

**Phi-tau [T].**  Flat at START, T-meeter B' at END:  s >= 1 + d + d' + w(th')  (B''s far point above the flat).  B' meets
the flat's slot (ADJ) iff d' + w(th') > s - 2; the flat meets B''s slot iff d > s - 3.  Both together need
s - 3 < d <= s - 1 - d' - w(th') < 1: a nonempty range on W.  No lower bound above 2 + d.

**Pi-tau [T].**  Poker B (tilt th) at START, T-meeter B' at END, a := cos th (B's top abscissa), b := cos th' (B''s low
vertex abscissa).  (i) b <= a:  s - d' - w(th') >= y_L + b tan th >= 1 + psi(th) + cos th' tan th.  (ii) b >= a:
s - d' - w(th') >= y_T - (b - a) cot th  AND  B's top T below B''s right-rising edge: y_T <= y_lo(B')(a) = (s - d' -
w(th')) + (b - a) cot th'.  In both cases  s >= 1 + psi(th) + d' + w(th') + (a non-negative interleave term) with
infimum, over the T-corner poses with d' -> 0 and th -> 0, equal to 3: **no wall bound above s-hat** -- the mixed F/T
wall is realisable at 3.82 (exact witnesses, part 7).  The interleaving (ii) with th' > 45 deg has B and B' of the SAME
global sense, (i) and (ii) with th' < 45 deg of OPPOSITE senses (frame handedness is swapped at END: `two_grains.md`
s.1.4); so "F beside T on a wall" fixes no sense by itself (part 3 says what the SUPPLY of the poker's slot fixes).

**tau-tau [T-computed, A249].**  The mutual type-T pair has sup SAT margin +0.078 at 3.82: realisable on all of W; the
common-tilt rows of two exist on all of W (least side 1 + sec + cos + sin + tan(phi/2) -> 3).

**Summary F1.**  By SIDE alone, exactly ONE wall type is dead on W: PiPi.  The along-wall costs are
  Phi: 1 + d exclusive, and it forces its poker-neighbour's bottom to >= 1 + d (so the poker's END corner is within
  s - 3 of ... i.e. d < s - 3); Pi: exclusive E(B) >= 1 + min(psi(th), 1 - cos th) and reach y_T >= 2; tau: d' +
  min(cos th', sin th') exclusive, reach d' + w(th').  The brief's "F corner and T corner on one wall: is it > s-hat?"
  -- NO [T]: both Phi-tau and Pi-tau have realisations at 3.82 (part 7).  The flat ROW's 3.9347 is the ROW pinwheel's
  least side [M] (A258) and stays [M]; a flat row inside a HYBRID ring is priced in part 7.

(FLAT-ABOVE, its bottom >= y_T >= 2); a poker: never on W (PiPi dead); a T-meeter B': iff it is ADJ, d' + w(th') > s - 2,

## 4 -- F3 TORQUE [T-cond]: the load path of an F corner

**Conditionality, stated exactly.**  Force / torque facts are statements about Fritz-John points with the meeter in
question LOADED (positive multiplier on its contacts).  A233's LOCKED (no retreat at the Psi-minimal optimum) is a
KINEMATIC property and does not imply load: rattler crowds are locked-but-unloaded at 61 of 70 doubly-met corners of the
genuine k0 FJ points (`k0_count.md` s.5), and a caged rattler is locked.  So every line below is [T-cond] on (i) the
k >= 1 window rows (so that the optimum is k0 and locked) AND (ii) the named meeter carrying load.  Nothing here is
in the chain (s.8); it answers the brief's F3.

**F3.1 [T] The flat has no forced wall torque.**  A flat toucher meets its wall along an EDGE; the wall's normal pressure
is a distribution along that edge whose resultant W may act anywhere on the edge: the wall supplies any torque in
[-W/2, W/2] about the flat's centre.  (Lemma W's tau = W (sin th - cos th)/2 is the VERTEX-contact formula; at th = 0 it
is the extreme -W/2 of the edge range.)  So A is never torque-limited by its wall; its along-wall balance needs a
corner-ward pusher (HOLD) because its partner pushes it outward along the wall (PUSH (ii)): A's holder is the square
pressing its far edge along the floor (the floor chain's F_in), or A is unloaded.

**F3.2 [T] The poker needs two further loaded contacts (TWO-CONTACT).**  B is tilted; th = 45 deg (sin 45 = 0.707 <= d
is allowed) is TWO-CONTACT's listed exception (a single vertical load at T balances B, A217's torque-neutral diamond).
For th != 45 deg B's loaded contacts besides the wall are >= 2 among: (c1) its flat A, at A's corner (d, 1) on B's edge
V->R [force on A along (sin th, -cos th): down and AWAY from the wall, so A's holder is its floor neighbour]; (c2) the
wall neighbour above: a FLAT A_TL touching B's top vertex T vertically (only with A_TL's bottom = y_T, FLAT-ABOVE
tight), or a T-meeter B' on B's edge T->R (a vertex-edge contact at a FREE position along the edge); (c3) a free /
interior square on B's edge V->R (the notch square G) or on T->R; (c4) BR's floor meeter at B's right vertex R (only if
it reaches x = w(th) <= 1.414 at height y_R = y_V + sin th; not excluded, listed).

**F3.3 [T] GEAR and LEVER for the three-contact poker (A239 s.3).**  With exactly (wall, c1, a vertical load at T):
F_out = cot th . F_in, and torque balance forces d = sin th + cos^3 th, which is > 1 for th < 57.1 deg and >= 0.98 on
[57.1 deg, 90 deg).  Consequences on W:
 (a) [T-cond] BL's poker B on x = 0 with TL's FLAT above it (a (Pi_BL, Phi_TL) wall) is torque-balanced by {wall, A_BL,
     A_TL at T} only if d_BL >= 0.98.  If the floor is a (Phi_BL, Pi_BR) wall, SIDE(PhiPi) gives d_BL < s - 3 <= 0.877:
     contradiction.  So **in the all-F pinwheel every LOADED poker has a contact of type (c3)/(c4): an interior square
     (or the far floor meeter at R)** -- the PI's "pinned by an internal square", as a theorem for the pinwheel under
     LOAD.  If the floor is (Phi_BL, Phi_BR) or (Phi_BL, tau_BR), d_BL >= 0.98 is not excluded by side; the poker is
     then steep (th >= 57.1 deg), y_T >= 1 + psi(th) + sin th >= 2.13 and A_TL sits at [y_T, y_T + 1]: s >= 3.13 only.
 (b) [T-cond] A T-neighbour B' on B's edge T->R has a free lever arm: torque balance of B is then possible for every th
     (A239's [C] beyond the family); the load leaves along B' with a gain fixed by the contact position, not by cot th.
     So F3 closes NOTHING for Pi-tau walls: the chase reaches an interior square only where the poker's top load is a
     flat's (vertical) load, i.e. on Pi-Phi walls.
 (c) [T-cond] Loop gain.  A closed wall-to-wall load loop through F corners alone (every poker with a vertical top
     load) needs Prod cot th_c = 1 with every th_c >= 57.1 deg, every factor < 0.65: impossible.  So a loaded chain
     through F corners only cannot close on itself; by CHAIN it ends at far walls -- which says nothing about interior
     squares (Trump's TL crowd does exactly this).  RING (A219) is [M] 193/193 at s > 4 and measured on the 12 rings
     below 4 (A239 s.2), a theorem only in its CHAIN form; the interior contact an F corner "needs" is the one named
     in (a), under LOAD, on a Pi-Phi wall whose flat partner's wall is PhiPi.
**F3.4 Bottom line.**  Torque adds to the room count exactly one conditional statement, (a).  It duplicates what
FAMILY-STUCK already gives unconditionally for the same wall (H-F1: the poker's slot on a Pi-Phi wall needs a free
square), so it is not used in part 6's table; it is recorded because the brief asked what the load path looks like.

## 5 -- class T: T1 SIDE, T2 HOLDERS, T3 TORQUE

**T1 SIDE.**  [T] A T-meeter of frame tilt th and offset d (A249) reaches d + w(th) along its wall and excludes only

for a locked open pair  s = a + b + sec phi_1 + w(phi_1) + e  with the open gap e > 0 (<= 0.349 with ADJ, <= 1.169
without [M], A263).  Mixed senses: with per-wall tilts + + + - exists from 3.9298 [M]; on W the mixed pinwheels of rows

the slab [C].

**T2 HOLDERS.**  [T] The bare one-sense ring of rows is never family-stuck: every one of its 12 cells has exactly two
ring meeters, and the ring family (4 boxes + one slot per wall) has |M| = 8 = |Pi| (A258 "the ninth square"), so a
ninth square meets a box (a third meeter) or both slots of one wall (a RIBBON poker; necessary a + sec phi +
