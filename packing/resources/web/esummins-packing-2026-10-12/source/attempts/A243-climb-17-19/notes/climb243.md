## 2. The census (`code/census243.py`) — A53's definition at any n

A class is a 4-tuple of corner-anchored Young diagrams (offsets i, j ≤ ⌊s⌋ − 1 = 3 here) whose slot squares are
pairwise interior-disjoint, D4-reduced.  [T] Window stability: two slot squares of opposite corners along an axis are
disjoint iff i + i′ + 2 ≤ m, and no integer lies in [s_lo, s_hi]; the census is computed at both ends and asserted
equal.  n = 17 and n = 19 have the SAME census (both windows lie in (4, 5)): 2,500 classes, k ≤ 16, of which 632
contain all four corners.  The record classes: n = 17 `n17_k08_0156` = BL{00, 10, 01}, BR{00, 10, 01}, TL00, TR00
(Bidwell; 7 tilted + 2 upright off-lattice squares); n = 19 `n19_k11_0236` = TL{00, 10, 01}, BR{00, 10, 20, 01, 11,
02}, TR00, BL00 (8 squares at 45°).

## 3. The climb rule — Lemma K1 run forward (`code/root243.py`, `code/descend243.py`, `code/climb243.py`)

**Page of a class S** (`root243`): the pins at S's slots, each an upright box of half-width d = (λ_max − 1)·max(u, v)
(u, v = the slot centre's coordinates in its own corner frame: the dilation moves it by (λ − 1)u per coordinate)
with exact core a = 1/2 − d; H = ONE non-pin square anywhere ([1/2, s − 1/2]² × [0°, 90°]: vacuous since n − k ≥ 1);
n − k − 1 anonymous squares; pad "window".

**The dichotomy (Theorem FC / Lemma SLOT-CROWD, n-general).**  Let p be a candidate cell of S (Young-completable,
unclipped, inside), T_p = S + p (a census class).  For a packing P at side m containing S's pins: either p's open
slot box is met by ≥ 2 non-pin squares (then, after the dilation, the widened box B_p⁺ is: FC Step 2), or the
teleport replaces its ≤ 1 meeter by the slot square, giving a packing at the SAME side containing T_p's pins.  Hence

    dead(S)  ⇐  dead(S with every candidate cell doubly met)  ∧  dead(T_p) for every p.

**The SPINE node** `S__spine` assumes the first horn at every candidate cell (`nparam243.set_extra`; the cells whose
T_p is already DEAD are Theorem FC's forced cells proper, the others are the assumption).  Its verdict:

* **DEAD** — the spine tree closed and every T_p is DEAD on the table: **S is closed** (theorem on the window);
* **REDUCES → {T_p open}** — the spine tree closed but some T_p are not DEAD: every packing of S contains the pins
  of one of those T_p (the climb's k + 1 branches; S's survival is theirs);
* **ALIVE** — the spine tree did not close in its wall (nothing claimed).

**The descent** (task 2b) decides the classes level by level, k = 16 → k_stop, so every T_p is decided before S
runs; a DEAD T_p is CITED (Lemma K1), never re-solved.  **The climb** (task 2) is then read off the table
(`climb243.py`): root = the four-corner class; a REDUCES node's children are its open T_p; leaves are DEAD / ALIVE /
the record's k (cap).  Because a superset of a four-corner set contains the four corners, the four-corner classes are
closed under extension: the descent restricted to them (`--corners4`, phases P1/P2) is exactly the climb's node set.

**Soundness statement.**  A DEAD branch of the climb is the theorem: *no packing of n unit squares in a square of
side m ∈ [s_lo, s_hi] contains axis-aligned unit squares at the slots of that class* (all D4 images), proved by the
A211 coarse tree's leaf lemmas (Lemma CT, EmptyBox / FC / REACH / NOROOM(-CN) / CC / SNAP into a DEAD stratum), each
n-general, on the page of §3, with every FC cell either forced by a DEAD extension (cited) or assumed on the spine
(and then paid for by the REDUCES targets).  Kills are [C: closed-form / library clip trace], no verifier clause, as
at n = 11.  **Canary**: the record realises its class and every class it contains inside the window, so none of those
may ever be DEAD; `climb243.canaries` checks it on every report.

## 4. Walls and resume (`code/chain243.sh`, `code/worker243.sh`)

Tree walls: 90 s per census class with k ≥ k_rec, 300 s for 4 < k < k_rec, 1800 s at the k ≤ 4 roots (the brief's
start-node wall).  Phases: P1 n = 17 four-corner classes k 16 → 4; P2 n = 19 the same; P3 / P4 the full census
k 16 → 1 (the k = 2 / k = 1 roots of task 3 are the k ≤ 2 levels).  Two workers (one python each, BelowNormal,
affinity 0xFFFF), barrier per level, commit per level (worker 0) and per phase.  RESUME: re-run chain243.sh (the
verdict table is the skip test); `touch logs/STOP` stops after the current class; `logs/TASKS_w1.txt` runs analysis
scripts inside worker 1's process (no third python).  `--rewall` re-runs ALIVE classes at a larger wall.
