# Evan Daniel’s Exact-Form and Local-Minimum Reports

[Issue #419](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/419) reports an exact-form
catalogue, and [issue #420](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/420) reports
local-minimum formalizations.
Both refer to [evand/square-packing](https://github.com/evand/square-packing) at
`f58a01774a733b232a88576470332185d8d11641`, committed on 7 October 2026. This packet
retains the unchanged catalogue, claim summary, exact checker, local-minimum generators,
chain-lemma discussion, licences and complete committed Lean source tree.
The acquisition declaration defines this selected scope.

## What Is Reported

The catalogue lists 269 exact configurations and 55 open counts.
Its four new side-polynomial claims are n = 102, 106, 152 and 177, with degrees 8, 32,
40 and 32. It reports 258 Lean `Packs` checks.
Configuration feasibility, identity of a selected polynomial root, irreducibility or
minimality, and equality to a published expression are distinct claims.
The source’s `verify_exact.py` checks geometry and root identities under its input
conditions; it does not establish irreducibility.
Decimal agreement or matching degrees alone do not establish published-expression
identity.

The 178 local-minimum claims comprise exactly 176 rows marked `band lemma`, plus n = 11
and 28. The definition in `Sqpack/LocalMin.lean` uses a neighbourhood of centres and
angles modulo quarter turns, with squares matched by index.
It is non-strict: a nearby packing must have side at least the declared side.
It permits plateaux, and implies neither rigidity nor a global lower bound.
The twelve exceptions to the simple integer-band shorthand are retained by the catalogue
rather than inferred away.
The chain-lemma discussion preserves its staggered-drift counterexample.

## Project Status and Replay Boundary

No source checker or Lean build was executed for this intake.
The `reported-catalogue.json` view is generated by `devtools.evand_report_catalogue`: it
checks roster completeness, metadata types, degree/coefficients and interval shape, and
keeps all checker flags labelled as source claims.
It establishes no geometry, polynomial minimality or formal proof.
Its mutation tests refuse duplicated counts, malformed intervals, incorrect degrees,
source flags with the wrong types and altered claim scopes.

Referenced `minpoly/data/` geometry payloads are outside the retained scope.
The generated 258-count `Packs` output is not fully committed upstream; the catalogue’s
flags are not a substitute for those inputs and outputs.
Kernel replay requires the source’s pinned toolchain/dependencies, complete certificate
input bindings and theorem/axiom review.
The n = 28 memory and wall figures are source reports, not measurements of this intake.

Claims refer to the pinned configurations.
Later SQUISH poses at n = 129, 153 and 258, and issue #399’s n = 266 arrangement, must
not inherit an old configuration’s polynomial or local-minimum result by count alone.
The existing algebraic pipeline [PR #403](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/pull/403)
owns canonical exact-value integration; this packet supplies source claims for that work
rather than creating a second polynomial registry.
The n = 17 work remains with its existing owner.
