## What the Gain Pattern Changes

The two retained geometric tuples are the four reflected `m1:j0` patches and their
diagonal images, the four reflected `m2:j7` patches.
All of their wall footprints equal their old endpoints.
Thus exp146 does not shrink either already-certified residual domain.
Repeating the unchanged baseline cover would check the same geometry again.

The twelve enlargements strengthen other class domains, so they justify BC318. They do
not predict its answer.
Containing a complete certified polygon is a stronger requirement than gaining area: the
added region must occur where the old certified patch lies.
A proper gain can have no useful relation to either certified component.
No such relation was evaluated in this review.

The four unchanged per-corner classes also leave a $4^4=256$-label subfamily whose
occupied unions are entirely unchanged.
The retained congruence and equal-area argument still prevents new occupied-union
containment within that subfamily.
It says nothing against direct dot coverage there.
Conversely, a new containment certificate must use at least one class whose wall
footprint was enlarged.
This is a structural observation from the completed equality dispositions, not a BC318
matrix entry.

Every frame remains individually allowed, so exp146 supplies no impossible class and no
elimination of an owner orientation.
Its information is the restriction of legal centre positions within each frame.
It does not establish joint compatibility of four owners, much less a packing.
The unchanged sectors also do not imply that no wall clipped their centre sets: a
clipped corner may leave support extrema unchanged, and another frame can remove a local
gain.

If BC318 returns no new tuple, direct coverage outranks another containment variant.
The existing five-dot certificate can cover a tuple even when no certified component, or
even whole certified union, is contained in its wall union.
The next experiment should distinguish that possibility before changing the dots or
splitting the owner classes.

## Validity and Verdict

The minimum new controls are exact synthetic dot-free sampling, mask products, overlap
deduplication, and strict witness rejection.
Include a centre on a dot boundary, on an owner-collision boundary, and on the container
boundary; each must fail the corresponding strict escape predicate.
A thin open strip must yield an interior witness.
Compare toy mask products with direct enumeration and test an empty mask.
Existing constructor and union controls remain prerequisites.

Replay mask bits through square-versus-convex-polygon separating axes, using exact
rational projections, rather than trusting only the Minkowski polygon membership used to
produce the masks. Dot avoidance can be checked directly in core-axis coordinates.
Reject any witness whose product contains an already-certified tuple in C; that
contradicts the input certificates and signals an instrument, input, or transport error.

| Verdict | Exact rule |
| --- | --- |
| Accept expansion | The selected tuple lies outside C; the independent checker completes all 361 unique required directions with zero uncovered area, all controls and input bindings pass, and the retained physical transfer applies. |
| Refute expansion by D | Every tuple outside C has at least one independently checked strict escape. This can occur from masks without a complete geometric census; every bit must have a replayable witness. |
| Unresolved | An unclassified additional tuple remains and no new complete certificate has passed. This includes a failed selected candidate, guard expiry, or a seed bank with no useful mask. Retain all checked escapes, the remaining count, and the selected candidate’s disposition. |
| Invalid | Any source, strict-witness, mask, known-certificate, direction, or area guard fails. Negative uncovered area is invalid, not a strong cover. |

Freeze the outcome question as existence of a new fixed-pattern cover.
Counts of removed tuples and new mask products are mechanism measurements; they cannot
replace that question after an incomplete run.
A selected tuple’s positive uncovered area disproves D for that tuple, not the existence
hypothesis over all other tuples and not the feasibility of a packing.

D has diagonal and central symmetry.
Exact simultaneous transport can add witnesses under that stabilizer, but the initial
adapter can simply record and replay those images rather than introducing an orientation
fold. The reflected second dot pattern H(D) is related by a whole-container permutation
of the full tuple family.
Searching it separately across all labels would duplicate the existence question once
that permutation is verified.
A failure of D for one tuple does not imply that H(D) fails for the same tuple.

## Which Slice Follows This Discriminator

If the one candidate passes, publish the new conditional exclusion and exact certificate
chain before choosing another unresolved case.
If it fails and its mask removes many labels, a successor can continue deterministic
candidate selection from the saved bank.
If the seed bank removes few labels, a limited split along owner-collision boundaries is
the next sampling refinement; a general arrangement engine is still premature.

If repeated checked failures localize the obstruction to the same residual regions,
choose one selected case for a bounded six-dot candidate or the stronger
existential-owner domain.
Six dots still contradict seven pairwise disjoint residual cores.
The existential-owner contract tests whether a residual core can coexist with at least
one legal owner pose, and can exclude cores that avoid the common wall footprint.
Its possible gain is not implied by exp146’s area gains.
Refine one owner coordinate or angle block only when the failures identify that
uncertainty, and prove the children cover the parent.

This ranking keeps the next scientific cost at one witness bank and one selected cover
check. It does not schedule 65,536 LPs or require a complete membership arrangement
before producing another useful result.

## T-023 Grade and the Physical Implication

**Keep T-023 at V3/C3.** Exp146 changes the research geometry available for other owner
classes and leaves T-023’s occupied geometry unchanged.
It provides no new independent confirmation or mechanization of the shared analytic
transfer. Even a successful BC318 or the proposed discriminator supplies wider
conditional case coverage, which is a new result or an explicitly scoped extension, not
an automatic grade increase for T-023.

No new gap in the reviewed finite-to-physical argument follows from this gain pattern.
Its premises must nevertheless be carried into each new certificate:

1. Four distinct selected cores own the four corner pairs.
   The unavoidable-mark theorem concerns selected rational-net cores, not merely their
   unit parents. Each selected owner lies in at least one declared signed-frame class.
2. Every physical unit parent contains its selected concentric B-core strictly, using
   the retained exact inequality $B(1+D_{\rm net})<1$ with
   $D_{\rm net}=207107/90000000$. All required orientations are represented.
3. An actual owner centre belongs to its retained Z_r. Intersecting the support
   rectangles over every allowed frame puts the wall footprint inside that actual
   owner’s core. Closed owner-centre constraints conservatively contain the actual strict
   poses.
4. Selected cores of distinct physical parents are closed and positively separated, even
   if the parents touch.
   Residual centres lie in the open legal container domain and avoid the four closed
   owner-collision sets.
   Dot contact counts as a hit; owner contact is excluded.
   A finite zero-deficit union proof covers the required boundaries, or the retained
   event-boundary transfer supplies the corresponding implication for an event-cell
   certificate.
5. Every one of the seven residual cores meets one of the five fixed dots.
   Their disjointness prevents two residual cores from sharing a dot, giving the
   contradiction. A six-dot successor would retain the same strict inequality between
   available dots and required residual cores.

The remaining global gap is exhaustive owner-case disposition or an explicit
owner-selection theorem routing every hypothetical packing into already excluded cases.
Individually possible classes, overlapping labels, one new certificate, and a partial
witness table do not supply that theorem.
The composed analytic premises remain audited prose rather than a complete mechanized
proof; a grade change needs the review or mechanization stated in the frontier record.
