# If the Fixed Six-Dot Test Refutes

**Conditional design note, 2026-09-09.** This note assumes a completed exp151 refutation
with a strictly replayed new escape.
It evaluates no target geometry and does not preregister or launch a successor.
A partial exp151 instead requires an instrument diagnosis.

## First: Screen Every Possible Replacement Sixth Site

The highest-value next discriminator is the exact intersection of the two retained
escaping closed B-cores: Q0 from exp149 and Q1 from the prospective exp151 deficit.
Both avoid the original five-dot set D and the same four wall patches.
Therefore any cover of this relaxed tuple domain by D plus one arbitrary site p
necessarily has

$$p\in I=Q_0\cap Q_1.$$

One tiny computation can decide whether repositioning only the sixth site remains
possible.
It carries more decisive information per unit of source work than another blind
placement or one unsuccessful joint-owner LP branch.

**Registered question:** Is I nonempty?
Freeze both source-bound escape receipts, tuple (0,0,0,7), and the endpoint/wall
sources; replay both escapes against their declared dot sets and the selected patches
before forming the two exact squares.
The first escape uses D, and the new one uses D plus x149.

If I is empty, independently confirm strict square-square separation.
This refutes the entire family D plus one site, wherever that site is placed.
It does not refute arbitrary six-site arrangements, weighted certificates, the physical
tuple, or n11. No new all-direction cover check is needed for this negative family
result.

If I is nonempty, retain its dimension and exact vertices and choose the arithmetic mean
of its distinct vertices as the single canonical replacement site.
Independently check closed membership in both cores.
This accepts only the necessary two-witness screen.
A later separately frozen all-361 check of D plus that site is still required.
No second candidate is selected in this screen.

**Point and segment intersections count as nonempty.** Use
`owner_footprints.convex_polygon_intersection`, whose clipping preserves them, and
direct closed membership/SAT replay.
Do not use `independent_union.convex_intersection`: that area-oriented routine
deliberately discards zero-area intersections and would give an invalid universal
negative here. Target-blind controls must include separated, overlapping, edge-touching,
and point-touching squares.

The smallest source addition is a bound two-receipt screen composed from the existing
square construction, closed intersection, membership, and SAT helpers.
Record the intersection or strict separator and source identities; do not construct a
witness bank, LP, or angle sweep.
A prospective 30-second internal / 60-second external single run is ample as a declared
cap, not a measured runtime promise; source parsing has recently cost about nine
seconds.

## Second: A Joint-Core Witness, With a Fixed Residual Pose

An exact joint witness would establish that an escape survives owner-owner coupling in
the chosen snapped-core model.
Freeze the four exp150 owner frames, permit their centres to vary over the complete
corresponding transformed Z polygons, and fix one residual pose.
Select one signed SAT branch for each of the ten square pairs before running the target:
six owner-owner pairs and four owner-residual pairs.

This is a small linear feasibility problem in eight owner-centre coordinates and one
common strict margin.
Maximize eta subject to centre membership and each selected pair gap at least eta.
A floating LP can propose rational centres, but acceptance requires eta>0 and
independent exact replay of every centre/class/container/mark condition and all ten
pairwise strict SAT tests.
Preserve the oblique reflection convention from the admitted compatibility source.

One successful witness proves existence of five mutually separated B-cores in that
relaxation. It does not prove unit parents or an eleven-square packing.
An infeasible or nonpositive-margin selected branch is only a failed witness search;
even exact infeasibility of that branch does not exclude other branches, centres, or
owner frames. Do not build an all-frame infeasibility engine.

For x149, the four owner-residual branches can be frozen from exp150’s positive witness
axes. A joint witness there would show that joint-owner tightening cannot remove x149,
but the proposed sixth dot already hits x149. After an exp151 refutation, a joint
witness for the **new** escape has more direct value for the active deficit.
Reusing exp150’s owner frames is permissible; reusing its old residual separation claims
for a different residual pose is not.
Any new branch selection must be part of that separately registered instrument.

This places the joint witness second, especially if the two-core intersection is empty.
It can decide whether a concrete gap survives a stronger necessary domain, whereas a
failed witness attempt cannot justify refinement or exclusion.

## Third: Reposition More Sites or Reweight a Fixed Support

Seven unit-weight dots have total mass seven and cannot contradict seven residual cores.
A valid weighted alternative needs nonnegative mass at least one on every admissible
residual B-core, with total mass strictly below seven.
More generally, if the exact minimum covered mass is m>0 and total mass W, require
W/m<7. Equality is insufficient.

If Q0 and Q1 are disjoint and the original five dots retain unit weight, any added
nonnegative measure must put at least one mass on each of those two disjoint cores.
The total is then at least seven.
Fractionally splitting an added mass budget below two cannot rescue that frozen
five-unit-dot base. Reweighting or moving the original sites is a different family and
can evade this obstruction.

The existing `run_owner_footprint_cover` LP and `exact_minimum_covered_mass_on_pieces`
replay provide the relevant weighted machinery.
A future smallest weighted slice would freeze one small support, such as D plus the two
escaped centres, use the two exact escape constraints as mandatory rows, and seek a
rational candidate with W/m<7 on the selected wall domain.
A floating objective or finite sample of rows is only a proposal.
All 361 directions and exact domain/mass replay are required for acceptance; a
finite-support dual rejection concerns that support, not arbitrary atom locations.
This costs more adaptation than the two-core intersection and should follow its evidence
rather than precede it.

## Global Routing Remains Open

Each proposed result concerns the same selected owner tuple or a particular residual
pose. A global bound still needs every hypothetical packing to admit a valid owner
selection inside an excluded tuple family, or an exhaustive disposition of the other
selections. Neither the old five-dot witness masks nor D’s unchanged symmetry can be
assigned to a moved, enlarged, or reweighted site set without new evidence.
T023’s grade and the global n11 bound do not change from these exploratory screens
alone.
