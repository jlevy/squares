# Exact Regularization at 127 Squares

T-341 certifies a packing below T-330. Uneven axis-aligned squares fell from 15 to 2,
and near-axis squares fell from 2 to zero.
The replay ran the conservative, coincide regularizer in the KKT frame at the source
orientation.

The run used Evan Daniel’s MIT-licensed solver and regularizer at
`675a5eaa8712542daac8b4515b0f69ad7044df44`. The
[native certificate](certificates/n-127.cert) fixes the exact side.
The [replay and controls](receipts/) cover all walls and square pairs.
The
[mathematical review](../../../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-11-23-squares-project-regularized-n127.md)
records the finite claim and the predecessor comparison.
