    Geometry

    A feasible construction establishes a packing bound. Source-invalid records are retained as source history and must not be used as packing bounds.

    Optimality

    A feasible packing or a KKT local minimum does not establish a global optimum. Read the original relation and status below.

    Side decimals are recorded approximations. A root check named contains_recorded_side records rounding or truncation agreement, not literal inclusion of the printed decimal in the isolating interval. For a replay-backed SQUISH rational upper bound, this check permits the registered upward decimal ceiling: at least the exact side and less than one final printed unit above it. A source root ordinal with no count is not independently verified, even when the interval has exactly one real root.
