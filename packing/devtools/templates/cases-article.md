# Case Records

<p class="subtitle">Every tracked case, <var>n</var> = 1 to {{LAST_N}}, one record each</p>

</div>

Each record gathers what the project knows about packing $n$ unit squares in the
smallest square. It opens with the case’s visual summary: the best packing known, drawn
large, then the number line of its lower and upper bounds, where each comes from and
what is still open. Then come every bound, reported and verified, with its exact form or
minimal polynomial and its credit; every result in the register that concerns the case,
with its rungs; and the case file’s own account.
All {{COUNT}} are read from their records, `packing/frontier/n-NNN.md`, when the site is
built.
Each has an address of its own to share, such as [`cases/11.html`](cases/11.html),
and a case in the [frontier survey](atlas.html#the-frontier-survey) or the overview’s
atlas grid opens the same record.
In the index below, a solved case is set in the accent.
