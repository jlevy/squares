# Lazy Catalogue Payload

The criterion was declared in `think-mo36` before measuring the generated browser: count
the default unselected page, its automatic script/style resources and the index JSON;
require at most 25% of the preserved complete HTML’s 4,811,981 bytes.
This is a deterministic byte-count determination, not a latency experiment.
Timing, compression, browser memory and rendering cost remain unmeasured.

Before accepting the comparison, independently restore every current and historical
record, including nested note polynomials, and compare all original metadata and exact
coefficient strings with the canonical register.
Polynomial text and LaTeX are redundant renderings; the complete archives retain them.
Required Chromium controls must show that opening the index does not fetch individual
records or coefficients, that selection fetches the correct record, and that coefficient
expansion preserves signs, zeros and large integers.
Astra reviews the separation of algebraic identity, geometry, optimality, source history
and unchecked root ordinals.

The complete HTML and 366-page PDF remain useful archival exports.
They are the control, and the compact browser is the candidate.
No coefficient truncation or new mathematical admission is part of this hypothesis.
