# Reuse Admitted Gupta Facts Within One Confirmation Plan

Registered before source edits or timing.
Operator: Astra subagent news_security_c; parent owns campaign indexes, source
integration, and independent review.
Status: rejected; the implementation is withdrawn.
The frozen performance criterion was not met.

## Hypothesis and Frozen Criterion

`register.reported_bound` reads and admits the complete seventeen-case fact roster.
`confirmation.case_plan` calls it directly and through `section` for each selected case.
A call-local admitted map and private pure rendering helpers should reduce inclusive
wall for the exact result-scope refusal node above by at least 15%, preserving its full
private input fixture and atomic-refusal assertion.
All seventeen facts and all fourteen selected improving cases remain present; the other
three facts are withdrawals.

Alternate three control/candidate pairs.
Every observation must pass; require at least 15% reduction in median wall,
nonoverlapping ranges, and allocated worker-seconds at most 1.25 times control.
Report median and range and a separate complexity judgment.
No confirmatory speedup, whole-shard improvement, or three-minute-loop claim follows.

## What the Prediction Missed

The plan replaces 28 per-case full-roster reads while preserving complete admission, but
the measured node still prepares the full function-scoped private proposal, checks
houses and history, parses registries, and validates schemas.
Its setup remains roughly four seconds.
The proposed change addresses only part of the inclusive cost.
The experiment leaves any immutable-fixture preparation change for a separately
registered comparison; changing that fixture after seeing these results would invalidate
this criterion.

The source delta is a bounded call-local reuse with a new byte/custody check before the
plan leaves its owning function.
Public render requests always admit the complete seventeen-case roster, including
withdrawals. New controls reject changed withdrawn facts and acquisition pins during
planning, and reject changed full facts on a subsequent public request.
Receipt and history validation remain fresh and unchanged.
The parent may retain the refactor for reduced repeated work and explicit late-input
protection, but this experiment does not satisfy its performance acceptance threshold.

## Final Disposition

The three-file production/fixture proposal is withdrawn.
The original thin driver called `run_sample` directly; its receipts lack the maintained
CLI’s complete provenance and registered arm labels.
Its 11.97% arithmetic reduction already misses the frozen 15% rule.
Preserve those observations as limited evidence, not campaign acceptance.
The extra batching and freshness machinery had no separately demonstrated correctness
requirement sufficient to retain it after that failure.
