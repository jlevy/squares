# `s(295)` — open

**Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form, 14 October 2026.** Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form
(wand125, 13 October 2026, T-180) states $s(N) \ge a(N)$ for every non-square
$N \ge 49$, from a measure of line segments on 34 intervals of the fractional part.
At $n = 295$, $k = 17$ and interval 6 give
$a(295) = 17.218111176930066152549269061890\ldots$, so the reported lower bound here is
now $s(295) \ge 17.218111176930$, rounded down, above Nagamochi’s closed form,
$17.18641405623$. The source replayed its certificate with its own checker; no record of
it has been replayed here, so the verified lower bound is unchanged.
The source states that its search, certificates, checkers and text were produced with AI
assistance (Claude and Codex), under the direction of and reviewed by Hiroaki Hosono.

**House adoption, 11 October 2026.** Evan Daniel’s display regularization of Ryan Xu’s
packing (T-146) is now this case’s house: its exact certificate proves
$s(295) \le 17.7042327907360304$, the reported and the verified upper bound, `7.82e-10`
below the ceiling the case held until then, $17.7042327915177043$ from Ryan Xu’s packing
(T-125), which stays retained as history
([the certificate](#evan-daniels-certificate-t-146)).

**Exact certificate, 2026-10-06.** Evan Daniel’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md) published
on 5 October 2026 an exact rational certificate of this packing, decided here over
$\mathbb{Q}$ by two exact checkers that share no code with each other or with the
source, and by the source’s own two run here: it previously proved
$s(295) \le 17.70710678118655$, the verified upper bound (T-101). Until then the
verified upper bound was the trivial grid bound $18$.

Previously, the packing gave $s(295) \le 17.70710679$, and the strongest lower bound
independently verified here is $17.180827$ from Karakuş’s general theorem, leaving a gap
of $0.5263$. General bound: s(N) >= 1/2 + sqrt(N - floor(sqrt(N)) + 1/4).

## The earlier verified ceiling

The verified ceiling at that intake was $17.70710678118655$, proved by Evan Daniel’s
exact rational certificate of this packing
(`E-evand-exact-ceilings-2026-10-05-exact-replay`, T-101). It was **larger** than the
then-reported $17.70710678118654$, by `1e-14`.

It is not the value of $s(295)$ and not a different packing: it is the certificate’s own
side, $17.7071067811865475246\ldots$, rounded up at the fourteen decimals the catalogue
prints. The catalogue gives the packing’s side exactly, as `17 + (1/2)sqrt(2)`, and the
certificate’s side lies `1.8e-19` above that: its squares are rational and each is kept
clear of its neighbours and the walls, so it bounds the exact side from above and does
not reach it. That side is irrational, so no rational certificate reaches it.
That certificate gap was the upper-bound blocker at that intake.
Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
