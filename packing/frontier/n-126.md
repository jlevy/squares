# s(126) — Square Packing Case

## The Upper Bound

Nate Chaoweeraprasit (itsnaka), using SQUISH, reports $s(126) \le S_n$, with
$S_n = \frac{4828972106460286237253237344483}{410153066106902321950462312448}$ in
[SQUISH’s ten-packing release](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/401). The
certificate gives rational centres and half-angle parameters for 126 unit squares.
Its decimal display is $11.7735852916961079$; the rational fraction states the claimed
bound exactly.

The new packing improves the earlier reported side $11.774735132387832842560264587322$
from [evand exact optima 2026-10-05]. The verified lane retains the earlier packing
until the new certificate’s pair and wall checks and mathematical review are recorded
here. These are upper bounds; no optimality or rigidity evidence for the new packing is
claimed.

The author credits David Ellsworth’s records and tools and states that SQUISH’s solver,
verification and issue were written with Claude Opus 5.5 under his direction,
management, review and steering.
The [source packet](../resources/web/squish-401-2026-10-07/README.md) retains derived
packing facts and source metadata.

## Earlier Packing

Joost de Winter found the earlier packing; Evan Daniel improved it.
Its reported side was $11.774735132387832842560264587322$. The earlier verified ceiling
$11.774735132387832842560264587322$ remains in this case’s verified lane (T-098),
supported by
[Daniel’s certificate packet](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md).
This repository replayed Daniel’s two source checkers and independently decided the
rational certificate with its own exact-rational routes; the packet records the shared
premises and receipts.
Any optimality or rigidity observations about that packing concern its own pose.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
