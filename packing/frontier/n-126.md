# s(126) — Square Packing Case

## The SQUISH Update

Nate Chaoweeraprasit, using SQUISH,
[reports a tighter bound](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/401#issuecomment-6043191866)
$s(126) \le 11.7733036066072403$, with exact side
$3196078786847066046655612475207/271468306062659398105946914816$. This is a reported
V0/C0 claim pending complete replay and new scoped review.
The author reports the seed as Francisco Couzo’s s(105), grafted into n’s Kingbird
record. The
[revision-specific packet](../resources/web/squish-401-update-2026-10-07/README.md)
retains the source print $11.7733036066072394$ separately from the safe decimal ceiling.
The atlas drawing below uses the update’s exact rational geometry, checked for the
drawing independently of the frontier assurance.
The earlier certified bound and its retained evidence remain unchanged; this reported
claim stays V0/C0.

Separately, this repository’s
[numerical translation screen](../atlas/known-best/translation-escape-screen.json) finds
a nontrivial feasible motion in the current update geometry drawn by the atlas.
That numerical assessment concerns this update pose alone; it does not settle the
optimum or describe the earlier certified pose.

## The Earlier Verified Upper Bound

Nate Chaoweeraprasit (itsnaka), using SQUISH, reports $s(126) \le S_n$, with
$S_n = \frac{4828972106460286237253237344483}{410153066106902321950462312448}$ in
[SQUISH’s ten-packing release](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/401). The
certificate gives rational centres and half-angle parameters for 126 unit squares.
The source’s original finite decimal display is $11.7735852916961079$; the rational
fraction states the claimed bound exactly.

The earlier SQUISH packing improves the earlier reported side
$11.774735132387832842560264587322$ from [evand exact optima 2026-10-05]. This
repository confirmed the upper bound by independently re-implemented exact-rational
checks: native `exact_verify` and `devtools.check_rational_witness_independent` accept
every pair and wall of the earlier packing and reject both geometric controls.
The verified display is $11.7735852916961071$, the exact source side rounded upward at
sixteen decimals. Both accepted reviews cover the shared half-angle conversion and the
complete roster. The submitted certificates establish feasible upper bounds and make no
optimality or rigidity claim.

The author credits David Ellsworth’s records and tools and states that SQUISH’s solver,
verification and issue were written with Claude Opus 5.5 under his direction,
management, review and steering.
The [source packet](../resources/web/squish-401-2026-10-07/README.md) retains derived
packing facts and source metadata.

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

The verified ceiling is $s(126) \le 11.7735852916961071$, while the reported packing has
side $11.7733036066072403$, smaller by $0.0002816850888668$. The `verified_upper_bound`
certifies a ceiling, not the value of $s(126)$; `reported_upper_bound` records the
stronger source claim, whose independent exact replay remains pending in this record.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
