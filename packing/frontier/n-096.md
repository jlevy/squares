# `s(96)` — open

**Verified from $n = 95$, 6 October 2026.** wand125’s $s(95) \ge 1993/200 = 9.965$
(T-090), decided here by sqverify-fast at all 201 net directions, is above this count’s
verified $249/25$, and a packing of 96 squares contains one of 95, so it bounded this
case as well: the verified lower bound until this count’s own $997/100$ (T-082) was
decided here later that day.
The reported field keeps the $s(96) = 10$ reported below (T-081), which has not been
replayed here.

**External intake, 2026-10-03.** Evan Daniel reports $s(96) = 10$ as an instance of
$s(k^2 - 4) = k$ at $k = 10$
([jlevy/squares#316](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/316)). The source’s Lean
proves the general reduction conditional on `ValidTilt9`; the exact Python checker for
that premise has not been replayed here, so the verified lower bound is unchanged.
The source’s CREDITS.md says this work was produced by Claude (Anthropic) in a single
session under human direction.

**External intake, 2026-10-06.** A second, separately written exact checker, wand125’s,
reports `ValidTilt9` on the same cover
([6 October packet](../resources/web/wand125-valid7-independent-check-2026-10-06/README.md),
`E-k2m4-wand125-validtilt9-report`). Its record check and a sample of its roots ran
here; the full run has not been repeated, so the verified lower bound is unchanged.

**External intake, 2026-10-03.** wand125’s
[mixed-certificate source](../resources/web/wand125-mixed-bounds-2026-10-03/README.md)
reports the certificate `mixed_n96_L997` at side $997/100 = 9.97$ (T-082), from a
density of 376 rectangles of total mass $9599999/100000 < 96$, accepted there at every
net angle by its research copy of Tokoharu’s checker at threshold one.
It is below the $s(96) = 10$ reported above (T-081), and above the verified $249/25$ of
that day by $0.01$, superseding the source’s `mixed_n96_L996`, and above the $1993/200$
carried from $n = 95$ since 6 October by $0.005$. sqverify-fast, this repository’s
clean-room verifier, decided it here on 6 October 2026 at all 201 net directions.
Its first control failed closed: the mutant scaled to $99/100$ verified too, at the one
direction that control runs it, which showed the certificate’s slack there and not a
fault (think-0uia). The repaired control, which runs that mutant at every net direction,
refused it at 187 of the 201, so it is also the verified lower bound: confirmed,
independently re-implemented.
wand125’s README says parts of the work were produced with AI assistance under human
direction.

**External intake, 2026-10-02 (afternoon).** wand125’s

Open. The best known packing gives $s(96) \le 10$, and the verified lower bound is

## The packing

The record catalogue does not picture $n = 96$: no arrangement has ever been found that
beats the trivial $\lceil\sqrt{96}\rceil = 10$ grid, so the grid is still the best known
packing. That is a statement about what has been searched, not a proof.

Karakuş’s general bound, independently verified here and below this certificate at this

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).
