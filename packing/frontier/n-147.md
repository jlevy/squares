# `s(147)` — open

**House adoption, 15 November 2026.** Roberto Ono’s packing from a band cut of Evan
Daniel’s n = 229 packing, the first below 7 + 4√2 (T-302) is now this case’s house: its
exact certificate proves $s(147) \le 12.6510435565876945$, the reported and the verified
upper bound, rounded outward from its exact side.
The exact certificate side improves the previous verified exact side by about
`5.81e-03`. The earlier exact ceiling,
$316421356237309504880168872421/25000000000000000000000000000$ from the catalogue’s
packing (T-155), applied until this adoption and stays retained as history; the reported
upper bound it replaces, $12.65685424949238$ from [Kingbird], lies `5.81e-03` above the
new side ([the certificate](#roberto-onos-certificate-t-302)).

**Exact regularized certificate, 2026-10-12.** This project’s regularization pass, Evan
Daniel’s exact solver and regularizer run here on this register’s witness, produced an
exact rational packing of this arrangement, decided here over $\mathbb{Q}$ by two exact
checkers: it proves $s(147) \le 12.65685424949238019520675489684$, the verified upper
bound until 15 November 2026 (T-155), which the record printed as $12.65685424949239$.
Until then the verified upper bound was exactly $12.65685424949239$, the side of Evan
Daniel’s exact certificate of this packing rounded up at the printed precision (T-101).

**Linear-measure validation, 4 November 2026 (T-179).** Hosono’s
[linear source](../resources/web/wand125-linear-n145-2026-10-13/README.md) of 13 October
gives $s(145) \ge 306/25 = 12.24$ by the certificate `mixed_n145_L1224`: a measure of
594 point, 1,541 segment and 9 rectangle orbits under D4, of total mass
$14499999/100000 < 145$, on T-080’s net of 201 half-angles at core side $9977/10000$,
checked by T-080’s checker.
Its mass is below 147, so the bound holds here as well.
Its complete replay here, from 2 November 2026 to 4 November 2026, returned the
certificate’s own record at all 201 angles, the route’s control refused two mutated
measures, and the
[mathematical review of 14 October](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-14-wand125-fine-net-and-linear-lower-bounds.md)
accepted the argument: it raises both lower lanes here, the verified from Karakuş’s
$12.129703$ and the reported from Green’s $12.21386694779$, which DS7 reports and which
has no recovered proof.
It is confirmed by reproduction with the producer’s code; no second implementation
stands beside it.
Hosono’s README says parts of the work were produced with AI assistance
under human direction.

**Exact certificate, 2026-10-06.** Evan Daniel’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md) published
on 5 October 2026 an exact rational certificate of this packing, decided here over
$\mathbb{Q}$ by two exact checkers that share no code with each other or with the
source, and by the source’s own two run here: it proves $s(147) \le 12.65685424949239$,
which was the verified upper bound from 6 to 12 October 2026 (T-101). Until then the
verified upper bound was the trivial grid bound $13$.

Open. Previously, the best known packing gave $s(147) \le 12.65685425$, and the verified
lower bound is $s(147) \ge 306/25 = 12.24$, from Hosono’s n145 linear certificate
(T-179), leaving a gap of $0.4169$. General bound: s(N) >= 1/2 + sqrt(N - floor(sqrt(N))
\+ 1/4).

## The earlier verified ceiling

Until 15 November 2026 the verified upper bound here was
$12.65685424949238019520675489684$, exactly, proved by the exact regularized certificate
of this packing (`E-regularized-bounds-2026-10-12-exact-decision`, T-155), and the
record prints it as $12.65685424949239$. As printed it was **larger** than the
catalogue’s printed side $12.65685424949238$, then the best known, by `1e-14`.

It was not the value of $s(147)$ and not a different packing: it is the certificate’s
own exact side. The catalogue gives the packing’s side exactly, as `7 + 4 sqrt(2)`, and
the certificate’s side lies `1.2e-30` above that, so it bounds the exact side from above
and does not reach it.

certificate (T-302) replaced both lanes.

## The regularized exact certificate

This project’s regularization pass of 11 and 12 October 2026 ran Evan Daniel’s exact
solver and regularizer
([`evand/square-packing`](https://github.com/evand/square-packing), MIT, vendored here)

tight contacts over $\mathbb{Q}$ at the witness’s own side.
The result is an exact rational packing of the same 147 squares in a square of side
$12.65685424949238019520675489684$, `9.8e-15` below the verified upper bound the record
held before, and `1.3e-19` below the side of Evan Daniel’s certificate of this packing
(T-101): it is the witness’s own side.
Measured against the witness under the box’s eight symmetries, its squares pair one to
one with the witness’s; none moves by more than `1e-3`, and none turns by more than
`1e-6` as a sine.
It draws the same picture as the witness, so the atlas drawing does not
change.

Each pair and each wall is decided over $\mathbb{Q}$ twice, by `sqpack`’s exact
separating-axis test and by an independent checker that shares no code with it, and both
accept it; both refuse it with its tightest pair pushed into overlap and with its box
shrunk past its least clearance ([certificate](../cases/regularized_bounds/README.md)).
The same two checkers accepted it in the run that made it, so this is a reproduction
with the producer’s code.
That proves $s(147) \le 12.65685424949238019520675489684$, the verified upper bound

It says nothing about optimality, and the packing stays its finders’.

## The lower bound

The verified field rests on the complete replay here of Hosono’s linear certificate for
[145 squares](n-145.md) ([`E-wand125-linear-n145-1224-source-replay`](evidence.yaml),
`V3/C3`): all 201 directions, from 2 November 2026 to 4 November 2026, each returning
the certificate’s own record.
Its mass, $14499999/100000$, is below 147, so a packing of 147 at a side below $306/25$
would capture at least 147 from it.

Until 4 November 2026 the verified lower bound was Karakuş’s general bound, which
applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.

The reported lower-bound expression for $s(147)$ is
`22*sqrt(6)/145 + 2*sqrt(2) + 1307/145` (approximately $12.21386694779$). Friedman’s DS7
survey, Theorem 9, k=12, reports this bound at n=145; reference [8] is Green’s private
communication (2000). The source proof has not been recovered.
The unavoidable-set argument DS7’s Figure 34 illustrates does not prove it: at k=12 that
point pattern leaves a unit square empty (devtools.check_green_ds7). Inherited at n=147
by monotonicity. This is a source-reported lower bound; it was the reported lower bound
here until 4 November 2026, when T-179’s replayed $306/25$, above it, took both lower
lanes. The exact specialization and comparison are retained by
[`audit_ds7_lower_bounds`](../devtools/audit_ds7_lower_bounds.py).

## Roberto Ono’s certificate (T-302)

Roberto Ono’s packing from a band cut of Evan Daniel’s n = 229 packing, the first below
7 + 4√2, reported on [jlevy/squares#985](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/985),
at an exact rational side, displayed upward as $12.6510435565876945$ (T-302). The
certificate gives a rational side and, for each of the 147 unit squares, a rational
centre and the tangent of its half-angle; the
[retained packet](../resources/web/ono-issue985-2026-11-14/README.md) holds it with the
complete native jobs that decided it.

Both maintained exact routes decided it here, `sqpack`’s exact separating-axis verifier
and the independent rational corner checker, with its duplicate-square and
outside-container controls, and the 14 November review’s clean-room checker, frozen
before any producer or witness code was read, decided it again by separating axes in
integers and by exact clipping in GMP rationals over every square, wall and pair; its 20
controls at this count, each stated beforehand, were each decided as stated.
No route here is the source’s code, so the replay is independently re-implemented.
The
[review of 14 November 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-11-14-ono-984-985.md)
accepted the replay for finite feasibility.
That proves $s(147) \le 12.6510435565876945$, the verified upper bound: confirmed,
independently re-implemented, by a Claude agent, a system family that also produced the
certificate. It says nothing about optimality, rigidity or a local minimum.

Issue 985 says the packing was found, certified and checked by an AI coding agent
(Claude, by Anthropic) working in the author’s harness, directed by a person who set the
harness up and read the request before it was posted.

The issue says the packing was made from Evan Daniel’s packing of 229 squares, this
registry’s record there, a three-wide diagonal band of tilted squares between two
corner-anchored grids: every square with 13 ≤ x + y < 19 deleted and the squares beyond
moved by (−3, −3), which removes one period of the band, then a penalty step and an
exact-constraint polish that remove the overlaps left; it asks that Daniel’s packing be
credited as the start.
Against the case’s house best packing before this adoption, the Kingbird catalogue’s
45-degree packing at 7 + 4√2 that 147 shares with 148, it is a new arrangement: no
symmetry pairs nearest centres one to one, and the optimal assignment moves 92 squares
beyond a tenth; its 44 tilted squares lean one way at 25.8 to 27.7 degrees, where the
house’s 64 stand at 45. So Roberto Ono is the packing’s finder, from Daniel’s band, the
first below 7 + 4√2; the issue claims the upper bound only.

The house it replaces, $316421356237309504880168872421/25000000000000000000000000000$
from the catalogue’s packing (T-155), stays retained with its own replay as history.
