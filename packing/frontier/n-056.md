## The packing

The record catalogue does not picture $n = 56$: no arrangement has ever been found that
beats the trivial $\lceil\sqrt{56}\rceil = 8$ grid, so the grid is still the best known
packing. That is a statement about what has been searched, not a proof.

([`E-n056-wand125-mixed-78025-sqverify-fast-replay`](evidence.yaml), `V3/C3`) of

