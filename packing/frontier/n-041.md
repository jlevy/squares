## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is $7$, which is **larger** than the best known
$6.92669309446880$ two fields above it.
It is not a tighter reading of the same packing and it is not the value of $s(41)$: it
is the strongest ceiling this repository can certify from its own evidence — the trivial
grid bound $\lceil\sqrt{41}\rceil = 7$ — and it trails the reported construction by
$0.07330690553120$.

Its `exact_form` is the exact form of that ceiling, never of $s(41)$. $s(41)$ is not
known exactly; `status` stays `open`, and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter
names the gap. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The lower bound

The verified lower bound is $169/25 = 6.76$, from wand125’s own n41 rectangle-density
certificate of 1 October, replayed in full here (`T-074`); its certificate of 28
September, $1351/200 = 6.755$ (`T-070`), held the field earlier on 2 October.
Until then it was $13/2$: the complete n40 exact replay verifies that bound, and
deleting one square from any hypothetical packing proves it for n41 too.
A second interval implementation or generalized native importer would add confirmation
and reuse, but it is not a missing premise of either bound.

**Corrected 2 October 2026:** Nagamochi’s Lemma 1 is false (Karakuş 2026), so his closed
form below is now a reported bound whose published proof is incomplete
([review](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).
Nagamochi’s earlier general closed form remains part of the evidence history and applies
to every $N \ge 4$:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```
