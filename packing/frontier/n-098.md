# `s(98)` — solved

$s(98) = 10$. Established by chelokot’s Lean proof (2026), replayed here with its axiom
receipt, of the family $s(k^2 - 2) = k$ that Nagamochi’s general theorem (2005) stated
first; Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, on which his proof rests, is false.
Lean theorem: s(n^2 - 2) = n for every integer n >= 2.

## The packing

The record catalogue does not picture $n = 98$: no arrangement has ever been found that
beats the trivial $\lceil\sqrt{98}\rceil = 10$ grid, so the grid is still the best known
packing. That is a statement about what has been searched, not a proof.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is chelokot’s Lean
theorem, replayed here with its axiom receipt, which applies to every $n \ge 2$:

```
s(n² − 2) = n
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
gives the same value at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false, and
chelokot’s proof does not use it
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

See the [Frontier corpus summary](README.md#what-the-corpus-shows) for the current
aggregate count; source-reported bounds are recorded separately.
