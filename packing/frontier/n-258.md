# `s(258)` — open

## The SQUISH Update

Nate Chaoweeraprasit, using SQUISH,
[reports a tighter bound](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/401#issuecomment-6043191866)
$s(258) \le 16.5634480021391540$, with exact side $582774517581285/35184372088832$. This
update is confirmed at V3/C3 by the complete retained dual exact replay and separately
prompted scoped AI reviews.
It establishes a feasible upper bound; optimality and human oversight have not been
established. The author reports the seed as Francisco Couzo’s s(259), squares removed.
The [revision-specific packet](../resources/web/squish-401-update-2026-10-07/README.md)
retains the source print $16.5634480021391539$ separately from the safe decimal ceiling.
The atlas drawing below uses the update’s exact rational geometry, checked for the
drawing independently of the frontier assurance.
Previously, Earlier certified geometries, evidence and reviews remain retained as
historical provenance, separate from this update’s confirmation.

**Exact certificate, 2026-10-06.** Evan Daniel’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md) published
on 5 October 2026 an exact rational certificate of this packing, decided here over
$\mathbb{Q}$ by two exact checkers that share no code with each other or with the
source, and by the source’s own two run here: it proves $s(258) \le 16.57106781186548$,
the verified upper bound (T-101). Until then the verified upper bound was the trivial
grid bound $17$.

Open. Previously, the earlier verified packing gives $s(258) \le 16.57106782$, and the
strongest lower bound independently verified here is $16.064382$ from Karakuş’s general
theorem, leaving a gap of $0.5067$. General bound: s(N) >= 1/2 + sqrt(N - floor(sqrt(N))
\+ 1/4).

## Earlier catalogue ceiling

The earlier certified ceiling was $16.57106781186548$, proved by Evan Daniel’s exact
rational certificate of this packing (`E-evand-exact-ceilings-2026-10-05-exact-replay`,
T-101). It is **larger** than the earlier catalogue print $16.57106781186547$, by
`1e-14`.

It is not the value of $s(258)$ and not a different packing: it is the certificate’s own
side, $16.5710678118654752442\ldots$, rounded up at the fourteen decimals the catalogue
prints. The catalogue gives the packing’s side exactly, as `(19/2) + 5 sqrt(2)`, and the
certificate’s side lies `1.7e-19` above that: its squares are rational and each is kept
clear of its neighbours and the walls, so it bounds the exact side from above and does
not reach it. That side is irrational, so no rational certificate reaches it.
The earlier catalogue-geometry blocker in the frontmatter records that difference.
The current `reported_upper_bound` instead names the tighter, confirmed SQUISH update.

The earlier catalogue side $16.57106781186547$ had also been conjectured optimal in this
record. The smaller update geometry rules out that conjecture; it is retained here as
history, and no current optimum is conjectured.

$16.5710678118654752442\ldots$, `5.2e-15` above the side the Kingbird catalogue prints,
$16.57106781186547$. Rounded to binary64, square for square, its pose is the one the
atlas previously pictured for this count.
Evan Daniel’s solver moves the binary64 pose to a nearby exact KKT point of the problem
of minimizing the side under non-overlap, computed at 80 digits, and rounds it outward
to rationals.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.

The reported lower-bound expression for $s(258)$ is `574*sqrt(2)/257 + 3343/257`
(approximately $16.16637581635$). Friedman’s DS7 survey, Theorem 9, k=16, reports this
bound at n=257; reference [8] is Green’s private communication (2000). The source proof
has not been recovered.
The unavoidable-set argument DS7’s Figure 34 illustrates does not prove it: at k=16 that
point pattern leaves a unit square empty (devtools.check_green_ds7). Inherited at n=258
by monotonicity. This is a source-reported lower bound; it does not replace the verified
bound above. The exact specialization and comparison are retained by
[`audit_ds7_lower_bounds`](../devtools/audit_ds7_lower_bounds.py).
