The verified interval is $5.865 \le s(30) \le 6$, with width $0.135$. Its lower end
is wand125’s replayed rectangle-density certificate, $1173/200$ (`T-045`, 2026-10-02),
which superseded Tokoharu’s $571/100$ carried from $n = 29$.

## The packing

The record catalogue does not picture $n = 30$: no arrangement has ever been found that
beats the trivial $\lceil\sqrt{30}\rceil = 6$ grid, so the grid is still the best known
packing. That is a statement about what has been searched, not a proof.

## The lower bound

The verified lower bound is $47/8 = 5.875$, from wand125’s own n30 rectangle-density
certificate of 1 October, replayed in full here (`T-074`); its certificate of 27
September, $1173/200 = 5.865$ (`T-045`), held the field earlier on 2 October.
Until then it was $571/100$: the complete n29 interval replay verifies that bound, and
deleting one square from any hypothetical packing proves it for n30 too.
A method-distinct rectangle coverage implementation would add confidence and improve
generalization, but it is not a missing premise of either bound.

**Corrected 2 October 2026:** Nagamochi’s Lemma 1 is false (Karakuş 2026), so his closed
form below is now a reported bound whose published proof is incomplete
([review](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).
Nagamochi’s earlier general closed form remains part of the evidence history and applies
to every $N \ge 4$:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```
