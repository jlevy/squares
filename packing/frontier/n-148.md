## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is $12.65685424949238019520675489684$, exactly,
proved by the exact regularized certificate of this packing
(`E-regularized-bounds-2026-10-12-exact-decision`, T-155), and the record prints it as
$12.65685424949239$. As printed it is **larger** than the best known $12.65685424949238$
two fields above it, by `1e-14`.

It is not the value of $s(148)$ and not a different packing: it is the certificate’s own
exact side. The catalogue gives the packing’s side exactly, as `7 + 4 sqrt(2)`, and the
certificate’s side lies `1.2e-30` above that, so it bounds the exact side from above and
does not reach it. That side is irrational, so no rational certificate reaches it.

## The lower bound

The verified field rests on the complete replay here of Hosono’s linear certificate for
[145 squares](n-145.md) ([`E-wand125-linear-n145-1224-source-replay`](evidence.yaml),
`V3/C3`): all 201 directions, from 2 November 2026 to 4 November 2026, each returning
the certificate’s own record.
Its mass, $14499999/100000$, is below 148, so a packing of 148 at a side below $306/25$
would capture at least 148 from it.

Until 4 November 2026 the verified lower bound was Karakuş’s general bound, which
applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.

The reported lower-bound expression for $s(148)$ is
`22*sqrt(6)/145 + 2*sqrt(2) + 1307/145` (approximately $12.21386694779$). Friedman’s DS7
survey, Theorem 9, k=12, reports this bound at n=145; reference [8] is Green’s private
communication (2000). The source proof has not been recovered.
The unavoidable-set argument DS7’s Figure 34 illustrates does not prove it: at k=12 that
point pattern leaves a unit square empty (devtools.check_green_ds7). Inherited at n=148
by monotonicity. This is a source-reported lower bound; it was the reported lower bound
here until 4 November 2026, when T-179’s replayed $306/25$, above it, took both lower
lanes. The exact specialization and comparison are retained by
[`audit_ds7_lower_bounds`](../devtools/audit_ds7_lower_bounds.py).
