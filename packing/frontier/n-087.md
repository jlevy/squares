# `s(87)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(87) ≤ 9.83881744`, and the best proved lower bound
is `9.3666` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a gap of `0.4722`. General closed
form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N - 2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is `10`, which is **larger** than the best known
`9.83881743996618` two fields above it.
It is not a tighter reading of the same packing and it is not the value of `s(87)`: it
is the strongest ceiling this repository can certify from its own evidence — the trivial
grid bound `⌈√87⌉ = 10` — and it trails the reported construction by `0.16118256003382`.

Its `exact_form` is the exact form of that ceiling, never of `s(87)`. `s(87)` is not
known exactly; `status` stays `open`, and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter
names the gap. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The packing

Found by David Ellsworth in 2024, via simulated annealing.
Later improved by David W. Cantrell and David Ellsworth.
Its side length is algebraic of degree **44** over `ℚ`.

## The lower bound

In the current verified register, this case uses Nagamochi’s general lower bound.
The bound is Nagamochi’s general closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

In the current verified register, Nagamochi supplies the lower bound for 58 of the 65
open cases at `n ≤ 100`.
