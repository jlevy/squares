# `s(67)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(67) ≤ 8.70710678`, and the best proved lower bound
is `8.211103` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a gap of `0.496`. General closed
form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N - 2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The packing

Found by Frits Göbel in 1979, via a diagonal-strip construction — the strip
family at `a = 7`, whose side is `8 + sqrt(2)/2` exactly.
`cases/gobel_strip` builds it and verifies it exactly over `Q(sqrt 2)`, which is
what moved `verified_upper_bound` from the grid ceiling onto the exact side.
The retained witness declares that side rounded down at its own digits — about
`4.85e-30` below the exact value — and a different slack choice for the strip, so
the certificate carries the construction’s coordinates rather than the witness’s
(`D-398`).

## The lower bound

Nothing specific to this `n` has ever been proved.
The bound is Nagamochi’s general closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

This is the default across almost the whole open frontier — 63 of the 65 open cases at
`n ≤ 100` are governed by it, and it has not been improved since 2005.
