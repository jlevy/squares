## The packing

Found by Frits Göbel in 1979, via a diagonal-strip construction — the strip family at
$a = 7$, whose side is `8 + sqrt(2)/2` exactly.
`cases/gobel_strip` builds it and verifies it exactly over `Q(sqrt 2)`, which is what
moved `verified_upper_bound` from the grid ceiling onto the exact side.
The retained witness declares that side rounded down at its own digits — about
`4.85e-30` below the exact value — and a different slack choice for the strip, so the
certificate carries the construction’s coordinates rather than the witness’s (`D-398`).

## The lower bound

The earlier external report, [Friedman DS7], gives the lower-bound expression
`2*sqrt(2) + 71/13` for $s(67)$ (approximately $8.289965586285$). Friedman’s DS7 survey,
Theorem 9, k=8, reports this bound at n=65; reference [8] is Green’s private
communication (2000). The source proof has not been recovered.
The unavoidable-set argument DS7’s Figure 34 illustrates does not prove it: at $k = 8$
that point pattern leaves a unit square empty
([review](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-green-ds7-theorem9.md)).
Inherited at n=67 by monotonicity.
wand125’s rectangle-density certificate above has since replaced it in the reported
field, and its replay here on 2026-10-02 made it the verified lower bound until 5
October. The [source audit](../devtools/audit_ds7_lower_bounds.py) compares the exact
theorem expressions separately from opaque table decimals.

The verified field rests on a complete decision here
([`E-n067-wand125-mixed-848-sqverify-fast-replay`](evidence.yaml), `V3/C3`) of wand125’s
mixed certificate of 5 October by sqverify-fast, this repository’s clean-room measure
verifier: all 201 net directions of the retained candidate on 5 October 2026, with two
mutants scaled below coverage one refused.
It shares no code with the source’s checker, so it decides coverage a second way with
the same method; the source’s own checker was not run here.
From 2 October 2026 until this decision the verified lower bound was $1691/200$,
wand125’s rectangle-density certificate
([`E-wand125-rectangle-2026-09-28-source-replay`](evidence.yaml)).

Nagamochi’s general closed form, the verified lower bound before the rectangle
certificate, is now a reported bound (see the correction below).
Karakuş’s general bound, independently verified here and below this certificate at this
$n$, applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

See the [Frontier corpus summary](README.md#what-the-corpus-shows) for the current
aggregate count; source-reported bounds are recorded separately.
