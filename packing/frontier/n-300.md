# `s(300)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(300) ≤ 17.82412339`, and the best proved lower
bound is `17.340134` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a gap of `0.484`. General
closed form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N - 2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is `18`, which is **larger** than the best known
`17.82412338847854` two fields above it.
It is not a tighter reading of the same packing and it is not the value of `s(300)`: it
is the strongest ceiling this repository can certify from its own evidence — the trivial
grid bound `⌈√300⌉ = 18` — and it trails the reported construction by
`0.17587661152146`.

Its `exact_form` is the exact form of that ceiling, never of `s(300)`. `s(300)` is not
known exactly; `status` stays `open`, and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter
names the gap. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The packing

Found by David Ellsworth in 2025, via an unrecorded method.
Its side length is algebraic of degree **40** over `ℚ`.

## The lower bound

Nothing specific to this `n` has ever been proved.
The bound is Nagamochi’s general closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

This is the default across almost the whole open frontier, and outside this repository’s
own displacements it has not been improved since 2005.
