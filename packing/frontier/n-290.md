# `s(290)` — open

**Exact regularized certificate, 2026-10-13.** This project’s grid-slack regularizer,
its port of Evan Daniel’s coincidence placement, run here on this register’s witness,
produced an exact rational packing of this arrangement, decided here over $\mathbb{Q}$
by two exact checkers: it proves $s(290) \le 17.53553390593273762200422181052425$, the
verified upper bound (T-156), which the record still prints as $17.53553390593274$. The
bound was not new: the certificate registered at $n = 291$ (T-155), at the same side,
already proved it by monotonicity; this one certifies this count’s own packing.
Until then the verified upper bound was exactly $17.53553390593274$, the side of Evan
Daniel’s exact certificate of this packing rounded up at the printed precision (T-101).

**Exact certificate, 2026-10-06.** Evan Daniel’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md) published
on 5 October 2026 an exact rational certificate of this packing, decided here over

the verified upper bound from 6 to 13 October 2026 (T-101). Until then the verified
upper bound was the trivial grid bound $18$.

Open. The best known packing gives $s(290) \le 17.53553391$, and the strongest lower
bound independently verified here is $17.030275$ from Karakuş’s general theorem, leaving
a gap of $0.5053$. General bound: s(N) >= 1/2 + sqrt(N - floor(sqrt(N)) + 1/4).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is $17.53553390593273762200422181052425$, exactly,
proved by the exact regularized certificate of this packing
(`E-regularized-bounds-2026-10-13-grid-slack-exact-decision`, T-156), and the record
prints it as $17.53553390593274$. As printed it is **larger** than the best known
$17.53553390593273$ two fields above it, by `1e-14`.

It is not the value of $s(290)$ and not a different packing: it is the certificate’s own
exact side. The catalogue gives the packing’s side exactly, as `14 + (5/2)sqrt(2)`, and
the certificate’s side lies `4.8e-33` above that, so it bounds the exact side from above
and does not reach it.
That side is irrational, so no rational certificate reaches it.
The `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter records the difference.
Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The regularized exact certificate

This project’s grid-slack regularizer (`devtools.regularize_grid_slack`), its port of
Evan Daniel’s coincidence placement
([`evand/square-packing`](https://github.com/evand/square-packing), MIT), ran on this
register’s witness for this count on 13 October 2026. Starting from the witness’s exact
frame, it kept every rotation the witness’s own, since no tilted square lies within 5
degrees of the axes, closed what free slack it could while keeping every contact the
witness has, and then fixed the result’s tight contacts over $\mathbb{Q}$ at the
witness’s own side. The result is an exact rational packing of the same 290 squares in a
square of side $17.53553390593273762200422181052425$, `2.4e-15` below the verified upper
bound the record held before, and `1.8e-19` below the side of Evan Daniel’s certificate
of this packing (T-101): it is the witness’s own side.
Measured against the witness under the box’s eight symmetries, its squares pair one to
one with the witness’s; none moves by more than `1e-3`, and none turns by more than
`1e-6` as a sine.
It draws the same picture as the witness, so the atlas drawing does not
change.

Each pair and each wall is decided over $\mathbb{Q}$ twice, by `sqpack`’s exact
separating-axis test and by an independent checker that shares no code with it, and both
accept it; both refuse it with its tightest pair pushed into overlap and with its box
shrunk past its least clearance
([certificate](../cases/regularized_bounds/README.md#the-grid-slack-batch)). The same
two checkers accepted it in the run that made it, so this is a reproduction with the
producer’s code. That proves $s(290) \le 17.53553390593273762200422181052425$, the
verified upper bound (T-156), the exact form of the case’s `verified_upper_bound`, which
prints it as $17.53553390593274$, the decimal it printed before and an upper display of
it. The bound was not new when it was registered.
The exact certificate registered at $n = 291$ (T-155) is at the same side, and
$s(290) \le s(291)$, since deleting a square from a packing of 291 leaves a packing of
290 in the same square, so that certificate already proved the bound.
This one is a certificate of this count’s own packing, the one the record credits.
It says nothing about optimality, and the packing stays its finders’.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.

The reported lower-bound expression for $s(290)$ is
`8*sqrt(34)/145 + 2*sqrt(2) + 2031/145` (approximately $17.15703136722$). Friedman’s DS7
survey, Theorem 9, k=17, reports this bound at n=290; reference [8] is Green’s private
communication (2000). The source proof has not been recovered.
The unavoidable-set argument DS7’s Figure 34 illustrates does not prove it: at k=17 that
point pattern leaves a unit square empty (devtools.check_green_ds7). This is a
source-reported lower bound; it does not replace the verified bound above.
The exact specialization and comparison are retained by
[`audit_ds7_lower_bounds`](../devtools/audit_ds7_lower_bounds.py).
