# `s(272)` — open

**Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form, 14 October 2026.** Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form
(wand125, 13 October 2026, T-180) states $s(N) \ge a(N)$ for every non-square
$N \ge 49$, from a measure of line segments on 34 intervals of the fractional part.
At $n = 272$, $k = 16$ and interval 27 give
$a(272) = 16.565085529013318748350114228388\ldots$, so the reported lower bound here is
now $s(272) \ge 16.565085529013$, rounded down, above Nagamochi’s closed form,
$16.52417469626$. The source replayed its certificate with its own checker; no record of
it has been replayed here, so the verified lower bound is unchanged.
The source states that its search, certificates, checkers and text were produced with AI
assistance (Claude and Codex), under the direction of and reviewed by Hiroaki Hosono.

**House adoption, 15 October 2026.** PingYou Ltd’s exact re-solve of Nate
Chaoweeraprasit’s SQUISH packing (T-171) is now this case’s house: its exact certificate
proves $s(272) \le 16.9655864748376707$, the reported and the verified upper bound,
`5.26e-16` below the ceiling the case held until then, $16.9655864748376712$ from Nate
Chaoweeraprasit’s packing (T-151), which stays retained as history
([the certificate](#pingyou-ltds-certificate-t-171)).

**House adoption, 12 October 2026.** Nate Chaoweeraprasit’s SQUISH packing, composed
from SQUISH’s own n = 240 packing and the Kingbird catalogue’s former n = 182 packing
(T-151) was this case’s house until 15 October 2026: its exact certificate proves
$s(272) \le 16.9655864748376712$, the reported and the verified upper bound, `2.52e-03`
below the ceiling the case held until then, $16.968110145769600505005359235592$ from
Evan Daniel’s packing (T-119), which stays retained as history
([the certificate](#nate-chaoweeraprasits-certificate-t-151)).

**New exact construction, 7 October 2026.** The complete #399 certificate proves
$s(272) \le 16.9681101457696006$ (T-119). The display is the least upward sixteen-place
ceiling of the full rational side.
It replaces the atlas pose; earlier source reports and certifications below describe
their original, different geometries.

Open. Previously, the best known published packing, Francisco Couzo’s at Evan Daniel’s
exact optimum (T-098), gives $s(272) \le 16.9681658678521583329\ldots$, and the
strongest lower bound independently verified here is $16.507810$ from Karakuş’s general
theorem, leaving a gap of $0.4604$. General bound: s(N) >= 1/2 + sqrt(N - floor(sqrt(N))
\+ 1/4).

## The earlier exact optimum

Evan Daniel’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md) published
on 5 October 2026 an exact rational certificate of this packing at its exact optimum
(T-098): the same 272 squares in a square of side $16.9681658678521583329\ldots$,
`1.2e-11` below the side Francisco Couzo prints.
Square for square, its pose lies within `1.8e-4` of the binary64 pose the atlas pictures
for this count. 6 squares move by more than `1e-8`, all of them squares the source lists
as carrying no force; every other square moves by at most `1.8e-11`. The earlier
known-best witness was that binary64 pose, posed at its finder’s larger side; the side
above is witnessed by the certificate itself.
Evan Daniel’s solver moves the binary64 pose to a nearby exact KKT point of the problem
of minimizing the side under non-overlap, computed at 80 digits, and rounds it outward
to rationals, each square a rational centre and a rational $t = \tan(\theta/2)$.

This repository decides the certificate exactly.
Converted without rounding, every pair and every wall is decided over $\mathbb{Q}$
twice, by `sqpack`’s exact separating-axis test and by an independent checker that
shares no code with it, and the source’s own two checkers, run here as retained, accept
it as well
([receipts](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md#replayed-here)).
Previously, that proves $s(272) \le 16.9681658678521583329\ldots$, the verified upper
bound; it says nothing about optimality.
The source reports the exact point as a certified bound only: its numerical checks do
not show it to be a local minimum of the side.
On jlevy/squares#375 its author wrote that the solver, its checkers and the batch “were
written with Claude (Anthropic) as a coding and research agent, directed and reviewed by

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
