## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.

The reported lower-bound expression for $s(293)$ is
`8*sqrt(34)/145 + 2*sqrt(2) + 2031/145` (approximately $17.15703136722$). Friedman’s DS7
survey, Theorem 9, k=17, reports this bound at n=290; reference [8] is Green’s private
communication (2000). The source proof has not been recovered.
The unavoidable-set argument DS7’s Figure 34 illustrates does not prove it: at k=17 that
point pattern leaves a unit square empty (devtools.check_green_ds7). Inherited at n=293
by monotonicity. This is a source-reported lower bound; it does not replace the verified
bound above. The exact specialization and comparison are retained by
[`audit_ds7_lower_bounds`](../devtools/audit_ds7_lower_bounds.py).

## Evan Daniel’s certificate (T-257)

Evan Daniel’s n = 260 packing, from a search started from Kirill Tymchishin’s Kaggle
notebook packing, with one L-shaped strip added, reported on
[jlevy/squares#845](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/845), at exact side
$8816991251061390991251052573823/500000000000000000000000000000$, displayed upward as
$17.6339825021227820$ (T-257). The certificate gives a rational side and, for each of
the 293 unit squares, a rational centre and the tangent of its half-angle; the
[retained packet](../resources/web/evand-issue845-2026-10-29/README.md) holds it with
the complete native jobs that decided it.

Both maintained exact routes decided it here, `sqpack`’s exact separating-axis verifier
and the independent rational corner checker, with its duplicate-square and
outside-container controls, and the 31 October review’s clean-room checker decided it
again in rationals, by separating axes over every pair and by the exact clipped area of
every pair within reach; its controls at $10^{-30}$ refused the box below the packing’s
extent, the packing moved past the near walls and the closest pair moved past contact.
Both routes read the certificate through an adapter whose line filter and corner
construction are transcribed from Evan Daniel’s own checkers, the ones the certificate
names, so the replay shares the producer’s components.
The review’s checker shares no code with either maintained route or the source, and the
independent re-implementation rests on it.
The
[review of 31 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-31-daniel-845-172-204-260-293.md)
accepted the replay for finite feasibility.
That proves $s(293) \le 17.6339825021227820$, the verified upper bound: confirmed,
independently re-implemented, by a Claude agent, a system family the producer also used.

The source’s commit names Claude as co-author, and its credits say the repository’s work
was produced by Claude (Anthropic) under human direction.
