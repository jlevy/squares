# `s(55)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(55) ≤ 7.94577101`, and the best proved lower bound
is `7.480741` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a gap of `0.465`. General closed
form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N - 2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is `8`, which is **larger** than the best known
`7.94577100750391` two fields above it.
It is not a tighter reading of the same packing and it is not the value of `s(55)`: it
is the strongest ceiling this repository can certify from its own evidence — the trivial
grid bound `⌈√55⌉ = 8` — and it trails the reported construction by `0.05422899249609`.

Its `exact_form` is the exact form of that ceiling, never of `s(55)`. `s(55)` is not
known exactly; `status` stays `open`, and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter
names the gap. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The packing

Found by Thomas Schadt in 2026, via simulated annealing.
Later improved by David Ellsworth.

## The lower bound

Nothing specific to this `n` has ever been proved.
The bound is Nagamochi’s general closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

This is the default across almost the whole open frontier — 63 of the 65 open cases at
`n ≤ 100` are governed by it, and it has not been improved since 2005.
