## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is $6$, which is **larger** than the best known
$5.82444461667405$ two fields above it.
It is not a tighter reading of the same packing and it is not the value of $s(28)$: it
is the strongest ceiling this repository can certify from its own evidence — the trivial
grid bound $\lceil\sqrt{28}\rceil = 6$ — and it trails the reported construction by
$0.17555538332595$.

Its `exact_form` is the exact form of that ceiling, never of $s(28)$. $s(28)$ is not
known exactly; `status` stays `open`, and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter
names the gap. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The packing

Found by David Ellsworth in 2025, via simulated annealing.
The catalogue flags it **rigid**: no continuous deformation is possible, so the contact
conditions pin the side length exactly as an algebraic number.
Its side length is algebraic of degree **6** over $\mathbb{Q}$. Its Galois group over
$\mathbb{Q}$ is `S6`, computed here from the recorded minimal polynomial with
`sympy.galois_group`, and `S6` is not solvable.
**The side length is therefore not expressible in radicals**, so the empty `exact_form`
is a permanent property of this case rather than a transcription gap.

## The lower bound

The earlier external report, [Friedman DS7], gives the lower-bound expression
`6*sqrt(5)/5 + 2*sqrt(2)` for $s(28)$ (approximately $5.511708697746$). Friedman’s DS7
survey, Theorem 10, k=5, reports this bound at n=28; reference [8] is Green’s private
communication (2000). The source proof has not been recovered.
The [source audit](../devtools/audit_ds7_lower_bounds.py) compares the exact theorem
expressions separately from opaque table decimals.

The verified lower bound is instead wand125’s $28/5$, inherited from the fully replayed
n27 rectangle-density certificate, whose mass $26999/1000$ is below 28, by monotonicity.
It exceeds Green’s literal report by about $0.0883$. wand125’s direct n28 certificate at
$143/25$, which replaced the $1139/200$ certificate on 2026-09-28, is the reported lower
bound; its coverage replay has not yet run here.
A method-distinct rectangle coverage implementation would add confidence and improve
generalization, but it is not a missing premise of the verified fixed bound.

**Corrected 2 October 2026:** Nagamochi’s Lemma 1 is false (Karakuş 2026), so his closed
form below is now a reported bound whose published proof is incomplete

