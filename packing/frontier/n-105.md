# `s(105)` — open

**Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form, 14 October 2026.** Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form
(wand125, 13 October 2026, T-180) states $s(N) \ge a(N)$ for every non-square
$N \ge 49$, from a measure of line segments on 34 intervals of the fractional part.
At $n = 105$, $k = 10$ and interval 6 give
$a(105) = 10.354760059211635897156128812338\ldots$, so the reported lower bound here is
now $s(105) \ge 10.354760059211$, rounded down, above a smaller count’s bound by
monotonicity, $10.28$. The source replayed its certificate with its own checker; no
record of it has been replayed here, so the verified lower bound is unchanged.
The source states that its search, certificates, checkers and text were produced with AI
assistance (Claude and Codex), under the direction of and reviewed by Hiroaki Hosono.

**House adoption, 17 October 2026.** PingYou Ltd’s tighter certificate of Francisco
Couzo’s refinement of Ryan Xu’s packing (T-174) is now this case’s house: its exact
certificate proves $s(105) \le 10.789303783748158831$, the reported and the verified
upper bound, rounded outward from its exact side.
The exact certificate side improves the previous verified exact side by about
`1.08e-79`. The earlier ceiling, displayed outward as $10.78930378374815884$ from Ryan
Xu’s packing, improved by Francisco Couzo and PingYou Ltd (T-170), applied until this
adoption and stays retained as history
([the certificate](#pingyou-ltds-certificate-t-174)).

**House adoption, 15 October 2026.** PingYou Ltd’s tighter certificate of Francisco

2026: its exact certificate proves $s(105) \le 10.78930378374815884$, the reported and
the verified upper bound, `1.08e-19` below the ceiling the case held until then,
$10.7893037837481589$ from Ryan Xu’s packing, improved by Francisco Couzo (T-130), which
stays retained as history ([the certificate](#pingyou-ltds-certificate-t-170)).

**House adoption, 11 October 2026.** Francisco Couzo’s refinement of Ryan Xu’s packing

**External intake, 2026-10-02.** Hiroaki Hosono’s
[linear-certificate source](../resources/web/wand125-linear-certificates-2026-10-02/README.md)
reports $s(101) \ge 257/25 = 10.28$ (T-073), from a measure of points, segments and
rectangles accepted there at every net angle by its linear verifier, a checker the 2
October review read with no blocking defect.
A packing of 105 squares contains one of 101, so the bound holds here by monotonicity,
above Nagamochi’s $10.2736184\ldots$, which this case held until Karakuş’s finding made
it a reported bound (see the correction below).
Its complete replay here on 2 October 2026 returned the certificate’s own record at all
201 directions, so it is also the verified lower bound here.

Before the 7 October refinement, the best known published packing, Francisco Couzo’s
(T-056), reported the displayed side $10.806077865540567$, and the verified lower bound
is $s(105) \ge 257/25 = 10.28$, from Hosono’s n101 linear certificate by monotonicity,
leaving a gap of $0.5261$.

## The lower bound

The verified field rests on the complete replay here of Hosono’s linear certificate for
[101 squares](n-101.md) ([`E-n101-wand125-linear-1028-source-replay`](evidence.yaml),
`V3/C3`): all 201 directions on 2 October 2026, each returning the certificate’s own
record. Its mass, $10099999/100000$, is below 105, so a packing of 105 at a side below
$257/25$ would capture at least 105 from it.

Until 2 October 2026 the verified lower bound was Nagamochi’s general closed form, now a
reported bound (see the correction below), and from then until this replay was recorded
on 3 October it was Karakuş’s general bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
