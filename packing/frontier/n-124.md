# `s(124)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(124) ≤ 11.65685425`, and the strongest lower bound
independently verified here is `11.148891` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a
gap of `0.508`. General closed form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N -
2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is `12`, which is **larger** than the best known
`11.65685424949238` two fields above it.
It is not a tighter reading of the same packing and it is not the value of `s(124)`: it
is the strongest ceiling this repository can certify from its own evidence — the trivial
grid bound `⌈√124⌉ = 12` — and it trails the reported construction by
`0.34314575050762`.

Its `exact_form` is the exact form of that ceiling, never of `s(124)`. `s(124)` is not
known exactly; `status` stays `open`, and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter
names the gap. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The packing

Found by an unrecorded author, via a hand construction.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Nagamochi’s general
closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.

The reported lower-bound expression for `s(124)` is `5*sqrt(22)/61 + 2*sqrt(2) + 489/61`
(approximately `11.22928087555`). Friedman’s DS7 survey, Theorem 9, k=11, reports this
bound at n=122; reference [8] is Green’s private communication (2000). The source proof
has not been recovered.
Inherited at n=124 by monotonicity.
This is a source-reported lower bound; it does not replace the verified bound above.
The exact specialization and comparison are retained by
[`audit_ds7_lower_bounds`](../devtools/audit_ds7_lower_bounds.py).
