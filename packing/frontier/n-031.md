## The packing

The record catalogue does not picture $n = 31$: no arrangement has ever been found that
beats the trivial $\lceil\sqrt{31}\rceil = 6$ grid, so the grid is still the best known
packing. That is a statement about what has been searched, not a proof.

## The lower bound

wand125’s n31 rectangle-density certificate, replayed here over all 201 directions,
verifies $148/25$ directly: 155 rectangle orbits, total mass $3099/100$, below 31 by
$1/100$. Before it, the complete n29 interval replay verified $571/100$, which deleting
two squares carried to n31. A method-distinct rectangle coverage implementation would
add confidence and improve generalization, but it is not a missing premise of either
bound.

**Corrected 2 October 2026:** Nagamochi’s Lemma 1 is false (Karakuş 2026), so his closed
form below is now a reported bound whose published proof is incomplete
([review](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).
Nagamochi’s earlier general closed form remains part of the evidence history and applies
to every $N \ge 4$:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```
