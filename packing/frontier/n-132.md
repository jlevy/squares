# `s(132)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(132) ≤ 11.99143644`, and the strongest lower bound
independently verified here is `11.535653` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a
gap of `0.4558`. General closed form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N -
2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is `12`, which is **larger** than the best known
`11.99143643966336` two fields above it.
It is not a tighter reading of the same packing and it is not the value of `s(132)`: it
is the strongest ceiling this repository can certify from its own evidence — the trivial
grid bound `⌈√132⌉ = 12` — and it trails the reported construction by
`0.00856356033664`.

Its `exact_form` is the exact form of that ceiling, never of `s(132)`. `s(132)` is not
known exactly; `status` stays `open`, and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter
names the gap. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The packing

Found by M.Z. Arslanov, S.A. Mustafin and Z.K. Shangitbayev in 2019. Improved by David
W. Cantrell in March 2025. Improved by David Ellsworth in January 2026, via simulated
annealing.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Nagamochi’s general
closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
