# `s(164)` — open

**External intake, 2026-11-14.** Mohsen Khalili and Sara Gharabaghi report $s(164) = 13$
as an instance of $s(k^2 - 5) = k$ at $k = 13$
([jlevy/squares#973](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/973), T-298). For every
$k \ge 10$ the source reduces the lower half to one finite statement on an
$11 \times 11$ board, decided by Evan Daniel’s `zmx2` and `qx2_zm.py` and the authors’
Tight-Tile checker, with a Lean reduction that takes it as a hypothesis.
Its fast checks, a sample of the `zmx2` sweep, the root `zmx2` leaves to `qx2_zm.py` and
its controls ran here; the full replay and the Lean build have not, so the verified
lower bound is unchanged.
It replaces Hosono’s closed form as the reported lower bound.
The paper says the search, the certificate, the Tight-Tile checker and its proof, and
the verification runs were produced with Claude (Anthropic) under the authors’
direction.

**Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form, 14 October 2026.** Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form
(wand125, 13 October 2026, T-180) states $s(N) \ge a(N)$ for every non-square
$N \ge 49$, from a measure of line segments on 34 intervals of the fractional part.
At $n = 164$, $k = 12$ and interval 33 give
$a(164) = 12.927540649712385206912009857906\ldots$, so the reported lower bound here was
that value rounded down at 12 places, above Nagamochi’s closed form, $12.87434208703$.
The source replayed its certificate with its own checker; no record of it has been
replayed here; it remains a historical reported bound.
The source states that its search, certificates, checkers and text were produced with AI
assistance (Claude and Codex), under the direction of and reviewed by Hiroaki Hosono.

Open. The best known packing gives $s(164) \le 13$, and the strongest lower bound
independently verified here is $12.838962$ from Karakuş’s general theorem, leaving a gap
of $0.161$. General bound: s(N) >= 1/2 + sqrt(N - floor(sqrt(N)) + 1/4).

## The packing

The record catalogue does not picture $n = 164$: no arrangement has ever been found that
beats the trivial $\lceil\sqrt{164}\rceil = 13$ grid, so the grid is still the best
known packing. That is a statement about what has been searched, not a proof.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
