# s(108) — Square Packing Case

**Fine-net lower bound, 18 October 2026.** The certificate `mixed_n108_L1062`, published
by Hosono on 16 October, raises both lower lanes to $s(108) \ge 531/50 = 10.62$. Its
exact nonnegative rectangle measure has total mass $10799999/100000 < 108$. The retained
maintained `sqverify-fast` census verifies capture at every centre in all 201
declared-net directions, and its whole-net control receipt refuses the scaled mutants.
The two accepted mathematical reviews check the certificate’s implication and premises;
the separate exact audits check saved points.
No native verifier was rerun during those reviews or this adoption.
The producer’s checker and the maintained replay share `sqverify_fast` components, so
this is shared component confirmation.
It establishes neither independent full confirmation nor optimality.
The upper lanes remain unchanged.

**Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form, 14 October 2026.** Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form
(wand125, 13 October 2026, T-180) states $s(N) \ge a(N)$ for every non-square
$N \ge 49$, from a measure of line segments on 34 intervals of the fractional part.
At $n = 108$, $k = 10$ and interval 23 give
$a(108) = 10.484093855851092636476980027521\ldots$, so the reported lower bound here was
$s(108) \ge 10.484093855851$, rounded down, above Nagamochi’s closed form,
$10.43398113205$. The source replayed its certificate with its own checker; no record of
it has been replayed here; it remains a historical reported bound.
The source states that its search, certificates, checkers and text were produced with AI
assistance (Claude and Codex), under the direction of and reviewed by Hiroaki Hosono.

**House adoption, 17 October 2026.** PingYou Ltd’s refinement of Francisco Couzo’s
refinement of Ryan Xu’s packing (T-174) is now this case’s house: its exact certificate
proves $s(108) \le 10.90482101232035643$, the reported and the verified upper bound,
rounded outward from its exact side.
The exact certificate side improves the previous verified exact side by about
`1.09e-19`. The earlier ceiling, displayed outward as $10.9048210123203565$ from Ryan
Xu’s packing, improved by Francisco Couzo (T-128), applied until this adoption and stays
retained as history ([the certificate](#pingyou-ltds-certificate-t-174)).

**House adoption, 11 October 2026.** Francisco Couzo’s refinement of Ryan Xu’s packing
(T-128) was this case’s house until 17 October 2026: its exact certificate proves
$s(108) \le 10.9048210123203565$, the reported and the verified upper bound, `3.77e-06`
below the ceiling the case held until then, $10.9048247851109049$ from Ryan Xu’s packing
(T-125), which stays retained as history
([the certificate](#francisco-couzos-certificate-t-128)).

## The lower bound

The current verified lower bound is $s(108) \ge 531/50 = 10.62$, from Hiroaki Hosono’s
fine-net measure and the maintained replay described above.
That confirmation shares the producer’s verifier components.

Karakuş’s general bound supplied the earlier verified lower bound.
It applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
stood above that earlier Karakuş bound and was recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
