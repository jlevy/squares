# s(153) — Square Packing Case

**Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form, 14 October 2026.** Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form
(wand125, 13 October 2026, T-180) states $s(N) \ge a(N)$ for every non-square
$N \ge 49$, from a measure of line segments on 34 intervals of the fractional part.
At $n = 153$, $k = 12$ and interval 16 give
$a(153) = 12.446832330892889010549072664797\ldots$, so the reported lower bound here is
now $s(153) \ge 12.446832330892$, rounded down, above Nagamochi’s closed form,
$12.40175425099$. The source replayed its certificate with its own checker; no record of
it has been replayed here, so the verified lower bound is unchanged.
The source states that its search, certificates, checkers and text were produced with AI
assistance (Claude and Codex), under the direction of and reviewed by Hiroaki Hosono.

**House adoption, 15 October 2026.** Mykhailo Polkovskyi’s arrangement of PingYou Ltd’s
n = 153 design, after Ryan Xu’s n = 70 packing and Evan Daniel’s exact certificate of it
(T-173) is now this case’s house: its exact certificate proves
$s(153) \le 12.8700138504044800$, the reported and the verified upper bound, `2.56e-12`
below the ceiling the case held until then, $12.8700138504070441$ from PingYou Ltd’s
packing (T-148), which stays retained as history
([the certificate](#mykhailo-polkovskyis-certificate-t-173)).

**House adoption, 11 October 2026.** PingYou Ltd’s construction, after Ryan Xu’s n = 70
packing and Evan Daniel’s exact certificate of it (T-148) was this case’s house until 15
October 2026: its exact certificate proves $s(153) \le 12.8700138504070441$, the
reported and the verified upper bound, `9.67e-03` below the ceiling the case held until
then, $12.8796793733293146$ from Nate Chaoweeraprasit’s packing, improved by Siddharth
Gupta (T-127), which stays retained as history
([the certificate](#pingyou-ltds-certificate-t-148)).

The certificate gives rational centres and half-angle parameters for 153 unit squares.

The new packing improves the earlier reported side $12.88166675700900$ from [Kingbird].
This repository confirmed the upper bound by independently re-implemented exact-rational

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
