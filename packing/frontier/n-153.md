# s(153) — Square Packing Case

## The Upper Bound

Previously, Nate Chaoweeraprasit (itsnaka), using SQUISH, reports $s(153) \le S_n$, with
$S_n = \frac{7250614903299225}{562949953421312}$ in the
[n = 153 supplement](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/401#issuecomment-6031977107).
That historical comment is no longer available.
The
[pinned update certificate](https://github.com/itsnaka/squish-certs/blob/5e32bbd7028b6e3b869979278079cd37ed6770aa/squish-submission-2026-10-07/n153/n153.cert.json)
contains the identical side and rational triples, as the
[retained comparison](../resources/web/squish-401-update-2026-10-07/acquisition/n153-comparison.json)
records. This supplies a live source for the existing T-114 certificate; it adds no
packing or assurance claim.
The certificate gives rational centres and half-angle parameters for 153 unit squares.
The source’s original finite decimal display is $12.8796793733329640$; the rational
fraction states the claimed bound exactly.

The new packing improves the earlier reported side $12.88166675700900$ from [Kingbird].
This repository confirmed the upper bound by independently re-implemented exact-rational
checks: native `exact_verify` and `devtools.check_rational_witness_independent` accept
every pair and wall of the new packing and reject both geometric controls.
The verified display is $12.8796793733329640$, the exact source side rounded upward at
sixteen decimals. Both accepted reviews cover the shared half-angle conversion and the
complete roster. The submitted certificates establish feasible upper bounds and make no
optimality or rigidity claim.
Separately, this repository’s
[numerical translation screen](../atlas/known-best/translation-escape-screen.json) finds
a nontrivial feasible motion in the retained configuration.
That numerical assessment concerns this configuration alone; it does not settle the
optimum or all packings at this side.

The author credits David Ellsworth’s records and tools and states that SQUISH’s solver,
verification and issue were written with Claude Opus 5.5 under his direction,
management, review and steering.
The [source packet](../resources/web/squish-401-2026-10-07/README.md) retains derived
packing facts and source metadata.

## Earlier Packing

Its reported side was $12.88166675700900$. The earlier certified ceiling

Any optimality or rigidity observations about that packing concern its own pose.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.

## The Gupta precision refinement

Siddharth Gupta refines the earlier SQUISH construction at exact rational side
$2575935874665862918815262035657/200000000000000000000000000000$, displayed upward as
$12.8796793733293146$ (T-127). The
[complete factual packet](../resources/web/gupta-square-packing-refinements-2026-10-08/README.md)
retains every original certificate, complete comparator and actual deciding outcome for
all seventeen source cases, including three withdrawals.

Complete dual-route native replay, full-roster controls and separately prompted source
and production review confirm finite feasibility at V3/C3 using independently
re-implemented deciding code.
The repository routes share parsing, half-angle conversion, Fraction arithmetic and SAT
methodology; the recorded code relation is to the source producer’s verification, not to
each other. The full source poses are converted without dilation or rounding.
The source credits Nate Chaoweeraprasit for SQUISH and Evan Daniel for the optimizer.
No author program is executed here.
Feasibility establishes no optimizer, local-minimum, rigidity, novelty, priority,
global-optimality, formal proof or human-oversight assurance.
