# `s(153)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives $s(153) \le 12.88166676$, and the strongest lower
bound independently verified here is $12.384864$ from Karakuş’s general theorem, leaving
a gap of $0.4968$. General bound: s(N) >= 1/2 + sqrt(N - floor(sqrt(N)) + 1/4).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is $13$, which is **larger** than the best known
$12.88166675700900$ two fields above it.
It is not a tighter reading of the same packing and it is not the value of $s(153)$: it
is the strongest ceiling this repository can certify from its own evidence — the trivial
grid bound $\lceil\sqrt{153}\rceil = 13$ — and it trails the reported construction by
$0.11833324299100$.

Its `exact_form` is the exact form of that ceiling, never of $s(153)$. $s(153)$ is not
known exactly; `status` stays `open`, and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter
names the gap. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The packing

Found by David Ellsworth in 2024, via an unrecorded method.
Its side length is algebraic of degree **4** over $\mathbb{Q}$.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
