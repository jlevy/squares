# `s(292)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(292) ≤ 17.60257142`, and the strongest lower bound
independently verified here is `17.093476` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a
gap of `0.5091`. General closed form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N -
2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is `18`, which is **larger** than the best known
`17.60257141234448` two fields above it.
It is not a tighter reading of the same packing and it is not the value of `s(292)`: it
is the strongest ceiling this repository can certify from its own evidence — the trivial
grid bound `⌈√292⌉ = 18` — and it trails the reported construction by
`0.39742858765552`.

Its `exact_form` is the exact form of that ceiling, never of `s(292)`. `s(292)` is not
known exactly; `status` stays `open`, and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter
names the gap. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The packing

Found by an unrecorded author, via extension of a smaller record.
Its side length is algebraic of degree **8** over `ℚ`.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Nagamochi’s general
closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.

The reported lower-bound expression for `s(292)` is
`8*sqrt(34)/145 + 2*sqrt(2) + 2031/145` (approximately `17.15703136722`). Friedman’s DS7
survey, Theorem 9, k=17, reports this bound at n=290; reference [8] is Green’s private
communication (2000). The source proof has not been recovered.
Inherited at n=292 by monotonicity.
This is a source-reported lower bound; it does not replace the verified bound above.
The exact specialization and comparison are retained by
[`audit_ds7_lower_bounds`](../devtools/audit_ds7_lower_bounds.py).
