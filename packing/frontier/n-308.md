# `s(308)` — open

**Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form, 14 October 2026.** Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form
(wand125, 13 October 2026, T-180) states $s(N) \ge a(N)$ for every non-square
$N \ge 49$, from a measure of line segments on 34 intervals of the fractional part.
At $n = 308$, $k = 17$ and interval 28 give
$a(308) = 17.616620106628016467339740238257\ldots$, so the reported lower bound here is
now $s(308) \ge 17.616620106628$, rounded down, above Nagamochi’s closed form,
$17.58312395177$. The source replayed its certificate with its own checker; no record of
it has been replayed here, so the verified lower bound is unchanged.
The source states that its search, certificates, checkers and text were produced with AI
assistance (Claude and Codex), under the direction of and reviewed by Hiroaki Hosono.

**House adoption, 20 October 2026.** Evan Daniel’s arrangement of Kevin Fang’s two-wedge
design in a search started from Nate Chaoweeraprasit’s n = 307 packing (T-221) is now
this case’s house: its exact certificate proves $s(308) \le 17.9971491212709088$, the
reported and the verified upper bound, rounded outward from its exact side.
The exact certificate side improves the previous verified exact side by about
`1.12e-03`. The earlier ceiling, displayed outward as $17.9982698795262558753$ from Evan
Daniel’s packing, improved by PingYou Ltd (T-174), applied until this adoption and stays
retained as history ([the certificate](#evan-daniels-certificate-t-221)).

**Reported certificate, 2026-10-19.** Evan Daniel reported a 308-square certificate at
container side $17.997149121271$, rounded upward from the
[retained exact certificate](../resources/web/evand-n308-2026-10-19/README.md).
The exact rational side is registered in [the result register](RESULTS.md).
Its V0/C0 registration left both case upper lanes, the house witness and its drawing
unchanged until the house adoption of 20 October 2026, and until then the rigidity and
arrangement notes below described that retained house.

The source calls this a new arrangement, found by inserting one square into Nate
Chaoweeraprasit’s 307-square packing of #481 and descending with its explorer, and says
it neither refines nor descends from Kevin Fang’s #484 or Evan Daniel’s #489, whose
packing the house refined until 20 October 2026. Its original and regularized display
certificates have the same side and yield one result.
The registration asserted no measured packing identity or lineage; the

measured it pose by pose: under a quarter turn, 15 of the 308 squares lie beyond a tenth

two-wedge design, as [its certificate’s section](#evan-daniels-certificate-t-221) says.

**House adoption, 17 October 2026.** PingYou Ltd’s refinement of Evan Daniel’s packing
based on Kevin Fang’s two-wedge construction (T-174) was this case’s house until 20
October 2026: its exact certificate proves $s(308) \le 17.9982698795262558753$, the
reported and the verified upper bound, rounded outward from its exact side.
The exact certificate side improves the previous verified exact side by about
`1.80e-19`. The earlier exact ceiling,
$17998269879526255875387992744507/1000000000000000000000000000000$ from Kevin Fang’s

**House adoption, 11 October 2026.** Evan Daniel’s move of Kevin Fang’s two-wedge
construction (T-141) was this case’s house until 17 October 2026: its exact certificate
proves $s(308) \le 17.9982698795262559$, the reported and the verified upper bound,

which stays retained as history ([the certificate](#evan-daniels-certificate-t-141)).

Open. Previously, the best known packing gave $s(308) \le 18$, and the strongest lower
bound independently verified here is $17.566048$ from Karakuş’s general theorem, leaving
a gap of $0.434$. General bound: s(N) >= 1/2 + sqrt(N - floor(sqrt(N)) + 1/4).

## The packing

The record catalogue does not picture $n = 308$: until October 2026 no arrangement was
known that beats the trivial $\lceil\sqrt{308}\rceil = 18$ grid, and the catalogue’s
conjecture that the grid is optimal here was a statement about what had been searched,
not a proof. Kevin Fang’s two-wedge construction (T-135, issue 484) beat it first, and
Evan Daniel’s move of that construction (T-141, issue 489) is smaller again, and his
arrangement of the same design (T-221, issue 689), the house from 20 October 2026,
smaller still; the grid stays retained as history.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
