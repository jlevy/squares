# `s(86)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(86) ≤ 9.82287566`, and the best proved lower bound
is `9.306624` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a gap of `0.5163`. General
closed form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N - 2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The packing

Friedman’s 1997 generalisation of the `n = 18` packing, at the same DS7-named tilt, for
which the survey states the angle but no generating rule.
`cases/lifted_q7` lifts the retained witness’s every coordinate into `Q(sqrt 7)` at
small height — the repository’s first exact verification outside `Q(sqrt 2)` — and
verifies the lifted pose exactly, which is what moved `verified_upper_bound` from the
grid ceiling onto the published exact side.
The lift is a candidate generator and the exact verifier is the proof (`D-398`, and the
operation `D-402` does not foreclose).

## The lower bound

Nothing specific to this `n` has ever been proved.
The bound is Nagamochi’s general closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

This is the default across almost the whole open frontier — 58 of the 65 open cases at
`n ≤ 100` are governed by it, and outside this repository’s own displacements it has not
been improved since 2005.
