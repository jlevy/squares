## The packing

Friedman’s 1997 generalisation of the $n = 18$ packing, at the same DS7-named tilt, for
which the survey states the angle but no generating rule.
`cases/lifted_q7` lifts the retained witness’s every coordinate into `Q(sqrt 7)` at
small height — the repository’s first exact verification outside `Q(sqrt 2)` — and
verifies the lifted pose exactly, which is what moved `verified_upper_bound` from the
grid ceiling onto the published exact side.
The lift is a candidate generator and the exact verifier is the proof (`D-398`, and the
operation `D-402` does not foreclose).

## The lower bound

The verified field rests on the complete replay here of wand125’s mixed certificate for
[85 squares](n-085.md) ([`E-n085-wand125-mixed-946-source-replay`](evidence.yaml),
`V3/C3`): all 201 directions on 2 October 2026, each returning the certificate’s own
record. Its mass, $8499999/100000$, is below 86, so a packing of 86 at a side below
$473/50$ would capture at least 86 from it.

From the morning of 2 October 2026 until that replay the verified lower bound was
$471/50$, by the replay of the source’s earlier $n = 85$ certificate
([`E-n085-wand125-mixed-942-source-replay`](evidence.yaml)).

Before the replayed rectangle certificate of 28 September, which held it until 2 October
2026, the verified lower bound was Nagamochi’s general closed form, now a reported bound
(see the correction below).
Karakuş’s general bound, independently verified here and below these certificates at
this $n$, applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

See the [Frontier corpus summary](README.md#what-the-corpus-shows) for the current
aggregate count; source-reported bounds are recorded separately.
