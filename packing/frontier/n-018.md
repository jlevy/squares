# `s(18)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(18) ≤ 4.82287566`. The current first-party lower
bound is `s(18) ≥ 459/100 = 4.59` (`T-019`, `V4/C4`), leaving a gap of
`0.23287565553229`. The same frozen 1,184-atom certificate used at `n = 17` proves this
directly: its total mass is already below 17, hence below 18, and only the mass
condition depends on `n`. Monotonicity gives the same inequality but is not the recorded
proof step.

## The packing

Hämäläinen’s 1980 packing, at the tilt `arcsin((sqrt(7) − 1)/4)` DS7 names exactly, for
which the survey states the angle but no generating rule.
`cases/lifted_q7` lifts the retained witness’s every coordinate into `Q(sqrt 7)` at
small height — the repository’s first exact verification outside `Q(sqrt 2)` — and
verifies the lifted pose exactly, which is what moved `verified_upper_bound` from the
grid ceiling onto the published exact side.
The lift is a candidate generator and the exact verifier is the proof (`D-398`, and the
operation `D-402` does not foreclose).

## The lower bound

The operative bound is the first-party weighted certificate at side `459/100`. Its exact
event-cell and interval branch-and-bound decisions agree on the least covered mass.
The certificate was generated at `n = 17`, but its C1 mass is `423327/25000 < 17`; all
other conditions omit `n`, so the same object proves the `n = 18` statement directly.
Massaccesi’s externally proposed `4.5058` certificate remains a historical source
control, not the current bound.
Nagamochi’s general closed form remains the external published baseline and applies to
every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

It is weaker here than the verified `4.59` bound recorded above.
