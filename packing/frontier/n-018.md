# `s(18)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(18) ≤ 4.82287566`. The verified lower bound is
inherited by monotonicity from the `n = 17` Massaccesi certificate adopted 2026-09-03
(`[Burns–Massaccesi n17]`, externally proposed, source-backed and replayed here, `T-016`
at `V4/C3`): `s(18) ≥ s(17) ≥ 22529/5000 = 4.5058`, above the first-party sixteen-point
certificate’s `4.426213` (`T-002`, now the second strongest bound) and Nagamochi’s
general `4.316625` (closed form:
`s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N - 2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1)`), leaving a bound gap
of `0.3171` to the reported record.

## The packing

Hämäläinen’s 1980 packing, at the tilt `arcsin((sqrt(7) − 1)/4)` DS7 names exactly, for
which the survey states the angle but no generating rule.
`cases/lifted_q7` lifts the retained witness’s every coordinate into `Q(sqrt 7)` at
small height — the repository’s first exact verification outside `Q(sqrt 2)` — and
verifies the lifted pose exactly, which is what moved `verified_upper_bound` from the
grid ceiling onto the published exact side.
The lift is a candidate generator and the exact verifier is the proof (`D-398`, and the
operation `D-402` does not foreclose).

## The lower bound

The operative repository bound is the externally proposed `n = 17` Massaccesi
certificate — 168 rationally weighted atoms of total mass `203/12 < 17` in
`[0, 4.5058]²`, every closed unit square capturing mass at least one — inherited by
monotonicity: a packing of 18 unit squares contains a packing of 17. It is a 2026
blog-post result, not peer reviewed, whose author marks the value “(?)”; it is replayed
here by the retained source verifier and by an accumulation-independent repository
instrument (H-052) and audited lemma by lemma (BC-150, BC-151), and no new first-party
mathematics is claimed for it.
The first-party sixteen-point certificate (`cases/green17`, `4.426213`, two independent
methods) is now the second strongest bound.
No separate theorem specific to `n = 18` is claimed.
Nagamochi’s general closed form remains the external published baseline and applies to
every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

It is weaker here than the verified `4.5058` bound recorded above.
