# `s(18)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(18) ≤ 4.82287566`. The verified lower bound is
`s(18) ≥ 459/100 = 4.59`, from this repository’s weighted fractional unavoidable-set
certificate at that side (`T-019`, 2026-09-04, `V4/C4`), leaving a bound gap of `0.2329`
to the reported record.
It is not inherited by monotonicity.
Only `Condition 2` of the five mentions `n`, so an atom set of total mass `423327/25000`
certifies its side for every integer strictly above that mass — 17 and upward — and
eighteen is one of them directly.
The bounds it displaced: the `n = 17` Massaccesi certificate’s `22529/5000 = 4.5058`,
carried here by monotonicity as `T-016` and superseded on 2026-09-04; below that the
first-party sixteen-point certificate’s `4.426213` (`T-002`); and Nagamochi’s general
`4.316625` (closed form:
`s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N - 2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1)`).

## The packing

Hämäläinen’s 1980 packing, at the tilt `arcsin((sqrt(7) − 1)/4)` DS7 names exactly, for
which the survey states the angle but no generating rule.
`cases/lifted_q7` lifts the retained witness’s every coordinate into `Q(sqrt 7)` at
small height — the repository’s first exact verification outside `Q(sqrt 2)` — and
verifies the lifted pose exactly, which is what moved `verified_upper_bound` from the
grid ceiling onto the published exact side.
The lift is a candidate generator and the exact verifier is the proof (`D-398`, and the
operation `D-402` does not foreclose).

## The lower bound

The selected external report, [GitHub n17 certificates 2026], gives the lower-bound
expression `9141/2000` for `s(18)` (approximately `4.5705`). The indexed external v0.2.0
report states s(17) >= 9141/2000 = 4.5705; the source checker has not been replayed
completely here. Inherited at n=18 by monotonicity.
This changes the reported source field only; the independently verified lower bound
remains `4.59`. The [source audit](../devtools/audit_ds7_lower_bounds.py) compares the
exact theorem expressions separately from opaque table decimals.

David R. MacIver’s manuscript dated 8 August 2026 separately reports
`s(18) ≥ s(17) > (40sqrt(2)+19)/17 + 1/200 ≈ 4.450208382054341`, in Corollary 1.2. The
[archived source review](../resources/web/maciver-square-packing-2026-09-07/README.md)
records it as a historical claim whose essential certificate and replay files are absent
from the inspected public snapshot.
It does not change the stronger verified bound below.

The operative bound is this repository’s own weighted fractional unavoidable-set
certificate at side `459/100`: 1184 rationally weighted atoms on a `D4`-symmetric site
set, total mass `423327/25000 < 18`, every closed `B`-square at every net direction
capturing mass at least one, the least being `200009/200000`. It is decided from its own
bytes by an exact event-cell sweep and by an interval branch and bound over centre
boxes, which agree on that least value to the digit.
No monotonicity step is involved and none is needed: the covering program the search
solves does not contain `n` at all, and `Condition 2` — total mass strictly below `n` —
is the only place `n` enters, so the same atoms certify the side at every size above
their own mass. A side specific to eighteen squares has been attempted and not settled.
A run at `117/25 = 4.68` drove three site sets in succession, 538, 578 and 618 orbits,
and each returned a restricted optimum of exactly `18.000000`; adding sites can only
lower a restricted optimum and across those three it did not move.
Either the covering value at that side is at or above eighteen, in which case no
certificate exists there, or the optimum is sitting on a degenerate vertex and the site
sets are still short of it.
The run was stopped on cost, not on its answer (`T-019`, `next_rung`). Two weaker bounds
are retained for provenance: the Massaccesi certificate’s `4.5058`, which held this case
by monotonicity until 2026-09-04, and the first-party sixteen-point certificate
(`cases/green17`, `4.426213`, two independent methods) below it.
Nagamochi’s general closed form remains the external published baseline and applies to
every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

It is weaker here than the verified `4.5058` bound recorded above.
