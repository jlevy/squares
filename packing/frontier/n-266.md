# `s(266)` — open

**Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form, 14 October 2026.** Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form
(wand125, 13 October 2026, T-180) states $s(N) \ge a(N)$ for every non-square
$N \ge 49$, from a measure of line segments on 34 intervals of the fractional part.
At $n = 266$, $k = 16$ and interval 7 give
$a(266) = 16.382131212207268779454260030055\ldots$, so the reported lower bound here is
now $s(266) \ge 16.382131212207$, rounded down, above Nagamochi’s closed form,
$16.32970971675$. The source replayed its certificate with its own checker; no record of
it has been replayed here, so the verified lower bound is unchanged.
The source states that its search, certificates, checkers and text were produced with AI
assistance (Claude and Codex), under the direction of and reviewed by Hiroaki Hosono.

**Held for the owner, 15 October 2026.** T-174 at $16.8230287507564760$, `1.68e-19`
below this case’s verified ceiling, is confirmed here and accepted by the
[review of 14 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-14-upper-bound-imports-574.md),
but PR #574’s houses are held for the owner: their witnesses would add about 18.5 MB to
Git (OR-18) and their custody inputs about 5.8 MB to every worker’s snapshot; the case
keeps its house until the owner decides, and moves to T-174’s side if the owner adopts
them.

**New exact construction, 7 October 2026.** The complete #399 certificate proves
$s(266) \le 16.8230287507564760$ (T-119). The display is the least upward sixteen-place
ceiling of the full rational side.
It replaces the atlas pose; earlier source reports and certifications below describe
their original, different geometries.

Previously, **Exact certificate, 2026-10-06.** Evan Daniel’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md) published
on 5 October 2026 an exact rational certificate of this packing, decided here over
$\mathbb{Q}$ by two exact checkers that share no code with each other or with the
source, and by the source’s own two run here: it proves $s(266) \le 16.82306208283781$,
the verified upper bound (T-101). Until then the verified upper bound was the trivial
grid bound $17$.

Open. Previously, the best known packing gives $s(266) \le 16.82306209$, and the
strongest lower bound independently verified here is $16.319292$ from Karakuş’s general
theorem, leaving a gap of $0.5038$. General bound: s(N) >= 1/2 + sqrt(N - floor(sqrt(N))
\+ 1/4).

## The earlier exact certificate

Evan Daniel’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md) published
on 5 October 2026 exact rational certificates of the packings this register lists as
best known, solved from its own witnesses, and offered those that do not lower a printed
side as a replay of the existing bounds, asking for nothing to be registered from them.
The certificate for this count holds the same 266 squares, each a rational centre and a
rational $t = \tan(\theta/2)$, in a square of side $16.8230620828378046464\ldots$,
`4.6e-15` above the side the Kingbird catalogue prints, $16.82306208283780$. Square for
square, its pose lies within `1.5e-4` of the binary64 pose the atlas pictures for this
count. 1 square moves by more than `1e-8`, one the source lists as carrying no force;
every other square’s centre rounds to the witness’s. Evan Daniel’s solver moves the
binary64 pose to a nearby exact KKT point of the problem of minimizing the side under
non-overlap, computed at 80 digits, and rounds it outward to rationals.

This repository decides the certificate exactly.
Converted without rounding, every pair and every wall is decided over $\mathbb{Q}$
twice, by `sqpack`’s exact separating-axis test and by an independent checker that
shares no code with it, and the source’s own two checkers, run here as retained, accept
it as well
([receipts](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md#replayed-here)).
Previously, that proves $s(266) \le 16.82306208283781$, the verified upper bound: the
certificate’s side rounded up at the fourteen decimals the catalogue prints, as the
record writes any certificate of a printed side.
It says nothing about optimality.
On jlevy/squares#375 its author wrote that the solver, its checkers and the batch “were

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
