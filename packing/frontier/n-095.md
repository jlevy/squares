# `s(95)` — solved

$s(95) = 10$. Established by Benjamin Greff (2026) with a mixed cover in Evan Daniel’s
format, decided by Daniel’s interval checker `zmx2` and his exact-rational
`zm_mixed.py`, whose checks were replayed here in full (T-191). It is the case $k = 10$
of $s(k^2 - 5) = k$. The source says the search, LP and verification pipeline were built
and run with Claude (Anthropic) as an AI assistant, under the author’s direction.

## The packing

The upper bound is trivial: the $10 \times 10$ grid holds 100 unit squares, so it holds
95 with five cells empty, and $s(95) \le 10$. The record catalogue does not picture
$n = 95$, and no arrangement below side 10 has ever been found.
What is new is that none exists.

## The lower bound

The [certificate](../resources/web/bengreff-s95-certificate-2026-10-15/README.md) is a
mixed cover in Daniel’s format: 48,352 rationally weighted points of $[0,10]^2$ plus
mass spread uniformly along 11,438 axis-parallel segments, total
$3791753447/40000000 = 94.793836175 < 95$, exactly invariant under the square’s eight
symmetries, such that every closed unit square inside $[0,10]^2$, at every centre and
every angle, captures mass at least $1$. If 95 unit squares fit in a side $s < 10$,
scaling by $10/s$ and shrinking each square about its centre gives 95 pairwise disjoint
closed unit squares in $[0,10]^2$, which would capture at least 95 from a total of
$94.794$.

The verified field rests on a complete replay here of both of Daniel’s checkers, read as
one decision ([`E-bengreff-610-cover-replay`](evidence.yaml), `V3/C3`): `zmx2`, built
from the retained source the source’s `verify.sh` pins, over all 10,000 root cells of
the `D4` region, and `zm_mixed.py`, in exact rationals, over the 304 cells that tile the
19 roots `zmx2` leaves, every census equal to the source’s records.
`zmx2` refuses two mutated copies of the cover, and `zm_mixed.py`, with the source’s
flags and depth limit, refuses two copies with every mass scaled just below one at a low
pose, each on a cell where it certifies the published cover; the receipts are in the
[packet](../resources/web/bengreff-s95-certificate-2026-10-15/README.md).
The `zmx2` part assumes correctly rounded binary64 arithmetic on the host, both parts
rest on the reduction to the `D4` region, and both programs are Daniel’s, the checkers
the producer used: a reproduction with the producer’s code.
The
[stage 4 review](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-16-greff-k2m5-mixed-covers-76-95.md)
of 16 October found no mathematical defect in the claim.

Until 2 November 2026 the verified field rested on a complete decision here
([`E-n095-wand125-mixed-9965-sqverify-fast-replay`](evidence.yaml), `V3/C3`) of Hosono’s
mixed certificate of 4 October for this count by sqverify-fast, this repository’s
clean-room measure verifier: all 201 net directions of the retained candidate on 6
October 2026, with two mutants scaled below coverage one refused.
It shares no code with the source’s checker, so it decides coverage a second way with
the same method; the source’s own checker was not run here.

Earlier on 6 October 2026 the verified field rested on a complete decision here
([`E-n094-wand125-mixed-995-sqverify-fast-replay`](evidence.yaml), `V3/C3`) of Hosono’s
mixed certificate of 4 October for [94 squares](n-094.md), whose mass, $9399999/100000$,
is below 95, by sqverify-fast, this repository’s clean-room measure verifier: all 201
net directions of the retained candidate on 6 October 2026, with two mutants scaled
below coverage one refused.
Before that the verified lower bound was $49259/5000$
([`E-wand125-rectangle-2026-10-01-source-replay`](evidence.yaml)).

Nagamochi’s general closed form, the verified lower bound before this certificate, is
now a reported bound (see the correction below).
Karakuş’s general bound, independently verified here and below this certificate at this
$n$, applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

aggregate count; source-reported bounds are recorded separately.
