# `s(262)` — open

**Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form, 14 October 2026.** Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form
(wand125, 13 October 2026, T-180) states $s(N) \ge a(N)$ for every non-square
$N \ge 49$, from a measure of line segments on 34 intervals of the fractional part.
At $n = 262$, $k = 16$ and interval 6 give
$a(262) = 16.234323076190945154058719002583\ldots$, so the reported lower bound here is
now $s(262) \ge 16.234323076190$, rounded down, above Nagamochi’s closed form,
$16.19868415357$. The source replayed its certificate with its own checker; no record of
it has been replayed here, so the verified lower bound is unchanged.
The source states that its search, certificates, checkers and text were produced with AI
assistance (Claude and Codex), under the direction of and reviewed by Hiroaki Hosono.

**House adoption, 18 November 2026.** Mykhailo Polkovskyi’s further arrangement of Joost
de Winter’s band packing, the first below the Kingbird catalogue’s 16 + √2/2 at 262 the
record adopts (T-319) is now this case’s house: its exact certificate proves
$s(262) \le 16.6981150186993945$, the reported and the verified upper bound, rounded
outward from its exact side.
The exact certificate side improves the previous verified exact side by about
`8.99e-03`. The earlier exact ceiling,
$16707106781186547524567915429917/1000000000000000000000000000000$ from the catalogue’s
packing (T-101, T-155), applied until this adoption and stays retained as history; the
reported upper bound it replaces, $16.70710678118654$ from [Kingbird], lies `8.99e-03`
above the new side ([the certificate](#mykhailo-polkovskyis-certificate-t-319)).

**Exact regularized certificate, 2026-10-12.** This project’s regularization pass, Evan
Daniel’s exact solver and regularizer run here on this register’s witness, produced an
exact rational packing of this arrangement, decided here over $\mathbb{Q}$ by two exact
checkers: it proves $s(262) \le 16.707106781186547524567915429917$, a bound Evan
Daniel’s exact certificate of this packing already proves at the same side (T-101). The
verified upper bound was that side exactly until 18 November 2026, resting on both
certificates (T-101, T-155), and the record printed it as $16.70710678118655$. Until
then the verified upper bound was exactly $16.70710678118655$, the side of Evan Daniel’s
exact certificate of this packing rounded up at the printed precision (T-101).

**Exact certificate, 2026-10-06.** Evan Daniel’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md) published
on 5 October 2026 an exact rational certificate of this packing, decided here over
$\mathbb{Q}$ by two exact checkers that share no code with each other or with the
source, and by the source’s own two run here: it proves $s(262) \le 16.70710678118655$,
the verified upper bound from 6 to 12 October 2026 (T-101). Until then the verified
upper bound was the trivial grid bound $17$.

Open.
Previously, the best known packing gave $s(262) \le 16.70710679$, and the strongest
lower bound independently verified here is $16.192354$ from Karakuş’s general theorem,
leaving a gap of $0.5148$. General bound: s(N) >= 1/2 + sqrt(N - floor(sqrt(N)) + 1/4).

solver and regularizer
([`evand/square-packing`](https://github.com/evand/square-packing), MIT, vendored here)

`coincide` placement, which wrote its certificate at the side the solver reached, the
solved point scaled by $1 + 10^{-20}$ and the side rounded up.
The result is an exact rational packing of the same 262 squares in a square of side
$16.707106781186547524567915429917$, `2.5e-15` below the verified upper bound the record
held before, which was that same side rounded up at the printed precision: it is the
side of Evan Daniel’s certificate of this packing (T-101). Measured against the witness
under the box’s eight symmetries, its squares pair one to one with the witness’s; none
moves by more than `1e-3`, and none turns by more than `1e-6` as a sine.
It draws the same picture as the witness, so the atlas drawing does not change.

separating-axis test and by an independent checker that shares no code with it, and both

shrunk past its least clearance ([certificate](../cases/regularized_bounds/README.md)).

with the producer’s code.
That proves $s(262) \le 16.707106781186547524567915429917$, the verified upper bound

It says nothing about optimality, and the packing stays its finders’.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.

## Mykhailo Polkovskyi’s certificate (T-319)

Mykhailo Polkovskyi’s further arrangement of Joost de Winter’s band packing, the first
below the Kingbird catalogue’s 16 + √2/2 at 262 the record adopts, reported on
[jlevy/squares#1009](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/1009), at exact side
$521816094334356075877658927847/31250000000000000000000000000$, displayed upward as
$16.6981150186993945$ (T-319). The certificate gives a rational side and, for each of
the 262 unit squares, a rational centre and the tangent of its half-angle; the
[retained packet](../resources/web/mishapolk-issue1009-2026-11-16/README.md) holds it
with the complete native jobs that decided it.

Both maintained exact routes decided it here, `sqpack`’s exact separating-axis verifier
and the independent rational corner checker, with its duplicate-square and
outside-container controls, and the 17 November review’s clean-room checker, frozen
before any producer, witness or verifier code was read, decided it again by separating
axes in integers from its centres and half-angle tangents and by exact clipping in GMP
rationals from its corners, each run on both of its forms, over every square, wall and
pair; its 37 controls at this count, stated beforehand as rules, were each decided as
stated. Both routes read the certificate through an adapter transcribed from Evan
Daniel’s checkers, the family of the source’s own checker, and the second is the
independent rational corner checker the issue says the author ran on the same packing’s
