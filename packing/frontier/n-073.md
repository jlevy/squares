## The packing

The record catalogue does not picture $n = 73$: no arrangement has ever been found that
beats the trivial $\lceil\sqrt{73}\rceil = 9$ grid, so the grid is still the best known
packing. That is a statement about what has been searched, not a proof.

## The lower bound

The verified field rests on a complete decision here
([`E-n073-wand125-mixed-8813-sqverify-fast-replay`](evidence.yaml), `V3/C3`) of
wand125’s mixed certificate of 4 October by sqverify-fast, this repository’s clean-room
measure verifier: all 201 net directions of the retained candidate on 5 October 2026,
with two mutants scaled below coverage one refused.
It shares no code with the source’s checker, so it decides coverage a second way with
the same method; the source’s own checker was not run here.
Until this decision the verified lower bound was $1737/200$
([`E-wand125-rectangle-2026-09-28-source-replay`](evidence.yaml)).

From 2 October until that decision the verified lower bound was wand125’s replayed
rectangle-density certificate for 71 squares, carried here by monotonicity (T-070). It
superseded wand125’s exact weighted point certificate for $n = 72$, replayed here in
full (T-044) and carried to $n = 73$ the same way: deleting one square from a packing of
73 leaves a packing of 72, so $s(73) \ge s(72) \ge 861/100 = 8.61$, which was the
strongest registered bound once Nagamochi’s formula left the verified lane.
Karakuş’s general bound gives only $8.577747$ here.

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).
Nagamochi’s value had masked the registered bound above.
The review’s text would put Karakuş’s weaker bound here; the record keeps the strongest
registered verified bound instead.
