# `s(128)` — open

**Exact regularized certificate, 2026-10-12.** This project’s regularization pass, Evan
Daniel’s exact solver and regularizer run here on this register’s witness, produced an
exact rational packing of this arrangement, decided here over $\mathbb{Q}$ by two exact
checkers: it proves $s(128) \le 11.825091968213687029149282424753443193$, the verified
upper bound (T-155) rounded up at 36 places, which the record still prints as
$11.82509196821369$. Until then the verified upper bound was exactly
$11.82509196821369$, the side of Evan Daniel’s exact certificate of this packing rounded
up at the printed precision (T-101).

**Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form, 14 October 2026.** Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form
(wand125, 13 October 2026, T-180) states $s(N) \ge a(N)$ for every non-square
$N \ge 49$, from a measure of line segments on 34 intervals of the fractional part.
At $n = 128$, $k = 11$ and interval 10 give
$a(128) = 11.410198096219834384485926334692\ldots$, so the reported lower bound here is
now $s(128) \ge 11.410198096219$, rounded down, above Nagamochi’s closed form,
$11.34408043278$. The source replayed its certificate with its own checker; no record of
it has been replayed here, so the verified lower bound is unchanged.
The source states that its search, certificates, checkers and text were produced with AI
assistance (Claude and Codex), under the direction of and reviewed by Hiroaki Hosono.

**Exact certificate, 2026-10-06.** Evan Daniel’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md) published
on 5 October 2026 an exact rational certificate of this packing, decided here over
$\mathbb{Q}$ by two exact checkers that share no code with each other or with the
source, and by the source’s own two run here: it proves $s(128) \le 11.82509196821369$,
the verified upper bound from 6 to 12 October 2026 (T-101). Until then the verified
upper bound was the trivial grid bound $12$.

Open. The best known packing gives $s(128) \le 11.82509197$, and the strongest lower
bound independently verified here is $11.328203$ from Karakuş’s general theorem, leaving
a gap of $0.4969$. General bound: s(N) >= 1/2 + sqrt(N - floor(sqrt(N)) + 1/4).

## The regularized exact certificate

This project’s regularization pass of 11 and 12 October 2026 ran Evan Daniel’s exact
solver and regularizer
([`evand/square-packing`](https://github.com/evand/square-packing), MIT, vendored here)
on this register’s witness for this count, with the `conservative` angle policy and
`coincide` placement, and this project’s same-side re-solve then fixed the result’s
tight contacts over $\mathbb{Q}$ at the witness’s own side.
The result is an exact rational packing of the same 128 squares in a square of side
$11.825091968213687029149282424753443193$ (rounded up at 36 places), `3.0e-15` below the
verified upper bound the record held before, and `1.2e-19` below the side of Evan
Daniel’s certificate of this packing (T-101): it is the witness’s own side.
Measured against the witness under the box’s eight symmetries, its squares pair one to
one with the witness’s; none moves by more than `1e-3`, and none turns by more than
`1e-6` as a sine.
It draws the same picture as the witness, so the atlas drawing does not
change.

Each pair and each wall is decided over $\mathbb{Q}$ twice, by `sqpack`’s exact
separating-axis test and by an independent checker that shares no code with it, and both
accept it; both refuse it with its tightest pair pushed into overlap and with its box
shrunk past its least clearance ([certificate](../cases/regularized_bounds/README.md)).
The same two checkers accepted it in the run that made it, so this is a reproduction
with the producer’s code.
That proves $s(128) \le 11.825091968213687029149282424753443193$, the verified upper
bound (T-155) rounded up at 36 places.
The exact side, a rational, is the exact form of the case’s `verified_upper_bound`,
which prints it as $11.82509196821369$, the decimal it printed before and an upper
display of it. It says nothing about optimality, and the packing stays its finders’.

rational $t = \tan(\theta/2)$, in a square of side $11.8250919682136870293\ldots$,
`7.0e-15` above the side the Kingbird catalogue prints, $11.82509196821368$. Square for
square, its pose lies within `1.5e-4` of the binary64 pose the atlas pictures for this
count. 1 square moves by more than `1e-8`, one the source lists as carrying no force;
every other square’s centre rounds to the witness’s. Evan Daniel’s solver moves the
binary64 pose to a nearby exact KKT point of the problem of minimizing the side under
non-overlap, computed at 80 digits, and rounds it outward to rationals.

This repository decides the certificate exactly.
Converted without rounding, every pair and every wall is decided over $\mathbb{Q}$
twice, by `sqpack`’s exact separating-axis test and by an independent checker that
shares no code with it, and the source’s own two checkers, run here as retained, accept
it as well
([receipts](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md#replayed-here)).
That proves $s(128) \le 11.82509196821369$, the verified upper bound until 12 October
2026: the certificate’s side rounded up at the fourteen decimals the catalogue prints,
as the record writes any certificate of a printed side.
It says nothing about optimality.
On jlevy/squares#375 its author wrote that the solver, its checkers and the batch “were

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
