# `s(128)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(128) ≤ 11.82509197`, and the best proved lower
bound is `11.344080` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a gap of `0.481`. General
closed form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N - 2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is `12`, which is **larger** than the best known
`11.82509196821368` two fields above it.
It is not a tighter reading of the same packing and it is not the value of `s(128)`: it
is the strongest ceiling this repository can certify from its own evidence — the trivial
grid bound `⌈√128⌉ = 12` — and it trails the reported construction by
`0.17490803178632`.

Its `exact_form` is the exact form of that ceiling, never of `s(128)`. `s(128)` is not
known exactly; `status` stays `open`, and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter
names the gap. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The packing

Found by David Ellsworth in 2024, via an unrecorded method.
Improved by David Ellsworth in December 2024. Improved by David W. Cantrell and David
Ellsworth in January 2025. Its side length is algebraic of degree **40** over `ℚ`.

## The lower bound

Nothing specific to this `n` has ever been proved.
The bound is Nagamochi’s general closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

This is the default across almost the whole open frontier, and outside this repository’s
own displacements it has not been improved since 2005.
