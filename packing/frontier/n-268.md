# `s(268)` — open

**Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form, 14 October 2026.** Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form
(wand125, 13 October 2026, T-180) states $s(N) \ge a(N)$ for every non-square
$N \ge 49$, from a measure of line segments on 34 intervals of the fractional part.
At $n = 268$, $k = 16$ and interval 12 give
$a(268) = 16.429141319661638627012326214822\ldots$, so the reported lower bound here is
now $s(268) \ge 16.429141319661$, rounded down, above Nagamochi’s closed form,
$16.39480431834$. The source replayed its certificate with its own checker; no record of
it has been replayed here, so the verified lower bound is unchanged.
The source states that its search, certificates, checkers and text were produced with AI
assistance (Claude and Codex), under the direction of and reviewed by Hiroaki Hosono.

## The exact optimum

Evan Daniel’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md) published
on 5 October 2026 an exact rational certificate of this packing at its exact optimum
(T-098): the same 268 squares in a square of side $16.8788148210067647248\ldots$,
`1.2e-11` below the side Francisco Couzo prints.
Square for square, its pose lies within `1.5e-4` of the binary64 pose the atlas pictures
for this count. 7 squares move by more than `1e-8`, all of them squares the source lists
as carrying no force; every other square moves by at most `1.8e-11`. The known-best
witness this record lists is that binary64 pose, posed at its finder’s larger side; the
side above is witnessed by the certificate itself.
Evan Daniel’s solver moves the binary64 pose to a nearby exact KKT point of the problem
of minimizing the side under non-overlap, computed at 80 digits, and rounds it outward
to rationals, each square a rational centre and a rational $t = \tan(\theta/2)$.

This repository decides the certificate exactly.
Converted without rounding, every pair and every wall is decided over $\mathbb{Q}$
twice, by `sqpack`’s exact separating-axis test and by an independent checker that
shares no code with it, and the source’s own two checkers, run here as retained, accept
it as well
([receipts](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md#replayed-here)).
That proves $s(268) \le 16.8788148210067647248\ldots$, the verified upper bound until 17
October 2026; it says nothing about optimality.
The source reports the exact point as a KKT local minimum: multipliers that keep it in
equilibrium, a reduced Hessian positive definite once its exact flat motions are set
aside (its per-count report), and no first-order descent across corner-to-corner
contacts, each computed numerically at that point; none of that is verified here, and it
bears on this packing alone, not on $s(268)$. On jlevy/squares#375 its author wrote that
the solver, its checkers and the batch “were written with Claude (Anthropic) as a coding
and research agent, directed and reviewed by me.”

## The packing

Francisco Couzo’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/franciscouzo-square-packing-2026-09-27/README.md)
reports a packing of side $16.878814821018413$ for this count, dated 26 September 2026

first packing for this count, of side $16.879116936182307$, is dated 24 September 2026.
The repository names no method and no tolerance, and itself states no AI assistance; its
author said on [issue #227](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/227) that he found
the 102 and 103 packings “with the help of Claude”.

This repository certifies it exactly.
The retained decimal pose rounds to an exact rational packing at centre dilation 1, of
side $16.8788148210184113883\ldots$, no larger than the printed side, and every pair and
every wall is decided over $\mathbb{Q}$ twice, by the promotion’s exact separating-axis
test and by an independent checker that shares no code with it
([receipt](../resources/web/franciscouzo-square-packing-2026-09-27/README.md#certified-here)).
That proves $s(268) \le 16.878814821018413$, which was the verified upper bound until
Evan Daniel’s exact optimum above replaced it; it says nothing about optimality.
Interval arithmetic on the printed pose itself, with each angle’s true cosine and sine
and no rational rounding, decides every pair and wall again and gives the same bound
([interval route](../resources/web/franciscouzo-square-packing-2026-09-27/README.md#interval-route)).

Griffin Casson’s

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
