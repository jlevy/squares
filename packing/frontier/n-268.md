# `s(268)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(268) ≤ 16.8793321`, and the strongest lower bound
independently verified here is `16.394804` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a
gap of `0.4845`. General closed form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N -
2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is `17`, which is **larger** than the best known
`16.87933209237563` two fields above it.
It is not a tighter reading of the same packing and it is not the value of `s(268)`: it
is the strongest ceiling this repository can certify from its own evidence — the trivial
grid bound `⌈√268⌉ = 17` — and it trails the reported construction by
`0.12066790762437`.

Its `exact_form` is the exact form of that ceiling, never of `s(268)`. `s(268)` is not
known exactly; `status` stays `open`, and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter
names the gap. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The packing

Found by David Ellsworth in 2024, via an unrecorded method.
Improved by David W. Cantrell in February 2025. Its side length is algebraic of degree
**6** over `ℚ`.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Nagamochi’s general
closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
