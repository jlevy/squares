# `s(53)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(53) ≤ 7.82287566`, and the best proved lower bound
is `7.324555` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a gap of `0.4983`. General
closed form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N - 2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is `8`, which is **larger** than the best known
`7.82287565553229` two fields above it.
It is not a tighter reading of the same packing and it is not the value of `s(53)`: it
is the strongest ceiling this repository can certify from its own evidence — the trivial
grid bound `⌈√53⌉ = 8` — and it trails the reported construction by `0.17712434446771`.

Its `exact_form` is the exact form of that ceiling, never of `s(53)`. `s(53)` is not
known exactly; `status` stays `open`, and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter
names the gap. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The packing

Found by David W. Cantrell in 2002, via simulated annealing.
Later improved by David W. Cantrell and David Ellsworth.

## The lower bound

In the current verified register, this case uses Nagamochi’s general lower bound.
The bound is Nagamochi’s general closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

In the current verified register, Nagamochi supplies the lower bound for 58 of the 65
open cases at `n ≤ 100`.
