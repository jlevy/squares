# `s(6)` — solved

`s(6) = 3`. Kearney and Shiu (2002) gave the first located published proof.
Stromquist’s archived Memo I contains an earlier proof, dated September 11, 1984.

## The packing

Found by Erich Friedman in 1999, via a hand construction.

## The lower bound

Kearney and Shiu’s §3 uses two seven-point unavoidable sets, related by a quarter turn
and sharing the center.
They separate whether the center is covered, then use geometric compatibility and
segment-intersection bounds to exclude both cases.

Stromquist’s Memo I also needs a geometric helper: it excludes adjacent isolated dots,
then forces one square to consume four of eight final dots.
The
[memo review](../../docs/project/research/research-2026-09-07-stromquist-memos-and-helper-arguments.md)
retains an independently checked finite allocation control and a precise obstruction to
using only five unweighted dots.
The earlier generic five-dot pigeonhole description of this case was incorrect;
[D-479](../../defects.md) records the correction.
