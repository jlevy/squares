# `s(66)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(66) ≤ 8.65685425`, and the best proved lower bound
is `8.141428` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a gap of `0.5154`. General
closed form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N - 2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The packing

Stenlund’s diagonal strip of width three (1980), for which the survey states no
generating rule — only the figure.
`cases/lifted_q2` lifts the retained witness’s every coordinate into `Q(sqrt 2)` at
small height and verifies the lifted pose exactly, which is what moved
`verified_upper_bound` from the grid ceiling onto the published exact side.
The lift is a candidate generator and the exact verifier is the proof; the certificate
is the witness’s own geometry, made exact (`D-398`, and the operation `D-402` does not
foreclose).

## The lower bound

In the current verified register, this case uses Nagamochi’s general lower bound.
The bound is Nagamochi’s general closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

In the current verified register, Nagamochi supplies the lower bound for 58 of the 65
open cases at `n ≤ 100`.
