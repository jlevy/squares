# `s(199)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(199) ≤ 14.61898899`, and the best proved lower
bound is `14.114877` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a gap of `0.5041`.
General closed form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N - 2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is `15`, which is **larger** than the best known
`14.61898898660160` two fields above it.
It is not a tighter reading of the same packing and it is not the value of `s(199)`: it
is the strongest ceiling this repository can certify from its own evidence — the trivial
grid bound `⌈√199⌉ = 15` — and it trails the reported construction by
`0.38101101339840`.

Its `exact_form` is the exact form of that ceiling, never of `s(199)`. `s(199)` is not
known exactly; `status` stays `open`, and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter
names the gap. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The packing

Found by an unrecorded author, via extension of a smaller record.
Its side length is algebraic of degree **8** over `ℚ`.

## The lower bound

Nothing specific to this `n` has ever been proved.
The bound is Nagamochi’s general closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

This is the default across almost the whole open frontier, and outside this repository’s
own displacements it has not been improved since 2005.
