# `s(40)` — open

**External intake, 2026-10-10.** Guzhou0806’s
[clipped-corner release](../resources/web/guzhou-n40-clipped-corner-2026-10-10/README.md)
reports the strict bound $s(40) > 335427/50000 = 6.70854$, from wand125’s `rect_n40_L67`
density of the 2026-10-01 intake below, unchanged.
Every closed square of side $9977/10000$ inside $[0, 67/10]^2$, at each of 401
half-angle tangents of step $83/80000$, twice as fine as the 201 the density was first
decided on, captures mass at least $10001/10000$; a continuous-angle transfer, with a
bound on the clipped corners of a reference square that leaves its parent and the
density’s essential supremum, carries that to every unit square, and forty of them need
more than the mass $3999/100$. The release also gives the weaker full-core bound

peak. The complete 401-direction replay here by `sqverify-fast`, the crate the release

## The packing

Found by Frits Göbel in 1979, via a hand construction.
The catalogue flags it **rigid**: no continuous deformation is possible, so the contact
conditions pin the side length exactly as an algebraic number.

## The lower bound

The earlier record from [Friedman DS7] gives the lower-bound expression
`2*sqrt(2) + 8*sqrt(5)/5` for $s(40)$ (approximately $6.406135888746$). Friedman’s DS7
survey, Theorem 10, k=6, reports this bound at n=40; reference [8] is Green’s private
communication (2000). The source proof has not been recovered.
That report remains as historical evidence; the verified point certificate gave the
stronger bound $13/2$, the replayed rectangle-density certificates were stronger still
at $1339/200$ and then $67/10$, and Guzhou0806’s clipped-corner transfer on the latter’s
density now gives $335427/50000$. The
[source audit](../devtools/audit_ds7_lower_bounds.py) compares the exact theorem
expressions separately from opaque table decimals.

The current bound rests on two parts, each decided here.
The 401-direction nodal statement was decided by `sqverify-fast`, the code the release
ran, at every direction, with two mass mutants refused; the transfer’s finite steps, the
density’s mass, symmetry and essential supremum and the scalar chain, were decided again
in exact rationals by
[`audit_clipped_corner_transfer`](../devtools/audit_clipped_corner_transfer.py), with
four altered parameters refused.
A second method for the nodal statement, such as Tokoharu’s interval checker at all 401
directions, would add a method-distinct decision beside the rung; it is not a missing
premise of this bound.

**Corrected 2 October 2026:** Nagamochi’s Lemma 1 is false (Karakuş 2026), so his closed
form below is now a reported bound whose published proof is incomplete
([review](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).
Nagamochi’s earlier general closed form remains part of the evidence history and applies
to every $N \ge 4$:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```
