# `s(208)` — open

## The SQUISH Update

Nate Chaoweeraprasit, using SQUISH,
[reports a tighter bound](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/401#issuecomment-6043191866)
$s(208) \le 14.9245187720328190$, with exact side $2100439286887843/140737488355328$.
This is a reported V0/C0 claim pending complete replay and new scoped review.
The author reports the seed as Francisco Couzo’s s(209), squares removed.
The [revision-specific packet](../resources/web/squish-401-update-2026-10-07/README.md)
retains the source print $14.9245187720328190$ separately from the safe decimal ceiling.
The atlas drawing below uses the update’s exact rational geometry, checked for the
drawing independently of the frontier assurance.
The earlier certified bound and its retained evidence remain unchanged; this reported
claim stays V0/C0.

Open. The earlier verified packing, Francisco Couzo’s at Evan Daniel’s exact optimum
(T-098), gives $s(208) \le 14.9265344596994232470\ldots$, and the strongest lower bound
independently verified here is $14.437359$ from Karakuş’s general theorem, leaving a gap
of $0.4892$. General bound: s(N) >= 1/2 + sqrt(N - floor(sqrt(N)) + 1/4).

## The exact optimum

Evan Daniel’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md) published
on 5 October 2026 an exact rational certificate of this packing at its exact optimum
(T-098): the same 208 squares in a square of side $14.9265344596994232470\ldots$,
`4.1e-12` below the side Francisco Couzo prints.
Square for square, its pose lies within `5.8e-12` of the binary64 pose the atlas
previously pictured for this count.
The previous atlas witness was that binary64 pose, posed at its finder’s larger side;
the side above is witnessed by the certificate itself.
Evan Daniel’s solver moves the binary64 pose to a nearby exact KKT point of the problem
of minimizing the side under non-overlap, computed at 80 digits, and rounds it outward
to rationals, each square a rational centre and a rational $t = \tan(\theta/2)$.

This repository decides the certificate exactly.
Converted without rounding, every pair and every wall is decided over $\mathbb{Q}$
twice, by `sqpack`’s exact separating-axis test and by an independent checker that
shares no code with it, and the source’s own two checkers, run here as retained, accept
it as well
([receipts](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md#replayed-here)).
That proves $s(208) \le 14.9265344596994232470\ldots$, the verified upper bound; it says
nothing about optimality.
The source reports the exact point as a KKT local minimum: multipliers that keep it in
equilibrium, a reduced Hessian positive definite once its exact flat motions are set
aside (its per-count report), and no first-order descent across corner-to-corner
contacts, each computed numerically at that point; none of that is verified here, and it
bears on this packing alone, not on $s(208)$. On jlevy/squares#375 its author wrote that
the solver, its checkers and the batch “were written with Claude (Anthropic) as a coding
and research agent, directed and reviewed by me.”

## The packing

Francisco Couzo’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/franciscouzo-square-packing-2026-10-03/README.md)
reports a packing of side $14.926534459703511$ for this count, dated 3 October 2026, the
revision this record retained (T-092). It replaces his packing of side
$14.937018796984567$, dated 26 September 2026 (T-056). Its first packing for this count,
of side $14.937471987089294$, is dated 24 September 2026. The repository names no method
and no tolerance, and itself states no AI assistance; its author said on
[issue #227](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/227) that he found the 102 and 103
packings “with the help of Claude”.

This repository certifies it exactly.
The retained decimal pose rounds to an exact rational packing at centre dilation 1, of
side $14.9265344597035114471\ldots$, `4.471e-16` above the printed side, and every pair
and every wall is decided over $\mathbb{Q}$ twice, by the promotion’s exact
separating-axis test and by an independent checker that shares no code with it
([receipt](../resources/web/franciscouzo-square-packing-2026-10-03/README.md#certified-here)).
That proves $s(208) \le 14.926534459703512$, which was the verified upper bound until
Evan Daniel’s exact optimum above replaced it; it says nothing about optimality.
Interval arithmetic on the printed pose itself, with each angle’s true cosine and sine
and no rational rounding, decides every pair and wall again and gives the same bound
([interval route](../resources/web/franciscouzo-square-packing-2026-10-03/README.md#interval-route)).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

The verified ceiling is $s(208) \le 14.926534459699423246906428356506$, while the
reported packing has side $14.9245187720328190$, smaller by
$0.002015687666604246906428356506$. The `verified_upper_bound` certifies a ceiling, not
the value of $s(208)$; `reported_upper_bound` records the stronger source claim, whose
independent exact replay remains pending in this record.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
