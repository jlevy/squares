# `s(208)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(208) ≤ 14.93783045`, and the strongest lower bound
independently verified here is `14.453624` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a
gap of `0.4842`. General closed form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N -
2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is `15`, which is **larger** than the best known
`14.93783044811097` two fields above it.
It is not a tighter reading of the same packing and it is not the value of `s(208)`: it
is the strongest ceiling this repository can certify from its own evidence — the trivial
grid bound `⌈√208⌉ = 15` — and it trails the reported construction by
`0.06216955188903`.

Its `exact_form` is the exact form of that ceiling, never of `s(208)`. `s(208)` is not
known exactly; `status` stays `open`, and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter
names the gap. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The packing

Found by David Ellsworth in 2026, via simulated annealing.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Nagamochi’s general
closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
