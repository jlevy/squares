# `s(109)` — open

**House adoption, 21 November 2026.** Samanyu-dev’s public 109-square pinwheel (T-335)
is now this case’s house: its exact certificate proves $s(109) \le 10.9492895809453794$,
the reported and the verified upper bound, rounded outward from its exact side.
The exact certificate side improves the previous verified exact side by about
`4.58e-04`. The earlier exact ceiling,
$5474873734152916335457704004709/500000000000000000000000000000$ from the catalogue’s
packing (T-101, T-155), applied until this adoption and stays retained as history; the
reported upper bound it replaces, $10.94974746830583$ from [Kingbird], lies `4.58e-04`
above the new side ([the certificate](#samanyu-devs-certificate-t-335)).

The
[de Winter/Kodaj certificate](../resources/web/de-winter-kodaj-n109-2026-11-18/README.md)
is a verified restatement of Samanyu-dev’s pinwheel.
Its gain is within the contraction margin of the original packing.

**Earlier staging, 21 November 2026.** Samanyu-dev’s
[original pinwheel](../resources/web/samanyu-n109-2026-11-07/README.md) has an exact
rational certificate with 12-place ceiling $10.949289580946$ ([T-335](RESULTS.md)). The
[confirming review](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-11-21-samanyu-n109-canonical.md)
accepts this bound. The case kept its previous house until T-335’s adoption on 21
November 2026; the staging note required both strong generator runs in the adoption
change.

**External intake, 2026-11-18.** Joost de Winter’s
[lower bound at 108](../resources/web/de-winter-s108-lower-bound-2026-11-17/README.md),
which his issue #1018 reports, bounds 109 too, since a packing of 109 squares contains
one of 108: $s(109) \ge 1700799526134255846599/159999384348528500000 =
10.6300379408297\ldots$ (T-327), above Hosono’s closed form $a(109)$ (T-180) by $0.0787$
and Karakuş’s strip bound by $0.1676$, so it is the reported and the verified lower
bound. The certificate is Hosono’s T-216 density dilated by $1063/1062$ onto a declared
net of 401 directions, decided by this repository’s `sqverify-fast`, with a rational
enlargement past its side; the [108 record](n-108.md) gives the detail.
Its census replay here and the blind
[stage 4 review](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-11-18-de-winter-1018.md) of 18
November confirm it, reproduced with the producer’s code.

**Exact regularized certificate, 2026-10-12.** This project’s regularization pass, Evan
Daniel’s exact solver and regularizer run here on this register’s witness, produced an
exact rational packing of this arrangement, decided here over $\mathbb{Q}$ by two exact
checkers: it proves $s(109) \le 10.949747468305832670915408009418$, a bound Evan
Daniel’s exact certificate of this packing already proves at the same side (T-101). The
verified upper bound was that side exactly until 21 November 2026, resting on both
certificates (T-101, T-155), and the record printed it as $10.94974746830584$. Until
then the verified upper bound was exactly $10.94974746830584$, the side of Evan Daniel’s
exact certificate of this packing rounded up at the printed precision (T-101).

**Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form, 14 October 2026.** Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form
(wand125, 13 October 2026, T-180) states $s(N) \ge a(N)$ for every non-square
$N \ge 49$, from a measure of line segments on 34 intervals of the fractional part.
At $n = 109$, $k = 10$ and interval 27 give
$a(109) = 10.551290369783276731887101171077\ldots$, which was then the reported lower
bound here, rounded down at 12 places, above Nagamochi’s closed form, $10.4868329805$.
The source replayed its certificate with its own checker; no record of it has been
replayed here, so the verified lower bound is unchanged.
The source states that its search, certificates, checkers and text were produced with AI
assistance (Claude and Codex), under the direction of and reviewed by Hiroaki Hosono.

The result is an exact rational packing of the same 109 squares in a square of side
$10.949747468305832670915408009418$, `7.3e-15` below the verified upper bound the record
held before, which was that same side rounded up at the printed precision: it is the

under the box’s eight symmetries, its squares pair one to one with the witness’s; none

## The lower bound

The verified lower bound is de Winter’s at 108, by monotonicity, from 18 November 2026
(T-327, above). Karakuş’s general bound supplied the earlier verified lower bound.
It applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
