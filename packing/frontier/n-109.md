# `s(109)` — open

The
[de Winter/Kodaj certificate](../resources/web/de-winter-kodaj-n109-2026-11-18/README.md)
is a verified restatement of Samanyu-dev’s pinwheel.
Its gain is within the contraction margin of the original packing, registered as
[T-335](RESULTS.md) and awaiting house adoption.

**External intake, 2026-11-18.** Joost de Winter’s
[lower bound at 108](../resources/web/de-winter-s108-lower-bound-2026-11-17/README.md),
which his issue #1018 reports, bounds 109 too, since a packing of 109 squares contains
one of 108: $s(109) \ge 1700799526134255846599/159999384348528500000 =
10.6300379408297\ldots$ (T-327), above Hosono’s closed form $a(109)$ (T-180) by $0.0787$
and Karakuş’s strip bound by $0.1676$, so it is the reported and the verified lower
bound. The certificate is Hosono’s T-216 density dilated by $1063/1062$ onto a declared
net of 401 directions, decided by this repository’s `sqverify-fast`, with a rational
enlargement past its side; the [108 record](n-108.md) gives the detail.
Its census replay here and the blind
[stage 4 review](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-11-18-de-winter-1018.md) of 18
November confirm it, reproduced with the producer’s code.

**Exact regularized certificate, 2026-10-12.** This project’s regularization pass, Evan
Daniel’s exact solver and regularizer run here on this register’s witness, produced an
exact rational packing of this arrangement, decided here over $\mathbb{Q}$ by two exact
checkers: it proves $s(109) \le 10.949747468305832670915408009418$, a bound Evan
Daniel’s exact certificate of this packing already proves at the same side (T-101). The
verified upper bound is now that side exactly, resting on both certificates (T-101,
T-155), and the record still prints it as $10.94974746830584$. Until then the verified
upper bound was exactly $10.94974746830584$, the side of Evan Daniel’s exact certificate
of this packing rounded up at the printed precision (T-101).

**Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form, 14 October 2026.** Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form
(wand125, 13 October 2026, T-180) states $s(N) \ge a(N)$ for every non-square
$N \ge 49$, from a measure of line segments on 34 intervals of the fractional part.
At $n = 109$, $k = 10$ and interval 27 give
$a(109) = 10.551290369783276731887101171077\ldots$, which was then the reported lower
bound here, rounded down at 12 places, above Nagamochi’s closed form, $10.4868329805$.
The source replayed its certificate with its own checker; no record of it has been
replayed here, so the verified lower bound is unchanged.
The source states that its search, certificates, checkers and text were produced with AI
assistance (Claude and Codex), under the direction of and reviewed by Hiroaki Hosono.

**Exact certificate, 2026-10-06.** Evan Daniel’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md) published
on 5 October 2026 an exact rational certificate of this packing, decided here over
$\mathbb{Q}$ by two exact checkers that share no code with each other or with the
source, and by the source’s own two run here: it proves $s(109) \le 10.94974746830584$,
the verified upper bound from 6 to 12 October 2026 (T-101). Until then the verified
upper bound was the trivial grid bound $11$.

Open. The best known packing gives $s(109) \le 10.94974747$, and the strongest lower
bound verified here is $10.630037$ from de Winter’s certificate at 108 (T-327), leaving
a gap of $0.3197$; Karakuş’s general theorem gives $10.462429$. General bound: s(N) >=
1/2 + sqrt(N - floor(sqrt(N)) + 1/4).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is $10.949747468305832670915408009418$, exactly,
proved by Evan Daniel’s exact certificate of this packing
(`E-evand-exact-ceilings-2026-10-05-exact-replay`, T-101) and by the exact regularized
certificate of it (`E-regularized-bounds-2026-10-12-exact-decision`, T-155), and the
record prints it as $10.94974746830584$. As printed it is **larger** than the best known
$10.94974746830583$ two fields above it, by `1e-14`.

It is not the value of $s(109)$ and not a different packing: it is the certificate’s own
exact side. The catalogue gives the packing’s side exactly, as `6 + (7/2)sqrt(2)`, and
the certificate’s side lies `1.1e-19` above that, so it bounds the exact side from above
and does not reach it.
That side is irrational, so no rational certificate reaches it.
The `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter records the difference.
Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The regularized exact certificate

This project’s regularization pass of 11 and 12 October 2026 ran Evan Daniel’s exact
solver and regularizer
([`evand/square-packing`](https://github.com/evand/square-packing), MIT, vendored here)
on this register’s witness for this count, with the `conservative` angle policy and
`coincide` placement, which wrote its certificate at the side the solver reached, the
solved point scaled by $1 + 10^{-20}$ and the side rounded up.
The result is an exact rational packing of the same 109 squares in a square of side
$10.949747468305832670915408009418$, `7.3e-15` below the verified upper bound the record
held before, which was that same side rounded up at the printed precision: it is the
side of Evan Daniel’s certificate of this packing (T-101). Measured against the witness
under the box’s eight symmetries, its squares pair one to one with the witness’s; none
moves by more than `1e-3`, and none turns by more than `1e-6` as a sine.
It draws the same picture as the witness, so the atlas drawing does not change.

Each pair and each wall is decided over $\mathbb{Q}$ twice, by `sqpack`’s exact
separating-axis test and by an independent checker that shares no code with it, and both
accept it; both refuse it with its tightest pair pushed into overlap and with its box
shrunk past its least clearance ([certificate](../cases/regularized_bounds/README.md)).
The same two checkers accepted it in the run that made it, so this is a reproduction
with the producer’s code.
That proves $s(109) \le 10.949747468305832670915408009418$. Evan Daniel’s certificate of
this packing already proves the same bound at the same side (T-101), and its replay
here, by two checkers that share no code with each other or with the source, is
independent of the code that made this view.
That side is the exact form of the case’s `verified_upper_bound`, which cites both
certificates (T-101, T-155) and prints it as $10.94974746830584$, the decimal it printed
before and an upper display of it.
It says nothing about optimality, and the packing stays its finders’.

square, its pose lies within `1.0e-15` of the binary64 pose the atlas pictures for this
count. Evan Daniel’s solver moves the binary64 pose to a nearby exact KKT point of the

outward to rationals.

This repository decides the certificate exactly.

That proves $s(109) \le 10.94974746830584$, the verified upper bound until 12 October

## The lower bound

The verified lower bound is de Winter’s at 108, by monotonicity, from 18 November 2026
(T-327, above). Karakuş’s general bound supplied the earlier verified lower bound.
It applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
