# s(130) — Square Packing Case

**Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form, 14 October 2026.** Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form
(wand125, 13 October 2026, T-180) states $s(N) \ge a(N)$ for every non-square
$N \ge 49$, from a measure of line segments on 34 intervals of the fractional part.
At $n = 130$, $k = 11$ and interval 24 give
$a(130) = 11.485540560656512573001434419806\ldots$, so the reported lower bound here is
now $s(130) \ge 11.485540560656$, rounded down, above Nagamochi’s closed form,
$11.44030650891$. The source replayed its certificate with its own checker; no record of
it has been replayed here, so the verified lower bound is unchanged.
The source states that its search, certificates, checkers and text were produced with AI
assistance (Claude and Codex), under the direction of and reviewed by Hiroaki Hosono.

**Held for the owner, 15 October 2026.** T-174 at $11.9044830325157865$, `1.19e-19`
below this case’s verified ceiling, is confirmed here and accepted by the
[review of 14 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-14-upper-bound-imports-574.md),
but PR #574’s houses are held for the owner: their witnesses would add about 18.5 MB to
Git (OR-18) and their custody inputs about 5.8 MB to every worker’s snapshot; the case
keeps its house until the owner decides, and moves to T-174’s side if the owner adopts
them.

## The Upper Bound

Previously, Nate Chaoweeraprasit (itsnaka), using SQUISH, reports $s(130) \le S_n$, with
$S_n = \frac{6701628168660175}{562949953421312}$ in
[SQUISH’s ten-packing release](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/issues/401). The
certificate gives rational centres and half-angle parameters for 130 unit squares.
The source’s original finite decimal display is $11.9044830325168771$; the rational
fraction states the claimed bound exactly.

The new packing improves the earlier reported side $11.911187706548755$ from
[franciscouzo square-packing 2026-09-27]. This repository confirmed the upper bound by
independently re-implemented exact-rational checks: native `exact_verify` and
`devtools.check_rational_witness_independent` accept every pair and wall of the new
packing and reject both geometric controls.
The verified display is $11.9044830325168772$, the exact source side rounded upward at
sixteen decimals. Both accepted reviews cover the shared half-angle conversion and the
complete roster. The submitted certificates establish feasible upper bounds and make no
optimality or rigidity claim.
Separately, this repository’s
[numerical translation screen](../atlas/known-best/translation-escape-screen.json) finds
a nontrivial feasible motion in the retained configuration.
That numerical assessment concerns this configuration alone; it does not settle the
optimum or all packings at this side.

The author credits David Ellsworth’s records and tools and states that SQUISH’s solver,
verification and issue were written with Claude Opus 5.5 under his direction,
management, review and steering.
The [source packet](../resources/web/squish-401-2026-10-07/README.md) retains derived
packing facts and source metadata.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
