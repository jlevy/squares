# `s(131)` — open

Open. The best known published packing gives `s(131) ≤ 11.9565431081247732616…`, and the
strongest lower bound independently verified here is `11.488088` from Nagamochi’s
general theorem, leaving a gap of `0.4685`. General closed form: s(N) >=
min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N - 2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The verified upper bound is a ceiling

`verified_upper_bound` for this case is `12`, which is **larger** than the best known
`11.956543108124773261501115489978287643498579738` two fields above it.
It is not a tighter reading of the same packing and it is not the value of `s(131)`: it
is the strongest ceiling this repository can certify from its own evidence — the trivial
grid bound `⌈√131⌉ = 12` — and it trails the reported construction by
`0.04345689187522673849888451002`.

Its `exact_form` is the exact form of that ceiling, never of `s(131)`. `s(131)` is not
known exactly; `status` stays `open`, and the `mathematics` blocker in the frontmatter
names the gap. Read `reported_upper_bound` for the best known side length.

## The packing

The UnitSquare Project’s 29 July 2026 release improves the public Ellsworth parent by
`0.0000055853525567384986`. The release classifies the result as a construction-only
upper bound and says it used outward-rounded interval arithmetic, a 300-digit
zero-tolerance recomputation, and an independent published checker.
The public release does not include the interval boxes, governed receipt, or replayable
checker needed to inspect the formal claim.
We therefore record the value as reported.
The formal lane retains the exact `12 × 12` grid construction.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Nagamochi’s general
closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.
