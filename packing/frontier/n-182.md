# `s(182)` — open

**Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form, 14 October 2026.** Hiroaki Hosono’s closed form
(wand125, 13 October 2026, T-180) states $s(N) \ge a(N)$ for every non-square
$N \ge 49$, from a measure of line segments on 34 intervals of the fractional part.
At $n = 182$, $k = 13$ and interval 27 give
$a(182) = 13.582913235247782126819715568384\ldots$, so the reported lower bound here is
now $s(182) \ge 13.582913235247$, rounded down, above Nagamochi’s closed form,
$13.52996408614$. The source replayed its certificate with its own checker; no record of
it has been replayed here, so the verified lower bound is unchanged.
The source states that its search, certificates, checkers and text were produced with AI
assistance (Claude and Codex), under the direction of and reviewed by Hiroaki Hosono.

**House adoption, 17 October 2026.** PingYou Ltd’s refinement of Evan Daniel’s exact
optimum of Francisco Couzo’s packing (T-174) is now this case’s house: its exact
certificate proves $s(182) \le 13.9740907131214390071$, the reported and the verified
upper bound, rounded outward from its exact side.
The exact certificate side improves the previous verified exact side by about
`1.40e-19`. The earlier ceiling, displayed outward as
$13.974090713121439007150130032772$ from Francisco Couzo’s packing, improved by Evan
Daniel (T-098), applied until this adoption and stays retained as history
([the certificate](#pingyou-ltds-certificate-t-174)).

**Held for the owner, 15 October 2026; lifted 16 October 2026.** T-174 at
$13.9740907131214391$, `1.40e-19` below this case’s verified ceiling, is confirmed here
and accepted by the
[review of 14 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-14-upper-bound-imports-574.md).
From 15 October 2026 until the owner’s house rule of 16 October 2026 lifted the hold, PR
#574’s houses were held for the owner, since their witnesses would add about 18.5 MB to
a checkout and their custody inputs about 5.8 MB to every worker’s snapshot.

Open. Previously, the best known published packing, Francisco Couzo’s at Evan Daniel’s
exact optimum (T-098), gives $s(182) \le 13.9740907131214390072\ldots$, and the
strongest lower bound independently verified here is $13.509611$ from Karakuş’s general
theorem, leaving a gap of $0.4645$. General bound: s(N) >= 1/2 + sqrt(N - floor(sqrt(N))
\+ 1/4).

## The exact optimum

Evan Daniel’s
[`square-packing`](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md) published
on 5 October 2026 an exact rational certificate of this packing at its exact optimum
(T-098): the same 182 squares in a square of side $13.9740907131214390072\ldots$,
`3.4e-12` below the side Francisco Couzo prints.
Square for square, its pose lies within `1.5e-4` of the binary64 pose the atlas pictures
for this count. 1 square moves by more than `1e-8`, all of them squares the source lists
as carrying no force; every other square moves by at most `4.8e-12`. The known-best
witness this record lists is that binary64 pose, posed at its finder’s larger side; the
side above is witnessed by the certificate itself.
Evan Daniel’s solver moves the binary64 pose to a nearby exact KKT point of the problem
of minimizing the side under non-overlap, computed at 80 digits, and rounds it outward
to rationals, each square a rational centre and a rational $t = \tan(\theta/2)$.

This repository decides the certificate exactly.
Converted without rounding, every pair and every wall is decided over $\mathbb{Q}$
twice, by `sqpack`’s exact separating-axis test and by an independent checker that
shares no code with it, and the source’s own two checkers, run here as retained, accept
it as well
([receipts](../resources/web/evand-square-packing-2026-10-05/README.md#replayed-here)).
That proves $s(182) \le 13.9740907131214390072\ldots$, the verified upper bound until 17
October 2026; it says nothing about optimality.
The source reports the exact point as a KKT local minimum: multipliers that keep it in
equilibrium, a reduced Hessian positive definite once its exact flat motions are set
aside (its per-count report), and no first-order descent across corner-to-corner
contacts, each computed numerically at that point; none of that is verified here, and it
bears on this packing alone, not on $s(182)$. On jlevy/squares#375 its author wrote that
the solver, its checkers and the batch “were written with Claude (Anthropic) as a coding
and research agent, directed and reviewed by me.”

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is Karakuş’s general
bound, which applies to every nonsquare $N \ge 8$:

```
s(N) ≥ ½ + √(N − ⌊√N⌋ + ¼)
```

**Correction, 2 October 2026.** Until that date the verified lower bound here was
Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, which
is stronger at this $n$ and is now recorded as a reported bound.
Its published proof rests on Nagamochi’s Lemma 1, which Karakuş showed false; nothing is
disproved, and no packing beating it is known
([review of 2 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md).

Source-reported bounds are recorded separately from this independently verified theorem.

## PingYou Ltd’s certificate (T-174)

PingYou Ltd’s refinement of Evan Daniel’s exact optimum of Francisco Couzo’s packing,
reported in [pull request #574](https://github.com/jlevy/squares/pull/574), at an exact

[retained packet](../resources/web/pingyou-pr574-certificates-2026-10-13/README.md)

and the independent rational corner checker, with its duplicate-square and

its own, and the replay here is independently re-implemented.
The
[review of 14 October 2026](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-14-upper-bound-imports-574.md)
accepted the replay for finite feasibility.
That proves $s(182) \le 13.9740907131214390071$, the verified upper bound: confirmed,
independently re-implemented.
It says nothing about optimality, rigidity or a local minimum.

by the source’s statement, not decided here (T-174’s regularizations), so the packing
keeps its finder’s credit and PingYou Ltd is credited with the certificate.
