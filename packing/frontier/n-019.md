# `s(19)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(19) ≤ 4.88561808`. The current first-party lower
bound is `s(19) ≥ 459/100 = 4.59` (`T-019`, `V4/C4`), above Nagamochi’s general
`1 + √12 ≈ 4.464102` and leaving a gap of `0.29561808316412`. The same frozen 1,184-atom
certificate used at `n = 17` proves this directly: its total mass is below 17, hence
below 19, and no other certificate condition depends on `n`.

## The packing

Wainwright’s diagonal strip of width two (1979), for which the survey states no
generating rule — only the figure.
`cases/lifted_q2` lifts the retained witness’s every coordinate into `Q(sqrt 2)` at
small height and verifies the lifted pose exactly, which is what moved
`verified_upper_bound` from the grid ceiling onto the published exact side.
The lift is a candidate generator and the exact verifier is the proof; the certificate
is the witness’s own geometry, made exact (`D-398`, and the operation `D-402` does not
foreclose).

## The lower bound

The operative bound is the first-party weighted certificate at side `459/100`. Its exact
event-cell and interval branch-and-bound decisions agree on the least covered mass.
Although generated at `n = 17`, its C1 mass is `423327/25000 < 17`; all other conditions
omit `n`, so it proves the `n = 19` statement without a monotonicity step.
Massaccesi’s externally proposed `4.5058` certificate remains a historical source
control. Nagamochi’s general closed form remains the external published baseline and
applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

Here it gives `1 + √12 ≈ 4.4641`, now weaker than the direct certificate bound.
