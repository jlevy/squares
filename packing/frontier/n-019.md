# `s(19)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(19) ≤ 4.88561808`. The verified lower bound is
`s(19) ≥ s(17) ≥ 22529/5000 = 4.5058`, inherited by monotonicity from the `n = 17`
Massaccesi certificate adopted 2026-09-03 (`[Burns–Massaccesi n17]`, externally
proposed, source-backed and replayed here, `T-016` at `V4/C3`), above Nagamochi’s
general `1 + √12 ≈ 4.464102`, leaving a gap of `0.3798` to the reported record.

## The packing

Wainwright’s diagonal strip of width two (1979), for which the survey states no
generating rule — only the figure.
`cases/lifted_q2` lifts the retained witness’s every coordinate into `Q(sqrt 2)` at
small height and verifies the lifted pose exactly, which is what moved
`verified_upper_bound` from the grid ceiling onto the published exact side.
The lift is a candidate generator and the exact verifier is the proof; the certificate
is the witness’s own geometry, made exact (`D-398`, and the operation `D-402` does not
foreclose).

## The lower bound

Nothing specific to this `n` has been proved.
The operative bound is inherited from `n = 17`: a packing of 19 unit squares contains a
packing of 17, so the adopted Massaccesi certificate — 168 rationally weighted atoms of
total mass `203/12 < 17` in `[0, 4.5058]²`, every closed unit square capturing mass at
least one — carries `s(19) ≥ 4.5058`. It is a 2026 blog-post result, not peer reviewed,
whose author marks the value “(?)”; it is replayed here by the retained source verifier
and by an accumulation-independent repository instrument (H-052) and audited lemma by
lemma (BC-150, BC-151), and no new first-party mathematics is claimed for it.
Nagamochi’s general closed form remains the external published baseline and applies to
every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

Here it gives `1 + √12 ≈ 4.4641`, now weaker than the inherited bound; this is the first
movement of this case’s verified lower bound since 2005.
