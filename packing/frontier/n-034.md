# `s(34)` — solved

$s(34) = 6$, from **Nagamochi’s** infinite family $s(m^2 - 1) = s(m^2 - 2) = m$ at
$m = 6$. **Corrected 3 October 2026:** the verified lower bound here is chelokot’s Lean
theorem $s(n^2 - 2) = n$, replayed here with its axiom receipt (T-086); Nagamochi’s
result is a reported bound, his Lemma 1 being false (Karakuş 2026;
[review](../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-10-02-nagamochi-lemma1-karakus.md)).
This register had recorded that proof as verified, its own error, logged as defect
[D-516](../../defects.md); this record’s prose was missed by the correction of 2 October
and is corrected here.

## The packing

Found by Hiroshi Nagamochi in 2005, via a hand construction.

## The lower bound

The strongest lower bound independently verified in this record is chelokot’s Lean
theorem $s(n^2 - 2) = n$ at $n = 6$ (T-086). Nagamochi’s general closed form,
$s(N) \ge \min(\lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil, \sqrt{N - 2\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor + 1} + 1)$, is
recorded as a reported bound: its published proof rests on his Lemma 1, which Karakuş
showed false.

See the [Frontier corpus summary](README.md#what-the-corpus-shows) for the current
aggregate count; source-reported bounds are recorded separately.
