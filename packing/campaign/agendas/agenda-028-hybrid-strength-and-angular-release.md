## First Checkpoint and Overnight Continuation

The first four active hours are a planning horizon, not a predicted solver runtime.

Use the available three workers for capacity design, structural design and independent
review, with the coordinator integrating evidence and arranging the shared interface.

before pricing a compute run on the same host.

There is currently **no ready machine-only queue** for these new questions.
The existing LP helper chooses branches from a feasible source pose and does not export
the required infeasibility dual.
Building and reviewing one real uniform leaf is the first enabling task.
The unpublished spike receipts are not a substitute for it.

Once a producer and independent reader pass controls, a longer run can process one
frozen exact-certificate queue while a separate adversary proposes packings in the same
declared family.
Keep independent angles in the adversary and include coordinated contact
releases; repeated angle merging would bias the structural test.
Every proposed packing requires rigorous independent verification.
Failed searches do not produce lower bounds or a normal-form theorem.

The run contract must name the target set, resource family, exact comparator, command,
per-attempt cap, stopping rule, retained partial state and replay allocation before
execution. Use existing campaign records and tools; no new scheduler is required by this
plan. A guard failure stops the affected target.
At the next checkpoint, reprice a changed representation prospectively rather than
retrying the unchanged one.

## What Earns More of the Night

Give the principal mathematical allocation to direct compatibility and complete
restricted-family domains.
Keep the release investigation smaller until its argument survives a meaningful new
domain. H-121 has high potential payoff but no defensible overnight completion estimate.

Expand H-118 when a recurring target pattern gains a checked exclusion or cheaper
replay. Open H-119 only when the residual identifies lost anchor correlation.
Expand H-120 when a closed domain beyond retained local scope falls to a reusable
argument. An all-multiplicity theorem stays with the existing family program.

BC-259’s independent support adoption is a short parallel closure task, not a gate on
direct geometry. H-107 stays deferred with its owner; unchanged scalar and density
attempts receive no allocation here.
Pair kernels, curved resources and expanded supports need a concrete candidate or
obstruction before an overnight target budget.

The morning record should make four facts easy to find: any change to the global
bracket, new proved continuous domains, the exact remaining obligations, and what
attention/compute each decision cost.
Instrument work and failed searches remain visible at their own scope.
This agenda has one efficiency commitment in seven; BC-275 uses existing retained costs
rather than building reporting machinery.
