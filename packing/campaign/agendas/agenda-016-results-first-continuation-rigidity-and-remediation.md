## Stop and Outcome Rules

A lane stops on frozen-input drift, a known-answer or mutation failure, a missing
independent verifier, an invalid theorem transfer, an illegal lifecycle transition,
three consecutive execution or persistence failures, or the research wall.
It records the first applicable W10 classification rather than choosing the most
flattering label: never-opened, guard-refused, technical-failure, time-limited, then
achieved, bounded-negative or inconclusive under the frozen criterion.

A partial H-052 prefix is time-limited process evidence.
A valid exact disagreement can reject only the implementation-agreement claim after
review; it does not refute the 4.5058 theorem.
A gap in H-060’s proof leaves H-060 unresolved.
Only a verified nonconstant feasible arc through the pose or an exact sequence of
distinct feasible poses converging to it can reject it.
A lone point at positive distance only refutes the proposed neighborhood.
A W9 repair is engineering work and never a scientific result.

Every terminal scope receives one disposition: retire as success, retire as a completed
bounded negative, continue from a preserved state, fix and rerun, or defer to a named
dependency. Continue, fix-and-rerun and defer always name a live follow-up bead.

## Time and Cost Accounting

The closeout reports four clocks without collapsing them:

- the exact 600-minute session wall from BC-147’s start marker to the research stop;
- per-lane wall intervals, which overlap and therefore do not add to session wall time;
- recursive agent-active and command time from retained session receipts; and
- separately labeled post-wall mechanical time, if validation or hosted checks finish
  after the research stop.

Codex task-tree telemetry is a coverage-limited activity measure, not the session wall.
A value such as 2.4 hours cannot replace a ten-hour start-to-stop record when agents,
commands or waits ran outside the measured task-tree interval.

limitation, and then reports results, stop reasons, dispositions and file changes.
A pending gate is work still in progress, not permission to call the agenda done.
