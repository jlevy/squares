## Three Blocks, Eight Hours in Total

Use one coordinator and at most three worker agents.
Match high or maximum reasoning effort to mathematical authorship and adversarial proof
review; routine source and record checks can use a lower effort.
The ceiling at full utilization is 32 aggregate agent-hours across eight elapsed hours,
not an expectation that every agent remains busy throughout.
Record actual effort separately from CPU time.

Each block includes orientation, mathematical work, necessary implementation, review,
repair and publication.
Up to 20 minutes of recovery fits **inside** its allocation; further repair competes
explicitly with remaining mathematics.
No phase silently resets a scientific deadline or adds a publication extension.
These are prospective allocations, not measurements or a runtime prediction.

### Block Two: Three Hours of Sustained Attention

to one principal route.

independent.

most 30 minutes through minute 140; use minutes 140–160 for independent review and
160–180 for integration and publication.

substantial composition exclusion, or a uniform frame-conditioned capture contradiction.

earned the allocation.

precise obstruction from block one.

### Block Three: Two Hours to Finish and Assess the Consequence

BC-290 fixes the scope at entry.
Spend the first hour, in two at-most-30-minute intervals, resolving named remaining
obligations; spend the second on independent verification, root-case accounting,
documentation and publication.
The theorem author cannot supply the only acceptance review.
An unresolved case remains visible even when every other branch closes.

The final handoff states the strongest theorem or counterexample, whether either global
bound changed, the exact surviving domain, what failed and why, and the next question
worth a substantial allocation.
Distinguish a scoped impossibility from a timeout, an unopened target and a broken
instrument. If work ends early because of tokens or an external limit, publish the same
fields at the current scope rather than marking unfinished mathematics complete.

The default is 3h + 3h + 2h. A later user-directed launch may choose two to four hours
per block prospectively.
