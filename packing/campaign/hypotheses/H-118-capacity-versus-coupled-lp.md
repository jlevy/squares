# H-118 — Capacity Versus a Coupled Outer LP

[X-018](../explorations/X-018-hybrid-strength-and-angular-release.md) maps the review’s
H-TBD-CAPACITY to this question; BC-270/271 own the comparison.
Acceptance of a frozen instance requires both an independently checked uniform capacity
exclusion and an exact feasible point of its declared LP relaxation.
All measure budgets and strict-interior or boundary-null premises must hold.

At fixed exact angles with every SAT alternative selected, the LP is exact.
Resource strength therefore concerns the declared outer relaxation, unresolved
disjunctions or integer capacities.
Faster proof of the same exclusion is a separate cost result.
The four-slot band at side 3.9 is a control, already resolved by ordering; it is not the
target comparison.

An exact obstruction can reject the frozen finite resource family.
A rigorously feasible packing in the target domain refutes its proposed exclusion.
Unsuccessful search, loose bounds and timeout leave the question unresolved.
Even an accepted comparison excludes only its domain, not all eleven-square packings.

Session092’s
[first domain analysis](../series/series-000-smoke-and-calibration/results/agenda-028/bc-270-capacity-comparison-design.md)
derives a complete three-band count split and exact feasible packings for all three
isolated four-square subsystems.
The central packing also admits a bottom anchor.
These are analytical obstructions to those domain exclusions, with no target search.
BC270 must derive necessary compatibility restrictions from the remaining squares before
it can admit a useful comparison.
H118 remains open.
