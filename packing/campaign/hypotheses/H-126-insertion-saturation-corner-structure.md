# H-126 — Corner Structure from Insertion Saturation

[X-019](../explorations/X-019-structural-restrictions-and-conditional-dots.md#a-global-starting-point-four-distinct-corner-blockers)
gives the elementary four-blocker deduction and the local corner-penetration identity.
The proposed research begins after those facts: find a restriction that changes what a
conditional certificate or residual-capacity argument can prove.

A positive triangle-penetration threshold must come with an argument forcing its
occupation or a useful theorem about the avoiding cases.
The four blockers alone do not force deep penetration or axis alignment.
Their chosen labels also impose no central-location restriction on the seven others.

A verified packing violating a proposed universal inequality at the stated side refutes
that inequality. A witness at a larger side, an infeasible optimization output, or
failure to find a counterexample does not decide it.
A counterexample to a stronger local implication can still remove a proof strategy, but
its scope must be stated separately.
If a target question concerns a minimizing representative, a high-angle feasible packing
alone is not a refutation of the existential claim.

The agenda may count an exact obstruction or useful partial theorem as progress while
this broad question remains open.
None is automatically a new global lower bound.
