# H-163: T-025 Fixed-Support Compression

H-163 codifies
[X-032’s fixed family](../explorations/X-032-route-s-threshold-compression.md).
The support universe is exactly T-025’s 79 point-atom and 40 two-of-three threshold-atom
D4 orbits.
Candidate weights may be zero but every coordinate, threshold triple, symmetry
image, domain parameter, and budget coefficient remains fixed.

**Status at registration: blocked on the no-target instrument.** This artifact creates
no experiment and carries no scientific result.
In particular, `exp-161` is unallocated; no optimizer, candidate certificate, or
coverage target has run.

The acceptance boundary is literal.
At most 23 orbit representatives may have strictly positive rational weight, the exact
budget must remain below eleven, and both existing coverage routes must agree that every
closed T-025 core has charge at least one.
A source-distinct reader must reconstruct the same candidate from its canonical
manifest.

An exact proof that the frozen constraints require at least 24 positive orbits refutes
the claim. Search exhaustion, timeout, a failed control, an invalid candidate, or
disagreement between coverage routes does not.
Those outcomes leave H-163 unresolved or the instrument refused and may park only this
support-reweighting family.

T-026 checks the lineage of the support and its documented common weight rescaling.
It does not change H-163’s domain and does not imply that a successful H-163 certificate
inherits T-026’s stronger lower bound.
