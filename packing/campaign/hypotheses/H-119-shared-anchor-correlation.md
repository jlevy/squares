# H-119 — Shared-Anchor Correlation

[X-018](../explorations/X-018-hybrid-strength-and-angular-release.md) maps the review’s
H-TBD-ANCHOR here. The desired certificate is
$\inf_{a\in A}[\alpha(a)+\sum_i w_i(a)-M]>0$ over the entire unsplit box.
Residual domains use the same actual anchor; a midpoint replacement is invalid.

The comparator needs evidence of its limitation.
Conservative capture lower bounds whose sum fails to exceed $M$ do not prove that
independent minimization loses useful information.
Freeze and independently verify both representations before interpreting a gain.
Each measure retains its own budget, and atom capture is strictly interior.

One exact finite-family obstruction can reject the selected comparison; a timeout
cannot. Success compares these representations only, leaving other measures, arbitrary
anchor subdivision and global exclusion open.
