# H-056 — Degree-Five `n = 39` Interval Certificate

The minimal-polynomial candidate identifies the side parameter, not the square
coordinates. The certificate therefore needs both a unique rational root enclosure and
rigorous pose boxes whose full geometric validity can be replayed independently.

## Selection Boundary

This record becomes measurable only when BC-111 selects `n = 39`. Selection of `n = 54`
leaves it registered and unmeasured for this run.

The accepted certificate must match the retained witness under the frozen compatibility
manifest. An unrelated packing at the same algebraic side does not satisfy this record.
