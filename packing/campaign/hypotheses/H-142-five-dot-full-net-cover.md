# H142: Five Dots on the Complete Four-Owner Net

Exp143 found five equal-weight atoms while optimizing over nine orientations.
This new hypothesis tests that fixed proposal across every omitted orientation.
No weights, positions, support, or owner branch are changed.

A positive result has a simple counting interpretation: every admissible residual core
contains at least one of five dots.
Pairwise separated cores cannot share a dot, so at most five residual cores fit, while
an eleven-square packing in this branch would need seven.
The endpoint containment and strict-core transfer are separate analytic premises,
retained in the five-dot review.

A negative result refutes this candidate; it does not refute the numerical gain or the
broader conditional-cover method.
