This ledger reads no lane’s claim record, and `sources.yaml` declares no lanes.

[21 October packet](../../../../resources/web/couzo-issue715-2026-10-21/README.md)
retains or pins by digest; #678’s, #686’s and #702’s from their packets’ beyond-horizon
claims records; and the watched-repository publications’ from the packet’s
`receipts/earlier-publications.json`. Issues 666, 670, 672 and 675 are read as their
issue tables print them, and each earlier report’s certificates, exact headers and
validation price remain with the import bead the ledger names.
