## Contraction margins and identity (contraction_margin, measure_identity)

contraction_margin refuses a certificate unless both routes accept it and every exact
wall and pair check holds; it is expected to accept T-305 (the house, packet
joegun-upper-bounds-2026-11-16), C1, C2 and C3, and both routes to accept each
contracted packing. measure_identity decides nothing about any bound. No expectation
is stated for the margins, the gains' ratios to them, or the identities: those are
measurements, and option D (think-b0w5) is applied to whatever they show.

Any control that comes out otherwise than stated here is recorded as such, on the bead,
in the packet README and in the PR, and the certificate it concerns is not registered
at any rung above V1 until the difference is explained.
