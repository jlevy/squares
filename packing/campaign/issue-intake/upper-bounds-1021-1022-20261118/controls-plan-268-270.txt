11 controls a count. At exact contact both routes accept with margin exactly 0, and 1e-30

  or positive otherwise (W1);
- tightest-pair-closed-past-contact: accepted at contact, refused past it (overlap), by 0
  if a pair touches exactly (P0) or the least gap otherwise (P1);
- squares-moved-past-near-wall-contact: accepted at contact, refused past it (container),
  by 0 if a square touches the left or bottom wall (N0) or by the least near-wall
  clearance otherwise (N1); no branch is expected over the other;
- square-enlarged-past-pair-contact: refused at contact for its shape alone, past it for
  shape and overlap;
- square-enlarged-past-wall-contact: a square at its wall grown by 0 accepted at contact
  and refused past it for wall and shape (E0), or refused at contact for shape alone and
  past it for shape and container (E1);
- duplicate-square: refused (overlap).

## Contraction margins and identity

contraction_margin refuses a certificate unless both routes accept it and every exact wall
and pair check holds; it is expected to accept each house and each certificate, and the
contracted packings. No expectation is stated for the margins or the identities; option D
(think-b0w5) is applied to whatever they show.

Any control that comes out otherwise than stated here is recorded on the bead, in the
packet README and in the PR, and the certificate is not registered above V1 until the
difference is explained.
