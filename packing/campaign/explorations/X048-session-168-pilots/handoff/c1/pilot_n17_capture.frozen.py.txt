"""Slice 3's capture pilot: the n17 endpoint's own occupancy state at a cap near `S*`.

The question is the capture-feasibility review's: does n11's ownership induction
contract the endpoint's occupancy state toward the H256 family, and how fast? Per round
(one update of every contracting owner) this tool measures what the kernel adaptation
spec's section 4.5 asks for, and it stops at a wall ceiling, at a round cap, at the
guard, or at a stall. It claims no capture: no guard is part of the checker's grammar,
and a node without closure excludes nothing.

The seed. The seventeen cells of the endpoint's state on the unique-state cover
`ring-3-voronoi-8-tabbed-unique`, read in the composition review's frame A: the cells in
the cover frame `[0, U]^2`, `U = 1169/250`, and the family embedded with its lower-left
corner at `(sigma, sigma)`, `sigma = (U - S*)/2`, which is how `cover.endpoint` places
it. The capture cap `U'` is the exp-238 root box's enclosure of
`S* = (6 + 4t)/(1 + 2t - t^2)` rounded up to the `10^-12` grid, so
`0 < U' - S* <= 10^-12`. The frame keeps the cells and sets `capture_cap = U'`, so every
wall clip is the centred box `[(U - U')/2 + h, (U + U')/2 - h]`, which holds every
packing of side at most `S*` placed with its corner at `(sigma, sigma)`.
`--cap exclusion` runs the same seed at `U` itself: the control that must not contract
below the first-order blob of feasible packings above `S*` (spec section 4.1).

Owners. Every cell of the state owns one square; square 6 (`side-S2`) is coarse: its
seed rows stay as they are, it is never updated, and it still serves as a collision
partner and its owned hull still forbids. The sliders are not premises: H-268's bounds
hold only for packings already within `r` of the family, so using them as root
halfplanes would be circular, and the sequential grammar refuses constraints anyway.
They are measured instead, in the guard's own coordinates.

The rounds. `sqpack.hull_kernel.producer` builds the seed and every row
(`produce_row`, with collision regions from other owners' complete pose covers and owned
hulls capped in replace mode, as `producer.produce` does). The one thing capture needs
that the producer lacks is angle refinement: before an owner's update, every live row is
bisected in the half-angle chart while the owner stays within `max_live` live rows (the
widest first when it would not), and no row narrower than `min_width`. Every child
cites its parent row, which is the refinement the sequential grammar already admits
(`sequential.complete_refinement`). The rest are producer choices, none new to the
grammar:

* `--core octagon` (the default since the second pilot) makes each row's core the
  intersection of its two end-angle squares, scaled by `1 - (hi - lo)^2/2` into every
  square between and passed through `strict_core`; its loss is second order in the row
  width where the envelope square's (`--core envelope`, the first pilot's) is first;
* `--hull-limit` caps owned hulls (48 by default, 16 in the first pilot, 0 for none),
  and each step records the area the cap dropped;
* `--seed-grid N` adds to the producer's seed points the proved hull vertices of a `1/N`
  grid search for owned points (`admit_seed` re-proves every one);
* a step offers a partner's cover only when its pose set comes within `3B/2` of the
  owner's domain in both coordinates, and names only the covers its collision regions
  use, since the checker re-proves every core of every cover it is given.

Certification. Each step is certified before the next is produced, by the sequential
checker's own functions in `replay_sequential`'s order (`complete_refinement`,
`admit_partner_covers`, `check_row` with the indexed cover, the kernel planes,
compression and `derived_closure`); the producer's next step starts from the checker's
accepted state, and the two states must agree exactly. With `--replay-share F` the whole
node is then replayed by `sequential.replay_sequential` from the seed admitted by
`node.admit_seed`, which is the certificate.

The endpoint control. After every certified update, the endpoint's exact pose (centre
and chart value as exp-238 root-box intervals; an axis square at `t = 0` or `t = 1`) must
lie in some live row of every owner: its chart interval inside the row's and every
corner of its centre box in one residual polygon. A loss is a soundness failure and
stops the run.

The sub-case. The cell seed stalls at the cell scale, so `--box RHO` measures `g` below
it: every owner but square 6 has its cell cut to its guard polygon of section 4.2 widened
by `RHO` (a box, long in the slide for squares 5, 11 and 13). That is a sub-case of the
state, what position splits would hand the engine, and nothing it measures is a capture
of the state.

The n11 control. `--system n11` runs the same producer on n11's case-438 state from its
sixteen-cell cover's cells, at n11's cap, with the trump11 optimum as the endpoint (the
pose-inclusion receipt's roles, the construction's centres carried into the source frame
by the inverse of its quarter-turn map). Every owner contracts and nothing slides. The
capture after-pilot review uses it to calibrate its pairwise model against the real
kernel: n11's record contracted at about `g = 0.84`, the model at `0.50`.

The measurements are floating-point summaries of exact objects, in unit coordinates: per
owner, the residual union's low and high sides relative to the endpoint in the target
coordinates of section 4.2 (`x, y` or, for squares 11 and 13, `u* . dc` and the slide
`v* . dc`; for square 5, `y` and the slide `a = x* - x`), their largest magnitude, the
two-sided extent, the live rows' signed and largest turn deviations in radians, and the
widest live row in radians over the owner's largest two-sided position extent. The
contraction factor `g` is the ratio to the previous round, on the two-sided extent and on
each side separately. The after-pilot review's falsifier is read every round: three
rounds in a row with every contracting owner's widest live row under a twentieth of its
position extent and no two-sided position extent down by a tenth means the architecture
is wrong; otherwise the run records the round positions start contracting, the ratio at
which they did, and `g` after it.
"""

@dataclass(frozen=True)
class Target:
    """One square of the endpoint: its owner cell, exact pose enclosure and turn."""

    label: int
    owner: int
    cell: str
    centre: tuple[Box, Box]
    charts: tuple[tuple[Q, Q], ...]
    turn: float

    @property
    def approx(self) -> tuple[float, float]:
        return float((self.centre[0].lo + self.centre[0].hi) / 2), float(
            (self.centre[1].lo + self.centre[1].hi) / 2
        )


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class Endpoint:
    """The endpoint's state, its side and the capture cap, with centres in field units.

    For n17 these come from the exp-238 root box, the scale is one, squares 5, 11 and 13
    slide and square 6 is coarse; for the n11 control (`load_n11_endpoint`) the scale is
    n11's `B`, nothing slides and every owner contracts.
    """

    targets: tuple[Target, ...]
    side: Box
    capture_cap: Q
    u: tuple[float, float]
    v: tuple[float, float]
    provenance: dict[str, Any]
    system: str = "n17"
    scale: Q = Q(1)
    slides: Mapping[int, tuple[str, Q, Q]] = field(default_factory=lambda: dict(SLIDES))
    coarse: int | None = SIX

    def by_owner(self) -> dict[int, Target]:
        return {target.owner: target for target in self.targets}


def chart_turn(t: float) -> float:
    """The turn `2 atan t`, in `[0, pi/2]`."""
    return 2 * math.atan(t)


def load_endpoint(frame: Frame) -> Endpoint:
    """The endpoint's cells, poses and `U'`, each label's cell checked against the frame."""
    t_box, b_box, provenance = cover.load_root_box(cover.CERTIFICATE)
    require(provenance["criterion_passed"], "the exp-238 root certificate did not pass")
    point = cover.endpoint(t_box, b_box)
    side = Box.point(cover.U) - point.shift * 2
    direct = (Box.point(6) + t_box * 4) / (Box.point(1) + t_box * 2 - t_box * t_box)
    require(direct.lo <= side.hi and side.lo <= direct.hi, "S* differs from the layout's side")
    upper = max(side.hi, direct.hi)
    capture_cap = Q(math.ceil(upper * CAP_GRID), CAP_GRID)
    require(0 < capture_cap - side.hi <= Q(1, CAP_GRID), "U' is not within 10^-12 of S*")
    state = slider_tool.endpoint_state(cover.UNIQUE_24.name)
    require(sorted(state) == list(range(1, 18)), "the endpoint state does not label 17 squares")
    t16 = (
        (1 - b_box.hi) / (1 + b_box.hi),
        (1 - b_box.lo) / (1 + b_box.lo),
    )
    targets: list[Target] = []
    for label in range(1, 18):
        name = state[label].name
        require(name in frame.cell_names, f"cell {name} is not in the frame")
        owner = frame.cell_names.index(name)
        require(
            list(frame.cells[owner]) == list(state[label].vertices),
            f"cell {name} differs from the cover tool's",
        )
        if label in THETA_LABELS:
            charts: tuple[tuple[Q, Q], ...] = ((t_box.lo, t_box.hi),)
        elif label == 16:
            charts = (t16,)
        else:
            charts = ((Q(0), Q(0)), (Q(1), Q(1)))
        turn = chart_turn(float((charts[0][0] + charts[0][1]) / 2)) % QUARTER
        x, y = point.centres[label - 1]
        targets.append(Target(label, owner, name, (x, y), charts, turn))
    require(len({target.owner for target in targets}) == 17, "two squares share an owner cell")
    c, s = float(point.aux["c"].lo), float(point.aux["s"].lo)
    vx, vy = (float((box.lo + box.hi) / 2) for box in point.v)
    require(abs(c * vx + s * vy) < 1e-12, "u* and v* are not orthogonal")
    return Endpoint(
        targets=tuple(targets),
        side=side,
        capture_cap=capture_cap,
        u=(c, s),
        v=(vx, vy),
        provenance={
            **provenance,
            "s_star": [str(side.lo), str(side.hi)],
            "s_star_decimal": f"{float(side.lo):.15f}",
            "sigma": f"{float(point.shift.lo):.15e}",
            "capture_cap": str(capture_cap),
            "capture_cap_minus_s_star": [
                f"{float(capture_cap - side.hi):.6e}",
                f"{float(capture_cap - side.lo):.6e}",
            ],
        },
    )

def load_n11_endpoint(frame: Frame) -> Endpoint:
    """n11's optimum in case 438's frame: the contraction control's endpoint.

    The labels' cells are the accepted pose-inclusion receipt's roles; the centres are the
    trump11 construction's, enclosed over its root interval, carried into the source frame
    by the inverse of the receipt's map `(x_f, y_f) -> (y_f/B - U/2, U/2 - x_f/B)`, which is
    a quarter turn and so keeps every chart value: labels 0 to 5 at `t = 0` (or 1), labels
    6 to 10 at the root `u`.
    """
    require(list(frame.representatives[N11_MASK_INDEX]) == pose_tool.MASK, "case 438's mask")
    receipt = json.loads(N11_POSE_RECEIPT.read_bytes())
    require(receipt.get("status") == "PASS_POSE_INCLUSION", "the pose-inclusion receipt")
    roles = {entry["label"]: entry["owner"] for entry in receipt["owners"]}
    require(sorted(roles) == list(range(11)), "the receipt's roles are not eleven labels")
    require(sorted(roles.values()) == pose_tool.MASK, "the roles are not case 438's cells")
    root_lo, root_hi, _ = pose_tool.root_interval()
    squares, side, _ = trump11.build()
    side_lo, side_hi = pose_tool.field_interval(side, root_lo, root_hi)
    require(0 < side_lo <= side_hi < frame.cap, "the construction does not fit the cap")
    scale, cap = frame.scale, frame.cap
    targets: list[Target] = []
    for label in range(11):
        cx, cy = (
            pose_tool.field_interval(
                (squares[label][0][k] + squares[label][2][k]) / 2 - side / 2, root_lo, root_hi
            )
            for k in (0, 1)
        )
        centre = (
            Box(scale * (cap / 2 - cy[1]), scale * (cap / 2 - cy[0])),
            Box(scale * (cap / 2 + cx[0]), scale * (cap / 2 + cx[1])),
        )
        charts: tuple[tuple[Q, Q], ...] = (
            ((Q(0), Q(0)), (Q(1), Q(1))) if label < 6 else ((root_lo, root_hi),)
        )
        turn = 0.0 if label < 6 else chart_turn(float((root_lo + root_hi) / 2))
        owner = roles[label]
        targets.append(Target(label, owner, frame.cell_names[owner], centre, charts, turn))
    return Endpoint(
        targets=tuple(targets),
        side=Box(side_lo, side_hi),
        capture_cap=cap,
        u=(1.0, 0.0),
        v=(0.0, 1.0),
        provenance={
            "pose_inclusion_receipt": str(N11_POSE_RECEIPT.relative_to(REPO)),
            "pose_inclusion_sha256": hashlib.sha256(N11_POSE_RECEIPT.read_bytes()).hexdigest(),
            "mask_index": N11_MASK_INDEX,
            "root_interval_width": f"{float(root_hi - root_lo):.3e}",
            "cap_minus_side": f"{float(cap - side_hi):.3e}",
        },
        system="n11",
        scale=scale,
        slides={},
        coarse=None,
    )


def octagon_core(frame: Frame, lo: Q, hi: Q) -> Polygon:
    """The row's two end-angle squares intersected, shrunk into every square between.

    A point inside the squares at turns `a` and `b` lies within `h / cos((b - a)/2)` of
    the centre along every face normal at a turn between, so the octagon scaled by
    `cos((b - a)/2) >= 1 - (hi - lo)^2/2` (the turn moves at most twice as fast as `t`)
    is inside every square of the row; the core slack makes it strict, and
    `strict_core` decides. The loss is second order in the row width, where the
    envelope square's is first order.
    """
    half = frame.scale / 2
    c0, s0 = trig(lo)
    c1, s1 = trig(hi)
    region: Polygon = [
        (c0 * u - s0 * v, s0 * u + c0 * v)
        for u, v in ((half, half), (-half, half), (-half, -half), (half, -half))
    ]
    region = intersect(
        region,
        [(c1, s1, half), (-c1, -s1, half), (-s1, c1, half), (s1, -c1, half)],
    )
    width = hi - lo
    factor = 1 - width * width / 2 - frame.core_slack / frame.scale
    for _ in range(16):
        core = hull([(factor * x, factor * y) for x, y in region])
        try:
            strict_core(frame, core, lo, hi)
        except RefusalError:
            factor *= 1 - max(width * width, Q(1, 2**20))
            continue
        return core
    raise RefusalError("no octagon core is strictly inside the row")


def fill_core(frame: Frame, lo: Q, hi: Q, cores: dict[tuple[Q, Q], Polygon], kind: str) -> None:
    """Cache the row's core where `producer.cached_core` will find it."""
    if (lo, hi) not in cores:
        cores[(lo, hi)] = (
            octagon_core(frame, lo, hi)
            if kind == "octagon"
            else producer.envelope_core(frame, lo, hi)
        )

def neighbourhood(target: Target, rho: Q, u: tuple[Q, Q]) -> Polygon:
    """The guard polygon of section 4.2 widened by `rho`: a box, or for squares 5, 11 and
    13 a box or parallelogram long in the slide; square 6 gets none (its whole cell)."""
    cx = Q(round((target.centre[0].lo + target.centre[0].hi) / 2 * BOX_GRID), BOX_GRID)
    cy = Q(round((target.centre[1].lo + target.centre[1].hi) / 2 * BOX_GRID), BOX_GRID)
    if target.label in (11, 13):
        _, low, high = SLIDES[target.label]
        if target.label == 11:  # b = -v.dc, so v.dc runs over [-high, -low]
            low, high = -high, -low
        vx, vy = -u[1], u[0]
        return hull(
            [
                (cx + a * u[0] + b * vx, cy + a * u[1] + b * vy)
                for a in (-rho, rho)
                for b in (low - rho, high + rho)
            ]
        )
    left = cx - SLIDES[5][2] - rho if target.label == 5 else cx - rho
    return hull([(x, y) for x in (left, cx + rho) for y in (cy - rho, cy + rho)])


def box_frame(base: Frame, endpoint: Endpoint, rho: Q) -> tuple[Frame, Endpoint]:
    """A sub-case of the state: each owner's cell cut to its widened guard polygon.

    What position splits would hand the engine: every packing of the state whose squares
    other than 6 lie in these polygons, at any turn. It is a sub-case, not the state, so
    nothing it measures is a capture of the state; it measures `g` below the cell scale.
    The frame has the identity as its only symmetry, and the owners are renumbered in
    cell order.
    """
    t_q = Q(round(Q(endpoint.provenance["t_box"][0]) * BOX_GRID), BOX_GRID)
    u = ((1 - t_q * t_q) / (1 + t_q * t_q), 2 * t_q / (1 + t_q * t_q))
    ordered = sorted(endpoint.targets, key=lambda item: item.owner)
    cells: list[Polygon] = []
    for target in ordered:
        cell = base.cell(target.owner)
        if target.label != SIX:
            cell = hull(intersect(cell, convex_halfplanes(neighbourhood(target, rho, u))))
        require(len(cell) >= 3, f"square {target.label}: its cut cell is empty")
        cells.append(cell)
    frame = make_frame(
        name=f"{base.name}-box-{rho}",
        cap=base.cap,
        length=base.length,
        cells=cells,
        cell_names=[target.cell for target in ordered],
        occupancy=len(ordered),
        action_names=("r0",),
        core_slack=base.core_slack,
        provenance=f"{base.provenance}; cells cut to the guard polygons widened by {rho}",
        capture_cap=base.capture_cap,
    )
    targets = tuple(replace(target, owner=index) for index, target in enumerate(ordered))
    return frame, replace(endpoint, targets=targets)

def in_convex(polygon: Polygon, point: Point) -> bool:
    """Closed membership in a convex polygon given in hull normal form (or a segment or
    a point)."""
    region = hull(polygon)
    if not region:
        return False
    if len(region) == 1:
        return region[0] == point
    if len(region) == 2:
        (ax, ay), (bx, by) = region
        cross = (bx - ax) * (point[1] - ay) - (by - ay) * (point[0] - ax)
        dot = (point[0] - ax) * (bx - ax) + (point[1] - ay) * (by - ay)
        return cross == 0 and 0 <= dot <= (bx - ax) ** 2 + (by - ay) ** 2
    return all(
        (b[0] - a[0]) * (point[1] - a[1]) - (b[1] - a[1]) * (point[0] - a[0]) >= 0
        for a, b in zip(region, region[1:] + region[:1], strict=True)
    )


def endpoint_holds(target: Target, rows: Sequence[Mapping[str, Any]]) -> dict[str, Any] | None:
    """The first live row holding the endpoint's exact pose, or None."""
    corners = [
        (x, y)
        for x in (target.centre[0].lo, target.centre[0].hi)
        for y in (target.centre[1].lo, target.centre[1].hi)
    ]
    for index, row in enumerate(rows):
        if not row["residual_polygons"]:
            continue
        lo, hi = (Q(value) for value in row["interval"])
        if not any(lo <= low and high <= hi for low, high in target.charts):
            continue
        for piece, polygon in enumerate(row["residual_polygons"]):
            vertices = node.points(polygon)
            if all(in_convex(vertices, corner) for corner in corners):
                return {"row": index, "interval": [str(lo), str(hi)], "piece": piece}
    return None

def turn_deviation(t: float, turn: float) -> float:
    """The distance from `2 atan t` to the endpoint's turn, modulo a quarter turn."""
    delta = (chart_turn(t) - turn) % QUARTER
    return min(delta, QUARTER - delta)


def row_turn_deviation(lo: float, hi: float, turn: float) -> float:
    """The largest turn deviation over a chart row (the farthest turn if it is inside)."""
    far = (turn + QUARTER / 2) % QUARTER
    if chart_turn(lo) <= far <= chart_turn(hi):
        return QUARTER / 2
    return max(turn_deviation(lo, turn), turn_deviation(hi, turn))


def coordinates(
    label: int, offset: tuple[float, float], endpoint: Endpoint
) -> dict[str, float]:
    """The target coordinates of a centre offset from the endpoint's (spec section 4.2)."""
    dx, dy = offset
    if endpoint.system != "n17":
        return {"x": dx, "y": dy}
    if label == 5:
        return {"y": dy, "a": -dx}
    if label in (11, 13):
        along = endpoint.u[0] * dx + endpoint.u[1] * dy
        slide = endpoint.v[0] * dx + endpoint.v[1] * dy
        return {"u": along, "b": -slide} if label == 11 else {"u": along, "z": slide}
    return {"x": dx, "y": dy}


def measure_owner(
    target: Target, rows: Sequence[Mapping[str, Any]], group: Polygon, endpoint: Endpoint
) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """Live rows, live width, the residual's deviations and the owned hull, per owner."""
    live = [row for row in rows if row["residual_polygons"]]
    cx, cy = target.approx
    unit = float(endpoint.scale)
    low: dict[str, float] = {}
    high: dict[str, float] = {}
    turn = 0.0
    turn_low = turn_high = 0.0
    widest = 0.0
    widest_turn = 0.0
    width = 0.0
    volume = 0.0
    slide = endpoint.slides.get(target.label)
    at_endpoint = 0.0
    for row in live:
        lo, hi = (float(Q(value)) for value in row["interval"])
        holds = any(
            Q(row["interval"][0]) <= low_t and high_t <= Q(row["interval"][1])
            for low_t, high_t in target.charts
        )
        width += hi - lo
        widest = max(widest, hi - lo)
        widest_turn = max(widest_turn, chart_turn(hi) - chart_turn(lo))
        volume += (hi - lo) * sum(
            float(area2(node.points(polygon))) / 2 / unit**2
            for polygon in row["residual_polygons"]
        )
        turn = max(turn, row_turn_deviation(lo, hi, target.turn))
        for end in (lo, hi):
            signed = signed_turn(end, target.turn)
            turn_low, turn_high = min(turn_low, signed), max(turn_high, signed)
        for polygon in row["residual_polygons"]:
            for x, y in polygon:
                for key, value in coordinates(
                    target.label,
                    ((float(Q(x)) - cx) / unit, (float(Q(y)) - cy) / unit),
                    endpoint,
                ).items():
                    low[key] = min(low.get(key, value), value)
                    high[key] = max(high.get(key, value), value)
                    if holds and (slide is None or key != slide[0]):
                        at_endpoint = max(at_endpoint, abs(value))
    deviation = {key: max(abs(low[key]), abs(high[key])) for key in low}
    position = [value for key, value in deviation.items() if slide is None or key != slide[0]]
    extent = {key: high[key] - low[key] for key in low if slide is None or key != slide[0]}
    position_extent = max(extent.values(), default=math.inf)
    inside = bool(live) and turn <= float(RADIUS)
    inside = inside and all(value <= float(RADIUS) for value in position)
    if slide is not None and slide[0] in low:
        inside = (
            inside and float(slide[1]) <= low[slide[0]] and high[slide[0]] <= float(slide[2])
        )
    return {
        "label": target.label,
        "cell": target.cell,
        "rows": len(rows),
        "live_rows": len(live),
        "live_width": width,
        "widest_live_row": widest,
        "widest_live_row_turn": widest_turn,
        "turn_range": [turn_low, turn_high],
        "extent": extent,
        "position_extent": position_extent,
        "row_to_extent": widest_turn / position_extent if position_extent > 0 else math.inf,
        "residual_volume": volume,
        "deviation": deviation,
        "range": {key: [low[key], high[key]] for key in low},
        "turn_deviation": turn,
        "position_deviation": max(position, default=math.inf),
        "endpoint_row_position_deviation": at_endpoint,
        "inside_guard": inside,
        "owned_hull_vertices": len(group),
        "owned_hull_area": float(sum_area(group)),
    }


def signed_turn(t: float, turn: float) -> float:
    """`2 atan t` minus the endpoint's turn, reduced into `(-pi/4, pi/4]`."""
    delta = (chart_turn(t) - turn) % QUARTER
    return delta if delta <= QUARTER / 2 else delta - QUARTER


def sum_area(group: Polygon) -> Q:
    if len(group) < 3:
        return Q(0)
    return (
        abs(
            sum(
                (
                    p[0] * q[1] - p[1] * q[0]
                    for p, q in zip(group, group[1:] + group[:1], strict=True)
                ),
                Q(),
            )
        )
        / 2
    )


def round_summary(
    index: int,
    owners: Mapping[int, dict[str, Any]],
    previous: Mapping[int, dict[str, Any]] | None,
    contracting: Sequence[int],
) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """Per-owner extents and g, and the worst half-extent per coordinate class."""
    ratios: dict[str, dict[str, float | None]] = {}
    for owner in contracting:
        current = owners[owner]
        before = previous[owner] if previous is not None else None
        entry: dict[str, float | None] = {}
        for key, value in [*current["deviation"].items(), ("turn", current["turn_deviation"])]:
            if before is None:
                entry[key] = None
                continue
            old = before["turn_deviation"] if key == "turn" else before["deviation"].get(key)
            entry[key] = value / old if old else None
        ratios[current["cell"]] = entry
    two_sided: dict[str, dict[str, float | None]] = {}
    sides: dict[str, dict[str, list[float | None]]] = {}
    for owner in contracting:
        current = owners[owner]
        before = previous[owner] if previous is not None else None
        two_sided[current["cell"]] = {
            key: (
                value / before["extent"][key] if before and before["extent"].get(key) else None
            )
            for key, value in current["extent"].items()
        }
        sides[current["cell"]] = {
            key: [
                (
                    abs(current["range"][key][side]) / abs(before["range"][key][side])
                    if before and before["range"][key][side]
                    else None
                )
                for side in (0, 1)
            ]
            for key in current["range"]
        }
    ratios_now = [owners[owner]["row_to_extent"] for owner in contracting]
    falls = [value for row in two_sided.values() for value in row.values() if value is not None]
    worst_extent = max(owners[owner]["position_extent"] for owner in contracting)
    position = max(owners[owner]["position_deviation"] for owner in contracting)
    turn = max(owners[owner]["turn_deviation"] for owner in contracting)
    return {
        "round": index,
        "worst_position_extent": worst_extent,
        "g_extent": two_sided,
        "g_sides": sides,
        "min_g_extent": min(falls, default=None),
        "row_to_extent": {owners[o]["cell"]: owners[o]["row_to_extent"] for o in contracting},
        "max_row_to_extent": max(ratios_now),
        "median_row_to_extent": sorted(ratios_now)[len(ratios_now) // 2],
        "rows_fine": all(value < FINE_ROWS for value in ratios_now),
        "rows_past_model_threshold": all(value < MODEL_THRESHOLD for value in ratios_now),
        "no_extent_drop": previous is not None and all(value >= EXTENT_DROP for value in falls),
        "extent": {owners[o]["cell"]: owners[o]["extent"] for o in owners},
        "turn_range": {owners[o]["cell"]: owners[o]["turn_range"] for o in owners},
        "worst_position": position,
        "worst_endpoint_row_position": max(
            owners[owner]["endpoint_row_position_deviation"] for owner in contracting
        ),
        "endpoint_row_position": {
            owners[o]["cell"]: owners[o]["endpoint_row_position_deviation"] for o in owners
        },
        "worst_turn": turn,
        "worst": max(position, turn),
        "owners_inside_guard": sum(1 for owner in contracting if owners[owner]["inside_guard"]),
        "live_rows": {owners[o]["cell"]: owners[o]["live_rows"] for o in owners},
        "live_width": {owners[o]["cell"]: owners[o]["live_width"] for o in owners},
        "residual_volume": {owners[o]["cell"]: owners[o]["residual_volume"] for o in owners},
        "total_volume": sum(owners[owner]["residual_volume"] for owner in contracting),
        "widest_live_row": {owners[o]["cell"]: owners[o]["widest_live_row"] for o in owners},
        "deviation": {
            owners[o]["cell"]: {**owners[o]["deviation"], "turn": owners[o]["turn_deviation"]}
            for o in owners
        },
        "range": {owners[o]["cell"]: owners[o]["range"] for o in owners},
        "g": ratios,
        "owned_hull_vertices": {
            owners[o]["cell"]: owners[o]["owned_hull_vertices"] for o in owners
        },
        "owned_hull_area": {owners[o]["cell"]: owners[o]["owned_hull_area"] for o in owners},
    }

def refine(
    rows: Sequence[Mapping[str, Any]], *, max_live: int, min_width: Q
) -> tuple[list[tuple[Q, Q, Mapping[str, Any]]], int]:
    """Bisect the widest live rows while the owner has fewer than `max_live` live rows.

    Each split adds one live row, so at most `max_live - live` rows split; a dead row and
    a row at `min_width` never split. Children keep their parent as predecessor.
    """
    live = [index for index, row in enumerate(rows) if row["residual_polygons"]]

    def width(index: int) -> Q:
        lo, hi = (Q(value) for value in rows[index]["interval"])
        return hi - lo

    candidates = sorted(
        (index for index in live if width(index) / 2 >= min_width),
        key=lambda index: (-width(index), index),
    )
    chosen = set(candidates[: max(0, max_live - len(live))])
    out: list[tuple[Q, Q, Mapping[str, Any]]] = []
    for index, row in enumerate(rows):
        lo, hi = (Q(value) for value in row["interval"])
        if index in chosen:
            mid = (lo + hi) / 2
            out.extend(((lo, mid, row), (mid, hi, row)))
        else:
            out.append((lo, hi, row))
    return out, len(chosen)


def float_clip(
    polygon: list[tuple[float, float]], a: float, b: float, c: float
) -> list[tuple[float, float]]:
    """`a x + b y <= c` clipped in floating point; a filter, never a proof."""
    out: list[tuple[float, float]] = []
    for p, q in itertools.pairwise([*polygon, *polygon[:1]]):
        dp, dq = a * p[0] + b * p[1] - c, a * q[0] + b * q[1] - c
        if dp <= 0:
            out.append(p)
        if dp * dq < 0:
            z = dp / (dp - dq)
            out.append((p[0] + z * (q[0] - p[0]), p[1] + z * (q[1] - p[1])))
    return out


def float_owned(cell: Polygon, point: tuple[float, float], frame: Frame, samples: int) -> bool:
    """Whether a point looks owned: strictly inside the square at sampled turns for every
    legal centre (the cell's vertices clipped to the centred wall box). A filter only;
    `ownership` proves each kept point exactly."""
    low_cap = float((frame.cap - frame.inner_cap) / 2)
    high_cap = float(frame.cap) - low_cap
    for k in range(samples + 1):
        theta = QUARTER * k / samples
        c, s = math.cos(theta), math.sin(theta)
        h = (c + s) / 2
        region = [(float(x), float(y)) for x, y in cell]
        for a, b, bound in (
            (1.0, 0.0, high_cap - h),
            (-1.0, 0.0, -(low_cap + h)),
            (0.0, 1.0, high_cap - h),
            (0.0, -1.0, -(low_cap + h)),
        ):
            region = float_clip(region, a, b, bound)
        for x, y in region:
            dx, dy = point[0] - x, point[1] - y
            if abs(c * dx + s * dy) >= 0.49 or abs(-s * dx + c * dy) >= 0.49:
                return False
    return True


def grid_seed_points(
    frame: Frame, owner: int, *, spacing: int, budget: Budget, limit: int = 16
) -> list[Point]:
    """Hull vertices of the owned grid points of spacing `1/spacing`, each proved owned.

    The owned set of a cell is convex (an intersection of open squares), so its hull's
    vertices carry it; candidates are filtered in floating point and the hull's vertices
    are then proved by `ownership`, a failing vertex dropped and the hull re-formed.
    """
    cell = frame.cell(owner)
    xs = [x for x, _ in cell]
    ys = [y for _, y in cell]
    candidates: list[Point] = []
    x = Q(math.floor(min(xs) * spacing), spacing)
    while x <= max(xs):
        y = Q(math.floor(min(ys) * spacing), spacing)
        while y <= max(ys):
            if float_owned(cell, (float(x), float(y)), frame, 64):
                candidates.append((x * frame.scale, y * frame.scale))
            y += Q(1, spacing)
        x += Q(1, spacing)
    proved: set[Point] = set()
    while candidates:
        region = hull(candidates)
        keep = [region[index] for index in producer.bounded_vertices(region, limit)]
        failed = False
        for point in keep:
            if point in proved:
                continue
            try:
                ownership(frame, owner, point, budget=Budget(budget.deadline, 4000))
            except IncompleteError, RefusalError:
                candidates.remove(point)
                failed = True
                break
            proved.add(point)
        if not failed:
            return keep
    return []


def build_seed(
    frame: Frame, mask: Sequence[int], *, bins: int, seed_grid: int, budget: Budget
) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """`producer.build_seed`, with grid-searched owned points added to its own."""
    seed = producer.build_seed(frame, mask, bins=bins, budget=budget)
    if seed_grid > 0:
        for owner in mask:
            found = grid_seed_points(frame, owner, spacing=seed_grid, budget=budget)
            given = node.points(seed["groups"][str(owner)])
            merged = hull(given + found)
            seed["groups"][str(owner)] = [[str(x), str(y)] for x, y in merged]
    return seed


def produce_step(
    frame: Frame,
    *,
    node_id: str,
    step_index: int,
    owner: int,
    mask: Sequence[int],
    groups: dict[int, Polygon],
    rows: dict[int, list[dict[str, Any]]],
    cores: dict[tuple[Q, Q], Polygon],
    max_live: int,
    min_width: Q,
    hull_limit: int | None,
    core_kind: str = "octagon",
) -> tuple[dict[str, Any], dict[str, Any], list[dict[str, Any]]]:
    """One complete step for `owner`, as `producer.produce` builds it, with refinement.

    Every row's core is the `core_kind` core, cached where `produce_row` and
    `partner_cover` read it. Partner covers are offered only for owners whose pose set
    comes within `3B/2` of this owner's domain in both coordinates, since two squares
    of side `B` whose centres differ by `sqrt(2) B` or more in a coordinate cannot
    overlap; of those, only the covers some collision region names are kept in the
    step, since the checker re-proves every core it admits.
    """
    reach = 3 * frame.scale / 2
    mine = bounding_box(rows[owner], "outer_domain")
    covers: dict[str, list[dict[str, Any]]] = {}
    partners: dict[int, list[producer.PartnerRow]] = {}
    for other in mask:
        if other == owner:
            continue
        theirs = bounding_box(rows[other], "residual_polygons")
        if mine is None or theirs is None or separated(mine, theirs, reach):
            continue
        for row in rows[other]:
            if row["residual_polygons"]:
                lo, hi = (Q(value) for value in row["interval"])
                fill_core(frame, lo, hi, cores, core_kind)
        given, live = producer.partner_cover(frame, rows[other], cores)
        if live:
            covers[str(other)], partners[other] = given, live
    children, splits = refine(rows[owner], max_live=max_live, min_width=min_width)
    for lo, hi, parent in children:
        if parent["outer_domain"]:
            fill_core(frame, lo, hi, cores, core_kind)
    produced = []
    for index, (lo, hi, parent) in enumerate(children):
        view = {**parent, "interval": [str(lo), str(hi)]}
        produced.append(
            producer.produce_row(
                frame,
                node_id=node_id,
                step_index=step_index,
                row_index=index,
                owner=owner,
                predecessor=view,
                groups=groups,
                partners=partners,
                cores=cores,
            )
        )
    planes = [plane for _, _, row_planes in produced for plane in row_planes]
    kernel = producer.kernel_points(frame, planes)
    named = {item["partner"] for row, _, _ in produced for item in row["collision_regions"]}
    offered = sum(len(live) for live in partners.values())
    offered_partners = len(partners)
    covers = {key: value for key, value in covers.items() if int(key) in named}
    partners = {key: value for key, value in partners.items() if key in named}
    step: dict[str, Any] = {
        "index": step_index,
        "owner": owner,
        "allowed_half_angle": ["0", "1"],
        "prior_partner_pose_covers": covers,
        "prior_owned_hulls": {str(other): encode(groups[other]) for other in mask},
        "rows": [row for row, _, _ in produced],
        "complete": True,
        "common_owned_kernel": [[str(x), str(y)] for x, y in kernel],
    }
    hull_lost = 0.0
    if any(accepted["residual_polygons"] for _, accepted, _ in produced) and (
        groups[owner] or kernel
    ):
        original = hull(groups[owner] + kernel)
        new_points, witnesses = producer.compress(original)
        receipt: dict[str, Any] = {
            "vertices": [[str(x), str(y)] for x, y in new_points],
            "witnesses": witnesses,
            "denominator": producer.GRID,
            "original_vertices": len(original),
            "retained_vertices": len(new_points),
        }
        if hull_limit is not None and new_points == original:
            keep = producer.bounded_vertices(original, hull_limit)
            new_points = [original[index] for index in keep]
            lost = area2(original) - area2(hull(new_points))
            hull_lost = float(lost / 2 / frame.scale**2)
            receipt.update(
                vertices=[[str(x), str(y)] for x, y in new_points],
                witnesses=[witnesses[index] for index in keep],
                retained_vertices=len(new_points),
                mode="replace",
            )
        step["compression_source_hull"] = encode(original)
        step["inner_grid_compression"] = receipt
    stats = {
        "rows": len(children),
        "splits": splits,
        "partner_rows_offered": offered,
        "partner_rows": sum(len(live) for live in partners.values()),
        "collision_regions": sum(len(row["collision_regions"]) for row, _, _ in produced),
        "promoted_points": len(kernel),
        "hull_area_lost": hull_lost,
        "partners_offered": offered_partners,
    }
    return step, stats, [accepted for _, accepted, _ in produced]


def bounding_box(rows: Sequence[Mapping[str, Any]], key: str) -> tuple[Q, Q, Q, Q] | None:
    """The box of every vertex of the rows' outer domains or residual polygons."""
    points_: list[tuple[Q, Q]] = []
    for row in rows:
        polygons = [row[key]] if key == "outer_domain" else row[key]
        for polygon in polygons:
            points_.extend((Q(x), Q(y)) for x, y in polygon)
    if not points_:
        return None
    xs = [x for x, _ in points_]
    ys = [y for _, y in points_]
    return min(xs), max(xs), min(ys), max(ys)


def separated(first: tuple[Q, Q, Q, Q], second: tuple[Q, Q, Q, Q], reach: Q) -> bool:
    """Whether the boxes are at least `reach` apart along some axis."""
    return (
        second[0] - first[1] >= reach
        or first[0] - second[1] >= reach
        or second[2] - first[3] >= reach
        or first[2] - second[3] >= reach
    )


def agree(
    predicted: Sequence[Mapping[str, Any]], accepted: Sequence[Mapping[str, Any]]
) -> bool:
    """The producer's rows are the checker's accepted rows, interval and residual."""
    return len(predicted) == len(accepted) and all(
        [Q(value) for value in mine["interval"]] == [Q(value) for value in theirs["interval"]]
        and len(mine["residual_polygons"]) == len(theirs["residual_polygons"])
        and all(
            same(node.points(left), node.points(right))
            for left, right in zip(
                mine["residual_polygons"], theirs["residual_polygons"], strict=True
            )
        )
        for mine, theirs in zip(predicted, accepted, strict=True)
    )


def certify_step(
    frame: Frame,
    step: Mapping[str, Any],
    groups: dict[int, Polygon],
    rows: dict[int, list[dict[str, Any]]],
    *,
    node_id: str,
    mask: Sequence[int],
    budget: Budget,
) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """The body of `sequential.replay_sequential`'s loop on one step, in its order.

    Updates `groups` and `rows` to the checker's accepted state and returns the step's
    checker counts and derived closure.
    """
    owner = step["owner"]
    require(
        step["allowed_half_angle"] == ["0", "1"] and step["complete"] is True,
        "unsupported angle or incomplete step",
    )
    require(
        set(step["prior_owned_hulls"]) == set(map(str, mask))
        and all(same(node.points(step["prior_owned_hulls"][str(i)]), groups[i]) for i in mask),
        "step previous accepted state",
    )
    predecessors = sequential.complete_refinement(
        step["rows"], rows[owner], max_rows=budget.max_nodes
    )
    partners = sequential.admit_partner_covers(frame, step, rows, mask=mask)
    source = {"node_id": node_id}
    results = [
        sequential.check_row(
            frame,
            source,
            step,
            row_index,
            prior=groups,
            predecessor=predecessor,
            budget=budget,
            cover="indexed",
            partners=partners,
        )
        for row_index, predecessor in enumerate(predecessors)
    ]
    all_vertices = [point for result in results for point in result[1]]
    all_planes = [plane for result in results for plane in result[2]]
    kernel = node.points(step["common_owned_kernel"])
    for point in kernel:
        require(
            all(0 <= coordinate <= frame.length for coordinate in point)
            and all(nx * point[0] + ny * point[1] <= upper for nx, ny, upper in all_planes),
            "promoted point lacks full-row ownership proof",
        )
    if all_vertices and (groups[owner] or kernel):
        groups[owner] = (
            hull(node.compression_points(step, groups[owner], kernel))
            if step["inner_grid_compression"].get("mode") == "replace"
            else node.compressed(step, groups[owner], kernel)
        )
    else:
        require("inner_grid_compression" not in step, "nothing to promote, yet compression")
    rows[owner] = [result[3] for result in results]
    return {
        "checked_rows": len(results),
        "events": sum(result[0]["events"] for result in results),
        "probes": sum(result[0]["probes"] for result in results),
        "collision_checks": sum(result[0].get("collision_checks", 0) for result in results),
        "admitted_partner_rows": sum(len(live) for live in partners.values()),
        "closure": sequential.derived_closure(owner, step["index"], groups, rows),
    }

@dataclass
class Pilot:
    seed: dict[str, Any]
    node: dict[str, Any]
    rounds: list[dict[str, Any]] = field(default_factory=list)
    updates: list[dict[str, Any]] = field(default_factory=list)
    outcome: str = "round_cap"
    endpoint_lost: dict[str, Any] | None = None
    closure: dict[str, Any] | None = None
    refusal: dict[str, Any] | None = None


def order(frame: Frame, mask: Sequence[int], coarse: int | None) -> list[int]:
    """Contracting owners: corners, then side cells, then interior cells, in cell order
    (n11's numbered cells keep cell order)."""
    rank = {"corner": 0, "side": 1, "interior": 2}
    return sorted(
        (owner for owner in mask if owner != coarse),
        key=lambda owner: (rank.get(frame.cell_names[owner].split("-")[0], 0), owner),
    )


def run_pilot(
    frame: Frame,
    endpoint: Endpoint,
    *,
    bins: int,
    max_rounds: int,
    max_live: int,
    min_width: Q,
    hull_limit: int | None,
    max_seconds: float,
    seed_grid: int = 0,
    max_steps: int | None = None,
    core_kind: str = "octagon",
    rounds_after_start: int | None = None,
    node_id: str = "n17-capture-pilot",
    progress: bool = True,
    partial: Path | None = None,
) -> Pilot:
    """Seed, then rounds of certified owner updates with the endpoint control after each.

    Besides the wall ceiling, the round cap, the guard and a stall, a run stops when the
    review's falsifier is met (three rounds in a row with every owner's widest live row
    under a twentieth of its position extent and no two-sided extent down by a tenth), or
    `rounds_after_start` rounds after positions start contracting (the worst owner's
    two-sided position extent down by a tenth in one round).
    """
    started = time.monotonic()
    budget = Budget(started + max_seconds, MAX_EVENTS)
    targets = endpoint.by_owner()
    mask = sorted(targets)
    coarse = next(
        (target.owner for target in endpoint.targets if target.label == endpoint.coarse), None
    )
    contracting = order(frame, mask, coarse)
    seed = build_seed(frame, mask, bins=bins, seed_grid=seed_grid, budget=budget)
    admitted = node.admit_seed(
        frame, seed, mask=mask, bins=bins, budget=budget, allow_empty_groups=True
    )
    groups = dict(admitted.groups)
    rows: dict[int, list[dict[str, Any]]] = {
        owner: list(admitted.rows[owner]) for owner in mask
    }
    initial = {
        "groups": {str(owner): encode(groups[owner]) for owner in mask},
        "cell_references": {
            str(owner): [row["reference"] for row in rows[owner]] for owner in mask
        },
    }
    pilot = Pilot(seed, {})
    cores: dict[tuple[Q, Q], Polygon] = {}
    steps: list[dict[str, Any]] = []

    def control(after: str) -> bool:
        for owner in mask:
            if endpoint_holds(targets[owner], rows[owner]) is None:
                pilot.endpoint_lost = {
                    "after": after,
                    "owner": owner,
                    "cell": frame.cell_names[owner],
                    "label": targets[owner].label,
                }
                return False
        return True

    def snapshot() -> dict[int, dict[str, Any]]:
        return {
            owner: measure_owner(targets[owner], rows[owner], groups[owner], endpoint)
            for owner in mask
        }

    if not control("seed"):
        pilot.outcome = "endpoint_lost"
    measured = snapshot()
    pilot.rounds.append(
        {
            **round_summary(0, measured, None, contracting),
            "steps": 0,
            "seconds": time.monotonic() - started,
        }
    )
    previous = measured
    stalled = 0
    fine_rounds = 0
    for round_index in range(1, max_rounds + 1):
        if pilot.outcome == "endpoint_lost":
            break
        round_splits = 0
        for owner in contracting:
            if time.monotonic() >= budget.deadline:
                pilot.outcome = "time_cap"
                break
            if max_steps is not None and len(steps) >= max_steps:
                pilot.outcome = "step_cap"
                break
            step_index = len(steps)
            wall, cpu = time.monotonic(), time.process_time()
            step, stats, predicted = produce_step(
                frame,
                node_id=node_id,
                step_index=step_index,
                owner=owner,
                mask=mask,
                groups=groups,
                rows=rows,
                cores=cores,
                max_live=max_live,
                min_width=min_width,
                hull_limit=hull_limit,
                core_kind=core_kind,
            )
            produced_wall, produced_cpu = time.monotonic(), time.process_time()
            try:
                checked = certify_step(
                    frame, step, groups, rows, node_id=node_id, mask=mask, budget=budget
                )
            except IncompleteError:
                pilot.outcome = "time_cap"
                break
            except RefusalError as error:
                pilot.outcome = "step_refused"
                pilot.refusal = {"step": step_index, "reason": str(error)}
                break
            if not agree(predicted, rows[owner]):
                pilot.outcome = "step_refused"
                pilot.refusal = {"step": step_index, "reason": "producer and checker differ"}
                break
            steps.append(step)
            round_splits += stats["splits"]
            update = {
                "round": round_index,
                "step": step_index,
                "cell": frame.cell_names[owner],
                "label": targets[owner].label,
                **stats,
                **{key: value for key, value in checked.items() if key != "closure"},
                "live_rows": sum(1 for row in rows[owner] if row["residual_polygons"]),
                "owned_hull_vertices": len(groups[owner]),
                "producer_wall": produced_wall - wall,
                "producer_cpu": produced_cpu - cpu,
                "checker_wall": time.monotonic() - produced_wall,
                "checker_cpu": time.process_time() - produced_cpu,
            }
            pilot.updates.append(update)
            if progress:
                print(
                    json.dumps(
                        {
                            key: update[key]
                            for key in ("round", "step", "cell", "rows", "live_rows", "splits")
                        }
                        | {
                            "producer": round(update["producer_wall"], 1),
                            "checker": round(update["checker_wall"], 1),
                            "seconds": round(time.monotonic() - started, 1),
                        }
                    ),
                    file=sys.stderr,
                    flush=True,
                )
            if not control(f"step {step_index}"):
                pilot.outcome = "endpoint_lost"
                break
            if checked["closure"] is not None:
                pilot.closure = checked["closure"]
                pilot.outcome = "closed"
                break
        measured = snapshot()
        summary = round_summary(round_index, measured, previous, contracting)
        complete = pilot.outcome not in (
            "time_cap",
            "step_cap",
            "step_refused",
            "endpoint_lost",
            "closed",
        )
        pilot.rounds.append(
            {
                **summary,
                "steps": len(steps),
                "complete": complete,
                "splits": round_splits,
                "seconds": time.monotonic() - started,
            }
        )
        if partial is not None:
            write_partial(partial, pilot)
        if not complete:
            break
        if summary["owners_inside_guard"] == len(contracting):
            pilot.outcome = "guard"
            break
        if progress:
            print(
                json.dumps(
                    {
                        "round": round_index,
                        "worst": round(summary["worst"], 6),
                        "extent": f"{summary['worst_position_extent']:.4e}",
                        "min_g_extent": summary["min_g_extent"],
                        "row_to_extent": round(summary["max_row_to_extent"], 4),
                        "volume": f"{summary['total_volume']:.4e}",
                        "live": sum(summary["live_rows"].values()),
                        "splits": round_splits,
                    }
                ),
                file=sys.stderr,
                flush=True,
            )
        fine_rounds = (
            fine_rounds + 1 if summary["rows_fine"] and summary["no_extent_drop"] else 0
        )
        if fine_rounds >= FALSIFIER_ROUNDS:
            pilot.outcome = "falsified"
            break
        start = contraction_start(pilot.rounds)
        if (
            rounds_after_start is not None
            and start is not None
            and round_index - start["round"] >= rounds_after_start
        ):
            pilot.outcome = "measured_after_start"
            break
        improved = any(
            measured[owner]["position_extent"] < previous[owner]["position_extent"]
            or measured[owner]["residual_volume"] < previous[owner]["residual_volume"]
            or measured[owner]["turn_deviation"] < previous[owner]["turn_deviation"]
            or measured[owner]["live_width"] < previous[owner]["live_width"]
            or any(
                value < previous[owner]["deviation"].get(key, math.inf)
                for key, value in measured[owner]["deviation"].items()
            )
            for owner in contracting
        )
        stalled = 0 if improved else stalled + 1
        previous = measured
        if stalled >= (1 if round_splits == 0 else 2):
            pilot.outcome = "stalled"
            break
    source = {"sha256": producer.content_sha256(seed)}
    pilot.node = {
        "schema": "exact_generic_owned_hull_v1",
        "node_id": node_id,
        "parent": None,
        "constraints": [],
        "guard_source": None,
        "mask_index": None,
        "mask": list(mask),
        "U": str(frame.cap),
        "B": str(frame.scale),
        "source": source,
        "initial": initial,
        "steps": steps,
        "final_state": {
            "mask_index": None,
            "mask": list(mask),
            "U": str(frame.cap),
            "B": str(frame.scale),
            "constraints": [],
            "guard": {},
            "guard_source": None,
            "source": source,
            "world": seed["world"],
            "groups": {str(owner): encode(groups[owner]) for owner in mask},
            "cells": {str(owner): rows[owner] for owner in mask},
        },
        "contradiction": pilot.closure,
        "closed": pilot.closure is not None,
        "terminal": pilot.closure is not None,
        "mask_exclusion_proved": False,
        "global_optimality_proved": False,
    }
    return pilot


def replay(frame: Frame, pilot: Pilot, *, max_seconds: float) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """The certificate: `admit_seed` and `replay_sequential` on the whole node."""
    started = time.monotonic()
    mask = pilot.node["mask"]
    budget = Budget(started + max_seconds, MAX_EVENTS)
    seed = node.admit_seed(
        frame,
        pilot.seed,
        mask=mask,
        bins=pilot.seed["bins"],
        budget=budget,
        allow_empty_groups=True,
    )
    trace = sequential.replay_sequential(
        frame,
        pilot.node,
        seed,
        mask=mask,
        seed_sha256=producer.content_sha256(pilot.seed),
        budget=budget,
        cover="indexed",
    )
    final = pilot.node["final_state"]
    agrees = all(
        same(node.points(final["groups"][str(owner)]), trace.groups[owner]) for owner in mask
    )
    return {
        "status": "PASS_REPLAYED",
        "steps": len(trace.steps),
        "rows": sum(step["rows"] for step in trace.steps),
        "events": sum(step["events"] for step in trace.steps),
        "closure": trace.closure,
        "final_state_agrees": agrees,
        "seconds": time.monotonic() - started,
    }

def write_partial(path: Path, pilot: Pilot) -> None:
    """The rounds and updates so far, rewritten after every round, so that a killed run
    keeps what it measured."""
    path.parent.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
    record = {
        "schema": f"{SCHEMA}/partial",
        "tool_sha256": TOOL_SHA256,
        "kernel_sha256": KERNEL_SHA256,
        "endpoint_control": (
            {"held": True, "checked_after": len(pilot.updates) + 1}
            if pilot.endpoint_lost is None
            else {"held": False, **pilot.endpoint_lost}
        ),
        "falsifier": falsifier_reading(pilot.rounds),
        "rounds": pilot.rounds,
        "updates": pilot.updates,
    }
    temporary = path.with_suffix(".tmp")
    temporary.write_text(
        json.dumps(record, sort_keys=True, default=str) + "\n", encoding="utf-8"
    )
    temporary.replace(path)


def contraction_start(rounds: Sequence[Mapping[str, Any]]) -> dict[str, Any] | None:
    """The first complete round in which the worst owner's two-sided position extent fell
    by a tenth, with the widest-row-to-extent ratios of the round before it."""
    complete = [entry for entry in rounds if entry.get("complete", True)]
    for before, entry in itertools.pairwise(complete):
        if entry["worst_position_extent"] <= EXTENT_DROP * before["worst_position_extent"]:
            return {
                "round": entry["round"],
                "max_row_to_extent_before": before["max_row_to_extent"],
                "median_row_to_extent_before": before["median_row_to_extent"],
                "worst_position_extent": entry["worst_position_extent"],
            }
    return None


def falsifier_reading(rounds: Sequence[Mapping[str, Any]]) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """The review's falsifier and its alternative, read from the complete rounds."""
    complete = [entry for entry in rounds if entry.get("complete", True)]
    run = best = 0
    for entry in complete:
        run = run + 1 if entry["rows_fine"] and entry["no_extent_drop"] else 0
        best = max(best, run)
    start = contraction_start(rounds)
    after = [
        entry for entry in complete if start is not None and entry["round"] >= start["round"]
    ]
    extents = [entry["worst_position_extent"] for entry in after]
    ratios = [current / before for before, current in itertools.pairwise(extents)]
    return {
        "falsified": best >= FALSIFIER_ROUNDS,
        "longest_fine_and_flat_run": best,
        "first_round_rows_under_a_tenth": next(
            (entry["round"] for entry in complete if entry["rows_past_model_threshold"]), None
        ),
        "first_round_rows_under_a_twentieth": next(
            (entry["round"] for entry in complete if entry["rows_fine"]), None
        ),
        "contraction_start": start,
        "g_worst_extent_after_start": ratios,
        "g_after_start_geometric_mean": (
            math.exp(sum(math.log(value) for value in ratios) / len(ratios)) if ratios else None
        ),
    }


def first_rounds(rounds: Sequence[Mapping[str, Any]]) -> dict[str, int | None]:
    """The first round whose worst half-extent is below each scale."""
    return {
        f"{scale:g}": next((entry["round"] for entry in rounds if entry["worst"] < scale), None)
        for scale in SCALES
    }


def contraction(rounds: Sequence[Mapping[str, Any]]) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """Round-over-round ratios of the worst half-extent, per class, and their tail."""
    complete = [entry for entry in rounds if entry.get("complete", True)]
    series: dict[str, list[float | None]] = {}
    for key in ("worst", "worst_position", "worst_turn"):
        values = [entry[key] for entry in complete]
        series[key] = [
            (current / before if before else None)
            for before, current in itertools.pairwise(values)
        ]
    tail = [value for value in series["worst"][-3:] if value is not None]
    g_tail = math.exp(sum(math.log(value) for value in tail) / len(tail)) if tail else None
    worst = complete[-1]["worst"] if complete else math.inf
    projected: dict[str, float | None] = {}
    for scale in SCALES:
        if worst < scale:
            projected[f"{scale:g}"] = 0.0
        elif g_tail is not None and g_tail < 1:
            projected[f"{scale:g}"] = math.log(worst / scale) / math.log(1 / g_tail)
        else:
            projected[f"{scale:g}"] = None
    return {
        "ratios": series,
        "g_tail_geometric_mean": g_tail,
        "worst_final": worst,
        "rounds_more_to_scale": projected,
    }


def owner_g_table(rounds: Sequence[Mapping[str, Any]]) -> dict[str, dict[str, float | None]]:
    """Each owner's geometric-mean ratio per coordinate over the complete rounds."""
    complete = [entry for entry in rounds if entry.get("complete", True)][1:]
    table: dict[str, dict[str, float | None]] = {}
    for entry in complete:
        for cell, ratios in entry["g"].items():
            for key, value in ratios.items():
                table.setdefault(cell, {}).setdefault(key, None)
                if value is not None and value > 0:
                    logs = table[cell]
                    logs[key] = (logs[key] or 0.0) + math.log(value)
    count = max(len(complete), 1)
    return {
        cell: {
            key: (math.exp(value / count) if value is not None else None)
            for key, value in row.items()
        }
        for cell, row in table.items()
    }


def verdict(
    cap: str,
    *,
    endpoint_lost: bool,
    replayed: Mapping[str, Any] | None,
    worst_final: float,
    refused: bool = False,
    floor: float = BLOB_FLOOR,
) -> str:
    """The run's status: a lost endpoint is a soundness failure; the exclusion-cap control
    passes only if the worst half-extent stays at or above the blob floor (half the box
    radius when that is smaller, since the blob then fills the box)."""
    if endpoint_lost:
        return "REFUSED_ENDPOINT_LOST"
    if refused:
        return "REFUSED_STEP"
    if replayed is not None and replayed["status"] != "PASS_REPLAYED":
        return "INCOMPLETE_REPLAY"
    if cap == "exclusion":
        held = worst_final >= floor
        return "PASS_CONTROL_BLOB_HELD" if held else "REFUSED_CONTROL_CONTRACTED"
    return "PASS_PILOT_MEASURED"


def run(
    *,
    cap: str,
    bins: int,
    max_rounds: int,
    max_live: int,
    min_width: Q,
    hull_limit: int | None,
    max_seconds: float,
    replay_share: float,
    save_objects: Path | None,
    seed_grid: int = 0,
    box: Q | None = None,
    system: str = "n17",
    core_kind: str = "octagon",
    rounds_after_start: int | None = None,
    partial: Path | None = None,
) -> dict[str, Any]:
    started, cpu = time.monotonic(), time.process_time()
    if system == "n11":
        require(
            box is None and cap == "capture", "the n11 control runs at n11's cap, from cells"
        )
        frame = n11_frame()
        endpoint = load_n11_endpoint(frame)
    else:
        base = capture_frame(None)
        endpoint = load_endpoint(base)
        frame = capture_frame(endpoint.capture_cap if cap == "capture" else None)
        if box is not None:
            frame, endpoint = box_frame(frame, endpoint, box)
    pilot_seconds = max_seconds * (1 - replay_share)
    pilot = run_pilot(
        frame,
        endpoint,
        bins=bins,
        max_rounds=max_rounds,
        max_live=max_live,
        min_width=min_width,
        hull_limit=hull_limit,
        max_seconds=pilot_seconds,
        seed_grid=seed_grid,
        core_kind=core_kind,
        rounds_after_start=rounds_after_start,
        node_id=f"{system}-capture-pilot",
        partial=partial,
    )
    replayed: dict[str, Any] | None = None
    if replay_share > 0 and pilot.endpoint_lost is None:
        remaining = max_seconds - (time.monotonic() - started)
        try:
            replayed = replay(frame, pilot, max_seconds=max(remaining, 1.0))
        except IncompleteError as error:
            replayed = {"status": "INCOMPLETE", "reason": str(error)}
    if save_objects is not None:
        save_objects.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
        for kind, value in (("seed", pilot.seed), ("node", pilot.node)):
            raw = json.dumps(value, sort_keys=True, separators=(",", ":")).encode()
            name = f"{kind}-{hashlib.sha256(raw).hexdigest()}.json.gz"
            (save_objects / name).write_bytes(gzip.compress(raw, mtime=0))
    targets = endpoint.by_owner()
    coarse = next(
        (target.owner for target in endpoint.targets if target.label == endpoint.coarse), None
    )
    contracting = order(frame, sorted(targets), coarse)
    reading = contraction(pilot.rounds)
    reached = first_rounds(pilot.rounds)
    status = verdict(
        cap,
        endpoint_lost=pilot.endpoint_lost is not None,
        replayed=replayed,
        worst_final=reading["worst_final"],
        refused=pilot.refusal is not None,
        floor=BLOB_FLOOR if box is None else min(BLOB_FLOOR, float(box) / 2),
    )
    result: dict[str, Any] = {
        "schema": SCHEMA,
        "status": status,
        "claim": (
            "a measurement: the certified rounds' residual extents around the endpoint; "
            "no capture, guard or exclusion is claimed"
            + ("" if box is None else "; the cells are cut to a sub-case around the endpoint")
        ),
        "cap": cap,
        "system": system,
        "falsifier": falsifier_reading(pilot.rounds),
        "frame": frame.name,
        "U": str(frame.cap),
        "inner_cap": str(frame.inner_cap),
        "endpoint": endpoint.provenance,
        "owners": [
            {
                "label": target.label,
                "cell": target.cell,
                "owner": target.owner,
                "turn": target.turn,
                "coarse": target.owner == coarse,
                "order": contracting.index(target.owner)
                if target.owner in contracting
                else None,
            }
            for target in endpoint.targets
        ],
        "settings": {
            "bins": bins,
            "max_rounds": max_rounds,
            "max_live": max_live,
            "min_width": str(min_width),
            "hull_limit": hull_limit,
            "collision": True,
            "cover": "indexed",
            "max_seconds": max_seconds,
            "replay_share": replay_share,
            "seed_grid": seed_grid,
            "core": core_kind,
            "rounds_after_start": rounds_after_start,
            "box": None if box is None else str(box),
            "radius": str(RADIUS),
        },
        "outcome": pilot.outcome,
        "closure": pilot.closure,
        "refusal": pilot.refusal,
        "endpoint_control": (
            {"held": True, "checked_after": len(pilot.updates) + 1}
            if pilot.endpoint_lost is None
            else {"held": False, **pilot.endpoint_lost}
        ),
        "seed_points": {
            frame.cell_names[owner]: len(pilot.seed["groups"][str(owner)])
            for owner in sorted(targets)
        },
        "rounds": pilot.rounds,
        "updates": pilot.updates,
        "scales_first_round": reached,
        "contraction": reading,
        "owner_g": owner_g_table(pilot.rounds),
        "splits": {
            "branch_predicates": 0,
            "row_bisections": sum(update["splits"] for update in pilot.updates),
            "row_bisections_before_1e-3": sum(
                update["splits"]
                for update in pilot.updates
                if reached["0.001"] is None or update["round"] < reached["0.001"]
            ),
        },
        "replay": replayed,
        "node_sha256": producer.content_sha256(pilot.node),
        "seed_sha256": producer.content_sha256(pilot.seed),
        "tool_sha256": TOOL_SHA256,
        "kernel_sha256": KERNEL_SHA256,
        "wall_seconds": time.monotonic() - started,
        "process_cpu_seconds": time.process_time() - cpu,
    }
    return result


def main(argv: list[str] | None = None) -> int:
    parser = argparse.ArgumentParser(description=__doc__)
    parser.add_argument("--system", choices=("n17", "n11"), default="n17")
    parser.add_argument("--cap", choices=("capture", "exclusion"), default="capture")
    parser.add_argument("--bins", type=int, default=32)
    parser.add_argument("--max-rounds", type=int, default=12)
    parser.add_argument("--max-live", type=int, default=128, help="live rows per owner")
    parser.add_argument("--min-width-log2", type=int, default=22, help="rows >= 2^-N wide")
    parser.add_argument("--hull-limit", type=int, default=48, help="0 keeps every vertex")
    parser.add_argument("--core", choices=("octagon", "envelope"), default="octagon")
    parser.add_argument(
        "--rounds-after-start",
        type=int,
        help="stop this many rounds after positions start contracting",
    )
    parser.add_argument("--max-seconds", type=float, default=540.0)
    parser.add_argument(
        "--replay-share",
        type=float,
        default=0.0,
        help="the share of the ceiling kept for replay_sequential on the whole node",
    )
    parser.add_argument(
        "--seed-grid", type=int, default=0, help="also seed owned points on a 1/N grid"
    )
    parser.add_argument(
        "--box", type=Q, help="cut the cells to the guard polygons widened by this radius"
    )
    parser.add_argument("--save-objects", type=Path)
    parser.add_argument("--output", type=Path)
    args = parser.parse_args(argv)
    if not math.isfinite(args.max_seconds) or args.max_seconds <= 0:
        parser.error("the wall ceiling must be positive and finite")
    if args.bins <= 0 or args.max_rounds <= 0 or args.max_live <= 0:
        parser.error("bins, rounds and live rows must be positive")
    if not 0 <= args.replay_share < 1:
        parser.error("the replay share must lie in [0, 1)")
    result = run(
        cap=args.cap,
        bins=args.bins,
        max_rounds=args.max_rounds,
        max_live=args.max_live,
        min_width=Q(1, 2**args.min_width_log2),
        hull_limit=args.hull_limit or None,
        max_seconds=args.max_seconds,
        replay_share=args.replay_share,
        save_objects=args.save_objects,
        seed_grid=args.seed_grid,
        box=args.box,
        system=args.system,
        core_kind=args.core,
        rounds_after_start=args.rounds_after_start,
        partial=None if args.output is None else args.output.with_suffix(".partial.json"),
    )
    encoded = json.dumps(result, indent=1, sort_keys=True, default=str) + "\n"
    if args.output:
        args.output.parent.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
        args.output.write_text(encoded, encoding="utf-8")
    table = [
        {
            "round": entry["round"],
            "worst": f"{entry['worst']:.3e}",
            "position": f"{entry['worst_position']:.3e}",
            "turn": f"{entry['worst_turn']:.3e}",
            "inside": entry["owners_inside_guard"],
            "seconds": round(entry["seconds"], 1),
        }
        for entry in result["rounds"]
    ]
    summary = {
        key: result[key]
        for key in (
            "status",
            "system",
            "cap",
            "outcome",
            "endpoint_control",
            "scales_first_round",
            "falsifier",
        )
    }
    print(json.dumps({**summary, "rounds": table, "g": result["contraction"]}, indent=1))
    return 0 if str(result["status"]).startswith("PASS") else 2


if __name__ == "__main__":
    sys.exit(main())
