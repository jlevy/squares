# X-018 — Hybrid Strength and Angular Release

**Put the largest new allocation into a fair test of local compatibility, and a smaller
independent allocation into angular structure.** The first asks whether a certified
capacity can remove a continuous domain left by a strong geometric relaxation.
The second asks whether releasing one of Trump’s angle-forcing contacts opens a
tractable family beyond the retained local theorem.
These are concrete ways to test the structural direction emerging from the last day’s
work. A complete unrestricted exclusion at $q=96/25=3.84$ is the larger downstream
objective; no overnight completion time for it is known.

[Agenda 028](../agendas/agenda-028-hybrid-strength-and-angular-release.md) owns these
new decisions. Keep [X-017](X-017-compatibility-and-complete-case-covers.md) as the
packet synthesis and keep Agenda 027’s broad anchor and six-plus-five pilots intact.
A separate exploration is justified by independently managed questions and acceptance
rules, rather than by the extra review file alone.
The
[PR108 review](../../../docs/project/reviews/review-2026-09-07-n11-hybrid-strategy.md)
retains the detailed derivations; this document records their assessment and allocation.

## The Comparison That Earns a Larger Run

At exact fixed angles with all SAT alternatives selected, the translation LP is exact.
A valid resource cannot contradict a genuinely feasible instance of that complete LP.
Additional strength can arise from interval angle enclosures, relaxed disjunctions or
integrality; a resource can also save proof work without adding strength.
Keep those outcomes separate.

H-118’s first domain should be a small subsystem at $q=96/25$ that arises from an
explicit necessary split of the eleven-square problem.
Choose it before searching for its certificate; record its parent restrictions and the
sibling remainder. Do not select an arbitrary impossible micro-box after seeing which
proof succeeds.
The comparator includes applicable projection, ordering, interval SAT and
coupled LP deductions, under the same branching policy.

An accepted strength comparison needs both a uniform exclusion certificate and an exact
feasible witness for the declared outer relaxation on the identical domain.
A fully branched infeasible LP establishes no strict separation, even if the resource
finds the same contradiction faster.
Report that cost result separately.
Exhausting one finite resource family can reject that family comparison; a timeout
cannot reject H-111 or prove feasible geometry.

For a useful overnight outcome, a closed domain must connect to a complete parent cover,
or replace a recurring verified leaf with a cheaper certificate.
Retain every unresolved child.
Counts of failed samples, handpicked boxes or descriptor labels do not measure the
fraction of the global problem solved.
Any new global bound needs every root case closed or transferred through an accepted
lemma with its full premises.

H-119 is a conditional continuation.
It retains one common anchor parameter in $\inf_a[\alpha(a)+\sum_i w_i(a)-M]>0$ and
compares it with a specified static representation on one unsplit box.
The independent-minimum baseline needs sharp certified minima, a feasible relaxation
witness, or an exact finite-family obstruction.
Loose lower bounds that fail to prune do not establish a correlation advantage.
Neither comparison is against arbitrary subdivision or every possible measure.

## A Structural Lane With a Decidable First Task

H-120 selects **direct exclusion**, not a finite-motion theorem, for its opening pilot.
Release segment $(9,10)$ from the review’s zero-based Trump graph.
Retain segments $(3,4),(3,5),(6,7),(6,8),(7,9),(8,9)$ and the nine specified flush wall
incidences. The equality graph has two unanchored components and rank nine.
All eleven-square containment and all 55 nonoverlap conditions remain.

Freeze a nontrivial closed domain with variable side $381/100\le L\le96/25$ and exact
center, angle and feature restrictions.
Use positive rational lower bounds for retained segment lengths in the interior feature
case; give vanishing-length boundaries explicit sibling scopes when claiming a larger
cover. Retain the released-contact equality seam and the coincident/axis angle cases.
The parent family contains Trump at $U$; that is a feasible control, not a witness
inside the sublevel target.
Keeping $L$ variable matters: enlarging a container can destroy the prescribed wall
attachments, so a fixed-$q$ attachment test alone does not cover every smaller side.

For a closed pilot beyond existing local closure, use the complement of the local
region’s interior, allowing boundary overlap, or impose a positive separation margin.
Bind this decision to the exact chart, labels and angle lifts; do not subtract a closed
region and silently leave an open target.
If no local lemma is used, say so.
One success here does not cover all six-axis/four-plus-one packings, still less all
minimizing packings.
The wall attachments and retained segments are substantial restrictions.

H-121 states the larger question precisely: **some global minimizer has orientations in
$\{0,\theta\}$ modulo quarter turns**. A low-angle representative theorem plus H-112
alone is insufficient: H-112 covers only multiplicity five.
The remaining axis-plus-one multiplicities require a complete bound, or H-113 could
supply a stronger complete family bound.
The review’s trimming reduces the one-angle candidate family to oblique multiplicities
$m=2,\ldots,11$ below $97/25$; it does not classify unrestricted packings.

The next proof obligation beyond H-120 is a finite, feasible, nonincreasing-side angle
elimination with every new contact and degeneracy covered, or another global
representative argument.
An infinitesimal flex or a stress count cannot replace it.
A stationary exclusion is another possible residual argument, but it must distinguish
original minimizers, with individual-square torque equations, from restricted
shared-angle minima, which have only class torque equations.
BC-267’s accepted physical formulation would be a prerequisite for that changed claim.
A high-angle feasible packing cannot refute existence of a different low-angle
minimizer. A verified packing strictly below a proved lower bound for the entire
axis-plus-one-angle family would refute the normal form.

Use numerical search as an adversary for the selected family and motion premises.
Include coordinated angle splitting and contact release in a deliberately unmerged arm:
the existing quench’s default `class_tol=1e-2` merges nearby angles, and its
one-coordinate free pass does not establish a coordinated-release search.
Promote any candidate through independent rigorous packing verification.
Repeated returns to two angles are not evidence for H-121.

## What Not to Give the Night Yet

H-107 remains deferred under its source coordinator: readiness alone does not make a
small scalar step the best use of attention.
Reopen it for a named downstream use or a revised allocation, not to occupy idle
compute. The unchanged exp-116 attempt and failed density source algorithms have no new
reason to run. The fixed-support ceiling says nothing decisive about expanded supports.

Keep pair PSD kernels, curved resources and expanded density supports as candidate-led
alternatives under their existing records.
Promote one when it has a concrete margin or exact obstruction and a priced continuum
reader, or when a direct pilot exposes the specific information it lacks.
Do not build a general stationary atlas, enumerate all contact graphs, or port every
unpublished spike before one selected consumer needs it.

The first checkpoint should report new closed geometry, the exact remaining cases, what
consumed mathematical attention and compute, and the next bottleneck.
A proved local statement or a scoped negative that changes the next choice is useful.
An instrument-only block is enabling work; call it that and reprice the next
mathematical decision.

opening sequence and the conditions for allocating a longer run.
