### A compact support result can replace an unnecessary target computation

The
[original review’s support proof](../../resources/papers/n11-complete-research-bundle-2026-09-07/original_review/n11_research_review.md#seven-necessary-rows-close-the-whole-support)
uses seven positive-area incidence boxes.
Their nonnegative rational combination gives total support weight at most eleven; the
known average of the eight feasible Trump packings supplies the matching lower bound.
Another box gives depth $7/5$ for the old $56/5$ candidate.
That violation is consistent with exp-115: several light overlapping squares can produce
excessive total depth even when no overweight pair overlaps.

This is already an allowed rejection route in
[H-099](../hypotheses/H-099-trump-d4-finite-support-dual.md).
It needs no new mathematical hypothesis or relaxed criterion.
The missing acceptance work is to bind all 88 source images to the 60 distinct
placements, their orbit order, the candidate weights, and the feasible average through
an independent construction and geometry check.
The supplied second assembly shares its root enclosure and source geometry.
Same-code replay and three read-only intake assessments do not replace that check.

If accepted, append the stronger H-099 determination; preserve exp-113’s finite-row
result and exp-115’s pair result.
Further exhaustive verification of the unchanged candidate would then have no decision
to resolve. An expanded support remains a separate question, H-116. Support optimum
eleven is neither existence of a global equality density nor a lower bound on the
container side.

### Physical stationarity changes a formulation, not every existing branch

The
[physical proof](../../resources/papers/n11-complete-research-bundle-2026-09-07/original_review/n11_research_review.md#proposition-a-every-physical-branch-satisfies-a-strict-feasible-direction-condition)
keeps all sixteen smooth corner-projection inequalities for each chosen pair axis/order,
all physical wall rows, a variable side, and open local angle charts.
Dilating centers and container, while keeping unit squares fixed, strictly increases
every active physical constraint.
This supports normal KKT, a multiplier bound of $L+1$, and a certificate with at most 34
positive scalar multipliers in that formulation.

The 34 rows are a stress support, not the full contact set or a list of cases.
The 23 independent rows of a fixed-angle center-and-side LP basis serve a different
purpose. Ties, zero multipliers, rattlers, alternate bases, determinant-zero loci, and
chart seams still require coverage.
Fixed-side or artificially partitioned problems cannot borrow the dilation direction.
An unrestricted stationary representative has individual square torque equations; a
restricted shared-angle minimizer has class torque equations.

The
[finite stationary-value deduction](../../resources/papers/n11-complete-research-bundle-2026-09-07/updates/enumeration_addendum.md#finite-stationary-side-values-do-not-require-rigid-optima)
uses complementarity along semialgebraic paths to make side constant on each stationary
component. Finite components then give finite side values under the stated algebraic
input assumptions. This avoids demanding finitely many rigid optima, but supplies no
usable component count, degree bound, or runtime forecast.
Exact value and uniqueness remain separate goals.

### Angle transfer is useful where there is spare margin

For $f(\theta)$, the optimum over all centers and separation alternatives at labelled
angles $\theta$, the source derives

$$
\frac{f(\alpha)}{\cos\delta+\sin\delta}
\le f(\theta)\le
(\cos\delta+\sin\delta)f(\alpha)
$$

when every actual orientation differs by at most $\delta\le\pi/4$ modulo quarter turns.
A theorem $f(\alpha)\ge V$ therefore transfers a lower bound $V/(\cos\delta+\sin\delta)$
to the covered neighborhood.
A single contact-cell optimum cannot substitute for $f$. A theorem with $V=U$ does not
exclude a fixed positive neighborhood below $U$ through this transfer alone.

The
[elementary source deduction](../../resources/papers/n11-complete-research-bundle-2026-09-07/updates/enumeration_addendum.md#a-quantitative-obliquity-statement-already-follows)
at $q=3.8771$ and $\delta=1/500$ radians depends on the exact 0/45-degree theorem.
It forces at least one square outside both near classes and separately bounds the
near-axis count by nine.
The resulting 65 bin profiles are modest discrete pruning.
They do not prove two middle-angle rotators or H-036’s stronger radius and side.

## The Alternative Programs

### A. Resources and boundary anchors: the principal global-bound pilot

[H-111](../hypotheses/H-111-resource-anchor-case-exclusion.md) asks whether a coarse
complete pose cover can become useful before a nearly optimal one-body measure exists.
The
[resource proposition](../../resources/papers/n11-complete-research-bundle-2026-09-07/original_review/n11_research_review.md#proposition-c-a-resource-and-conflict-certificate)
assigns every actual pose to a capacity-one cell.
For each measure $\mu_j$, keep its own budget $M_j$ and uniform cell minima $m_{ja}$:

The minima concern specified witnesses strictly inside each square when the measure can
charge boundaries, including T-018’s atomic measure.
Full-square minima are valid only for a boundary-null measure.
These conditions keep the selected mass disjoint even when the packed squares touch.

$$
\sum_a z_a=11,\qquad
\sum_a m_{ja}z_a\le M_j,\qquad z_a\in\{0,1\}.
$$

Uniformly proved cell conflicts and small-subsystem no-goods add compatibility.
Several angular cells inside one capacity-one spatial tile share one occupancy limit.
An unresolved minimum may safely become zero and an unproved conflict may be omitted.
Independent measures cannot share a cheaper invented budget.

The source’s twenty-tile cover uses the incircle center-distance implication; its
$\binom{20}{11}=167960$ subsets still contain continuous geometry.
Use that cover as a cheap starting representation, not a claim that 167960 cases will be
easy. Recompute resource minima at the actual target side.
The T-018 measure can supply resources even if it is no longer a global unit-threshold
cover at that side.

A joint downward translation gives a bottom-wall anchor in every packing.
The two-dimensional anchor domain includes both position and angle.
For an anchor box, the residual ten-square domain is the **union** over every allowed
anchor, or a proved superset of that union.
Compatibility with its midpoint is insufficient.
If separate residual poses each use a different possible anchor, their union remains a
safe outer relaxation for exclusion, but its feasibility proves no common completion.

The first target is $L=3.84$, a proposed milestone rather than a predicted result.
Compare the same geometry with and without resources on a frozen complete anchor domain.
Require a uniform exclusion or an explicit residual that changes the next decision.
Count closed domains and retained remainder, not sampled failures.
Stop expanding the selected representation if meaningful refinement leaves the same
unmanageable residual or almost all resource minima remain zero.

Mixed-size frames are an extension within this program.
Keep anchor/frame squares full size; replace only a proved angularly localized remainder
by safe orientation-specific cores.
The geometric loss must be recorded.
A feasible core relaxation is not a feasible unit-square packing.

This route is distinct from BC-248’s near-tight global-residue tree.
Its complete pose cover and arbitrary budgets explain why it need not wait for BC-248’s
near-eleven measure and census reduction.
Those old guards remain intact for that old object.

### B. Restricted families: the principal structural theorem pilot

[H-112](../hypotheses/H-112-six-axis-five-common-angle-optimum.md) states that every
packing of six axis-aligned squares and five sharing one arbitrary actual orientation
has side at least $U$. All centers, contacts, wall attachments, and separation
alternatives are free.
The known construction supplies the matching upper witness.
This is a concrete specialization of H-102, distinct from H-036’s neighborhoods and from
optimizing Trump’s known contact cell.

Use a uniform parametric LP certificate over a complete angle interval.
The
[robust Farkas inequality](../../resources/papers/n11-complete-research-bundle-2026-09-07/updates/enumeration_addendum.md#proposition-h-robust-farkas-certificates-with-residual-cancellation)
can tolerate residual coefficient error when valid variable bounds are included.
The supplied arithmetic helper does not construct geometry enclosures, export the actual
fixed-angle solver’s dual, or cover alternative branches.
These are the smallest shared interfaces worth commissioning.
Try selected basis elimination only where it simplifies unresolved leaves.

Expand after a complete nontrivial interval closes, or a small explicitly described
residual gains a credible closing mechanism.
Many sampled LP optima, one graph, or a growing catalogue of unrelated tiny boxes do not
meet that criterion.
An independent search lane should attack the same claimed family, including alternate
wall patterns, and verify any sub-$U$ witness rigorously.

[H-113](../hypotheses/H-113-at-most-two-angle-optimum.md) is the broader successor: all
multiplicities and both absolute class angles.
The square container permits only its finite symmetries; arbitrary rotation cannot set
the first class to zero.
Equal-angle collisions, wrap seams, and the one-angle family remain covered.
Even a complete two-angle theorem leaves every competitor with three or more actual
orientations open.

[H-117](../hypotheses/H-117-forced-angle-complexity.md) retains the missing structural
bridge: can forced positive-length edge contacts bound the number of angle components in
minimizing representatives?
Corner contacts, stress counts, and recurring numerical patterns do not establish that
lemma. The near-cluster and dispersed-cluster cases must both remain visible.

### C. Two-pose kernels: a separate, bounded opportunity

[H-114](../hypotheses/H-114-two-pose-kernel-exclusion.md) concerns an interaction
kernel, distinct from H-096’s geometric inner kernels.
The
[source certificate](../../resources/papers/n11-complete-research-bundle-2026-09-07/original_review/n11_research_review.md#proposition-e-a-kernel-certificate)
requires $K-1$ positive semidefinite, $K(p,p)\le b$, and $K(p,q)\le0$ on every distinct
compatible pair, including touching pairs.
Then every packing has at most $b$ members; $b<11$ excludes eleven.

A small fixed feature family is the first discriminator.
Finite samples propose a matrix; they do not certify the continuum.
Exact positive semidefiniteness, full diagonal coverage, and the complete pair domain
need separate evidence.
An exact necessary-constraint obstruction can reject the selected family without
claiming all kernels fail.

Use joint container symmetries on both poses.
Independent angle folding changes compatibility.
The pair separator has six continuous pose coordinates before valid reductions; its
price is a major uncertainty.
Stop increasing feature degree if each separation pass exposes new unresolved regions
without useful margin.
The discovered cases can still inform the resource pilot.
A mass-eleven kernel at $U$ alone excludes twelve, not eleven below $U$; exact-value
closure needs a below-$U$ family or an additional equality argument.

**Session097 follow-up, September 7.** The independently reviewed
[feature design](../series/series-000-smoke-and-calibration/results/agenda-027/bc-264-kernel-feature-design.md)

the compatible-pair sign condition, for any diagonal bound. A mixed-sign second
difference on nine touching axis-aligned squares annihilates those features and
contradicts positive semidefiniteness. This is an analytic family obstruction, not
a sampled failure or a refutation of all interaction kernels.

[H125](../hypotheses/H-125-biquadratic-two-pose-kernel.md) records that narrower

## A Shared Case Contract, Sized to the First Pilots

The
[source case-composition statement](../../resources/papers/n11-complete-research-bundle-2026-09-07/updates/enumeration_addendum.md#proposition-g-finite-cover-composition)
omits acyclicity from its listed assumptions, although its proof uses it.
Our adaptation requires a **finite directed acyclic graph**, or a separately proved
well-founded rank for every descent.
A cycle of unproved obligations cannot close a root.
Reusing a proved leaf or lemma remains allowed.

| Obligation | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Root | All feasible competitors, or an explicitly justified set of minimizing representatives; name which |
| Domain | Side range, full pose or parameter domain, actual orientation lifts, and all inherited assumptions |
| Split | Children cover the parent’s relevant configurations, including boundaries; overlap is permitted |
| Symmetry | A map of the entire configuration and constraints, not independent reflections of selected angles |
| Leaf | Exact exclusion, a lower-side inequality, or a local theorem whose full chart/radius hypotheses hold |
| Reuse | Proved implication from the new domain to every reused lemma’s domain |
| Remainder | Every unresolved branch remains present; unknown compatibility creates no conflict edge |
| Termination | A DAG or verified well-founded descent, with no circular acceptance |

An all-feasible node cannot be pruned with original-stationary equations without the
representative reduction.
A restricted family cannot stand in for the unrestricted root.
A sampled pose, descriptor, or successful parser is not a closed geometric case.

Keep this as an interface requirement until one pilot supplies concrete cases and
certificates. Reuse existing soft-schema artifacts and exact geometry; add a small case
contract only when its consumer and positive/negative controls are explicit.
Controls must include a missing boundary, invalid independent reflection, circular
dependency, omitted row, widened coefficient box, and incorrectly reused restricted
lemma. Every new fast regression then belongs on the normal CI surface.

## Allocation and the First Decision

The prepared agenda prioritizes compact, independently scoped reviews and one shared
interface, then compares the resource/anchor and six-plus-five pilots.
The support-ceiling review does not gate those pilots; physical KKT is required only for
a leaf that invokes original stationarity.
The kernel has a small independent discriminator; a resource-representation extension is
selected only when evidence earns it.
The source portfolio owns its near-45 and conditional-compatibility continuation; scalar
row completion is deferred under its latest amendment.
These remain separate allocations and do not become dependencies of the new questions.

The first checkpoint should answer whether any selected method closes a nontrivial
continuous domain with reusable certificates at a useful price.
Retain exact domains, open remainder, proof obligations discharged, and measured work.
Only compare methods on the same declared task when making a cost claim.
Prospective times in the agenda are planning estimates, not observed runtimes.

Expand the route that closes whole cases or reveals a sharply simpler residual.
Reprice or stop a representation when refinement multiplies work without changing the
remaining mathematical problem.
A small verified packing takes priority for exact construction verification, whatever
conjecture motivated the search.
Global bounds and source-agenda dispositions change only after the corresponding
reviewed evidence has been accepted and recorded.
