## A Concrete Strong Comparator Contract

The candidate contract is a coupled geometric outer LP for a frozen finite partition of
that exact child. The partition, row generators, nonlinear-expression representation,
deduction closure and branching policy must be fixed before any feasibility search.
The following inventory is the minimum proposed contract, not a claim that its rows or
reader already exist.

**Common variables and geometry.** Retain all eleven absolute centers, actual angle
coordinates and the same selected-pose auxiliary quantities.
The side is fixed exactly at $96/25$. Use BC261’s rational midpoint wall and
sixteen-corner selected SAT rows, including its complete changing-normal and
changing-corner errors.
Add every applicable source center bound, selected horizontal ordering, band bound,
projection contraction and independently accepted incircle/core consequence.
Shared quantities occur once; they cannot be independently minimized in different rows
or for different residual squares.

The physical angle chart must cover the original orientations modulo quarter turns.
One possible rational chart is $t\in[0,1]$, with

$$
c(t)=\frac{1-t^2}{1+t^2},\qquad s(t)=\frac{2t}{1+t^2}.
$$

It covers the first quadrant, which represents every square orientation modulo $\pi/2$.
Using it requires an explicit lift and corner/normal permutation map from the source
chart; it is not permission to reflect each square independently.
Both endpoints, axis angles, equal angles and every min/max tie remain.
The original irrational chart endpoints cannot simply be rounded inward to fit BC261’s
rational descriptor.

**Exact guard expressions.** Start from the frozen guard list and its expression graph.
In particular, retain

$$
d_i=\max(\lvert e_{i,x}\rvert,\lvert e_{i,y}\rvert,
\lvert f_{i,x}\rvert,\lvert f_{i,y}\rvert),\qquad
m_i=\frac{1}{2d_i},\qquad m=\min_{i=0,1,2,3}m_i.
$$

Use the exact definitions

$$
\ell=(y_0+y_2)/2,\qquad u=(y_1+y_3)/2,\qquad
\varepsilon=\tfrac12\max(\lvert y_0-y_2\rvert,\lvert y_1-y_3\rvert).
$$

The two virtual-row heights, their actual-pose scatter $\varepsilon$, the guard widths
$A=m+2/5-\varepsilon$ and $R=m+699/1000-\varepsilon$, and every region vertex must use
those same values.
Treating $m$ or $\varepsilon$ as a freely chosen favorable bound would
change the domain. All 24 corrected fence, vertex-location, nonnegative-size and
quadratic diameter guards must appear in the comparator inventory.
The accepted source list exists; its independent descriptor-to-row translation does not.

Min, max and absolute values have exact finite closed descriptions after selecting their
active arms. For $a=\lvert r\rvert$, use $a=\sigma r$ and $\sigma r\ge0$ for
$\sigma\in\{-1,1\}$. For a minimum use an active equality together with all weak
comparisons; reverse the comparisons for a maximum.
Scatter uses the same construction on the actual row deviations.
Retain every tied arm.
If these arms are not branched, their relaxation must be an explicitly bounded
convex-hull formulation of the declared outer branch polyhedra; an epigraph inequality
alone does not impose equality to a max or absolute value.

**Products, reciprocals and quadratics.** A finite LP cannot retain a general quadratic
guard exactly. A proposed outer representation should keep one auxiliary per repeated
product and all its certified bounds.
For $w=uv$ on a declared rectangle, include all four standard product-envelope
inequalities, not a one-sided inequality.
For a square $z=x^2$ on $[l,u]$, a concrete rational policy is

$$
z\le(l+u)x-lu,\qquad
z\ge2rx-r^2\quad\text{for }r\in\{l,(l+u)/2,u\},
$$

together with the exact interval range of $x^2$. Further tangents or subdivisions must
be frozen or follow one shared, deterministic strengthening policy.
This is a declared finite outer envelope, not the exact quadratic graph.

The identities $2d_i m_i=1$ use the same product representation and a certified positive
lower bound on $d_i$. A half-angle lift can use $g_i=1+t_i^2$, $c_i g_i=1-t_i^2$ and
$s_i g_i=2t_i$, with $g_i\ge1$; every repeated square and product is shared.
Its relaxation loss must be reported.
Alternatively, a proven uniform enclosure of the complete guard expression can avoid
these auxiliaries. Choosing that alternative still requires an independent
whole-expression error proof, especially for the scatter-dependent vertices and diameter
guards.

For guards such as $w_R^2+h_R^2\le(999/1000)^2$, retain lower tangents as well as the
auxiliary upper-bound guard so it yields necessary restrictions on the underlying
variables. Directly requiring a secant upper bound to be below the guard threshold would
instead impose a sufficient condition and could remove allowed poses.
For the necessary incircle inequality

$$
(x_i-x_j)^2+(y_i-y_j)^2\ge1,
$$

the upper secants are essential to obtaining restrictions from the auxiliary lower-bound
inequality: unrestricted epigraph auxiliaries could satisfy it without constraining the
centers. Directly requiring a lower tangent sum to exceed one would again be an
unjustified strengthening.
These opposite uses of a squared quantity need distinct negative controls.
A solver accepting either lifted system does not check that its square auxiliaries equal
the original squares.

**Geometric deductions.** Include every independently justified ordering, support and
projection implication applicable to the node.
The reviewed strict forbidden-center octagons and exhaustive four-region localization
qualify. Give their disjunctions explicit membership and branch semantics.
Because the octagons are closed subsets of a strict forbidden region, actual residual
centers avoid even their boundaries.
Weakening that strict avoidance to closed exterior halfspaces is sound for an outer LP,
but a boundary-only surviving point would diagnose that weakening and must be reported
as such. Physical SAT and wall equalities continue to allow legal square touching.

Within a selected residual-region assignment, the reviewed diameter bound makes every
pair assigned to the same region incompatible.
Include those pair exclusions and their applicable conflict-clique or occupancy
consequences in both arms.
The recommended strong comparator permits these already justified consequences.
In particular, a capacity-one spatial region has one budget across all angle bins and
all residual labels.
Fractional duplicate budgets or separate copies of the selected four poses would make an
unfair comparator.

“All applicable deductions” is not an executable finite specification by itself.
The packet must name the inference rules and their closure depth.
It must not promise the strongest possible LP: the exact convex hull of the empty
guarded packing domain is empty, and computing that hull would already solve the
comparison’s geometric problem.

## Identical SAT Branching and the Meaning of Survival

At each common node, preserve all eight directed alternatives of every unresolved pair;
each selected alternative is the conjunction of all sixteen corner-pair rows.
A concrete initial policy is lexicographic pair selection and the same eight child
labels, with the angle/center partition and its split order supplied separately in the
frozen manifest. Closed children cover ties.
No branch may be selected from a convenient candidate packing or removed because one arm
failed there.

If the comparison stops before resolving every pair, use the bounded convex hull of each
pair’s eight outer alternatives in a disaggregated LP, intersected on the common center
and angle variables.
This is a strong per-pair disjunctive relaxation of the declared rows.
It is not the joint convex hull of all 55 disjunctions.
The distinction is a legitimate potential source of survival, provided it is prospective
and identical in both arms.
An arbitrary large big-$M$ would introduce an avoidable weakness.

Min/max arms, region choices and quadratic-envelope refinements need the same shared
policy. Charging only SAT branches while giving the capacity arm free localization or
guard branching would conceal proof work.
A resource may close a common node earlier; both arms must receive the same node and the
same available deductions before that decision.

There are three distinct possible outcomes:

- A uniform capacity certificate and an exact feasible point of the frozen outer LP
  establish strength relative to that particular relaxation and branching policy.
  The receipt must identify which exact relations the point fails: physical angular
  rows, unresolved SAT choices, guard equalities, strict exterior membership, or
  integrality. Failure to find a dual is not a feasible point.
- If both arms exclude the same node, any advantage is proof cost.
  Retain proof size, branch count, production cost and independent replay cost
  separately. Actual timing claims need the campaign’s paired controls and spread; no
  speedup is measured here.
- If the LP includes the same regional capacity inequalities, the central comparison has
  already shared the candidate conclusion.
  It is a lemma-consumption or proof-cost control, not an H118 strength discriminator.

The survival reader should reconstruct every row and evaluate every auxiliary entry by
exact arithmetic. For an informative central strength claim, also require the candidate
point’s actual angle values and selected four poses to satisfy the original guard
definitions exactly, or explicitly classify the outcome as guard/angle-lift relaxation
loss. A rational half-angle point gives rational sine and cosine, but an arbitrary
feasible lifted $(t,c,s)$ triple need not describe any actual angle.

At fixed exact angles with all physical SAT alternatives selected, the wall and pair
system is an exact translation LP. The accepted fence rules out a genuine feasible point
that also satisfies the source guards.
Weakening guard representation does not overturn that observation; it can only admit a
point outside the guarded domain.
Thus fixed-angle complete branching cannot establish the intended strict separation by
calling an invalid guard assignment a same-domain witness.
