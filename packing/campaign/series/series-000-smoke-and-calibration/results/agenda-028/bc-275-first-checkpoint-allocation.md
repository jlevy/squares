## Selected Complete Closed Domain

Call the proposed domain $N$; this is a mathematical set name, not a new registry ID.
Use the labels, origin and contact sides of the
[accepted release design](bc-273-release-domain-design.md).
Its complete parameter bounds are

$$
381/100\le L\le96/25,\quad 1/3\le t\le2/5,\quad 0\le v\le1,
\quad -1/4\le a,b\le0,\quad 2\le z\le L-1,
\quad p,w\in[1/2,7/2]^2.
$$

For $r\in[0,1]$, define

$$
c(r)=\frac{1-r^2}{1+r^2},\quad s(r)=\frac{2r}{1+r^2},
\quad e(r)=(c(r),s(r)),\quad f(r)=(-s(r),c(r)).
$$

Squares 0–5 have basis $((1,0),(0,1))$; squares 6–9 have basis $(e,f)=(e(t),f(t))$;
square 10 has basis $(e(v),f(v))$. Their centers are

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_0&=(1/2,1/2),&C_1&=(L-1/2,1/2),\\
C_2&=(z+1/2,L-1/2),&C_3&=(1/2,L-1/2),\\
C_4&=(3/2,L-1/2),&C_5&=(1/2,L-3/2),\\
C_6&=p,&C_7&=p+ae-f,\\
C_8&=p+e+bf,&C_9&=p+(a+1)e+(b-1)f,\\
C_{10}&=w.
\end{aligned}
$$

For each declared basis $(e_i,f_i)$, put

$$
Q_i=\{\alpha e_i+\beta f_i: |\alpha|,|\beta|\le1/2\},\qquad
H_i(n)=\tfrac12\bigl(|n\cdot e_i|+|n\cdot f_i|\bigr),
\quad h_i=H_i((1,0))=H_i((0,1)).
$$

The domain requires every containment condition

$$
h_i\le C_{i,x},C_{i,y}\le L-h_i\qquad(0\le i\le10)
$$

and, separately for each of the 55 pairs $i<j$, the complete disjunction

$$
\bigvee_{n\in\{e_i,f_i,e_j,f_j\},\ \sigma\in\{-1,1\}}
\sigma(C_j-C_i)\cdot n\ge H_i(n)+H_j(n).
$$

contacts remain legal.
The six retained segments are 3–4, 3–5, 6–7, 6–8, 7–9 and 8–9; the nine wall incidences
are unchanged. The accepted feature identities give oblique segment lengths $1-|a|$ or
$1-|b|$, at least $3/4$ here.
No 9–10 contact condition is added.
Every relation is closed and the box is bounded, so $N$ is compact.
There is no fitted center restriction, fixed-side substitution, local-ball subtraction,
source SAT assignment or stationarity premise.
