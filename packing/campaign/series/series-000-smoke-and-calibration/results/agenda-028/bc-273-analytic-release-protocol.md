## Frozen Question and Acceptance

Does the entire closed $T_{\rm mid}$ family admit a legal packing of eleven unit
squares? The unchanged bounds are

$$
381/100\le L\le96/25,\quad 1/3\le t,v\le2/5,\quad
0\le a,b\le1/4,\quad 2\le z\le L-1,\quad p,w\in[1/2,7/2]^2.
$$

The ten-parameter center formulas, source labels, origin, retained contact sides, nine
wall incidences and six positive segments are fixed exactly as in the design.
All eleven square containment conditions and all fifty-five complete directed-SAT
disjunctions are included.
The retained angular equality graph has rank nine; allowed recontact can raise the full
graph rank. All diagonal, nonedge, recontact and equal-angle possibilities remain in the
SAT conditions. Interval endpoints and contact equalities are legal.

For each feasible ten-square skeleton and every admitted eleventh-square angle, its
contained center box must be covered by the **interiors** of the ten forbidden-center
polygons to prove exclusion.
Covering only sample centers, covering by closed polygons, assuming a source SAT
assignment or forgetting an admissible skeleton does not decide the question.
A different exact necessary-inequality proof is equally admissible if it covers all of
the declared domain and its implication is independently audited.

An exact feasible witness requires all center and angle parameters, exact unit-square
geometry, membership in every declared interval and feature equation, containment and
complete nonoverlap.
A numerical fit, optimizer status or apparent motion is a proposal only and cannot
refute exclusion. This analytical attempt does not authorize numerical search; a
numerical instrument would need its own controls and prospective budget.

Accept the determination only after the independent reader checks every premise and the
complete scope. Retain intermediate lemmas with their own exact scopes, but an exclusion
of a strict subdomain leaves this determination unresolved.
The independently replayed exact source packing is a positive parent control, outside
this target by side alone.
It is not a target sample.
The target is separated from the named local source ball by the reviewed square-3
coordinate argument; the local theorem supplies no target exclusion.
No outcome here proves a global normal form or improves the global packing bound by
itself.
