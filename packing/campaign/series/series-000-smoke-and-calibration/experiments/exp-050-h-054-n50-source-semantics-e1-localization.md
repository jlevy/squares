## Source and Retention Boundary

The sole permitted retrieval question is whether the current first-party
`square-50.svg`, adjacent catalogue material, generator artifact or attributable author
statement binds every center and rotation token to stable bytes, units, coordinate
frame, exporter/version and one explicit exact, nearest-rounded, truncated or interval
meaning with enough precision information to construct a closed cell.

Raw first-party SVG bytes remain ephemeral under the existing retention policy.
A durable receipt may retain URL, retrieval time, content hash, response identifiers,
attribution, licence finding, an appropriately bounded explicit semantics statement,
token-class coverage and derived canonical cells.

side `53/7`, the 3-4-5 tilt or numerical feasibility.
