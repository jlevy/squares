## Argument and Exact Coverage

Each row gives the center and signed counterclockwise half-angle tangent `t`. With
`c = (1-t*t)/(1+t*t)` and `s = 2*t/(1+t*t)`, the vectors `u = (c,s)` and `v = (-s,c)`
are perpendicular unit vectors because `c*c+s*s = 1`. The four vertices are the center
plus each choice of `±u/2 ±v/2`. Thus each constructed polygon is a unit square; no
rounded trigonometric conversion enters the argument.

All four vertices lie in `[0,S] × [0,S]` exactly.
Convexity then proves containment of each entire square.
For each unordered pair, a strict axis-aligned bounding-box gap is already a
separating-axis certificate.
Remaining pairs are tested on the four edge normals of the two squares.
The separating-axis theorem for convex polygons makes this complete: some projection
intervals may meet at equality, but their interiors do not overlap.
An exact projection gap of zero is therefore accepted.
