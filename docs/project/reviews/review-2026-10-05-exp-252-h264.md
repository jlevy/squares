inside N1’s 7,000 s ceiling, each re-proved in full by the standing kernel verifier, and

## Findings

### F1: The Wrong Certificate Is Named for 5500414 (Correction)

exp-252 says state 5500414’s orbit “was already excluded by lane K’s flag certificates”,
and so does the admission commit `6717b0b11`. Two checks on the head ledger show
otherwise:

- With every `s182-k*` entry removed, 5500414’s marginal is still 0 (`census-noK.json`).
- With SW9 removed, it is 1 orbit and 8 states (`census-noSW9.json`).

A direct check agrees: SW9’s class (flag 3, arity 9, admitted in exp-250) is contained
in a D4 image of 5500414, and no other admitted class is.
SW9 has arity 9, so it is not among the 90 arity-at-most-8 selector flags that define
the frame, which is how an already-certified orbit could be drawn.
The count claim stands; only the attribution changes.

### F2: Stale Non-Closure Count and Range (Correction)

The verdict paragraph says “the four counted states that did not close each reached a
producer fixed point in 634 to 924 s”. It was written before index 6 finished.
There are five counted non-closures, at 634.2 to 1,296.6 s of process CPU, and index 8’s
own row reads 925 s. The paragraph’s pointer to H-274 covers only the first four: H-274
froze masks 2817021, 3063677, 2878207 and 2784767, and index 6 stalled after it was
registered.

### F3: Round Status and Wall (Correction)

The opening still reads “It is in progress, and gains a row per draw until all twelve
have run”, though all twelve have run.
`effort.wall_seconds: 11744` and the prose “to the fifth verification at 12:02 UTC”
measure the round to the verdict, but the record’s results include indices 6 and 9,
which ended at 12:07:07 and 12:03:38, and H-264’s notes keep the remaining runs “for the
cost estimate”. The round’s wall is 08:46:26 to 12:07:07, 12,041 s.
`stopped_by: criterion` stays correct.
The generated views (the ledger’s “195.7m wall” for H-264 and SYNOPSIS’s effort row)
follow on `packing-ledger render`.

### F4: Three Precision Fixes (Correction)

- **Closure CPU range:** “351 to 868 s” truncates; the table rounds the same receipts to
  352 and 869.
- **Wall-to-CPU ratio:** “about 2.5 times” held for the first pair of runs.
  Over all thirteen kernel runs it is 1.86, and per run it ranges from 1.04 to 2.50.
- **Control mask:** the table labels the control “1900015 (endpoint)”, the orbit
  representative. The run was on the endpoint’s own state, mask 3439615.

Elsewhere the table mixes rounding and truncation within one second (2,072, 827, 333
truncated; 869, 989, 1,940 rounded).
That is not material and is left alone.

### F5: The Extrapolation and the Unsampled Strata Are Missing (Gap)

H-264’s claim asks what the closures “extrapolate to over the frame’s drawn strata”.
Its regime requires strata the draw does not reach to be “reported as unsampled, not
extrapolated”, and the plan’s handoff lists the extrapolation.
At `9bcddf6e9` exp-252 said the extrapolation would follow when the last two draws were
in; `0b94645d0` replaced that sentence and supplied neither the extrapolation nor the
unsampled list. A review may not write new analysis, so this is left to the operator.
These scope figures, from the survey receipt, bound any extrapolation:

- The ten counted draws come from 8 strata holding 1,354 of the frame’s 2,255
  non-endpoint orbits; the two distance-2 draws come from 2 strata holding 74.
- 21 strata holding 827 orbits are unsampled.
  430 orbits are in the 9 strata at distance 8 or more (the figure in H-264’s regime);
  397 are in 12 strata at distances 2 to 6 (the six c<=2 strata there, c3/i>=5 at d4 and
  d6, c3/i<=3/d6, c4/i<=3/d6, c3/i4/d2 and c3/i<=3/d2). H-264’s parenthetical “every
  distance-8-or-more stratum, 430 of 2,256 orbits” is right only if strata are read as
  distance bands. Under the survey’s own corner-by-interior-by-distance strata it
  understates the unsampled set by 397 orbits.
- The draw is Sainte-Laguë weighted toward distances 2 and 4 (weights 4 and 2), so the
  raw 5/10 is not a frame-wide closure rate.
  The verdict does not depend on that rate, since the criterion counts draws.

### F6: Per-State Table Omissions (Observation)

The plan’s output list asks for producer, checker and verifier seconds and peak memory
per state. exp-252 gives the producer and checker split only for closures.
The watchdog logs host memory, not per-job peaks, so the per-job peak was never
measured. The split for every run is in the draw table above.
