## From Certificate to Bound

The argument is the one in the review of #843, the same day
([review-2026-10-31-couzo-843-130.md](review-2026-10-31-couzo-843-130.md#from-certificate-to-bound)).
Each certificate’s comment line states the half-angle format: n unit squares, each a
centre (x, y) and t = tan(θ/2), with rational unit edge directions.
Four facts decided over ℚ give s(n) ≤ S:

1. each rotation is on the unit circle;
2. there are exactly n rows of three rational fields;
3. each closed square lies in [0, S]², by support functions (route A) and by corners
   (route B);
4. interiors are pairwise disjoint, by an edge normal of one square that separates the
   projections (route A, every pair), or by an exact clipped area of 0 for every pair
   whose centres are closer than √2, the rest disjoint by their discs (route B).
