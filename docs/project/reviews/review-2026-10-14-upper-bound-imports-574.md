# Review: PingYou Ltd’s Certificate Refinements of PR #574 (T-174)

**Date:** 14 October 2026. **Verdict: accepted, for finite feasibility only.** T-174’s
64 rational certificates prove the upper bounds the packet’s frozen claim record states,
each decided on both maintained routes in the import lane’s replay and again here on the
third exact route with its six controls, every number they share agreeing.
Its four algebraic certificates, at 70, 153, 207 and 302, are decided on one route,
`sqpack`’s exact witness verifier over each witness’s field, whose receipts were
replayed here again; no second route over a number field exists in this repository, and
none was run. Every pinned file and the four release assets were fetched at their pins
and found at the sizes and digests the packet and the pull request’s manifests record,
and every rational fact is the member of the release asset it names, read again by the
third route’s own readers.
The entry’s statement that each count but 176 regularizes a registered packing was
measured pose by pose: it holds with no square moved beyond $10^{-3}$ at 28 counts,
holds one to one with moved squares at 33, and pairs no square one to one at 199, 292
and 303. Seven findings are recorded, none blocking.
T-174 can take `V3`/`C3`; its rational certificates should become the houses at the 60
counts where they are smallest, and the three algebraic ones that are smallest wait on
the owner (RP-3).

## The Third Route on the 64 Rational Counts

Every positive passes and every control reaches its required outcome.
Every side, centre and rotation equals the maintained routes’ retained input; every
least wall clearance, exactly 0 at all 64, equals theirs; every least Euclidean distance
is at least their separating-axis gap; and every side is the exact side the frozen claim
record states and the 40-place print it offers rounds up.
T-174’s claim prints no side per count, so the frozen record is what the side is held to
(`CLAIMS_WITHOUT_SIDES`). The positives and controls took 2.7 s (88) to 97.0 s (146)
each; the receipt’s own totals, recomputed by the third route, are the 192 jobs,
9,164,850 pair decisions, 14,497.02 route CPU seconds and 16,968.51 job wall seconds the
entry’s notes print.

The certificates are exact contact solutions: in every positive a square touches a wall,
and at 63 counts two squares touch, the least Euclidean distance exactly 0, as both
maintained routes report; at 261 the closest pair is $4.84 \times 10^{-89}$ apart.
The contact controls then start from contact: the side shrunk by the least far-wall
clearance is the side itself, and $10^{-40}$ less is refused; the closest pair is
already in contact, and $10^{-30}$ of the centre distance further is refused.

Every rational fact was read from its release-asset member: the manifest the row names
must be the file the packet pins, the asset must have the size and SHA-256 the manifest
states, and the member the SHA-256 the row states (`archive_member`). The fact is then
the packing the member states, read by the third route’s PingYou JSON reader, and at 62
counts also the packing of the native corners witness beside it in the second asset; at
146 and 261 it is the supplement’s rational centre-basis witness, read with its labelled
squares in the order listed (`derive_center_basis`). The 126 upstream checks, 64 members
and 62 witnesses, found nothing amiss, and the four assets have the sizes and SHA-256
that `gh release download` gave and the manifests state.

## Findings

**RP-1, non-blocking: at 199 and 292 the certificate is not the named packing by pose.**
No symmetry pairs it one to one with T-127 at 199 or with T-146 or T-136 at 292, though
each side is within $1.5 \times 10^{-19}$ and $1.8 \times 10^{-19}$ of the named one.
At 199 the poses are those #593 states later at that count; at 292 they are no retained
certificate’s. The `regularizations` items should keep `basis: source-claim` with
`one_to_one: false`, and the reply should ask which certificate each count started from.

**RP-2, non-blocking: #593 restates PR #574’s packings.** At 42 of the 64 counts #593’s
certificate is this one’s packing: within $10^{-9}$ at 22 (68, 102, 105, 110, 123, 126,
156, 172, 182, 210, 238, 239, 266 to 271, 295, 301, 303 and 305) and within $10^{-3}$ at
20; at the other 22 it is a different certificate by pose: at 211, 241 and 304 Couzo’s
`b85dee2` and Ostashev’s #575, as the companion review measures, and at 84, 86 and 292
T-130’s and T-146’s certificates within $8 \times 10^{-16}$, by the third route’s
`unmoved` and `arrangement_gap` run as a diagnostic outside the recorded run.
#593 first committed those 42 between 15:51Z on 13 October and 01:24Z on 14 October,
after PR #574’s 00:24Z on 13 October.
This bears on #593’s other 71 certificates, which no entry registers, and on the owner’s
decision 2 on #602 about exact ties; it establishes no copying, as RQ-1 of the companion
review says.

**RP-3, non-blocking: the algebraic certificates rest on one route.** At 70, 153 and 207
they are the smallest sides known, by $8.9 \times 10^{-20}$, $1.6 \times 10^{-14}$ and
$2.4 \times 10^{-12}$. Adopting them would put a verified ceiling on `sqpack.field`’s
arithmetic alone, which no house does now; this review recommends `V3`/`C3`, as the
ladder allows one complete replay, and leaves the adoption at those three counts to the
owner. The conservative course keeps the case at 70 and takes the smallest rational
certificate at 153 and 207 (T-173, under decision 1’s credit), recording the algebraic
ones as pending a second route.

**RP-4, non-blocking: Ostashev’s 176.** The certificate at 176 is his #575 packing,
first committed at 23:06:00Z on 12 October, before PR #574’s 00:24:05Z; T-174 is the
only registered carrier of it.
The owner’s decision 8 on #602 covers it; whichever entry holds the house, the packing’s
credit is Ostashev’s.

**RP-5, non-blocking: the entry’s claim says each count but 176 is a regularization.**
In the record’s own voice that holds by pose at 28 counts; elsewhere it is the source’s
statement. The claim should say which, with this review as `measured_in`.

**RP-6, non-blocking: the 21 unrecorded rational and 56 unrecorded algebraic counts**
stay unreplayed, priced by the import lane at under an hour and about 2.1 hours; they
are no part of T-174.

**RP-7, non-blocking: the third route lifts Python’s integer-text limit for its own
numbers.** Margins and squared distances of these certificates pass 4,300 digits; the
route writes and reads back only its own numbers with the limit lifted and reads every
certificate under it, as `devtools.regularize_evand` does.
