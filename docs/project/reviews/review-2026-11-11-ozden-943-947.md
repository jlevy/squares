## The Claim and What It Needs

For each $n$ the issue claims $s(n) \le C$, where $C$ is its 12-place print.
The certificate proves $s(n) \le S$ for its side $S$: there are $n$ closed unit squares,
rotated freely, with pairwise disjoint interiors inside the closed square $[0, S]^2$.
Since $C \ge S$, the claim follows.
The six hypotheses are the precedent’s: the reading (envelope, `corners`, `rational`,
size `'1'`, `lower-left`, `x-right-y-up`, canonical $p/q$ scalars), the count, unit
squares, containment, disjoint interiors, and the rotation sense.

`l83_check.py` decides the first two through the reader, and the next three twice.

- **Method A** (`l83_sat.py`, `fractions.Fraction`). A square is accepted when
  $u = p_1 - p_0$ has $u \cdot u = 1$, $p_3 - p_0 = \pm u^\perp$ and
  $p_2 = p_0 + u + (p_3 - p_0)$; its edge normals are then exact rational unit vectors.
  A pair’s margin is the largest projection gap over its four edge normals: positive is
  clear, zero is contact, negative is overlap.
  A wall’s clearance is the least corner coordinate’s distance to it.
- **Method B** (`l83_clip.py`, `gmpy2.mpq`). A polygon is a unit square when its four
  edges have squared length 1, its turns one sign and its area 1. A pair is the subject
  clipped by the other’s four closed half-planes: empty is clear, area 0 is contact,
  positive area is overlap.
  A wall is the square clipped by the wall’s closed outer half-plane, read the same
  three ways, and containment is clipping to the box keeping area 1.

The two share only the reader, which hands them integers.
The reader composes the YAML with PyYAML, fails closed on any layout it does not expect,
and requires the JSON twin, read with the standard library, to hold exactly the same
tree. The verdict is `ACCEPT` only when both methods accept every square, wall and pair
and their three-way classes agree everywhere.

## Is the Improvement Real?

`l83_houses.py` writes each house witness on `main` as exact corners, decides it with
the frozen checker at its own side, and measures its uniform-contraction margin exactly.
Every centre is scaled by a common $\lambda \le 1$, orientations kept, and the box
shrinks to the packing’s extent.
On each axis a pair’s gap is linear in $\lambda$, so the first contact $\lambda^*$ is
exact, and the margin is the house side less the contracted extent.
The contracted packing is then decided at $\lambda^*$ (accepted, the binding pair
touching) and $10^{-30}$ below it (refused), over every square and pair.
