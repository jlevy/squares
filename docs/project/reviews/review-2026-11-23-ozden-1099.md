## Invariants and method

The invariants were: exactly 264 distinct square IDs; four cyclic rational corners per
square; unit edges and right angles; every square contained in the closed container; and
disjoint interiors for every unordered pair.
Near-contact checks must distinguish zero from small positive distance without a
floating-point tolerance.

[`check_n264.py`](../../../packing/resources/web/ozden-s264-upper-bound-2026-11-23/review/check_n264.py.txt)
is an independent, standard-library-only checker.
It imports no registry geometry or submitter module and executes no command from the
witness metadata. Its strict parser consumes all 264 corner blocks of the pinned file,
including the two single-quoted zero coordinates.
It validates the observed corner-list subset, not the complete Witness/v2 schema.

For each square, it clears denominators locally to obtain integer points `v/D`. Every
edge’s squared norm must equal `D²`; adjacent edges have zero dot product; the opposite
vertices have equal sums; and the orientation is nondegenerate.
These checks establish a convex unit square in the supplied cyclic order.
Containment follows from exact integer comparisons of its vertex extrema with zero and
the rational side.

For every pair, a positive gap between its axis-aligned bounding intervals proves
separation. Pairs not already separated by more than `1/100000000` are checked on both
edge directions of both squares.
For squares these are also the required edge-normal directions.
Integer cross multiplication compares the four-vertex projection extrema; a nonnegative
gap on at least one axis proves disjoint interiors.

The near-contact pass uses only exact integer comparisons.
Axis gaps exceeding the threshold exclude a pair because Euclidean distance is at least
its projected gap. For the remaining candidates, the checker takes the minimum of all 32
directed vertex-to-edge squared distances.
Endpoint distances and perpendicular distances with projection inside the segment cover
the closest-feature cases for disjoint squares.
With common scale `Q = D_a D_b`, these squared distances share denominator `Q⁴`. The
minimum and all threshold counts are exact rational results; decimal strings only help
readers interpret them.
The exclusions also prove that the reported positive minimum is global among all pairs,
not just the candidates checked in detail.

## Finding

**N1 — Low: certificate representation description differs from the pinned file.** The
issue’s Certificate paragraph calls this a “centre + rational half-angle basis” witness,
whereas `n-264-candidate.yaml:11` declares `representation: corners` and the file
supplies explicit rational vertices.
The corner geometry passes independently.
**Fix:** describe the distributed certificate as rational corners, and separately
identify any centre/half-angle representation used during its construction.
