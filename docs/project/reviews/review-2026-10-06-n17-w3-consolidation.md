## Selected Sequence

existing scientific contracts.

### Proof Interfaces and Portability

Publish the hosted objects through `think-jhgi`, fetch all manifest objects into a clean
location, verify their integrity and independently replay a representative full
certificate.
That establishes replay for that certificate; full-collection replay remains
a separate obligation.
Resolve the held-control decision separately, recording exactly which closures become
admissible.

Write the composed proof’s interfaces now: centred container and label/state assignment;
D4 transfer; closed-cell and branch coverage; cap monotonicity; exact-root enclosure;
near-endpoint cap; capture output frame; slider box; local radius and theorem.
The output is an obligation table with inputs and checkers, not a completed theorem.
A mismatch at a join blocks that join rather than being deferred to final prose.

### Capture Discriminator and Alternate Terminal Theorem

Repair the hull compression pull under `think-juy9`, retaining exact membership checks.
Run R9’s known-case control before interpreting n17 dynamics.
Codify its intermediate outcomes explicitly: a control extent between the proposed 0.5
success and 0.9 failure thresholds is inconclusive, not a pass.

Measure R9’s angle response at doubled and quadrupled rows on the declared owners.
Compare with a onefold-row baseline rebuilt using the same repaired producer; keep
historical pilot-2 comparisons separate.
Freeze side-W2’s into-wall turn side and side-N0/side-N2’s full turn ranges, and verify
that refinement actually occurred.
Freeze all-owner, mixed and intermediate outcomes before running.
Its proposed 20-percent and 40-percent contractions support the row-driven reading;
changes within 5 percent at fourfold rows support the box-set reading.
Neither outcome proves capture.
Position success must cover every coordinate required by the terminal theorem, not
merely one coordinate contracting by 10 percent.

Run the cheap widened-projection/dual-sheet reconnaissance before committing to R9 stage
1’s roughly 20 CPU-hour scaling run.
It can proceed alongside the compression repair and known-case control.
It can assess degeneracy, mixed-angle directions and feature-forcing margins without
waiting for the full pilot.
Keep its cost exploratory until measured.
X-048’s million-patch falsifier and R9’s 100,000-patch routing rule need one newly
frozen operational criterion; do not revise a prior experiment after its result.

For a fixed separating-feature branch, the LP value is a supremum of valid dual lower
bounds. Any outer minimum is over the different feasible feature branches.
A negative slope of one sampled dual sheet does not refute the primal theorem.
Sampled basis counts and unseen-basis estimates price reconnaissance; they certify
neither patch coverage nor the number of patches needed.
Require a complete branch/patch cover and valid lower bounds for a theorem.

Both capture engines owe a separate outer reach test from the actual cells.
A contraction from a `1/1024` seed cannot discharge that obligation.
If neither engine has a plausible cells-to-feature bridge, reconsider the cover or
capture representation before scaling.

untested strata. Record producer, self-check and standing-verifier costs, memory and
incomplete outcomes.
The sample closure rate alone must not price a full sweep.

After the held-control decision, reuse the established 48-round/2,304-row/octagon recipe

controls.

Scope C5 for consistency-limited cases after that discriminator.

including seams. One closed child never excludes its parent.
Define a cap-tightening disposition for states feasible or unresolved at `U` that still
must be excluded at certified caps at least `S*`, potentially tightened from `U` to
`U'`; do not assume H-273 has shown this class empty.

Native branch-and-bound routing resumes only after a newly frozen calibration across
representative classes and node/admission ceilings.
Cache and selective-refinement optimizations remain separately measured producer
proposals.
