## From Certificate to Bound

`check_certificates.py` reads each certificate as the format its comment line states: n
unit squares, each a centre (x, y) and t = tan(θ/2), so that its edge directions u = ((1
− t²)/(1 + t²), 2t/(1 + t²)) and v = (−u_y, u_x) are exact rational unit vectors.
The bound follows from four facts, each decided over ℚ:

1. **Each rotation is on the unit circle**, by the identity (1 − t²)² + (2t)² = (1 +
   t²)², which the reader also checks row by row.
2. **There are exactly n squares.** The reader refuses any other row count, any field
   that is not an integer or p/q, and any row without three fields.
3. **Each square lies in the closed box [0, S]².** Route A checks the four corners;
   route B checks each square’s support h(|u_x| + |u_y|) against each wall.
4. **Interiors are pairwise disjoint.** Two convex polygons have disjoint interiors
   exactly when some edge normal of one of them separates their projections, touching
   allowed: if the interiors are disjoint, the origin is not interior to the Minkowski
   difference, whose edges are parallel to the squares’ edges.
   Route A decides every pair this way, with no filter.
   Route B decides the pairs whose centres are closer than √2(h_i + h_j) by the exact
   area of their clipped intersection, which must be 0; every other pair is disjoint
   because each square lies in the closed disc of radius h√2 about its centre and its
   interior in the open disc.
