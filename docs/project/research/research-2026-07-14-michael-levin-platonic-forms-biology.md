# Research: Michael Levin, Platonic Forms, and the Mathematics of Living Systems

**Date:** 2026-07-14 (last updated 2026-07-14)

**Author:** OpenAI Codex

**Status:** Complete

## Executive Summary

Michael Levin’s recent claim that biological forms and minds “ingress” from a structured
**Platonic Space** is best understood as a speculative extension of a much larger,
empirically grounded research program.
His laboratory has produced important work on developmental bioelectricity, regenerative
pattern control, the plasticity of cell collectives, Xenobots, Anthrobots, and
computational models of basal cognition.
Those results support a serious scientific thesis: genes are not geometric blueprints,
and development is not merely a feed-forward execution of DNA. Cells use biochemical,
mechanical, and bioelectric communication to reach, preserve, and sometimes revise
large-scale anatomical outcomes.

The stronger Platonic thesis is different.
In the 2025 manuscript [“Ingressing Minds”](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/5g2xj_v1),
a series of 2025 talks, and a more explicit
[March 2026 statement](https://thoughtforms.life/a-short-argument-on-platonic-space-variable-agency-patterns-that-in-form-physics-biology-computer-science-and-cognitive-science/),
Levin proposes that:

1. Mathematical and computational truths are discovered rather than invented and form a
   structured, nonphysical possibility space.
2. Physical systems gain “free lunches” when their architecture makes mathematical or
   computational capacities available without separately evolving or engineering every
   consequence.
3. Evolution and bioengineering construct physical “interfaces” or “pointers” that
   access patterns in that space.
4. The space may contain not only low-agency structures, such as geometrical and
   computational regularities, but also high-agency patterns—“kinds of minds.”
5. The mind–brain relationship may therefore resemble the relationship between a
   mathematical structure and its physical realization.

Levin is unusually candid about the status of the proposal.
He calls the ordered structure of Platonic Space a metaphysical stance that he cannot
prove, says he does not know whether the space is finite, eternal, or dynamically
changing, and presents the project as provisional.
The 2025 manuscript is a preprint, not a peer-reviewed experimental demonstration.
Levin’s peer-reviewed 2026 framework with David Resnik, the **Technological Approach to
Mind Everywhere** (TAME), is more conservative.
It treats talk of goals, intelligence, and agency as testable hypotheses about which
intervention protocols will predict and control a system.
It explicitly says that this approach is compatible with physical mechanism and does not
require irreducible minds.
The operational TAME thesis therefore does not entail the later nonphysical ontology.

The empirical evidence also underdetermines the ontology.
Representative findings include:

- Briefly perturbing gap-junction-mediated bioelectric signaling in one planarian system
  can produce stable, rewritable changes in later regenerative anatomy.
- Altering membrane voltage can induce ectopic eye tissue in *Xenopus*, and some
  tadpoles can behaviorally use grafted eyes whose nerves project into unusual body
  regions.
- Frog and human cells can self-assemble outside their normal contexts into motile
  Xenobots and Anthrobots with behaviors not selected in those exact configurations.
- Neural cellular automata and gene-regulatory-network models can be trained to grow,
  repair, classify, associate signals, or reveal previously unrecognized competencies.

These are evidence for plastic, multiscale biological organization.
They are not by themselves evidence that information entered from outside physical
dynamics. The same results are compatible with inherited cellular machinery,
exaptation—the reuse of an evolved feature in a new role—generic epithelial repair,
altered boundary conditions, multistability, dynamical attractors, and selection for
flexible developmental systems.
A configuration never encountered by natural selection can display a new capacity
because its component cells retain cilia, adhesion, ion channels, stress responses,
transcriptional programs, and collective-control mechanisms shaped by long evolutionary
histories. “Not selected for in this exact arrangement” is not equivalent to “not
explainable by evolved physical mechanisms.”

Several established and emerging mathematical programs capture the productive core of
Levin’s intuition without requiring Platonic causation:

| Program | What It Contributes | Relation to Levin’s Claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reaction–diffusion, mechanics, and geometry | Physical mechanisms that generate and constrain form | Mathematics structures possible outcomes through physical dynamics |
| Dynamical systems and attractor landscapes | Multiple stable states, robustness, state persistence, and anatomical transitions | Formalizes target states without treating them as external entities |
| Cybernetics and control theory | Setpoints, error correction, feedback, and controllability | Gives operational content to goal-directed morphogenesis |
| Genotype–phenotype maps and developmental bias | Unequal accessibility of phenotypes and “arrival of the frequent” | Explains why evolution explores a highly biased morphospace |
| Neural cellular automata | Learned local rules that grow and regenerate global patterns | Demonstrates compact, distributed morphogenetic policies |
| Active inference | Probabilistic self-organization toward expected states | Models apparent goal pursuit, although its biological scope is debated |
| Information theory and causal emergence | Integration, synergy, functional connectivity, and useful macrovariables | Tests higher-level organization without adding nonphysical causes |
| Topological data analysis and compositional systems biology | Quantifies tissue shape and connects models across scales | Supplies tools for mapping empirical morphospaces and interfaces |
| Natural induction and learning in lineages | Adaptive reorganization within plastic networks | Broadens evolutionary dynamics while remaining a physical model |

The mathematically important insight is that possibility spaces are structured and
biological search is biased.
This is mainstream in theoretical morphospace, evolutionary developmental biology,
genotype–phenotype maps, and complex-systems research.
The philosophical leap occurs when a constraint or invariant is promoted from a
mathematical explanation to an autonomous causal source.
Levin invokes Judea Pearl’s counterfactual account, but Pearl’s causal calculus
ordinarily compares interventions on variables within a specified structural model.
“If the distribution of prime numbers were different” is not an ordinary intervention in
such a model; it changes mathematical necessity rather than manipulating a physically
variable cause. Philosophy of science already recognizes that mathematics can provide
genuine **noncausal** explanation.
Explanatory indispensability does not by itself establish a two-way causal interface
with a nonphysical realm.

The Platonic program can nevertheless generate valuable science if it is translated into
risky predictions. The strongest tests would predict new competencies before a construct
is built, compare those predictions against mechanistic and learned dynamical models,
quantify how capability changes with interface architecture, and show cross-substrate
regularities that cannot be attributed to common training, selection, chemistry, or
boundary conditions.
Cataloging surprises after they occur would map biological possibility, but it would not
distinguish Platonism from ordinary scientific discovery.

The balanced conclusion is therefore twofold.
Levin’s experimental and modeling program belongs in modern developmental, synthetic,
and systems biology, although some of its cognitive language remains contested.
His literal Platonic Space of variable-agency forms is a provocative research heuristic
and metaphysical proposal, not an established biological theory.
Mathematics unquestionably shapes biological evolution by defining invariants,
constraints, dynamics, and biased spaces of reachable form.
Whether mathematical structures also exist as causally active minds outside physical
realization remains unsupported by discriminating evidence.

## Questions Answered

1. What does Levin mean by “Platonic Space,” and how has the idea changed from his
   earlier bioelectric and TAME work?
2. Which experimental results motivate the proposal, and what do those experiments
   establish when stated narrowly?
3. What are Xenobots and Anthrobots, and how much evolutionary novelty do they
   demonstrate?
4. How do goal states, cognitive light cones, the Continuum of Persuadability, and
   “agential materials” fit together?
5. In what established senses does mathematics shape development and evolution?
6. Which newer mathematical approaches could improve biological explanation and control?
7. Does mathematical explanation imply nonphysical causation or a separate realm of
   forms?
8. What observations would distinguish Levin’s Platonic interpretation from
   dynamical-systems, evo-devo, and mechanistic alternatives?

## Scope and Evidence Standard

This report emphasizes Levin’s peer-reviewed experimental and conceptual work,
especially from 2020 through July 2026, and his 2025–2026 statements about Platonic
Space.
Older work is included where it supplies an indispensable experimental foundation.
The
[Tufts Levin Lab publication list](https://as.tufts.edu/biology/levin-lab/publications)
and [official media index](https://as.tufts.edu/biology/levin-lab/media) were checked
against journal, PubMed, conference, OSF, and talk records.

Four evidence levels are used:

1. **Established or replicated framework:** a standard mathematical construction or
   well-supported biological principle.
2. **Published result:** a peer-reviewed experiment or model, with its organism, sample,
   and boundary conditions kept in view.
3. **Research interpretation:** a proposed unifying vocabulary or model that is
   compatible with the data but not uniquely implied by them.
4. **Metaphysical speculation:** an ontological claim for which no discriminating
   experiment has yet been supplied.

The report does not assume that “emergent,” “cognitive,” “informational,” or “causal”
have one uncontested meaning.
Each is assessed through the measurements and interventions that give it content.
Talks and essays establish what Levin claims and how the proposal has evolved; they are
not treated as independent evidence for the claim.

Relevant commercial relationships are disclosed in the primary papers.
The 2023 Anthrobot study was partly funded through a Tufts sponsored-research agreement
with Astonishing Labs, and Levin was a scientific cofounder
([paper disclosure](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10811512/)). The 2025
Anthrobot follow-up also reports support from Astonishing Labs
([paper disclosure](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12376695/)). The 2025 basal
Xenobot transcriptomics paper identifies Levin as a scientific cofounder of Fauna
Systems, a company working on AI-designed biorobotics
([paper disclosure](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12015265/)). These
disclosures do not determine whether the results are correct, but they increase the
importance of independent replication and prospective validation.

Levin’s ideas overlap with the collection’s prior studies of mathematical structure and
biological possibility.
Chaitin’s metabiology and simplicity-biased genotype–phenotype maps are assessed in the
[Chaitin research report](research-2026-07-13-gregory-chaitin-ait-omega-metabiology.md).
All-computation and observer-dependent foundations are covered separately in the
[Ruliad report](research-2026-07-13-ruliad-foundations.md).
Similar vocabulary does not make these programs equivalent.

## 1. From Developmental Bioelectricity to Platonic Space

### 1.1 The Empirical Program Came First

Levin trained in both computer science and biology and has long framed morphogenesis as
an information-processing and control problem.
The starting observation is standard but consequential: DNA specifies proteins and
regulatory machinery, not a literal three-dimensional list of where every cell will go.
Organismal form arises through interactions among gene regulation, signaling, mechanics,
geometry, metabolism, and environmental conditions.
A mainstream review by Claudio Collinet and Thomas Lecuit similarly treats gene
expression, biochemical cues, mechanics, geometry, feedback, and self-organization as
interacting sources of morphogenetic information
([2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41580-020-00318-6)).

Levin’s distinctive contribution has been to emphasize **developmental bioelectricity**:
slowly changing patterns of resting membrane voltage, produced by ion channels and pumps
and coupled among non-neural cells through gap junctions.
These voltage states influence downstream transcription, proliferation, migration, and
differentiation. The proposal is not that bioelectricity replaces genes or biochemistry.
It is that physiological networks form an additional, dynamically rewritable layer of
control. Levin, Giovanni Pezzulo, and Joshua Finkelstein survey the molecular basis in
[“Endogenous Bioelectric Signaling Networks”](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28633567/),
while Levin and Christopher Martyniuk formulate an attractor-based “bioelectric code” in
their [2018 review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28855098/).

This work leads naturally to three claims of increasing strength:

- **Mechanistic claim:** voltage gradients and gap-junction connectivity can be
  instructive variables in development, regeneration, and disease.
- **Control-theoretic claim:** some tissues behave as feedback systems that reach
  anatomical setpoints despite perturbations.
- **Cognitive interpretation:** those systems solve problems in anatomical space and can
  profitably be modeled using concepts from neuroscience and behavioral science.

The first is experimentally established in specific systems.
The second is a productive modeling framework supported by robust regulation.
The third is Levin’s broader TAME interpretation and depends on operational definitions
of goal and intelligence.

### 1.2 TAME: Agency as an Experimental Stance

Levin’s [2022 TAME paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35401131/) proposes analyzing
systems by the size and kinds of goals they can pursue in different problem spaces.
A **cognitive light cone** is the spatiotemporal scale of states that can motivate a
system’s action.
A cell may regulate local pH or stress; a tissue may repair an organ; an
animal can act over a larger physical and temporal horizon.
The **Continuum of Persuadability** classifies systems by the kinds of intervention
needed to change their behavior—from direct rewiring, through rewards and training, to
rational argument at familiar human scales.

In the two-part 2026 article
[“Mind Everywhere”](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13752-025-00524-5), Levin
and David Resnik make the methodological commitment explicit.
Calling a system goal-directed is a hypothesis that cybernetic, behavioral, or cognitive
tools will yield better prediction and control.
Intelligence is graded by flexible success: reaching a similar goal by different means.
An agent need not be conscious, and its goals need not be deliberative purposes.

This formulation is compatible with mechanism.
The authors answer an objection from scientific materialism by saying that they do not
assume metaphysically irreducible minds and that agency terms can be compatible with
physics and chemistry.
Thus:

> TAME licenses higher-level, mentalistic models when they improve intervention.
> It does not, by itself, claim that a nonphysical mind supplies missing causal energy
> or information.

That distinction is essential.
A thermostat, cell, tissue, animal, and person can be placed on a graded map of control
capacities without asserting that every setpoint is an independently existing Platonic
entity.

### 1.3 Agential Materials and Evolution

Levin’s phrase **agential material** describes matter whose components already have
local competencies. Evolution does not build organisms from passive bricks; it modifies
collections of cells that sense, act, communicate, and regulate themselves.
Selection can therefore exploit lower-level problem solving rather than micromanage
every cell movement.
Levin develops the argument in
[“Darwin’s Agential Materials”](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37156924/) and with
Richard Watson in work on the collective intelligence of evolution and development.

This is a significant evolutionary claim, but it need not oppose natural selection.
Selection can favor developmental architectures that are robust, modular, plastic, and
capable of correcting perturbations.
Those architectures then change which phenotypic variations arrive and how reliably
genotypes produce viable bodies.
The result is a richer genotype–phenotype map, not an escape from evolutionary history.

## 2. What the Experiments Establish

### 2.1 Rewritable Pattern Memory in Planarians

Planarian flatworms are powerful models of whole-body regeneration.
In a 2017 study, Levin and colleagues briefly inhibited endogenous bioelectric coupling
during regeneration in *Dugesia japonica*. The treatment produced double-headed worms
and anatomically normal-looking “cryptic” worms.
When cryptic animals were later cut in plain water, a stable fraction again regenerated
with two heads. The altered outcome correlated with a changed global voltage pattern,
could outlast the initial treatment, and could be reset by a different bioelectric
intervention ([Durant et al. 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28538159/)).

The justified conclusion is strong: in this system, a transient physiological
perturbation can install a persistent, rewritable state that affects future anatomical
regeneration without changing the genome.
It supports multistable physiological memory and top-down control variables.

It does not show that the remembered shape is stored outside the worm.
Stable electrophysiological network states, their biochemical consequences, and their
coupling to regeneration are physical candidate mechanisms.
“Target morphology” is an apt control-theoretic description, but the experiment does not
locate that target in a nonphysical space.

### 2.2 Regulative Development in Tadpoles

Levin often illustrates regulative morphogenesis with perturbed *Xenopus* faces.
In one study, membrane-voltage manipulation initially displaced or malformed
craniofacial structures, after which substantial aspects of jaw and branchial
organization normalized during development
([Vandenberg, Adams, and Levin 2012](https://doi.org/10.1002/dvdy.23770)). The result is
evidence that development can correct relative positions through unusual trajectories.
It should not be summarized as every scrambled organ invariably returning to a perfect
face; the study reports structure-specific and incomplete normalization.

Related work found that a narrow membrane-voltage range can induce organized ectopic eye
tissue in non-eye regions
([Pai et al. 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22159581/)). In grafting experiments,
some tadpoles with an eye on the tail could use it in a light-mediated learning task
even when projections entered the trunk or spinal cord.
The effect was not universal: the paper reports reliable two-day learning in 20% of
tested animals with that innervation pattern
([Blackiston and Levin 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23447666/)).

These findings demonstrate developmental and neural plasticity.
They motivate the question “how does the system know what to do?”
but do not force the answer “by accessing an external form.”
Nervous-system remapping, distributed sensory integration, bioelectric prepatterns,
feedback, and selection for robust development remain testable physical explanations.

### 2.3 Xenobots

Xenobots are assemblies of embryonic *Xenopus laevis* cells placed into configurations
that do not occur as normal frogs.
Early work combined in-silico evolutionary design with microsurgery; later constructs
could self-assemble more extensively.
They can move through aqueous environments, coordinate ciliary activity, heal some
damage, and display behaviors that differ from those cells’ usual role in a tadpole.

The most publicized result is **kinematic replication**. Motile spheroids gathered loose
frog cells into piles that matured into ciliated offspring.
Under the reported conditions, ordinary spheroids reached at most two rounds; an
AI-designed semitoroidal shape reached at most four.
The process required researchers to supply large numbers of dissociated cells and did
not constitute open-ended autonomous reproduction
([Kriegman et al. 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34845026/)).

A 2025 transcriptomic study compared basal Xenobots with age-matched embryos and
reported substantial, variable gene-expression changes and a response to an acoustic
stimulus ([Pai et al. 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40263484/)). These data are
valuable because they begin to characterize what cells do when released from their
normal organismal constraints.
They do not establish that the response, locomotion, or replication policy existed as a
mind before the construct.
Frog cells bring with them cilia, cytoskeletons, adhesion, signaling, stress responses,
and an evolved genome.
New geometry and boundary conditions reorganize those inherited capacities.

### 2.4 Anthrobots

Anthrobots are motile spheroids self-assembled from adult human airway epithelial cells.
The cells normally use cilia to move mucus; when cultured under new conditions, they can
produce 30–500-micrometer multicellular bodies with different shapes and movement
trajectories. In a two-dimensional cultured-neuron scratch assay, some Anthrobot
aggregates bridged the gap and were associated with local closure beneath the bridge
([Gumuskaya et al. 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38032125/)). This was an
in-vitro monolayer assay, not healing of a nervous system in an animal.

A 2025 follow-up reported multiple self-construction paths, large transcriptomic
changes, short-term shape recovery after needle damage, and younger methylation-clock
estimates after Anthrobot formation
([Gumuskaya et al. 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40479594/)). The authors
themselves note that self-repair is plausibly an emergent property of generic epithelial
dynamics. This is exactly the kind of mechanistic alternative that must be compared
against a Platonic interpretation.

### 2.5 Aneural Information Structure

The lab has also begun to measure information flow directly.
A 2025 study recorded calcium dynamics from six *Xenopus* epidermal organoids and used
mutual information and network analysis.
It found nonrandom, modular functional connectivity, hub-like regions, and changes after
injury ([Blackiston et al. 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40228211/)). The sample
was small and the authors appropriately describe the result as suggestive.
Functional connectivity is statistical dependence; it does not by itself establish
cognition, semantic content, or causal influence from outside the tissue.

A computational 2025 study added an electrostatic field and feedback to a model of
membrane-voltage patterning.
Machine-learning optimization found transient boundary stimuli that generated face-like
prepatterns ([Manicka and Levin 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrp.2025.102865)). This
is a proof of principle in a model, not an in-vivo demonstration that a long-range field
stores a face. Its importance is methodological: it yields candidate interventions that
can be tested experimentally.

## 3. Levin’s Platonic Space Proposal

### 3.1 The 2025 “Ingressing Minds” Manuscript

The 49-page manuscript
[“Ingressing Minds: Causal Patterns Beyond Genetics and Environment in Natural, Synthetic, and Hybrid Embodiments”](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/5g2xj_v1)
is the fullest single presentation of the thesis.
The hosted PDF was created in February 2025; Levin’s March 2025
[introductory post](https://thoughtforms.life/platonic-space-where-cognitive-and-morphological-patterns-come-from-besides-genetics-and-environment/)
warns that the preprint is speculative and in flux.

The manuscript makes three connected moves:

1. **From construction to navigation.** Embryos and regenerating tissues are described
   as agents navigating anatomical morphospace rather than executing a fixed list of
   microinstructions.
2. **From surprise to structured latent space.** Unexpected competencies in synthetic
   organisms and minimal algorithms are treated as samples from a nonrandom space that
   can be mapped.
3. **From mathematical form to mind.** If nonphysical mathematical patterns can inform
   physical systems, Levin asks why the same space could not contain dynamic,
   self-referential, and increasingly agential patterns.

Physical bodies, algorithms, embryos, robots, and brains then become interfaces whose
architecture determines which patterns can appear.
Synthetic beings are “periscopes” for exploring nearby regions of the space.
The research agenda includes comparing novel constructs, searching minimal deterministic
systems for goal-directed competencies, relating interface architecture to outcome,
reconsidering memory, and extending the framework to unconventional minds.

The agenda mixes tractable and non-discriminating questions.
Mapping how morphology and behavior vary with cell type, connectivity, geometry, and
environment is ordinary empirical science of a high order.
Asking whether the resulting pattern was physically generated or nonphysically ingressed
requires an additional test.
Most of the proposed measurements characterize the physical interface and its output;
they do not yet distinguish the two ontologies.

### 3.2 The “Free Lunch” Argument

Levin’s cleanest example is a triangle.
If two angles in Euclidean geometry are known, the third is fixed; an evolutionary or
engineered search does not need to optimize it independently.
Likewise, once a system implements a suitable logic gate, the consequences of Boolean
algebra and the computational universality of NAND do not each need to be separately
selected.
Biology exploits network topology, symmetry, prime-number relations, diffusion,
differential adhesion, fractals, and many other structural regularities.

There is a valid insight here: search over a structured space can receive correlated
benefits. But “free” is relative to the accounting boundary.

- Logical implications do not require an additional search once premises and an
  implementation are fixed.
- Building and operating the implementation still has physical and evolutionary costs.
- A capacity can be a by-product or exaptation rather than a directly selected trait.
- Selection can favor general plasticity, making many unencountered situations
  manageable later.
- Biological components are not untrained: their molecular machinery embodies a long
  history even when the assembled organism is new.

The 2025 IEEE conference paper
[“Free Lunch? Low-Cost Intelligence Through Pattern-Guided Exploration”](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICDL63968.2025.11204411)
begins to make the idea quantitative in computational systems.
This is the right direction.
“How much performance is obtained per unit of training, search, or description?”
is a measurable question.
“Did the excess come from a nonphysical space?”
remains a separate inference.

### 3.3 The More Explicit 2026 Formulation

Levin’s
[March 31, 2026 argument](https://thoughtforms.life/a-short-argument-on-platonic-space-variable-agency-patterns-that-in-form-physics-biology-computer-science-and-cognitive-science/)
is more direct than the manuscript.
It says that mathematical facts are nonphysical, that they are causal because changing
them would change physical outcomes, and that physicalism is therefore insufficient.
It proposes a common space ranging from static mathematical facts through dynamical
patterns to kinds of minds.
It also raises the possibility that patterns interact, change through physical
projection, or contribute to memory.

The same essay supplies crucial limits:

- The order and navigability of the space is called a metaphysical stance that Levin
  cannot prove.
- The space’s cardinality, dynamics, temporality, and origin are left open.
- The term “Platonic” is a convenient pointer, not a claim of fidelity to Plato.
- Levin rejects religious and normative conclusions from the proposal.
- The research program is described as rapidly changing.

These admissions do not invalidate the idea, but they set its evidential class.
The literature reviewed through July 14, 2026 contains no peer-reviewed experimental
demonstration that a high-agency pattern exists independently of a physical
implementation, transmits information into it, or predicts a biological outcome better
than physical models.

## 4. What “Mathematics Shapes Biology” Can Mean

Several claims are often compressed into that phrase.
They should be separated.

### 4.1 Mathematical Description

Biological observations can be represented with geometry, statistics, differential
equations, graphs, information measures, and algorithms.
This is indispensable but ontologically neutral.
A coordinate system can reveal a transformation between two forms without being a
literal field inside the embryo.

### 4.2 Physical Law and Constraint

Geometry and physics restrict what tissues can do.
Surface tension, elasticity, viscosity, packing, diffusion, topology, and energy budgets
make some configurations stable and others impossible.
D’Arcy Thompson’s *On Growth and Form* made this structuralist program famous.
Alan Turing then showed that reacting and diffusing morphogens can destabilize a
homogeneous state and generate spatial pattern
([1952](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.1952.0012)). Modern morphogenesis integrates these
mechanisms with gene regulation and active cell behavior.

No separate form has to direct a Turing pattern.
The mathematical structure explains why a class of physical systems behaves as it does
when the relevant parameters are realized.

### 4.3 A Possibility Space or Morphospace

David Raup’s mathematical shell model used a few parameters to define a theoretical
space of coiled forms ([1961](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16578506/)). Occupied and
empty regions can then reveal constraints, selection, historical contingency, or missing
constructions. A morphospace is not necessarily a warehouse of pre-existing objects.
It can be a model-defined state space whose geometry summarizes possible configurations.

Levin adds an agential interpretation: tissues represent goals and navigate the space.
That claim becomes empirical when a model specifies the state variables, sensing,
setpoint, error signal, and interventions.
Without those elements, “navigation” can remain a suggestive redescription of a
trajectory.

### 4.4 Dynamical Attractors

Waddington’s epigenetic landscape is now often formalized as a dynamical system.
Stable cell types or anatomical configurations correspond to attractors; perturbations
can move a system within one basin or across a bifurcation into another.
Noise, feedback, and slow parameter changes reshape the landscape.
Recent work continues to develop rigorous random-dynamical and control formulations
([Vittadello et al. 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40991026/)).

This language fits planarian multistability particularly well.
A bioelectric intervention can change the effective landscape, converting the normal and
double-headed outcomes into alternative stable states.
The attractor is a system-level property of equations and boundary conditions.
Calling it a “target” may improve control, but does not show that it exists separately
from the dynamics.

### 4.5 Mathematical Explanation

Some scientific explanations are genuinely mathematical.
Network topology can make a route impossible; parity can constrain reproduction;
symmetry can imply a conservation law.
Philosophers dispute whether these are noncausal explanations or abstract
representations of causal constraints.
The
[Stanford Encyclopedia survey](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/causal-explanation-science/#NonCauMatExp)
documents both positions.

Levin goes further by treating a counterfactual change in mathematics as causal.
But Pearl’s [causal calculus](https://proceedings.mlr.press/r0/pearl95a.html) defines an
intervention by setting a variable within a structural model while holding specified
mechanisms fixed. Prime-number distribution, Euclidean theorems, or the truth table of
NAND are not ordinarily manipulable variables within a model of cicadas, embryos, or
circuits. Mathematical dependence can explain a pattern without functioning as an extra
input channel.

### 4.6 Mathematical Platonism

Mathematical Platonism holds that abstract mathematical objects exist independently of
human minds and practices.
It is a live position, not a settled consequence of mathematical objectivity.
Formalism, structuralism, nominalism, naturalism, and other realist and anti-realist
positions offer different accounts.
The Quine–Putnam indispensability argument supports ontological commitment to
mathematics only if its controversial premises succeed; the causal isolation of abstract
objects creates a well-known epistemological problem
([SEP overview](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mathphil-indis/)).

Even successful mathematical Platonism would establish neither high-agency forms nor
ingression. Those are additional theses.
The inference chain is:

$$ \text{objective mathematics} \not\Rightarrow \text{abstract causal agents}
\not\Rightarrow \text{pre-existing kinds of minds} \not\Rightarrow \text{biological
access to those minds}. $$

Each arrow needs its own argument and evidence.

## 5. Mainstream and Emerging Mathematical Research

### 5.1 Developmental Bias and Genotype–Phenotype Maps

Evolution does not sample phenotypes uniformly.
Development maps enormous genotype spaces onto a much smaller and highly uneven set of
forms. In RNA secondary-structure models, random sampling of genotypes predicts several
properties of naturally occurring noncoding RNAs surprisingly well, because most
genotypes map into a small part of morphospace
([Dingle et al. 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4633861/)). Models show
that a frequently generated phenotype can arrive before a fitter but rare one, allowing
arrival bias to dominate realistic evolutionary searches
([Schaper and Louis 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3914804/)).

Algorithmic-information arguments predict a **simplicity bias**: many input–output maps
disproportionately produce outputs with short descriptions.
This pattern has been studied in RNA, protein structure, gene-regulatory networks, and
Dawkins-style biomorphs.
Recent work also bounds phenotype-transition probabilities using conditional complexity
([Johnston et al. 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12503940/)).

This literature gives a rigorous meaning to a structured possibility space.
Simple, robust, or highly connected forms may be disproportionately reachable before
selection acts. It is close to Levin’s “free lunch” intuition, but the bias comes from
the combinatorics and physics of a genotype–phenotype map.
No external ingress is needed.

### 5.2 Biological Inherency and Dynamical Patterning Modules

Stuart Newman and collaborators argue that multicellular materials inherit generic
physical pattern-forming capacities—adhesion, polarization, oscillation,
reaction–diffusion, and mechanical phase behavior.
These **dynamical patterning modules** can generate motifs that selection stabilizes and
modifies
([Newman, Bhat, and Mezentseva 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19920341/);
[Newman 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6593199/)). This is another
naturalistic account of “capabilities evolution did not pay for one by one.”
When multicellular organization mobilizes a physical process, a family of forms becomes
available because of material dynamics.

The difference from Platonic Space is causal placement.
Biological inherency locates the affordance in evolved cells interacting under physical
law; Levin regards the physical system as an interface to a pattern that is not
exhausted by those dynamics.
Experiments must distinguish those accounts rather than infer the latter from shared
surprise.

### 5.3 Neural Cellular Automata

Neural cellular automata (NCA) learn one local update rule shared across many cells.
From a seed, a trained NCA can grow a target image, maintain it, and sometimes
regenerate after damage.
The landmark [Growing Neural Cellular Automata](https://distill.pub/2020/growing-ca/)
study by Alexander Mordvintsev, Ettore Randazzo, Eyvind Niklasson, and Levin shows how
global form and repair can arise from compact local policies.
Later isotropic models remove an externally supplied orientation and study spontaneous
symmetry breaking.

NCA are valuable for Levin’s agenda in two ways.
They are fully inspectable minimal systems, and they allow experimental ablation of
state channels, update rules, training curricula, and damage regimes.
But they also demonstrate why surprise is not ontological evidence.
A model can exhibit an untrained side capacity because the learned policy, architecture,
and objective constrain many situations at once.
The capacity is implicit in the physical computation even if the engineer did not
foresee it.

### 5.4 Active Inference and Bayesian Morphogenesis

Levin, Karl Friston, Franz Kuchling, and Georgi Georgiev model morphogenesis as Bayesian
inference: cells act under local signaling to minimize variational free energy and
realize expected sensory states ([2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31320316/)).
This supplies a mathematical language for setpoints, prediction errors, message passing,
and self-organization.
It can unify perception-like and action-like aspects of development.

The framework’s breadth is also its risk.
A model must identify measurable states, generative assumptions, and predictions that
outperform simpler dynamical or control models.
Critics note that the free-energy principle can become a formal redescription of any
system with an attracting steady state unless additional empirical structure is imposed
([Radomski and Dołęga 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11431360/)). Active
inference is therefore a serious nearby framework, not independent confirmation of
Platonic minds.

### 5.5 Natural Induction

In **natural induction**, connections in a dynamical interaction network change under
stress, allowing the system to acquire adaptive organization without a population of
replicators undergoing conventional natural selection.
The precursor model appears in [Buckley et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.3390/e26090765),
followed by Watson, Levin, and Tim Lewens’s
[2025 analysis](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/ddc65375-17d7-4740-9e00-0c90562600fa)
of its evolutionary implications and interactions with selection.

The work is relevant because it separates adaptation from one specific mechanism of
population selection.
Networks can learn within their lifetimes, and evolution can select networks that learn.
It remains an explicit physical dynamical model.
It broadens evolutionary theory without showing that adaptation entered from a
nonphysical possibility space.

### 5.6 Information Structure and Causal Emergence

Information theory offers multiscale quantities for collective organization.
In 2025, Federico Pigozzi, Adam Goldstein, and Levin simulated 29 curated
gene-regulatory networks trained on associative tasks.
An integrated-information-decomposition measure of causal emergence often increased
during training, with heterogeneous patterns across networks
([Communications Biology](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-025-08411-2)). This makes a
specific claim: a macrodescription of the whole can carry predictive information not
available from components considered separately under the chosen decomposition.

Such results argue against crude explanatory reductionism.
They do not violate physical causal closure.
Higher-level variables can be more informative and useful for intervention because
coarse-graining captures stable organization.
“Causal emergence” in this technical sense should not be conflated with a cause outside
the modeled network.

### 5.7 Topology, Geometry, and Compositional Modeling

Two newer areas could make morphospace research more precise:

- **Topological data analysis (TDA)** measures components, loops, cavities, and
  multiscale persistence in complex data.
  It has already been applied to classify heterogeneous tissue patterns and
  induced-pluripotent-stem-cell colonies
  ([TDA of cell populations](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41540-023-00302-8);
  [TDA of colony patterning](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-90592-1)). TDA can
  compare shapes without choosing fragile landmark coordinates.
- **Compositional systems biology** uses explicit interfaces to connect molecular,
  cellular, mechanical, and population submodels.
  Process bigraphs and the Vivarium framework aim to make multiscale models recombinable
  and testable
  ([Agmon et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11312625/)).

These methods address exactly the difficult middle layer between genes and anatomy.
They can reveal invariants, modular interfaces, and transition paths.
Finding such structure would support the empirical claim that biological possibility is
organized; it would not decide whether the organization is immanent in physical
relations or transcendent.

### 5.8 The Platonic Representation Hypothesis in AI

Levin also points to the AI community’s
[Platonic Representation Hypothesis](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/huh24a.html).
Minyoung Huh and colleagues report that representations in some large vision and
language models become more aligned as performance and scale increase, and propose
convergence toward a shared statistical model of reality.
Their paper explicitly discusses limitations and counterexamples.

The name should not create a false equivalence.
Representation convergence can arise because models see correlated human-generated data,
solve related tasks, optimize similar losses, and must encode common environmental
structure. It is evidence about learned statistical geometry, not evidence that models
download an independently agential form.

## 6. Where Levin Fits in Modern Biology

### 6.1 Points of Strong Agreement

Levin’s program aligns with mainstream research on several important points:

- Genomes do not contain a pixel-by-pixel anatomical blueprint.
- Development integrates genetic, biochemical, electrical, mechanical, and environmental
  information.
- Feedback, robustness, plasticity, and multistability are central to morphogenesis.
- Selection acts on developmental systems and therefore on biased production of
  variation, not only on completed adult phenotypes.
- Higher-level variables can be indispensable for prediction and control even when every
  component is physical.
- Novel cell configurations are powerful probes of latent developmental capacities.
- Mathematical models should predict interventions rather than merely retell results.

These are substantial points.
They challenge simplistic genetic determinism but are not outside contemporary evo-devo,
systems biology, or biological physics.

### 6.2 Active but Contested Research Claims

More controversial, but scientifically tractable, claims include:

- Cell collectives are usefully described as cognitive agents rather than only as
  control systems.
- Bioelectric patterns encode high-level anatomical goals rather than local positional
  cues and distributed state.
- Behavioral-science methods will systematically outperform conventional biochemical
  intervention for tissues.
- Goal-directedness and intelligence form a substrate-independent continuum extending
  through cells, algorithms, and engineered matter.
- Natural induction and multiscale competency require material revisions to standard
  evolutionary explanations.

These can be evaluated through comparative prediction, control, and intervention.
Disagreement over vocabulary is less important than whether the framework discovers
capacities and therapies that competing models miss.

### 6.3 Claims Not Established by Current Evidence

Current evidence does not establish that:

- a structured nonphysical realm exchanges information with biological tissue;
- target morphologies exist independently of the systems that realize them;
- Xenobot or Anthrobot capacities cannot be explained by evolved cellular machinery in
  new boundary conditions;
- algorithms have hidden agency beyond the behavior of their physical or formal
  implementations;
- minds pre-exist brains or are selected from a space of autonomous mental forms;
- some memory is stored outside living tissue;
- mathematical counterfactual dependence is causal in Pearl’s interventionist sense;
- the failure to predict an outcome is evidence that its information came from outside
  physics.

These are not minor missing details.
They are the propositions that distinguish the strong Platonic ontology from
naturalistic structuralism.

## 8. A Disciplined Research Program

The Platonic heuristic becomes scientifically valuable to the extent that it produces
predictions that competing accounts do not.

### 8.1 Define the Accounting Boundary

A “free lunch” requires a measured input and output.
For each system, specify:

- inherited structure, pretraining, and evolutionary history
- training examples, optimization steps, energy, and physical resources
- architecture and boundary conditions
- target task and generalization distribution
- a baseline model and a complexity or performance metric

Only then can a capacity be called unexpectedly cheap.
Otherwise, uncounted evolutionary, engineering, or environmental work may be mislabeled
as free.

### 8.2 Predict Before Building

Construct a quantitative map from interface variables—cell type, geometry, connectivity,
voltage state, mechanical constraints, and environment—to distributions over form and
behavior. Register predictions for a new Xenobot, Anthrobot, organoid, or NCA before
observing it. Compare:

1. a detailed mechanistic model;
2. a learned black-box predictor;
3. a control-theoretic or active-inference model;
4. a proposed Platonic-space model.

The Platonic model earns scientific credit only if it supplies additional predictive
structure rather than a retrospective name for residual error.

### 8.3 Search for Cross-Substrate Invariants

If the same pattern is accessible through very different substrates, define what counts
as the same pattern and test whether its transition geometry, error-correction policy,
or learning curve is invariant.
Control for shared datasets, objectives, designer choices, evolutionary homology, and
physical symmetry. Category-theoretic and topological tools may help identify
substrate-independent equivalence classes.

Multiple realizability would support structural realism.
It would still not by itself show that the structure exists as an agent, so the
hypothesis must also predict novel interactions attributable to that agency.

### 8.4 Intervene on Representations and Setpoints

For a tissue claimed to represent a target morphology:

- identify a measurable code or sufficient statistic
- decode the target prospectively from the state
- alter the state while holding relevant local mechanisms controlled
- predict the new endpoint and corrective trajectory
- restore the state and recover the original behavior
- test generalization across injuries and environments

Planarian bioelectric memory already satisfies parts of this program.
Extending it with blinded decoding, causal mediation, and cross-laboratory replication
would strengthen the representational claim.

### 8.5 Separate Constraint, Explanation, and Agency

For every mathematical structure, ask three different questions:

1. Does it delimit physically possible states?
2. Does it compress or explain observed regularities?
3. Does it act as an agent with state, memory, preferences, and counterfactual
   responsiveness?

Evidence for the first two is not automatically evidence for the third.
A high-agency Platonic pattern would need operational marks of agency beyond the fact
that a physical system follows mathematics.

### 8.6 Use Adversarial Alternatives

The strongest naturalistic alternatives should be modeled, not dismissed as “emergence”:

- exaptation of evolved cilia, adhesion, repair, and signaling
- multistable physical dynamics
- generic epithelial mechanics
- developmental bias and neutral-network structure, in which many genotypes share a
  phenotype
- selection for plasticity and robustness
- implicit consequences of a learned algorithm
- observer-dependent coarse-graining
- ordinary model misspecification

If the Platonic framework defeats those alternatives on prediction and intervention, its
evidential status changes.
If it merely encourages scientists to search more widely, it remains a useful heuristic
rather than a confirmed ontology.

## 9. Overall Assessment

### 9.1 What Is Durable

Levin’s most durable contribution is a research style: treat living tissues as competent
systems whose high-level states can sometimes be measured, communicated with, and
reprogrammed. This style has generated real experiments, synthetic organisms, and
computational tools.
It correctly resists both genetic blueprint language and the assumption that knowing
every molecular part automatically supplies the best control variable.

The associated mathematical claim is also durable.
Evolution occurs in structured spaces.
Physical law, topology, network architecture, developmental dynamics, and
genotype–phenotype maps make some forms easy, some rare, and some impossible.
Evolution exploits general competencies and correlated consequences rather than paying
independently for every trait.

### 9.2 What Remains Open

The open scientific problem is to predict the latent competencies of a material from its
history, architecture, and environment.
This is where complex-systems mathematics can materially change biology: identifying
attractors, transition paths, control kernels, invariants, causal macrovariables, and
simplicity biases.

The open philosophical problem is whether such mathematical structure is best understood
as description, immanent organization, abstract reality, or an interacting nonphysical
domain. Current biological experiments do not decide among those options.

### 9.3 Final Judgment

Levin’s experimental work warrants serious attention; the strong Platonic conclusion
warrants serious skepticism.
The right synthesis is neither to dismiss his program because it uses cognitive language
nor to treat surprising morphogenesis as proof of nonphysical minds.

The productive middle position is:

> Biological form is controlled by multiscale physical systems that exploit deep
> mathematical structure.
> Mapping that structure can transform evolutionary theory, regenerative medicine, and
> synthetic biology. A causally active Platonic Space of minds is one possible
> interpretation, but it has not yet produced evidence that distinguishes it from
> naturalistic mathematics of complex systems.
