## Suggested Reading Order

Read the Chaitin and Ruliad reports first for the two programs' definitions and
histories. The foundations-of-physics report assumes that background and focuses on
their intersection rather than repeating it.

## Maintenance

Reports carry research and last-updated dates because the literature and assessments
can change. Revisions should preserve the distinction between proved results,
conditional constructions, analogies, and philosophical claims. New factual claims
should cite primary or authoritative sources near the relevant text.
