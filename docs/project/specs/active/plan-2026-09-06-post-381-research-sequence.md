## Mathematical Discriminators

**Fixed-support density test.** On the declared full-size placement support, use exact
test points certified off every placement boundary, with membership constant on a
positive-area neighborhood.
Their depth constraints are a relaxation of the full almost-everywhere depth problem;
arbitrary closed-boundary samples are not.
Maximize total placement weight under those rows, counting each orbit’s multiplicity
when an orbit variable represents the weight of one image.
The objective coefficient is the orbit’s size; a depth-row coefficient counts its
distinct placements covering that point.
An exact LP upper certificate at most eleven, together with the existing value-eleven
control, closes that support without requiring a complete arrangement checker.
A value above eleven is only a candidate: add violated depth constraints and require the
full BC-243 containment and a.e.-depth proof before claiming a certified dual lower
bound. Freeze the initial rows, deterministic row-generation rule, and any support
extension before measurement.
Select at most one further support family on a stated geometric reason; no unregistered
weight search. Only a sound value above eleven rules out equality density at Trump’s
side. It does not rule out useful full-size densities at smaller container sides.

**Changed relative weights and sites.** Start from retained D4 site orbits and exact
bad-pose rows, transported to the chosen target with their geometry checked.
Minimize covering mass on this finite-row relaxation.
An exact dual floor of eleven rules out a mass-below-eleven certificate on those sites
at the declared side, net, and witness geometry; a mass below eleven only earns global
separation. If new sites are needed, choose them from active witness boundaries or
intersections and compare against an unrelated seed.
A coordinate perturbation that changes no incidence cannot improve the fixed incidence
LP. Repeating the rejected inset/release seed is not a new mechanism.

**Exact basis recovery.** Use the floating active set to propose an exact rational
basis, then check every inactive row and run the global separator.
Select this work when recorded rationalization loss consumes a promising candidate’s
margin. It cannot overcome a certified fixed-model optimum of at least eleven.
Compare total mass divided by the exact minimum coverage, together with containment
slack, rather than celebrating extra unnormalized coverage alone.

**Richer witnesses.** A rational angle-cell polygon must improve covered positive mass
at a limiting pose, not merely have larger area.
An existential menu must prove that every unit-square pose in a whole closed pose box
admits at least one strictly interior heavy witness.
This is the quantified statement “for every pose, there exists a witness”; a sampled
menu is insufficient.
Nested concentric sizes add nothing beyond their largest member when that member is
admissible throughout the pose domain.
These small theory tests may be designed before the global verifier; BC-235/236’s
implementation prerequisites remain in force.

**Restricted structural theorem.** Choose one complete angle-composition or wall-support
case statement and attempt an exact LP/Farkas or interval exclusion.
A wall-support signature names which square, feature, and angle class provides each
outer support; it is more informative than a wall-contact count.
Preserve missing cases explicitly.
Use a known-feasible case to test the model and one adversarial search to challenge the
conjecture. A restricted theorem can justify the block; an unpriced `n=11` atlas cannot.
