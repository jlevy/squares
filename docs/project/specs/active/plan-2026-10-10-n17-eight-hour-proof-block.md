# n17 Eight-Hour Proof Block

**Prepared, not launched.** The objective is mathematical distance toward a complete n17
proof: discharge a required obligation, exclude a new part of the domain, or derive a
stronger exact constraint that narrows the remaining route.
Capture and terminal work (BC-456/457) and nonterminal exclusions or stronger domain
constraints (BC-460) are co-primary from kickoff.
Existing results may be named, scoped premises while independent confirmation remains
pending; new artifacts still need their first soundness decision, replay and admission
checks before their conclusions enter the proof or official census.

conditional further eight-hour blocks, each replanned from this block’s evidence.

[operating rules](../../../../operating-rules.md),
[session contract](../../../../packing/campaign/agent-sessions/README.md) and

bounded nonterminal result or complete diagnosis of its frozen domain, with the

separately, with exact remaining obligations and defined coverage, never a percentage of
the proof complete. Every selected branch declares its frozen purpose and mathematical
obligation, even where the record has no dedicated proof field.
