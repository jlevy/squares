`s(35) = 6`. Established by an unavoidable point set, Erich Friedman (1999).

## The packing

Found by Erich Friedman in 1999, via a hand construction.

## The lower bound

Proved by exhibiting an **unavoidable set**: a set of points in the container that every
unit square placed inside must contain.
With `n − 1` such points, `n` disjoint squares are impossible by pigeonhole.
