# `s(88)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(88) ≤ 9.88815305`, and the best proved lower bound
is `9.42615` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a gap of `0.462`. General closed
form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N - 2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The packing

Found by David Ellsworth in 2024, via a hand construction.
Its side length is algebraic of degree **20** over `ℚ`. As at `n = 37`, the rational
polynomial is not the sharpest form available: over `ℚ(√2)` the archived catalogue
records a degree-10 factor, whose real root `9.88815305375857238274` was checked here
against the recorded value.

## The lower bound

Nothing specific to this `n` has ever been proved.
The bound is Nagamochi’s general closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

This is the default across almost the whole open frontier — 63 of the 65 open cases at
`n ≤ 100` are governed by it, and it has not been improved since 2005.
