# `s(3)` — solved

`s(3) = 2`. Established by an elementary counting argument.

![The exact quotient map of optimal configurations for three unit squares.](../atlas/n-003-optimal-moduli.svg)

*The full proved optimum is a configuration space rather than one privileged pose: two
labelled cycles reduce to one quotient interval.*

## The packing

The record catalogue does not picture `n = 3`: no arrangement has ever been found that
beats the trivial `⌈√3⌉ = 2` grid, so the grid is still the best known packing.
That is a statement about what has been searched, not a proof.

## The complete optimal configuration space

[Exp-014](../campaign/series/series-000-smoke-and-calibration/experiments/exp-014-h-032-n3-optimal-moduli.md)
excludes genuinely rotated side-2 packings and enumerates the full axis-aligned space.
The labelled space is two circles, the unlabelled space one circle, and the `D4 x S3`
quotient the interval `[0,1/2]`. Its exact
[quotient and stratum map](../atlas/n-003-optimal-moduli.svg) is a permanent
known-answer control for later landscape visualizations.
