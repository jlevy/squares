# `s(37)` — open

Open. The best known packing gives `s(37) ≤ 6.59861961`, and the best proved lower bound
is `6.09902` from Nagamochi’s general theorem, leaving a gap of `0.4996`. General closed
form: s(N) >= min(ceil(sqrt(N)), sqrt(N - 2*floor(sqrt(N)) + 1) + 1).

## The packing

Found by David W. Cantrell in 2002, via a hand construction.
Its side length is algebraic of degree **8** over `ℚ`.

## The lower bound

Nothing specific to this `n` has ever been proved.
The bound is Nagamochi’s general closed form, which applies to every `N ≥ 4`:

```
s(N) ≥ min{ ⌈√N⌉,  √(N − 2⌊√N⌋ + 1) + 1 }
```

This is the default across almost the whole open frontier — 63 of the 65 open cases at
`n ≤ 100` are governed by it, and it has not been improved since 2005.
