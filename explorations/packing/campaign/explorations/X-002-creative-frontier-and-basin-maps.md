# X-002 — creative frontier and honest basin maps

This exploration is the provenance pointer for the final creativity review.
It does not repeat that review.
Its role is to connect the broad argument to the sixteen registry artifacts compressed
from it.

The decisive correction was conceptual: an endpoint key, an algorithmic attraction
class, a mathematical terminal family, an active stratum, and a feasible-sublevel
component are different objects.
The hypotheses split along those boundaries.
The portfolio likewise separates local geometry, global proof, stochastic search,
construction genealogy, and asymptotics so failure in one culture does not collapse the
whole program into a larger annealing run.

The first rotation is fast-first.
Every mechanism receives its smallest known-answer or falsifier cell before hour-scale
compute. Promotion requires a retained valid witness, a response in the mechanism’s own
observable, and a named reason the next budget rung could cross a decision threshold.
