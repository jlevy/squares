check_round_effort_claims in devtools/check_synopsis.py requires SYNOPSIS.md to contain the sentence 'There are N terminal rounds registered in series-000', where N is taken from the ledger footer's TOTAL round count. The ledger's own breakdown separates terminal verdicts from in-progress ones: today it lists rejected 10, unresolved 4, blocked 4, accepted 15, baseline 11 = 44 terminal, plus 1 in-progress (exp-045), total 45.

So whenever any round is in-progress, the checker forces SYNOPSIS to assert a number of 'terminal rounds' that is one or more too high. SYNOPSIS currently says 45 terminal rounds; 44 are terminal.

This is a small factual defect in the campaign's most-read durable summary, and it is enforced rather than merely tolerated, so it cannot be fixed by editing SYNOPSIS alone.

Fix options: (a) compare against the terminal-round count and let the narrative keep the word 'terminal'; or (b) keep comparing against the total and change the required wording to 'registered rounds'. (a) preserves the sentence's meaning and is preferred.

Found while repairing a related dead negative control (see notes on controls.yaml anchor rot in the same commit).
