Found in review A of jlevy/squares#1010 (A1, https://github.com/jlevy/squares/pull/1010#issuecomment-6962745009) and while addressing it (think-4kry). The record credits a seed two ways: by the seed's whole credit line (T-226 after T-186, T-198 after T-227, T-243 after T-188, and since #1010 T-287 after T-243), or by the seed's house holder alone. The coordinator ruled on 17 November 2026 that attribution.md's Credit Text and Lineage (lines 100-105, 'A chain through an intermediate author retains every supported link') governs, and #1010 applied it to T-287 because its seed lies on #1008's chain. Lines seen to follow the holder-only form, not on that chain: T-303 at 90 (jazivxt after de Winter, a crop of T-293, whose line is de Winter after Samanyu-dev); [Özden s172 upper bound 2026-11-09] (T-282, Ozden after Daniel, Levy; a further arrangement of Daniel's T-257, whose lineage Daniel states starts at Ostashev's s(176)); [Özden s269 upper bound 2026-11-11] (T-290, Ozden after Daniel, Levy; a strip cut of the registry's 304 by Daniel). Not checked: T-284, T-300 and other Özden transplant lines. Do: for each line whose source names a registry seed, read the seed's credit line, decide by the chain rule whether the seed's antecedents are supported links, and carry them (bibliography credit and note, claim sentence, contributor_census --reconcile, release record, re-pin); or record why not.
