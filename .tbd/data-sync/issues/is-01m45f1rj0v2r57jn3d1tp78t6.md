Review B finding B3 on jlevy/squares#354 (https://github.com/jlevy/squares/pull/354#pullrequestreview-5411026306), at head f19ab81bd. current_memory_bytes() raises OSError on darwin by design (packing/devtools/process_memory.py:55); packing/tests/test_process_memory.py:21 and :26 call it unconditionally, so a macOS developer's push tier fails there (CI's macOS job does not run these tests). Fix: pytest.mark.skipif(sys.platform not in {'linux', 'win32'}, ...) on those two tests.
