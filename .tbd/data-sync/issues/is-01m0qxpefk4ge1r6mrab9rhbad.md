Category: creative alternatives. Combine delta-inflation, container aspect-ratio and superdisk continuations with MAP-Elites or novelty search and explicit packing-surgery emitters that add, remove, straighten, duplicate or splice motifs from neighboring n. Archive descriptors such as angle classes, contact topology, wall contacts and LP basis.

Acceptance: each emitter is a runnable proposer behind one interface, continuation branches log bifurcations and branch switches, and descriptors are versioned and resistant to quantization artifacts. Compare the portfolio with random restart at equal pair-test budget on known-answer and mechanism-matched controls before n = 11, 12, 61, 78 or 97. Reconcile with think-v2m1, think-v9bp, think-gfqb, think-c7mb, and think-nt5w.
