Known defect in the v2-transitions prototype, recorded in revisions 9, 11 and 12 and deferred each time.

The collision routine is written twice: collide on the tween path and optCollide on the optimise path. Revision 11 made it worse rather than better -- both now carry the force law, the relationship mask lookup and the square size, written out twice -- and revision 12 recorded that the mask lookup they both carry is now reading a graph a pointer can change mid-run.

The fix named in the notes each time is a single shared collide(ctx, i, j) that both paths call.

Why it is worth doing now rather than fourth time of asking. Everything the force law gains from here -- a torque term is already the named prerequisite for the contact-graph work -- has to be written into both copies, and a law that differs between the two paths would make a Pack run and an optimise run of the same settings disagree for a reason nobody would look for. The duplication is currently the highest-leverage thing standing between the prototype and any further physics.

Prototype: packing/atlas/known-best/video/spikes/v2-transitions/. Notes: NOTES.md, revisions 9, 11, 12.
