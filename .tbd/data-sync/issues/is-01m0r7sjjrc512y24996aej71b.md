Specify visualization-driven questions and encodings for the basin atlas: canonical contact type, symmetry orbit, terminal-manifold dimension, adjacency and observed transitions, continuation across n and parameter regimes, objective gap, proposer-conditioned frequency with uncertainty, and representative geometry. Treat layout and embedding as exploratory evidence, never proof of topology.

Acceptance: a view specification gives each panel a mathematical question, data contract, invariant quantities, uncertainty display, falsifiable use case, and misleading-interpretation warning; at least three views lead to registered follow-up hypotheses on calibration data; and the design separates observed samples, inferred relationships, and certified facts.
