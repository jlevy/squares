The stage 4 reviews of 9 November (#927–#931, #935/#936, #938) and 11 November (#943–#947; #954 F10) route "whether the upstream starts need notices of their own" to think-mdxq. That bead settled only the licence of Doğa Özden's own repositories (CC BY 4.0 data, MIT code) and closed on 4 November, so the question has no owner.

Sharpest case: #954's seed band (T-291, house at 179) is the registry's n = 70 packing: Ryan Xu's (licence open, think-oq4l), improved by Evan Daniel (MIT) and PingYou Ltd. PingYou's PR 574 algebraic supplement has no licence file and is retained here derived-only, asserting no redistribution permission (PR held under think-9v2k). #942's and #958's starts (Samanyu-dev's merge of public Kaggle notebooks, no licence stated) raise the same question.

Owner decision:
1. Does a packing seeded from another's coordinates and then moved by search need any notice beyond the record's credit?
2. If so, should the project ask Özden, de Winter and/or PingYou Ltd for a notice or permission?
3. Does think-cnn7's presumption of permission cover retention in the meantime?

This holds no import, rung, adoption or reply.

Resolution: record the decision in the affected packets' READMEs and declarations, and repoint every note that cites think-mdxq for an upstream start (results.yaml, result-requests.yaml, bibliography.yaml, intake-watch.yaml, the Özden packet READMEs).
