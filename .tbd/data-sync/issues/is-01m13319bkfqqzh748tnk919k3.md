The numeric half of the round trip, and the half that decides whether the symbolic chain generalizes.

SYNOPSIS names it under D-021: the float LP solver has a noise floor of about 1e-11 in the side, no numerical comparison may claim a difference finer than that floor, and 'the general fix is an exact LP over certified rational or algebraic coefficients, which is unbuilt'. It is purely rational only for rational-coefficient cells.

Why it matters for promotion: on a published high-precision pose the contact structure is unambiguous -- the n=29 reconstruction separates contact from non-contact by about 99 orders of magnitude. On a pose this project quenches, the 1e-11 floor is exactly the ambiguity that makes contact inference a guess. Without exact LP the symbolic chain only ever consumes someone else's data.

This does not gate the n=29 result, which needs no LP of ours. It gates generality.

Related: D-052, coordinatewise stopping is not a certified local optimum.
