Epic. The shared pipeline every proposer runs on: LP-in-cell quench, canonical basin identity, descriptors, the atlas artifact, and the pair-test meter. scipy plus standard library - no Rust, no FFI, no build step, because the work is quench-dominated at ~780 candidates/s/core and that rate is the same in any language. Until this exists 'basin' is undefined and no two search strategies are comparable. First phase, and the cheapest.
