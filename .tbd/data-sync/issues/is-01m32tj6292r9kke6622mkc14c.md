Owner's report: during a transition, while the physics jiggles squares off their axis-aligned orientation, they keep the green of an axis-aligned square. The colour says 'this square is at 0 degrees' while the picture shows it tilted, so for the length of the shake the two disagree.

The identity scheme colours a square by its tilt ANGLE CLASS, and the class is the one the record assigns, held fixed across the transition so a square keeps its identity while it moves. That is deliberate -- recolouring every frame would make the picture strobe as angles drift -- but it means the colour is briefly a claim the frame contradicts.

The owner wants both behaviours available: a setting, exposed in the UI, for whether a square's colour is preserved across a transition or follows the angle it is actually drawn at, so the two can be compared on the real corpus before one is chosen.

See `fillOf` and the identity/angle schemes in `packages/workbench/src/view/colour.ts`, and `setColorScheme` in the public API. The setting belongs beside the existing view toggles (THE VIEW: colour, animate rests in angle colours, desaturate while moving), and needs a capture-control command so a captured video can be cut either way.
