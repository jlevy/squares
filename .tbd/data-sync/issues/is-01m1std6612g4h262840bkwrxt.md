Measured by a Playwright/axe-core audit, 2026-09-05.

Figure 4's atom inspection is bound to pointermove and pointerdown only; there is no keydown listener on any canvas and all three report tabIndex -1, so the prompt 'Hover or tap an atom for its position and weight' names a function a keyboard user cannot invoke, and no textual list of the 1,121 atoms exists anywhere on the page. Figures 5 and 6 are NOT affected in the same way and should not be lumped in: figure 6's drag only sets phi and #phi-19-5 sets the same value (ArrowRight x10 moved phi 19.600 -> 20.600); figure 5's slider plus 'Tightest placement' plus 'Scan this direction' reach the extremal placements that matter (k=90 gave mass 1.010530, verdict COVERS >= 1; Enter on Scan returned 1.000060 into the hint text).

Separately, all three canvases have no accessible name at all: CDP Accessibility.getFullAXTree shows three role=Canvas nodes with name '', no role, no aria-label, and no fallback children. The figcaptions are substantive and are in the tree as ordinary text, but nothing associates one with its graphic, and the captions are qualitative where the pictures are quantitative. Figures 1, 3 and 7 are done properly and are the model: alt of 196 chars on 1, role=img plus aria-label of 150 chars giving all three numbers on 3, and 130 chars enumerating every plotted point on 7.

Where: packing/devtools/templates/explainer-article.md lines 198, 249, 328 for the markup; the handlers are in explainer-shell.html.
