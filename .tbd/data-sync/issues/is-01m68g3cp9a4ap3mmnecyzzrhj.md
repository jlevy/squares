The owner's ruling, asked on 31 October 2026 (review A4 of #849; the coordinator puts it to the owner): does exactness alone earn an exact view the default picture when nothing visible changes?

At 55, 290 and 291 the runs of 31 October 2026 (think-yk2h; packing/cases/regularized_drawings/think-yk2h-2026-10-31/) drew views that are exactly verified on both routes at U, one to one with the numerically checked decimal Kingbird witness, and that tie the house drawing on every measure of the improvement rule. On the rule's goal "exactness" they are better (policy section 11; the selection's note "an exactly verified view at most U may replace it with no measure better"). regularize_select adopts only row-10 `admit`, and each of these is row 10 `no-change` ("nothing visible changes"), so none is drawn and SETTLED records them decided-no-better. At 39 a view that also moved squares (square 26 turned) was admitted on exactness, as at 37, 41, 50 and 54.

The question reaches beyond these three counts: any numerically checked house whose exact view moves nothing gets the same treatment.

Options: (a) keep today's reading: row 10 no-change keeps the house picture; (b) let an exactness gain over a numerically checked house admit a no-change view, which changes regularize_select's adoptable set and redraws 55, 290 and 291 (and any like them) from their exact views.
