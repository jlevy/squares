The owner (2026-09-22): 128 n show an upper bound the register reports from the catalogue but has not certified (think-n56i), and the stage now marks them reported. Turning a reported bound into a verified one is repeatable work: take the next batch from a queue, certify each construction the way the 19 certified ones were (interval or exact certificates), register the result, and move the bound to verified. Define that as a validation block that can be scheduled on its own, with a queue ordered by value and difficulty, a stop condition per block, and what each block leaves in the record, so it can be run a block at a time until nothing is left reported.
