In flight in the v2-transitions prototype as of 2026-09-08.

The mode that renders a range of n at speed with nobody intervening is called Sweep. Rename it Animate everywhere: the visible label, the mode identifier in the page state, setMode and mode() and anything keyed on the string, the trajectory cache keys if they carry it, the test needles in test_candidate.py and check_workbench.py, and the prose in NOTES.md that introduces it.

The reason is not cosmetic. The third mode, Calibrate, is the mode that sweeps -- over parameters rather than over n -- and an instrument with two modes called sweep would be permanently ambiguous about which axis was moving. Animate says what the mode does and leaves the word for the thing that does it.

Leave a one-line note in NOTES.md recording the old name, so a reader of an earlier revision's stills is not lost. The atlas video plan and X-025 both use Animate as of 2026-09-08 and neither should have to carry a translation table.
