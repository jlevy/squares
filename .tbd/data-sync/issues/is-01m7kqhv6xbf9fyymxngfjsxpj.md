Asked: 'Should that count as a new result that is registered, credited and made the best packing? 27 counts are waiting on this answer.' Owner's answer: "Only if it beats the gap-free original (Recommended)". Option D of the 17 November dossier: a re-certification of a standing packing (padding removed, or the same arrangement re-solved to its exact local optimum) is a new result only if its side is below the predecessor's side once the predecessor is itself shrunk until its squares first touch, measured through any construction (devtools.contraction_margin); otherwise it is a restatement credited to the original finder. Applies at every count, extending the 23 October ruling above 324.
