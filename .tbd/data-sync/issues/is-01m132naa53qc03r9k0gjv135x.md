The step the first draft of agenda-005 omitted, and a two-minute probe showed it is on the critical path rather than beside it.

Running mpmath.pslq directly on the serialized n=29 side value returns relations at almost every degree from 8 to 21 -- the signature of an under-determined search, where the coefficient budget rather than the mathematics decides where relations appear. The degree-8 candidate has a relative residual of 1.26e-90 against roughly 98 available digits: it consumed almost exactly the (8+1)*10 = 90 digits the search was allowed. A genuine minimal polynomial would vanish to the full input precision.

So 98 digits cannot identify the minimal polynomial, and no processing of the serialized value alone will change that.

More digits require Newton refinement of the closed contact system, which means the system must exist first. That forces the order: contact structure (already done, 89 unambiguous incidences), assemble and close (think-va53), refine here, then solve (think-3lro) with real margin, then certify against the built exact back end.

Scope: given a closed contact system and the serialized pose as a seed, refine to a declared precision -- 1000+ digits is the target -- with a residual bound reported rather than assumed. Precision is the deliverable; no algebraic claim is made here.
