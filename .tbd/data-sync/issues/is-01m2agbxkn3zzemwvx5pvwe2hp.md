After the weighted five-site format is admitted, freeze one five-site motif and compare its cost on the complete geometrically realizable core-trace universe against all 80 ordinary threshold types on the same sites. Exact realizing cores and an exact domination cost above one establish a local certificate-language advantage. An ordinary mixture of cost at most one over the complete trace universe removes that advantage. Partial trace enumeration, numerical separation, or a single favorable core is unresolved. Retain the exact trace manifest, primal/dual witnesses, independent replay, and the scope boundary; this does not by itself prove a global lower bound.
