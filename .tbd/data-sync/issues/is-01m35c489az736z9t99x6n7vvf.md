The owner (2026-09-22), looking at n = 29: 'I see reported and confirmed both on the same bound e.g. n=29. And they're formatted differently. Can we format them the same way and put these as gray, but in parentheses always? Or is there a more sensible format? Let's just be consistent and clear.' The stage drew two annotations of one bound in two shapes: 'reported' as a bare grey word after the reference, and '(confirmed, T-009)' baked into the reference itself, which at n = 29 -- the one bound that is both -- read as two contradictory marks. They do not contradict: reported is the register's standing on the bound, confirmed is this project's own checking. The record now composes one note per bound, '(reported)', '(confirmed T-009)' or '(reported; confirmed T-009)', the stage draws it in one grey span after the reference, and the width the line is checked against counts it.
