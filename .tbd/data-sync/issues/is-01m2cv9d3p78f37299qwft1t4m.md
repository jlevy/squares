Review repair format, the spine the other reader findings depend on. Ordinary/all-ones atoms retain {points, threshold, weight} EXACTLY. Weighted atoms use {variant: 'weighted-threshold/v1', weighted_points: [[x_string, y_string, positive_integer_count], ...], threshold, weight} with NEITHER legacy points NOR additive multiplicities. Reject ambiguous or malformed shapes. No compatibility shim for this unmerged prototype. An unweighted-only admission reader must refuse the declared weighted variant even if its counts are all ones. Touches threshold.py serialisation, admit_threshold_atom_orbits.py, decide_threshold_certificate.py, replay_weighted_atom_source.py, cases/n11_threshold_certificate/verify_claim.py and their tests.

## Notes

Verified 2026-09-13T07:58Z, before integration: the hard refusal of the record key 'multiplicities' does NOT break any retained evidence, and the reason is worth keeping because it is easy to get backwards.

'multiplicities' is pre-existing Chvatal-Gomory floor-atom vocabulary in this repo, not a name this PR coined. Two retained files carry it ALONGSIDE 'points' and 'threshold':
  packing/campaign/series/series-000-smoke-and-calibration/results/agenda-034/lane-a4-seed-atoms-k4k5.json
    keys: budget, charged, family, floor_charge, floor_violation, multiplicities, orbit_size, points, source, threshold, total_multiplicity
  .../lane-a6-seed-atoms-from-certificate.json
    keys: floor_charge, multiplicities, points, source, threshold
Six objects, two distinct key shapes. No data file anywhere in the repo uses 'weighted_points' or 'weighted-threshold', confirming the review's premise that the prototype is unmerged and needs no compatibility shim.

The two shapes are distinct and only one changed:
1. ThresholdAtom.to_record/from_record -- the atom serialisation. 'multiplicities' refused by name here; weighted atoms use {variant, weighted_points, threshold, weight}.
2. The retained seed-atom/plateau RECEIPTS above -- a producer's own output, consumed as input. They keep 'multiplicities' and 'points', they must keep reading as they do, and their bytes are not rewritten.

replay_weighted_atom_source.py line ~118 already builds the atom through the CONSTRUCTOR, not from_record, so the receipt path is unaffected. The constructor still takes a multiplicities argument and must continue to. The tool's own EMITTED record at line ~232 is the part that moves to the new shape.

The failure mode avoided: adding the refusal to the receipt-reading path would make the replay instrument refuse the very evidence it exists to replay. Lane C warned explicitly.

A side benefit of the review's rename: 'weighted_points' removes the collision with floor-atom 'multiplicities', so the two concepts no longer share a field name.
