The rung and its use become visible, labelled settings in the workbench, because a structure-informed run must never read as discovery.

- **Pack**: a rung selector and use toggles for one run, with the hint drawn on the stage (partition colours, contact edges, merged blocks), so the owner can watch what the run was given and what it did with it.
- **Search**: rung and use as sweep axes beside seeds and the shake dial, with the rewired and thinned controls and the success-against-rung chart.
- **Labels**: every readout names its rung, for example "given the contact graph and the record side".

Depends on the Search mode (think-vhgz) and the benchmark cells.
