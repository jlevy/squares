The pull-request `validate` job's checks tier has a 195 s ceiling, and since `main` (the n = 11 stack, #157 to #172) merged into the workbench stack it runs at the ceiling:

| run | head | checks tier | largest steps | outcome |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 34912025099 | #125 `79cac315` | 205.8 s | exact verification 126.4 s, type floor 114.8 s | failed; passed on rerun |
| 34912572369 | #160 `72629c03` | 200.7 s | exact verification 121.2 s, type floor 100.4 s | failed; rerun pending |

A gate that passes or fails on runner variance certifies nothing either way. Per `OR-13` and `OR-14`, the fix is measured, not a bigger number:
- clock the tier at its declared reference shape (`packing/devtools/gate-budgets.yaml`, `checks` entry) on the merged tree;
- find which of main's new steps pushed `exact verification` from its recorded ~62-80 s to ~121-126 s;
- either move an unavoidably slow step off the pull-request surface with its own measurement, split the tier onto another runner as `geometry` was, or speed up the step;
- record the new reading in the budget file.

This is stack-wide infrastructure, so it belongs to the lowest PR (#125) or to main directly.
