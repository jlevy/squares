The witness field `claim.assurance` (enum: reported | numerically-checked | verified, with `method`) describes the provenance of the COORDINATES in that one record: whether they are exact algebraic values, multiprecision decimals passing a feasibility check, or carried from a source unchecked. It is set purely by source type in sqpack/known_best.py: exact_grid_witness() sets verified/exact-algebraic, kingbird_derived_witness() sets numerically-checked/numerical-multiprecision.

The name and the vocabulary say something else. 'assurance', and the EvidenceTier it feeds (candidate / numerically-checked / certified-upper-bound / proved-optimum), are all the vocabulary of epistemic status of a mathematical claim. Consumers read it as 'how well do we know s(n)'.

Concrete harm, observed: while building the n=1-100 composite an agent derived figure badges from EvidenceTier and labelled n=5 -- proved optimal, s = 2 + sqrt(2)/2 -- as 'numerically verified'. 19 cases have a closed-form exact side in the frontier while their witness says numerically-checked: n = 5, 10, 18, 19, 26, 27, 38, 40, 50, 52, 53, 65, 66, 67, 82, 84, 85, 86, 89. Two of those (5, 10) are proved optimal. EvidenceTier.PROVED_OPTIMUM is never produced by this pipeline at all, so reading the witness for optimality is silently always wrong; optimality lives in frontier/n-NNN.md packing.status (35 proved).

Not unsound -- no witness asserts a false statement, and claim.limitations does say 'direct retained numerical center/angle facts' -- but it converts an encoding detail into an apparent knowledge claim, and did so in a published figure on first contact with a renderer.

Done when:
- claim.assurance is renamed to something encoding-scoped (e.g. coordinate_provenance) or the witness gains an explicit side_exact_form so consumers stop reaching for assurance.
- EvidenceTier stops being fed from assurance for anything user-facing, or PROVED_OPTIMUM is removed from the enum since this pipeline cannot produce it.
- A packing-validate step cross-checks the layers: if the frontier records an exact form or minimal polynomial for s(n), flag a witness that carries only numerics. That would flag the 19 today.

Interim mitigation already landed: witness.schema.yaml documents the field's real meaning and its limits, and the composite now derives its badges from the frontier (status, exact_form/minimal_polynomial) rather than from the witness.
