PR #149's image wait catches every image.decode rejection and returns success. A consistently undecodable required atlas image can therefore be absent from both renders while the deterministic PDF check passes. Define the intended browser behavior, fail with an actionable diagnostic when a required image is neither decoded nor complete with usable dimensions, add focused controls, and keep the D-490 scope limited to diagnosability rather than claiming the PDF race is solved.
