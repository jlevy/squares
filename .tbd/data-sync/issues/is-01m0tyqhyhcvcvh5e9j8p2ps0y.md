Define the smallest general witness form and a documented command path to inspect, numerically check, formally verify, and attempt promotion of new square packings. Enforce n and shape, check every pair and containment, require arithmetic, actual precision, tolerance, and margins for numerical checks, emit nonzero status on malformed, incomplete, unsupported, or failed input, and require a replayable formal artifact for verification. Do not specialize the contract to the Schadt n=29 files.
