Own the public witness interchange and the documented inspect, numerically check, formally verify, and promote entry points. Enforce n and shape, check every pair and containment, require arithmetic, actual precision, tolerance, and margins for new numerical checks, and emit nonzero status on malformed, incomplete, unsupported, or failed input. Formal verification requires replayable exact or rigorous evidence and an explicit external or repository origin. Consume the low-level certificate type from toolkit bead think-0md2; do not duplicate the search or verifier core and do not specialize the contract to n=29.
