Category: tractable open questions. Test whether record contact graphs and angle-class patterns across common n are generated by a small grammar of local surgeries, and separately measure whether rigidity, algebraic degree or contact structure predicts basin-entry probability for a specified proposer and quench.

Acceptance: derive descriptors from the verified geometry corpus; preregister models and hold out n values; distinguish geometric invariants from algorithm-dependent probabilities; validate proposed surgeries by producing and polishing neighboring packings; and retain falsified relations. The result may be a constructive family, a predictor with out-of-sample uncertainty, or a documented negative theorem for a restricted grammar.

## Notes

H-030 supplies held-out public-parent surgery data; H-038 supplies exact field/mechanism metadata. Cross-n grammar is a verified genealogy, not neighbor seeding or inherited scalar metadata.
