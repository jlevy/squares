Read the generated citation JSON into the page build and draw, under the stage's PROVEN values, a CITATION head in the section-head style and one short line per cited bound. Off or on like the stage's other information (a control in the settings, a field in the API's state, persisted like the others), omitted for an n with nothing to cite, and handed over between n with the facts panel's own crossfade. Hold it with check_layout (fits the column at every review viewport, clears what is below it, the head in the heads' type) and check_animate_view (drawn only where the data has a line, and nowhere when the setting is off).
