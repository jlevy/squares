Mechanism descriptors computed from canonical data only, never raw float state: distinct-tilt-class count, contact-graph class, oblique-core size, boundary-vs-interior contact split. Versioned alongside the atlas as a soft-schema artifact so QD archive keys and atlas identities cannot drift. Single scalars are hackable - the grid maximizes contact count - so descriptors must be used in separating combinations, tilt-class x contact-class at minimum.
